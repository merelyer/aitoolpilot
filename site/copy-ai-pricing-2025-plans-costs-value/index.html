Detailed Review of Each Copy.ai Plan

1. Free Plan ($0/month)

The Free plan is a generous entry point. You get 2,000 words per month, access to all 90+ copywriting tools, and the ability to create up to 5 projects. It's perfect for testing the waters — write a few social media posts, draft an email sequence, or generate some product descriptions.

Pros:

  • No credit card required
  • Full access to tool library
  • Good for low-volume users

Cons:

  • Very limited word count (2,000 words flies by)
  • No Brand Voice or priority support
  • No API access

2. Starter Plan ($49/month)

The Starter plan bumps your word count to 40,000 words per month and adds priority support. But here's the kicker: you still don't get the Brand Voice feature — that's locked behind Pro. For $49/month, I'd expect more. The Starter plan feels like a cash grab to push you toward Pro.

Pros:

  • 40,000 words — enough for a small team
  • Priority email support

Cons:

  • No Brand Voice (huge miss)
  • No API access
  • Expensive for what you get

3. Pro Plan ($49/month — wait, same price?)

Yes, you read that right. The Pro plan is also $49/month (billed monthly) — but it includes unlimited words, Brand Voice (up to 3 brands), API access, and priority support. This is where Copy.ai shines. Unlimited words alone makes this a steal compared to competitors like Jasper, which charges $49 for only 50,000 words.

Pros:

  • Unlimited words — no cap
  • Brand Voice (customize tone and style)
  • API access for integrations
  • Best value for money

Cons:

  • Still no advanced analytics or team collaboration
  • Brand Voice limited to 3 brands

4. Enterprise Plan (Custom Pricing)

For large teams, Copy.ai offers custom Enterprise pricing. You get everything in Pro plus dedicated account manager, custom integrations, advanced analytics, SSO/SAML, and unlimited Brand Voices. Pricing isn't public — you'll need to contact sales. Expect to pay several hundred dollars per month.

Pros:

  • Tailored to large organizations
  • Security features (SSO, audit logs)
  • Priority support

Cons:

  • Expensive (no transparency)
  • Overkill for small teams

Who Should Use What

Freelancers & Solopreneurs

Go with the Pro plan. The unlimited words mean you can write as many social posts, emails, and landing pages as you need without worrying about overages. Brand Voice ensures your content stays consistent across client projects.

Small Marketing Teams

Again, Pro is the sweet spot. You get unlimited words for the entire team (shared pool), but note that there's no team collaboration features like shared workspaces — you'll need to manage access manually. If you need team features, consider Enterprise.

Large Enterprises & Agencies

You'll need the Enterprise plan for SSO, audit logs, and dedicated support. The custom pricing can be negotiated, but expect to pay $500+/month for a team of 10+.

Budget-Conscious Beginners

Start with the Free plan. Use it for a month to see if Copy.ai fits your workflow. If you hit the word cap, upgrade directly to Pro — skip Starter entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Copy.ai really free?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month. No credit card required. It's great for testing, but most users will hit the limit quickly.

2. How much does Copy.ai cost per month?

Copy.ai has four plans: Free ($0), Starter ($49/month), Pro ($49/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). Note that Starter and Pro are the same price, but Pro offers unlimited words and Brand Voice.

3. Does Copy.ai have a word limit?

Yes, on the Free plan (2,000 words/month) and Starter plan (40,000 words/month). The Pro plan has unlimited words, and Enterprise is also unlimited.

4. Can I cancel Copy.ai anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no long-term contracts. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

5. Is Copy.ai cheaper than Jasper?

For the Pro plan, Copy.ai ($49/month, unlimited words) is cheaper than Jasper's Creator plan ($49/month, 50,000 words). Copy.ai also offers a free tier, which Jasper doesn't have.

6. Does Copy.ai offer a student discount?

Copy.ai does not publicly offer a student discount. However, you can use the free plan indefinitely, which may be sufficient for students.

7. What payment methods does Copy.ai accept?

Copy.ai accepts major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. Enterprise plans may offer invoicing.
