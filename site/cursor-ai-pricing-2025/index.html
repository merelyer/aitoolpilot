Cursor AI Pricing Plans Explained

Cursor offers four main tiers: Free (Hobby), Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Here's what you get with each.

1. Free (Hobby) – $0/month

Best for: Casual users, students, and anyone wanting to test the waters.

The free tier gives you a taste of Cursor's AI magic but comes with limitations:

  • 2,000 AI code completions per month
  • 50 slow premium requests (GPT-4, Claude 3.5 Sonnet)
  • Access to Cursor's proprietary models
  • Basic chat and edit features

Pros: No credit card required, generous for light use.

Cons: Premium requests are slow and limited; you'll hit the cap quickly if you code daily.

2. Pro – $20/month

Best for: Professional developers, freelancers, and serious hobbyists.

Pro is where Cursor shines. For the price of a couple of coffees, you get:

  • Unlimited AI code completions
  • 500 fast premium requests per month
  • Unlimited slow premium requests
  • Access to GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and other top models
  • Background agents (alpha)
  • Priority support

Pros: Fast, responsive AI; huge productivity boost; flexible model choice.

Cons: 500 fast requests may not suffice for heavy users; overage costs $0.04 per request.

3. Business – $40/user/month

Best for: Teams and companies needing centralized management.

Everything in Pro, plus:

  • Centralized billing and user management
  • Admin dashboard with usage analytics
  • SAML/SSO integration
  • Enforced privacy mode (no code retention)

Pros: Peace of mind for orgs; easy onboarding/offboarding.

Cons: Double the Pro price; overkill for small teams.

4. Enterprise – Custom Pricing

Best for: Large enterprises with strict security and compliance needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use Cursor AI for free?

Yes, Cursor has a free Hobby plan with 2,000 completions and 50 slow premium requests per month. It's great for light use.

2. What is a 'fast premium request' in Cursor?

Fast premium requests use high-speed models like GPT-4o or Claude 3.5 Sonnet with priority processing. Pro includes 500 per month; after that, requests are slow or cost $0.04 each.

3. Is Cursor better than GitHub Copilot?

It depends. Cursor offers a more integrated AI-first editor with chat and multi-file edits. Copilot is cheaper and integrates seamlessly with GitHub. Many developers use both.

4. Does Cursor AI store my code?

By default, Cursor may store code snippets to improve models. Business and Enterprise plans offer enforced privacy mode with no retention.

5. Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings.

6. Is there a discount for students or non-profits?

Cursor does not publicly advertise discounts, but you can contact their sales team for special pricing.
