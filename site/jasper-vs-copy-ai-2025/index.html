Detailed Review: Jasper

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a leader in the AI writing space for years. It’s built on a powerful language model that can generate long-form content, ad copy, emails, and more. What sets Jasper apart is its focus on brand voice and consistency — you can train it to match your company’s tone, making it ideal for teams that publish a lot of content.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Create custom voice profiles that ensure all content sounds like you.
  • 50+ Templates: From blog posts to product descriptions, there’s a template for almost every use case.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize your content for search engines.
  • Collaboration: Workspaces for teams, with roles and permissions.
  • Integrations: Native integrations with Google Docs, WordPress, and Slack.
  • Chrome Extension: Use Jasper anywhere on the web.

Pricing

Jasper’s pricing is tiered:

  • Creator: $39/month (annual) or $49 month-to-month — includes 1 brand voice, 1 user, and 20,000 words per month.
  • Pro: $59/month (annual) or $79 month-to-month — adds 3 brand voices, 6 users, and 50,000 words.
  • Business: Custom pricing — unlimited words, advanced security, and dedicated support.

There’s a 7-day free trial (no credit card required) so you can test it risk-free.

Detailed Review: Copy.ai

Copy.ai burst onto the scene as a user-friendly, affordable alternative. It’s known for its simplicity and speed — you can generate catchy social captions or ad headlines in seconds. While it doesn’t have the same depth as Jasper, it’s perfect for quick tasks and for those who don’t need complex workflows.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: A wide variety of copywriting templates, from email subject lines to landing page copy.
  • Chat Interface: A conversational AI that can answer questions and brainstorm ideas.
  • Infobase: Store your brand facts and preferences, and Copy.ai will use them in its outputs.
  • Workflows: Automate content generation with multi-step workflows (available on higher tiers).
  • Integrations: Connect with Zapier, HubSpot, and more.
  • Free Plan: A limited free tier with 2,000 words per month.

Pricing

Copy.ai’s pricing is straightforward:

  • Free: $0 forever — 2,000 words/month, 1 seat, access to all templates.
  • Pro: $49/month (annual) or $36/month (billed annually, actually $49 is monthly, let’s clarify: $49/month when billed monthly, $36/month when billed annually) — unlimited words, 5 seats, and access to workflows.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — SSO, advanced security, and dedicated support.

There’s also a 7-day free trial for the Pro plan.

Who Should Use What?

Both tools are excellent, but they serve different audiences. Here’s a quick guide:

  • Bloggers and content creators: Jasper is the better choice because of its SEO features and long-form writing capabilities.
  • Social media managers: Copy.ai shines here — it’s fast, simple, and has templates for all major platforms.
  • Marketing agencies: Jasper’s collaboration tools and brand voice management make it ideal for handling multiple clients.
  • Startups on a budget: Copy.ai’s free plan and lower starting price are attractive, but if you need more power, Jasper’s Creator plan is worth the investment.
  • E-commerce stores: Both work, but Jasper’s product description templates are more detailed and customizable.

FAQ

Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

For most users, yes. Jasper offers more advanced features, better output quality, and stronger brand voice capabilities. However, Copy.ai is more affordable and simpler to use for basic tasks.

Can I use Jasper for free?

No, Jasper doesn’t have a free plan, but it does offer a 7-day free trial (no credit card required).

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month, which is great for testing.

Which tool is better for SEO content?

Jasper is better for SEO because it integrates with Surfer SEO and provides more control over content structure and keyword optimization.

Can I use both tools together?

Absolutely. Some users use Jasper for long-form articles and Copy.ai for quick social posts. But that adds cost — you might be better off with just Jasper.

What is the best AI writing tool for beginners?

Copy.ai is more beginner-friendly due to its simple interface and lower learning curve. Jasper has a steeper learning curve but is still manageable with practice.
