Jasper: The AI Writer for Serious Marketers

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a household name in AI writing since 2021. It's designed for marketing teams and businesses that need to produce high-quality, on-brand content at scale.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Train Jasper on your brand's tone, style, and vocabulary. It can then generate content that sounds like you — no more generic AI fluff.
  • 50+ Templates: From blog posts and social media captions to email subject lines and ad copy, Jasper has a template for almost any use case.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search engines as you write.
  • Jasper Chat: A conversational AI assistant that can help with brainstorming, editing, and research.
  • Team Collaboration: Multiple users, shared projects, and workflow tools for agencies and in-house teams.
  • Browser Extension: Use Jasper anywhere on the web, from Google Docs to LinkedIn.
  • Art Generation: Built-in AI image generation with Jasper Art (available in higher plans).

Pricing

Jasper offers three main plans:

  • Creator: $39/month (billed annually) or $49 month-to-month. For 1 seat, 1 brand voice, and 50+ templates.
  • Pro: $59/month (billed annually) or $69 month-to-month. For up to 5 seats, 3 brand voices, and SEO mode.
  • Business: Custom pricing. For larger teams, unlimited brand voices, and dedicated support.

There's a 7-day free trial, but no permanent free plan.

Pros

  • Exceptional output quality, especially for long-form content.
  • Brand Voice is a game-changer for consistency.
  • Seamless Surfer SEO integration.
  • Robust team features.
  • Constantly updated with new AI models and features.

Cons

  • Expensive compared to competitors, especially for individuals.
  • Steeper learning curve due to the sheer number of features.
  • No free forever plan.
  • Some users report occasional repetitive phrasing.

Copy.ai: The Speedy AI Copywriter

Copy.ai started as a tool for short-form copy but has evolved into a versatile AI writing platform. It's known for its speed, ease of use, and generous free plan.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Covers everything from blog intros to sales emails, social media posts, and website copy.
  • Chat Interface: A conversational mode that lets you interact with the AI to refine outputs.
  • Workflows: Automate repetitive writing tasks by chaining templates together.
  • Brand Voice (Beta): Available on higher plans, though less mature than Jasper's.
  • Multi-Language Support: Generate content in 25+ languages.
  • Free Plan: 2,000 words per month, 90+ templates, and 5 credits for premium features.

Pricing

Copy.ai has a free plan and two paid tiers:

  • Free: 2,000 words/month, limited features.
  • Pro: $36/month (billed annually) or $49 month-to-month. Unlimited words, 5 seats, brand voice, and workflows.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. Advanced security, dedicated support, and custom workflows.

Copy.ai's Pro plan is slightly cheaper than Jasper's Creator plan, but it lacks some of Jasper's advanced features like deep SEO integration.

Pros

  • Generous free plan — great for testing.
  • Very user-friendly; you can start generating copy in minutes.
  • Fast output generation.
  • Affordable for solopreneurs and small teams.
  • Workflows save time on repetitive tasks.

Cons

  • Long-form content quality isn't as strong as Jasper's.
  • Brand Voice is less customizable.
  • No built-in SEO optimization.
  • Some templates feel generic.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper If:

  • You run a marketing team or agency and need consistent, on-brand content at scale.
  • You produce long-form content like blog posts, whitepapers, and eBooks.
  • SEO is a priority and you want built-in optimization.
  • You need advanced collaboration and workflow features.
  • Budget isn't a primary concern.

Choose Copy.ai If:

  • You're a solopreneur, freelancer, or small business owner.
  • You mainly need short-form copy: social posts, ads, emails, product descriptions.
  • You want to test AI writing without committing financially.
  • You value speed and simplicity over advanced customization.
  • You work in multiple languages.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Jasper and Copy.ai are top-tier AI writing tools, but they serve different audiences. Jasper is the clear winner for teams and businesses that need high-quality, SEO-optimized, on-brand content at scale. Its Brand Voice and Surfer SEO integration are unmatched. However, it comes at a premium.

Copy.ai is the better choice for individuals and small teams who need a fast, affordable way to generate short-form copy. Its free plan is a great starting point, and the Pro plan is reasonably priced.

If you can afford it and content quality is paramount, go with Jasper. If you're budget-conscious and mostly write short-form, Copy.ai won't disappoint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper excels at long-form, SEO-optimized content and team collaboration. Copy.ai is better for short-form copy and budget-conscious users. Jasper is generally more powerful but also more expensive.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free forever plan with 2,000 words per month and access to 90+ templates. Jasper only offers a 7-day free trial.

Can I use Jasper and Copy.ai for SEO?

Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO to help you optimize content in real-time. Copy.ai does not have a native SEO integration, so you'd need to use a separate tool.

Which tool is easier to use?

Copy.ai is generally considered more beginner-friendly due to its simple interface and quick-start templates. Jasper has a steeper learning curve but offers more advanced features once you master it.

Do both tools offer a money-back guarantee?

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial, but no money-back guarantee. Copy.ai has a free plan, so you can test it indefinitely before upgrading. Both offer refunds in certain cases; check their terms.

Can I generate content in languages other than English?

Yes, both tools support multiple languages. Copy.ai supports 25+ languages, while Jasper supports 30+ languages including Spanish, French, German, and more.

Which tool is better for team collaboration?

Jasper is better for teams. It offers multiple seats, shared projects, brand voices, and workflow tools. Copy.ai's Pro plan includes 5 seats, but its collaboration features are less robust.
