Detailed Review: Jasper

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a leader in the AI writing space since 2021. It's designed for marketing teams, offering a suite of tools to create everything from blog posts to social media ads, all while maintaining a consistent brand voice.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper's standout feature. You can train it on your existing content to mimic your tone and style, ensuring every output sounds like you.
  • Knowledge Base: Upload documents, product info, and more to give Jasper context about your business, leading to more accurate and relevant copy.
  • Templates: Over 50 templates for various content types, from blog posts to email subject lines.
  • Collaboration: Team workspaces, user roles, and approval workflows make it easy for teams to work together.
  • Integrations: Connects with tools like Surfer SEO, Grammarly, and Google Docs to streamline your workflow.
  • Art: Jasper Art generates images from text prompts, a nice bonus for social media content.

Pricing

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial, but no free tier. Plans are:

  • Creator: $39/month (annual) or $49 month-to-month. For one seat, includes 1 brand voice.
  • Pro: $59/month (annual) or $69 month-to-month. For one seat, includes 2 brand voices and 3 knowledge assets.
  • Business: Custom pricing. Includes 5+ seats, unlimited brand voices and knowledge assets, and advanced collaboration features.

Pros

  • Excellent for maintaining brand voice across all content
  • Robust collaboration and team management features
  • Wide range of templates and integrations
  • High-quality output, especially for long-form content

Cons

  • No free plan (only a trial)
  • Can be pricey for small teams or solopreneurs
  • Learning curve for advanced features like knowledge base

Detailed Review: Copy.ai

Copy.ai started as a tool for short-form copy but has evolved into a comprehensive AI writing platform. It's known for its user-friendly interface and speed, making it a favorite among startups and sales teams.

Key Features

  • Workflows: Automate repetitive tasks by chaining together multiple AI actions. For example, generate a blog outline, then expand each section, then create social posts—all in one workflow.
  • Brand Voice: Similar to Jasper, you can train Copy.ai on your brand's tone, but it's less advanced (only one brand voice on most plans).
  • Templates: Over 90 templates covering marketing, sales, and general business content.
  • Chat Interface: A conversational AI assistant for brainstorming and quick copy generation.
  • Integrations: Connects with Zapier, HubSpot, and other tools to automate content creation.
  • Free Tier: Offers a free plan with limited features and 2,000 words per month.

Pricing

Copy.ai has a free plan and a 7-day free trial on paid plans. Plans are:

  • Free: 2,000 words/month, limited templates, 1 user.
  • Starter: $36/month (annual) or $49 month-to-month. 5 seats, unlimited words, 1 brand voice.
  • Advanced: $186/month (annual) or $249 month-to-month. 20 seats, 5 brand voices, workflows, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. Unlimited seats, custom workflows, and dedicated support.

Pros

  • Free plan available
  • Intuitive interface with a low learning curve
  • Powerful workflow automation for repetitive tasks
  • Great for short-form content and sales copy

Cons

  • Brand voice feature is less robust than Jasper's
  • Long-form content can require more editing
  • Advanced features are locked behind higher-priced plans

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper if:

  • You need to maintain a consistent brand voice across a large team
  • You produce long-form content like blog posts, whitepapers, and case studies
  • You require advanced collaboration features like approval workflows and user roles
  • You want to leverage a knowledge base to give AI context about your business

Choose Copy.ai if:

  • You're a small team or solopreneur on a budget (free plan available)
  • You need to generate short-form content quickly (social posts, ads, emails)
  • You want to automate repetitive writing tasks with workflows
  • You prefer a simple, intuitive interface with a low learning curve

Bottom Line Verdict

After thorough testing, Jasper emerges as the better choice for most content teams. Its superior brand voice customization, robust collaboration features, and focus on long-form quality make it a more complete solution for scaling content without sacrificing consistency. However, Copy.ai is an excellent alternative for teams that prioritize speed, simplicity, and budget—especially those focused on short-form content or sales copy.

Ultimately, the right tool depends on your specific needs. If brand consistency and team collaboration are top priorities, go with Jasper. If you need a free plan and fast, automated content creation, Copy.ai is a solid pick.

FAQ

1. Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper is better for teams that require advanced brand voice customization and collaboration features, while Copy.ai excels in speed and simplicity for short-form content. Jasper is generally considered more powerful for long-form content and brand consistency.

2. Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month and limited features. Jasper does not have a free plan, but offers a 7-day free trial.

3. Which is cheaper, Jasper or Copy.ai?

Copy.ai is cheaper, with paid plans starting at $36/month (annual) compared to Jasper's $39/month (annual). However, Jasper's plans include more features for teams, so the value depends on your needs.

4. Can Jasper and Copy.ai be used for SEO content?

Yes, both can be used for SEO content. Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO for optimization, while Copy.ai has templates for SEO-friendly content. However, Jasper's long-form capabilities and brand voice make it a stronger choice for SEO-driven blogs.

5. Which tool is better for team collaboration?

Jasper offers more advanced collaboration features, such as user roles, approval workflows, and shared workspaces. Copy.ai also supports teams but with fewer granular controls.

6. Do both tools offer a free trial?

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial on all plans. Copy.ai offers a free plan and a 7-day free trial on paid plans.
