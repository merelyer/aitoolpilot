Detailed Review: Jasper

What is Jasper?

Jasper is an AI writing assistant designed for marketing teams. It goes beyond simple text generation, offering a suite of tools for brand voice customization, collaboration, and workflow automation. Jasper is built to help teams create consistent, on-brand content at scale.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper allows you to define and enforce your brand's tone, style, and terminology across all generated content. You can create multiple brand voices for different products or audiences.
  • Templates: Over 50 templates for various content types, from blog posts and social media captions to ad copy and emails.
  • Collaboration: Team workspaces, shared projects, and commenting features make it easy for teams to collaborate on content.
  • Jasper Chat: A conversational AI interface that helps you brainstorm, outline, and refine content.
  • Integrations: Works with popular tools like Google Docs, WordPress, and Surfer SEO.
  • Plagiarism Checker: Built-in tool to ensure originality.

Pricing

Jasper offers three plans:

  • Creator: $39 per month (billed annually) for one seat. Includes 1 brand voice and 50+ templates.
  • Pro: $59 per month (billed annually) per seat. Includes 3 brand voices, collaboration features, and 10 plagiarism checks per month.
  • Business: Custom pricing. Includes unlimited brand voices, advanced collaboration, and dedicated support.

Pros and Cons

Pros:

  • Excellent brand voice customization ensures consistency.
  • Robust collaboration features for teams.
  • Wide range of templates and integrations.
  • High-quality output with minimal editing.

Cons:

  • Higher starting price compared to some competitors.
  • No free tier (only a 7-day free trial).
  • Steeper learning curve for beginners.

Detailed Review: Copy.ai

What is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai is an AI-powered copywriting tool that helps teams generate marketing and sales copy quickly. It has evolved into a go-to-market (GTM) AI platform, offering workflow automation for sales and marketing tasks beyond just content creation.

Key Features

  • Workflows: Automate repetitive tasks like prospecting, content creation, and email sequences with AI-powered workflows.
  • Templates: Over 90 templates for various copywriting needs.
  • Brand Voice: Customize tone and style, though less granular than Jasper's.
  • Collaboration: Team workspaces and shared projects.
  • Integrations: Connects with CRM tools like Salesforce and HubSpot, plus Zapier for automation.
  • Free Tier: Generous free plan with 2,000 words per month.

Pricing

Copy.ai offers four plans:

  • Free: 2,000 words per month, limited features.
  • Starter: $36 per month (billed annually) for unlimited words and 5 seats.
  • Advanced: $186 per month (billed annually) for 20 seats and advanced workflows.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing with dedicated support and SSO.

Pros and Cons

Pros:

  • Generous free tier and lower starting price.
  • Strong focus on GTM workflows and automation.
  • User-friendly interface with quick onboarding.
  • Unlimited words on paid plans.

Cons:

  • Brand voice customization is less robust than Jasper's.
  • Output quality can be inconsistent and may require more editing.
  • Collaboration features are basic compared to Jasper.

Jasper allows you to define your brand's tone, style, and terminology by analyzing existing content or manually setting guidelines. You can create multiple brand voices for different audiences or products.

6. Is Copy.ai good for long-form content?

Copy.ai can generate long-form content, but it's more geared towards short-form copy and workflows. For long-form articles, Jasper's templates and brand voice features may produce more consistent results.
