Detailed Reviews

Jasper

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is a powerhouse in the AI writing space. It’s designed for teams who need consistent, on-brand content at scale. With its new Brand Voice feature, you can train the AI to match your tone and style, ensuring every output feels like it came from your own team. Jasper also offers a Campaign workflow that automates multi-step writing tasks, from brainstorming to final draft.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice customization
  • Campaign workflows for complex projects
  • 50+ templates (blog posts, emails, ads)
  • Integration with Surfer SEO for optimization
  • Chrome extension for writing anywhere
  • Collaboration tools for teams

Pros

  • Highest quality output, especially for long-form content
  • Excellent brand voice consistency
  • Strong collaboration and workflow features
  • Great customer support

Cons

  • More expensive than competitors
  • Steeper learning curve
  • No free tier (only 7-day trial)

Pricing: Jasper offers a 7-day free trial. The Creator plan is $39/month (billed annually) and gives you 1 Brand Voice, 50+ templates, and 1 user seat. The Pro plan is $59/month (billed annually) for up to 5 users and 3 Brand Voices. For enterprise needs, custom pricing is available.

Copy.ai

Copy.ai is the go-to tool for quick, simple copy. It’s perfect for social media captions, product descriptions, and short ad copy. Copy.ai has a clean, user-friendly interface that makes it easy for beginners to get started. Its Infobase feature allows you to store information about your company and audience, which the AI uses to generate more relevant content.

Key Features

  • Infobase for storing company info
  • 90+ templates for various use cases
  • Chat interface for conversational generation
  • Integrations with Zapier, Shopify, and more
  • Free plan with limited credits

Pros

  • Very easy to use
  • Good for short-form copy
  • Free plan available
  • Fast generation speed

Cons

  • Output can be generic and requires editing
  • Limited long-form content capabilities
  • No brand voice customization (as of early 2025)

Pricing: Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month. The Pro plan starts at $49/month (billed annually) and includes unlimited words, 5 seats, and access to all features. There’s also a custom Enterprise plan.

Writesonic

Writesonic is a versatile AI writer that balances affordability with power. It excels at SEO content, making it a favorite among bloggers and online marketers. The Article Writer 5.0 can generate complete, well-researched articles in minutes. Writesonic also has a Chatsonic feature—a ChatGPT-like assistant that can answer questions and help with research.

Key Features

  • Article Writer 5.0 for long-form SEO content
  • Chatsonic assistant with real-time data
  • 25+ templates, including Google Ads and Facebook Ads
  • Integration with WordPress and Zapier
  • AI Article Rewriter and Paraphraser

Pros

  • Most affordable paid plan
  • Strong SEO features (includes Surfer SEO integration)
  • Chatsonic adds extra value
  • Good balance of features and price

Cons

  • Output can be less consistent than Jasper
  • UI can be cluttered
  • Some features are limited on the cheapest plan

Pricing: Writesonic offers a free trial with 25 credits. The Long-form plan starts at $16/month (billed annually) and includes 30,000 words, Chatsonic, and basic features. The Pro plan is $39/month (billed annually) for 100,000 words and more advanced tools. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Who Should Use What

Jasper is best for marketing teams, agencies, and businesses that need consistent brand voice across multiple channels. If you create long-form content like blog posts, white papers, or ebooks, Jasper’s quality and workflow tools are worth the investment.

Copy.ai is ideal for startups and small businesses that need quick, catchy copy for ads, social media, or product pages. Its simplicity and free tier make it a great choice for beginners or those with limited budgets.

Writesonic is perfect for solo bloggers, SEO specialists, and budget-conscious content creators. If you need to produce a high volume of SEO-optimized articles without breaking the bank, Writesonic offers the best value.

Bottom Line Verdict

All three tools are excellent in their own right, but they serve different needs. Jasper is the premium choice for teams that prioritize quality and consistency. Writesonic is the budget-friendly workhorse for SEO content. Copy.ai is the quick-and-easy option for simple copy.

In 2025, we recommend Jasper as the overall winner due to its superior output and brand voice features. However, if you’re just starting out or have a tight budget, Writesonic is a fantastic alternative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI writer is best for SEO content?

Writesonic excels at SEO content thanks to its integration with Surfer SEO and its Article Writer 5.0, which generates well-structured, keyword-optimized articles. Jasper also offers Surfer SEO integration but at a higher price point.

Can I use Jasper for free?

Jasper does not have a free plan, but it offers a 7-day free trial that gives you access to all features. After the trial, you’ll need to choose a paid plan.

Is Copy.ai good for long-form content?

Copy.ai is not ideal for long-form content. It focuses on short-form copy and lacks the advanced features needed for comprehensive articles. For long-form, consider Jasper or Writesonic.

What is the cheapest AI writing tool?

Writesonic is the most affordable, with plans starting at $16 per month (billed annually). Copy.ai has a free plan, but its paid plan is $49 per month, making it more expensive than Writesonic for paid users.

Can these tools write in multiple languages?

Yes, all three tools support multiple languages. Jasper supports over 30 languages, Copy.ai supports 25+, and Writesonic supports 24+. However, the quality may vary depending on the language.

Which tool has the best brand voice feature?

Jasper’s Brand Voice feature is the most advanced, allowing you to train the AI on your specific tone, style, and audience. Copy.ai and Writesonic have limited or no brand voice customization.

Do these tools have API access?

Yes, all three offer API access, but it’s typically available on higher-tier plans or enterprise plans. Check each tool’s documentation for details.
