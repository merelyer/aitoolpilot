Detailed Review: Otter.ai

Otter.ai has long been a household name in AI meeting transcription. It’s known for its clean interface, real-time transcription, and seamless Zoom integration. But how does it hold up in 2025?

Key Features

  • Real-time transcription: Otter transcribes meetings live, so you can follow along as the conversation happens.
  • Automated meeting notes: After each call, Otter generates a summary with key points, action items, and decisions.
  • Otter Assistant: This AI bot can join Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom calls automatically, taking notes without you lifting a finger.
  • Custom vocabulary: You can add industry-specific terms, names, and acronyms to improve accuracy.
  • Integration: Works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack. Also offers a Chrome extension for capturing virtual meetings.
  • Mobile app: Available on iOS and Android, allowing you to record and transcribe in-person meetings or voice memos.

Pricing

  • Basic (Free): 300 minutes per month, 30-minute per conversation limit, 3 integrations (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams).
  • Pro: $8.33/month (billed annually) or $16.99 month-to-month. Includes 1,200 minutes per month, 90-minute per conversation limit, and advanced search.
  • Business: $20/month per user (annual billing). Adds 6,000 minutes per user per month, admin controls, and team usage analytics.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for large organizations with SSO, compliance, and dedicated support.

Pros

  • Very user-friendly interface—great for beginners.
  • Generous free tier (300 minutes/month) is perfect for casual users.
  • Accurate transcription, especially for English speakers.
  • Real-time collaboration: you can comment and highlight parts of the transcript during the meeting.

Cons

  • Limited integration with third-party apps compared to Fireflies (no native Salesforce or HubSpot sync).
  • No built-in conversation analytics (like talk-to-listen ratio or sentiment).
  • Transcription minutes are per conversation limit—longer meetings eat up your quota quickly.
  • Search functionality is basic; finding specific topics can be a hassle.
