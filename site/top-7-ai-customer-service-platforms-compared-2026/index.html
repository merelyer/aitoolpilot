FAQ

  • What is the difference between AI chatbots and AI ticket routing? Chatbots handle direct customer interactions in real time, while ticket routing uses AI to assign incoming tickets to the most appropriate agent based on skill, workload, and sentiment.
  • Can I use multiple AI platforms together? Yes, many businesses layer a front‑end chatbot (e.g., Ada) with a back‑office ticketing system (e.g., Zendesk) using webhook integrations.
  • Do these platforms support multilingual customers? Most enterprise‑grade solutions (Zendesk, ServiceNow, LivePerson) offer 20+ language models. Smaller tools may need third‑party translation APIs.
  • How accurate are AI‑generated answers? Accuracy depends on training data and the underlying model. Platforms that allow custom model fine‑tuning (Zendesk, ServiceNow) typically achieve >85% first‑contact resolution.
  • Is there a hidden cost for AI usage? Some vendors charge per interaction or per AI model call. Always review the “usage” section of the pricing page to avoid surprise fees.
