FAQ

  • What is the difference between an AI bot and a chatbot? An AI bot uses machine‑learning models to understand intent and generate dynamic responses, while a chatbot often follows predefined rule‑based flows.
  • Can I use these platforms without a knowledge base? Most platforms require at least a basic FAQ or article repository; the AI pulls answers from that content. Building a solid knowledge base is essential for high deflection rates.
  • How do I measure ROI for an AI customer service platform? Track metrics such as tickets deflected, average handling time (AHT) reduction, CSAT improvement, and cost per ticket before and after implementation.
  • Do these tools support GDPR compliance? All major vendors (Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, etc.) provide data‑processing agreements and features like data residency controls to help meet GDPR requirements.
  • Is it possible to run a custom AI model on any of these platforms? Yes – Zendesk and Microsoft Dynamics allow you to upload custom intent models, while LivePerson offers on‑premise AI for highly regulated industries.
