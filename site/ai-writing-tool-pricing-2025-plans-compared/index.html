Detailed Reviews

1. Jasper

Best for: Marketing teams, agencies, and businesses that need high-quality, brand-aware content at scale.

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is one of the most popular AI writing tools on the market. It uses GPT-4 and proprietary models to generate blog posts, social media content, ad copy, and more. The interface is polished, and the “Brand Voice” feature lets you train the AI to match your tone.

Pricing:

  • Creator: $49/month (up to 1 user, 1 brand voice, 50+ templates)
  • Pro: $69/month (up to 5 users, 3 brand voices, advanced features like SEO mode)
  • Business: Custom pricing (unlimited users, custom workflows, API access)

Pros:

  • Excellent output quality with brand voice consistency
  • Built-in plagiarism checker and SEO tools
  • Strong integrations (Surfer SEO, Grammarly, etc.)

Cons:

  • Higher price point compared to competitors
  • Word limits on lower tiers can be restrictive
  • Learning curve for advanced features

2. Copy.ai

Best for: Solopreneurs, startups, and small teams who want a simple, effective tool without a steep learning curve.

Copy.ai focuses on ease of use. It offers a generous free tier and a clean interface. The tool excels at short-form content like emails, social posts, and product descriptions.

Pricing:

  • Free: $0/month (2,000 words, 1 user, limited templates)
  • Pro: $36/month (unlimited words, 5 user seats, all templates, chat interface)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing (SSO, dedicated support, custom workflows)

Pros:

  • Generous free plan for testing
  • Very user-friendly interface
  • Unlimited words on Pro plan

Cons:

  • Long-form content quality can be inconsistent
  • Fewer advanced features than Jasper
  • No native SEO integration

3. Writesonic

Best for: Content creators, e-commerce businesses, and those who need a versatile tool at a lower price.

Writesonic offers a wide range of templates and supports multiple languages. Its “Article Writer 5.0” feature generates long-form content with decent quality. The pricing is competitive, especially for unlimited words.

Pricing:

  • Free: $0/month (10,000 words, limited features)
  • Starter: $19/month (60,000 words, 1 user)
  • Professional: $49/month (unlimited words, 5 users, priority support)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing (custom models, API access)

Pros:

  • Affordable unlimited word plan
  • Built-in fact-checker and plagiarism checker
  • Good for e-commerce (product descriptions, ads)

Cons:

  • Output can sometimes feel robotic
  • User interface is less polished than Jasper
  • Customer support response times vary

4. Rytr

Best for: Freelancers, students, and anyone on a tight budget who still wants decent AI writing capabilities.

Rytr is one of the cheapest AI writing tools, starting at just $9/month. It offers over 30 templates and supports 30+ languages. The quality is good for short-form content, but long-form may require more editing.

Pricing:

  • Free: $0/month (5,000 characters per month, 1 user)
  • Unlimited: $9/month (unlimited characters, 1 user, 40+ templates)
  • Premium: $29/month (unlimited characters, 5 users, priority support, custom use case)

Pros:

  • Extremely affordable
  • Unlimited characters on paid plans
  • Built-in tone selector

Cons:

  • Character limit on free plan (not words)
  • Long-form content quality is average
  • Limited integrations

5. Sudowrite

Best for: Fiction writers, novelists, and creative writers who need help with storytelling, character development, and plot ideas.

Sudowrite is tailor-made for creative writing. It offers unique features like “Story Engine” for generating entire chapters, “Describe” for vivid descriptions, and “Brainstorm” for plot twists. It’s not designed for marketing copy.

Pricing:

  • Hobby & Student: $19/month (30,000 words, 1 user)
  • Professional: $29/month (90,000 words, 1 user)
  • Max: $59/month (300,000 words, 1 user)
  • Custom: For teams and higher word counts

Pros:

  • Unique features for creative writing
  • High-quality, nuanced output
  • Great for overcoming writer's block

Cons:

  • Not suitable for business or marketing content
  • Word limits are strict
  • No free tier (only trial)

Who Should Use What

  • Solopreneurs & startups: Copy.ai – easy to use with a free plan to start.
  • Budget-conscious content creators: Writesonic – affordable unlimited words and good features.
  • Freelancers on a shoestring: Rytr – unbeatable price for basic writing needs.
  • Fiction & creative writers: Sudowrite – purpose-built for storytelling.

Bottom Line Verdict

Your choice depends on your budget and use case. For most businesses, Jasper offers the best overall value despite its higher price. If you're just starting out or need a quick tool for social media, Copy.ai is a solid, risk-free choice. For unlimited words at a low cost, Writesonic is hard to beat. And if you're a writer penning the next great novel, Sudowrite is worth every penny.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is the cheapest AI writing tool?
    Rytr is the cheapest, starting at $9/month for unlimited characters. Copy.ai also offers a generous free plan.

  2. Which AI writing tool has the best free plan?
    Copy.ai offers 2,000 words free per month, while Writesonic gives 10,000 words. For most users, Copy.ai’s free tier is more practical for testing.

  3. Do AI writing tools charge per word or per month?
    Most charge a flat monthly fee with word or character limits. Some, like Writesonic and Rytr, offer unlimited plans. A few tools have pay-as-you-go options.

  4. Are there any completely free AI writing tools?
    Yes, but they are limited. Tools like Copy.ai and Writesonic have free tiers with word caps. For serious use, you’ll need a paid plan.

  5. Which AI writing tool is best for long-form content?
    Jasper and Writesonic are strong for long-form articles. Sudowrite is best for fiction. Copy.ai and Rytr are better for short pieces.

  6. Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
    Yes, all major AI writing tools offer monthly plans with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime.

  7. Do these tools offer discounts for annual billing?
    Most do. Jasper, Copy.ai, and Writesonic offer 20-30% off when you pay annually.
