Descript Review: AI Video Editing for Professionals

Descript is an AI-powered video and podcast editor that lets you edit media by editing text. It's like having a word processor for video. With features like Studio Sound, filler word removal, and eye contact correction, Descript aims to streamline post-production for professionals.

Key Features

  • Text-based editing: Edit video by deleting words in the transcript.
  • Studio Sound: Enhance audio quality to studio level with one click.
  • Filler word removal: Automatically remove "um," "uh," and repeated words.
  • Eye contact correction: Adjust gaze to look at the camera.
  • Green screen: Remove backgrounds without a physical green screen.
  • Multi-track editing: Supports complex timelines.
  • Collaboration: Real-time editing and comments for teams.

Pricing

Descript offers a free plan with limited features (1 transcription hour/month, watermarked exports). Paid plans:

  • Creator: $12/month (annual) or $15/month (monthly) — 10 transcription hours/month, no watermark, 720p export.
  • Pro: $24/month (annual) or $30/month (monthly) — 30 transcription hours/month, 1080p export, full AI features.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — unlimited transcription, advanced security, dedicated support.

Pros

  • Revolutionary text-based editing saves hours.
  • Excellent for podcast and interview editing.
  • AI features like Studio Sound are best-in-class.
  • Collaboration tools for teams.

Cons

  • Free plan is very limited (watermark, low transcription hours).
  • Can be overkill for simple social media edits.
  • Steeper learning curve than mobile-first editors.
  • No mobile app for editing (only viewing).

CapCut Review: The Free AI Video Editor for Everyone

CapCut (owned by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok) is a popular free video editor with AI-powered tools. It's designed for quick, trendy edits for social media, but also supports more advanced editing on desktop.

Key Features

  • Auto captions: Generate subtitles automatically.
  • Background removal: AI-powered cutout without green screen.
  • Text-to-speech: Convert text to voiceovers.
  • Templates: Thousands of trending templates.
  • Effects and filters: Extensive library of visual effects.
  • Multi-track editing: Available on desktop.
  • Cloud storage: Sync projects across devices.

Pricing

CapCut is free to use with optional Pro subscription:

  • Free: All core editing features, watermark-free exports, but limited cloud storage (1GB) and some AI features locked.
  • Pro: $7.99/month or $74.99/year — unlocks advanced AI features, 100GB cloud storage, and premium effects.
  • Team: $9.99/month per user (annual) — collaboration tools, shared assets.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — advanced admin controls, SSO.

Pros

  • Generous free plan with no watermark.
  • Very affordable Pro plan.
  • Mobile and desktop apps for editing anywhere.
  • Huge library of templates and effects.
  • Easy to learn for beginners.

Cons

  • AI features not as advanced as Descript's (e.g., no text-based editing).
  • Less suitable for long-form content like podcasts.
  • Privacy concerns due to ByteDance ownership.
  • Some features require Pro subscription.

Who Should Use What?

Your choice depends on your primary use case and budget:

  • Podcasters & Interviewers: Descript. Text-based editing and Studio Sound are game-changers for spoken-word content.
  • Social Media Creators: CapCut. Quick edits, trending templates, and mobile-first design make it ideal for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
  • YouTubers (Long-form): Descript for advanced editing and AI features; CapCut for simpler vlogs.
  • Teams & Agencies: Descript for collaboration and pro features; CapCut Team for budget-friendly social media teams.
  • Beginners: CapCut. The learning curve is gentler, and the free version is robust.
  • Professionals on a Budget: CapCut Pro offers incredible value at $7.99/month.

Bottom Line

Descript and CapCut serve different masters. Descript is a professional-grade AI editor that excels at text-based editing, audio enhancement, and collaboration—perfect for podcasters and serious YouTubers. CapCut is a versatile, free-first editor with powerful AI features and a massive template library, ideal for social media creators and beginners.

If you need advanced AI and are willing to invest, Descript is our top pick. If you want the best free editor with affordable Pro upgrades, CapCut is unbeatable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, CapCut's core editing features are free and exports have no watermark. However, some advanced AI features and cloud storage require a Pro subscription.

Can I use Descript for free?

Descript offers a free plan, but it's limited: 1 transcription hour per month and watermarked exports. For serious use, you'll need a paid plan.

Which is better for YouTube videos?

For long-form YouTube videos, Descript's advanced editing and AI tools give it an edge. For short, trendy videos, CapCut is faster and more template-driven.

Does CapCut have AI features like Descript?

CapCut has AI features like auto captions, background removal, and text-to-speech, but it lacks Descript's text-based editing and Studio Sound.

Is Descript worth the price?

If you regularly edit podcasts or interviews, Descript's time-saving AI features can be worth the investment. For casual editing, it may be overkill.
