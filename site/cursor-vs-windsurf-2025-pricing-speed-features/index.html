Detailed Review: Cursor

Cursor burst onto the scene in 2023 and quickly became the darling of the AI coding world. Built on top of VS Code, it feels instantly familiar but packs a serious AI punch. The editor is designed around AI-first workflows, with features like Chat, Edit, and Agent that let you converse with your codebase, make multi-file changes, and even delegate entire tasks.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Autocomplete: Predicts your next edit with uncanny accuracy, often completing entire functions.
  • Chat with Codebase: Ask questions about your code and get answers with references to specific files and lines.
  • Multi-File Editing: Instruct Cursor to refactor across multiple files, and it handles the changes coherently.
  • Agent Mode: Let Cursor autonomously plan and execute tasks, like adding a new feature or fixing a bug.
  • VS Code Compatibility: Supports your favorite extensions, themes, and keybindings out of the box.
  • Model Flexibility: Choose between GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and other top models.

Pricing

Cursor offers a generous free tier and straightforward paid plans:

  • Hobby (Free): 2,000 completions, 50 slow premium requests, and 200 chat requests per month.
  • Pro ($20/month): Unlimited completions, 500 fast premium requests, unlimited slow premium requests, and 10 Agent uses per day.
  • Business ($40/user/month): Everything in Pro plus centralized billing, admin dashboard, and SAML SSO.
  • Enterprise (Custom): Dedicated support, custom contracts, and advanced security.

Detailed Review: Windsurf

Windsurf, formerly Codeium, rebranded in late 2024 with a bold new vision: an AI editor that acts like a true collaborator. Its standout feature is Cascade, an agentic AI that can understand your entire project, plan multi-step tasks, and execute them with minimal hand-holding. Windsurf aims to reduce context-switching by keeping you in a flow state.

Key Features

  • Cascade Agent: An AI that can autonomously write, debug, and refactor code across files, with a visible plan and progress updates.
  • Flow-Based Editing: Suggests edits as you type, adapting to your coding style.
  • Supercomplete: Goes beyond autocomplete by predicting multi-line edits and entire blocks.
  • Codeium Chat: Ask questions and get answers with codebase context.
  • Multi-IDE Support: Available as a plugin for VS Code, JetBrains, and more (though the full editor is its own app).
  • Team Collaboration: Shared sessions and team-wide AI settings.

Pricing

Windsurf's pricing is competitive, with a free tier for individuals:

  • Individual (Free): Unlimited autocomplete, 5 Cascade messages per day, and basic chat.
  • Pro ($15/month): Unlimited Cascade messages, 500 fast premium requests, and priority support.
  • Teams ($30/user/month): Everything in Pro plus team management, shared sessions, and analytics.
  • Enterprise (Custom): SSO, on-premise deployment, and dedicated support.

Who Should Use What?

Choosing between Cursor and Windsurf depends on your workflow, team size, and budget. Here's our take:

  • Choose Cursor if: You're a solo developer or small team already using VS Code and want the most mature AI features. Cursor's deep codebase understanding and multi-file editing are unmatched. It's also great for those who want to stick with a familiar interface.
  • Choose Windsurf if: You're part of a larger team that needs collaboration features and an agentic AI that can handle complex tasks. Windsurf's Cascade is a game-changer for automating repetitive work, and its lower Pro price makes it attractive for budget-conscious teams.
  • Consider both if: You're a power user who wants to experiment. Both offer free tiers, so you can test drive them on your own projects before committing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cursor better than Windsurf?

It depends on your needs. Cursor is more mature and offers a smoother experience, especially for individual developers. Windsurf excels in team collaboration and autonomous task execution. For most solo developers, Cursor is the better choice.

Can I use Cursor for free?

Yes, Cursor has a free Hobby plan that includes 2,000 completions, 50 slow premium requests, and 200 chat requests per month. It's great for trying out the editor.

What is Windsurf's Cascade feature?

Cascade is Windsurf's agentic AI that can understand your entire codebase, plan multi-step tasks, and execute them with minimal input. It's designed to handle complex workflows like adding features or refactoring code.

Which editor is faster, Cursor or Windsurf?

In our tests, Cursor felt slightly faster for autocomplete and single-file edits, while Windsurf's Cascade could be slower on large codebases due to its deep analysis. However, both are highly responsive for most tasks.

Do both support VS Code extensions?

Cursor is a fork of VS Code and supports most extensions out of the box. Windsurf is a standalone editor but offers a VS Code extension for its AI features, though not all extensions may work seamlessly.

Is Windsurf cheaper than Cursor?

Yes, Windsurf's Pro plan is $15/month compared to Cursor's $20/month. However, Cursor's free tier offers more premium requests, so the value depends on your usage.

Can I use these editors for team projects?

Absolutely. Both offer team plans with collaboration features. Cursor's Business plan is $40/user/month, while Windsurf's Teams plan is $30/user/month. Windsurf has a slight edge in team-specific features like shared sessions.
