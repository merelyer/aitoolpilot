Deep Dive: Cursor

Cursor is a standalone AI-powered code editor built on the Visual Studio Code engine. It's not just a plugin—it's a full IDE that integrates AI deeply into every aspect of coding. Launched in 2023, it quickly became the go-to for developers who want AI assistance without leaving their editor.

Key Features

  • Multi-file editing: Cursor can apply changes across multiple files simultaneously, making large-scale refactoring a breeze.
  • Chat with codebase: The AI can answer questions about your entire project, not just the current file.
  • Agent mode: Automate complex tasks like writing tests or fixing bugs with minimal supervision.
  • Custom models: Choose from GPT-4, Claude 3.5, or custom models for specific needs.
  • Tab completion: Predicts your next lines with uncanny accuracy, even for repetitive patterns.

Pricing

Cursor offers a free tier with limited usage, but the Pro plan at $20/month is where the magic happens. The Ultra plan at $200/month includes unlimited usage and priority access. For teams, there's a custom enterprise plan.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Deep IDE integration, excellent multi-file handling, robust agentic features, frequent updates.
  • Cons: Can be resource-heavy, steep learning curve for non-VS Code users, pricey for heavy usage.

Deep Dive: Windsurf

Windsurf (formerly Codeium) is an AI assistant that integrates seamlessly into VS Code, JetBrains, and other editors. It's known for its agentic capabilities—allowing the AI to autonomously perform tasks like file creation and code generation.

Key Features

  • Agentic AI: Windsurf's Cascade feature can independently execute multi-step tasks, like creating a new feature end-to-end.
  • Context awareness: It understands your entire workspace, making suggestions that fit your project's style.
  • Command generation: Generate terminal commands, git operations, and more with natural language.
  • Multi-editor support: Works with VS Code, JetBrains, and even Vim (via extension).

Pricing

Windsurf has a generous free tier, and the Pro plan at $15/month is a steal. There's also a Teams plan at $30/user/month and an Enterprise option for custom pricing.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Lower cost, easier learning curve, excellent agentic features, lightweight on system resources.
  • Cons: Less polished multi-file editing, occasional context loss in large projects, fewer customization options.

Who Should Use What

Choose Cursor if: You're working on large, multi-file projects and need AI to handle complex refactoring. You're comfortable switching to a new editor, and you value deep IDE integration over cost.

Choose Windsurf if: You're a VS Code loyalist who wants a powerful AI assistant without changing your setup. You're on a budget, or you're new to AI coding tools and want a gentler introduction.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both tools are exceptional, but they serve different needs. Cursor is the heavyweight champion for power users and large codebases. Windsurf is the agile contender for everyday coding and those who prefer VS Code. In 2025, I'd recommend Cursor for professional developers who want the most advanced AI assistance, and Windsurf for hobbyists or those looking for a cost-effective boost.

FAQ

Is Cursor better than Windsurf?

Cursor offers deeper IDE integration and superior multi-file editing, making it better for complex projects. Windsurf is easier to adopt and cheaper, but may lag in advanced scenarios.

Can I use Cursor and Windsurf together?

Yes, technically you could, but it's not recommended as both provide similar features and might conflict. Stick with one to avoid confusion.

Which is more affordable: Cursor or Windsurf?

Windsurf is more affordable at $15/month for Pro, compared to Cursor's $20/month. Both have free tiers, but Windsurf's free tier is more generous.

Do Cursor and Windsurf support all programming languages?

Both support a wide range of languages, including Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, and more. They leverage models trained on diverse codebases, so language support is extensive.

How does the AI in these tools handle privacy?

Both tools offer privacy modes for enterprise users, ensuring your code isn't used for training. Cursor's Ultra plan includes zero data retention, while Windsurf's Enterprise plan offers similar guarantees.
