Detailed Review: Cursor

Cursor has been making waves since its launch, and for good reason. Built on top of VS Code, it offers the familiar interface millions of developers already love, but with AI superpowers baked in. You get all the extensions, themes, and keybindings you're used to, plus a suite of AI features that feel like having a senior developer looking over your shoulder.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Autocomplete: Goes beyond basic suggestions to predict entire blocks of code based on context.
  • Chat with Your Codebase: Ask questions about your project and get answers with references to specific files.
  • Multi-File Editing: Instruct the AI to make changes across multiple files, and it will handle the edits.
  • Code Generation from Prompts: Describe what you want in plain English, and Cursor writes the code.
  • Built-in Debugging: AI helps identify and fix bugs by analyzing error messages and suggesting fixes.
  • VS Code Compatibility: Use your existing extensions, settings, and keybindings.

Pricing

Cursor offers a generous free tier with limited AI completions (2,000 per month) and 50 slow premium requests. The Pro plan at $20/month gives you unlimited AI completions, 500 fast premium requests, and access to advanced models like GPT-4 and Claude 3.5 Sonnet. For teams, the Business plan is $40/user/month and adds centralized billing, admin controls, and privacy features. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Detailed Review: Windsurf

Windsurf (formerly Codeium) is the new kid on the block, but it's backed by a team with deep experience in AI and developer tools. Unlike Cursor, Windsurf is built from the ground up as an AI-native IDE. It combines a sleek interface with a powerful AI assistant called Cascade that can understand your entire project and help you write, refactor, and debug code.

Key Features

  • Cascade AI Assistant: A context-aware AI that can answer questions, generate code, and perform multi-step tasks.
  • Deep Codebase Understanding: Indexes your entire project to provide accurate suggestions and answers.
  • Real-time Collaboration: Built-in support for pair programming and team collaboration (coming soon).
  • AI-Powered Refactoring: Automatically improve code quality with smart refactoring suggestions.
  • Integrated Terminal and Debugger: All the tools you need in one place, enhanced with AI.
  • Customizable Workflows: Create and share AI workflows for repetitive tasks.

Pricing

Windsurf's free plan includes unlimited AI completions and 5 Cascade messages per day. The Pro plan at $15/month (billed annually) or $19/month (monthly) gives you unlimited Cascade messages, access to premium models, and priority support. For teams, the Team plan is $30/user/month and adds collaboration features, shared workflows, and admin controls. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • AI-native design means features feel more integrated and intuitive.
  • Unlimited AI completions even on the free plan.
  • Strong focus on team collaboration and enterprise needs.
  • Competitive pricing, especially for teams.

Cons

6. Can I use Cursor and Windsurf together?
