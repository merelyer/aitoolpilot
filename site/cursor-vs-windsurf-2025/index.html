Detailed Review: Cursor

What is Cursor?

Cursor is an AI-powered code editor built on top of VS Code. It looks and feels like VS Code, which means you get all your favorite extensions, themes, and keybindings right out of the box. But under the hood, Cursor packs a serious AI punch. It uses advanced models like GPT-4 and Claude to understand your codebase and assist you in ways that feel almost magical.

Key Features of Cursor

  • AI Chat: Ask questions about your code, get explanations, and generate snippets directly in the editor.
  • Composer: A multi-file editing mode where you can describe a feature and Cursor will write the code across multiple files.
  • Agent Mode: Let Cursor autonomously perform tasks like running tests, fixing errors, and even committing changes.
  • Codebase Indexing: Cursor indexes your entire project so it can provide context-aware suggestions and answers.
  • VS Code Compatibility: Seamlessly import your VS Code settings, extensions, and keybindings.

Pricing

Cursor offers a generous free tier (Hobby) with limited AI usage. The Pro plan at $20/month gives you unlimited AI completions, 500 fast requests per month, and access to advanced models. For teams, the Business plan is $40/user/month, and Enterprise pricing is custom.

Detailed Review: Windsurf

What is Windsurf?

Windsurf (formerly Codeium) is an AI-powered IDE that focuses on team collaboration and enterprise-grade security. It's built from the ground up with AI at its core, offering a sleek interface and a powerful AI assistant called Cascade. Windsurf aims to streamline the entire development lifecycle, from writing code to reviewing and deploying.

Key Features of Windsurf

  • Cascade AI: A proactive AI assistant that suggests code, fixes bugs, and even writes tests as you type.
  • Team Collaboration: Share AI sessions, collaborate on code in real-time, and maintain a shared knowledge base.
  • Enterprise Security: SOC 2 compliance, SSO, and on-premise deployment options.
  • Multi-Language Support: Works with over 70 programming languages.
  • Integrated Terminal: Run commands and get AI assistance directly in the terminal.

Pricing

Windsurf has a free Starter plan with basic AI features. The Pro plan is $15/month (billed annually) or $19/month (monthly), offering unlimited AI completions and advanced models. For teams, the Team plan is $30/user/month, and Enterprise pricing is custom.

FAQ

1. Is Cursor better than Windsurf?

It depends on your needs. Cursor is better for individual developers who want a VS Code-like experience with advanced AI features. Windsurf is better for teams and enterprises that require collaboration and security. Both are excellent, so consider your priorities.

2. Can I use Cursor for free?

Yes, Cursor offers a free Hobby plan with limited AI completions (2000 per month) and access to basic models. It's great for trying out the tool, but for serious work, you'll likely need the Pro plan.

3. Does Windsurf support VS Code extensions?

Windsurf has its own extension ecosystem, but it doesn't support VS Code extensions natively. However, it offers a wide range of built-in features and integrations to cover most needs.

4. Which tool has better AI models?

Both tools use state-of-the-art models like GPT-4 and Claude. Cursor gives you more flexibility to choose between models, while Windsurf's Cascade AI is optimized for its environment. In practice, both deliver high-quality suggestions.

5. Is Windsurf secure for enterprise use?

Yes, Windsurf is SOC 2 compliant and offers SSO, on-premise deployment, and other enterprise-grade security features. It's designed with security in mind.

6. Can I switch from Cursor to Windsurf easily?

Switching is relatively straightforward. Windsurf can import your settings and keybindings, but you may need to adjust to a different interface. The AI features are similar, so the learning curve is minimal.
