Detailed Review: Cursor

Cursor has been around since 2022 and has quickly become the darling of the AI coding world. Built by Anysphere, it's a fork of VS Code that integrates AI deeply into every aspect of the editor. The result is an experience that feels like VS Code on steroids—familiar but supercharged.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Autocomplete: Cursor's Tab autocomplete is eerily good. It predicts multi-line edits and entire function bodies based on context, not just the next token.
  • Composer: A multi-file editing mode where you can describe a change in natural language and Cursor will apply it across your project. It's like having a junior developer who never sleeps.
  • Chat with Codebase: Ask questions about your code, and Cursor will search your entire project to provide answers with references to specific files and lines.
  • Terminal Integration: Run commands and get AI suggestions for fixing errors directly in the terminal.
  • Privacy Mode: Ensures your code isn't stored or used for training (available on paid plans).

Detailed Review: Windsurf

Windsurf (formerly Codeium) is the new kid on the block, but it's backed by years of experience in AI code completion. Launched in late 2024, Windsurf aims to push the boundaries of what an AI editor can do with its "Cascade" agentic system. It's also a VS Code fork, so you get the familiar interface with a fresh AI twist.

Key Features

  • Cascade: An agentic AI that can autonomously plan and execute multi-step coding tasks, like building a feature from scratch or debugging a complex issue. It can run terminal commands, edit files, and even browse the web for solutions.
  • Supercomplete: Context-aware autocomplete that goes beyond single-line suggestions, offering whole-function completions and edits.
  • AI Chat: Similar to Cursor's chat, you can ask questions about your codebase and get detailed answers.
  • In-Editor Terminal: Integrated terminal with AI assistance for command suggestions and error resolution.
  • Team Collaboration: Built-in features for sharing code snippets and collaborating with team members.

Pricing

Windsurf has a free tier with basic AI completions and limited Cascade usage. The Pro plan is $15/month (or $144/year) and includes unlimited AI completions, 500 Cascade credits per month, and access to premium models. For teams, there's a Teams plan at $30/user/month with additional collaboration and admin features.

Pros

  • Cascade is incredibly powerful for autonomous coding tasks.
  • Lower entry price than Cursor.
  • Fast and lightweight.
  • Strong focus on team collaboration.
  • Frequent updates and responsive support.

Cons

  • Cascade can be unpredictable and sometimes goes off track.
  • Smaller community and fewer extensions compared to Cursor.
  • Privacy mode is not as robust as Cursor's.
  • Still maturing; some features feel beta.
