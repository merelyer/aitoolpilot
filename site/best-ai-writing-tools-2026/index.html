Cons

2. Copy.ai

Features: 30+ copy templates, AI image generation add‑on, brand voice presets.

  • Free plan: 2 k words/mo
  • Pro: $15/mo (unlimited)

Pros: fast short‑copy generation, intuitive UI. Cons: long‑form quality varies, limited SEO tools.

3. Writesonic

Features: AI article writer, ad copy wizard, landing‑page generator, multilingual support (30+ languages).

  • Starter: $13/mo (75 k characters)
  • Professional: $39/mo (unlimited)

Pros: strong ad copy, good for agencies. Cons: occasional factual errors, character‑based pricing can be confusing.

4. Rytr

Features: 25+ use‑case templates, tone selector, plagiarism checker.

  • Free: 5 k characters
  • Premium: $9/mo (200 k characters)

Pros: cheapest tier with decent quality, simple dashboard. Cons: limited advanced formatting, slower generation on busy servers.

5. GrowthBar

Features: AI blog writer, built‑in keyword research, SERP analysis, Chrome extension for on‑page SEO.

  • Basic: $29/mo (unlimited)
  • Pro: $59/mo (team features)

Pros: combines SEO research and writing in one place. Cons: not ideal for non‑SEO copy, UI feels dated.

6. Wordtune

Features: real‑time rewriting, tone adjustment, expand/shorten sentences, Chrome & Microsoft add‑ins.

  • Free: limited rewrites
  • Premium: $12/mo (unlimited)

Pros: excellent for polishing drafts, works inside Google Docs. Cons: No content generation from scratch.

7. Frase.io

Features: AI brief generator, content research, SEO audit, integration with Surfer.

  • Solo: $39/mo
  • Team: $79/mo

Pros: research‑first approach, great for content marketers. Cons: higher price, steep learning curve.

8. ChatGPT Plus

Features: GPT‑4 access, conversational drafting, code generation, plugin ecosystem.

  • Plus: $20/mo

Pros: versatile, strong language understanding. Cons: No built‑in templates, output needs editing for SEO.

9. Sudowrite

Features: creative story prompts, “describe” mode, AI‑assisted brainstorming.

  • Starter: $10/mo

Pros: fantastic for fiction writers, imaginative suggestions. Cons: limited business‑copy features.

10. LongShot AI

Features: fact‑checked long‑form, SEO keyword integration, citation generator.

  • Starter: $25/mo (30 k words)
  • Pro: $70/mo (unlimited)

Pros: emphasis on factual accuracy, good for pillar pages. Cons: slower generation, higher cost for heavy users.
