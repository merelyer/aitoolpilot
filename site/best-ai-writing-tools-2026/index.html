In‑Depth Reviews

1. Jasper AI

Features: 50+ content templates, SEO mode powered by Surfer, team collaboration, Boss Mode for long‑form control, multilingual support (20+ languages).

Pricing: Starter $29/mo (500 words), Boss Mode $59/mo (2,500 words), Enterprise custom.

  • Pros: Highly accurate tone control, built‑in SEO, strong integrations (WordPress, Surfer, Zapier).
  • Cons: Higher price than most competitors, learning curve for Boss Mode.

2. Copy.ai

Features: 90+ copy templates, AI image generation add‑on, brand kit, Chrome extension.

Pricing: Solo $19/mo (unlimited words), Business $49/mo (team features).

  • Pros: Fast idea generation, intuitive UI, affordable for freelancers.
  • Cons: SEO suggestions are basic, limited long‑form capabilities.

3. Writesonic

Features: Landing page builder, ad copy wizard, AI article writer, integration with Shopify and HubSpot.

Pricing: Basic $15/mo (75 credits), Professional $45/mo (unlimited credits), Business $125/mo (team admin).

  • Pros: Credit‑based model suits occasional users, strong ad copy output.
  • Cons: Credit system can be confusing, occasional factual errors.

4. Rytr

Features: 30+ writing tones, plagiarism checker, API access.

Pricing: Free tier (5,000 characters/month), Saver $9/mo (50,000 characters), Unlimited $29/mo.

  • Pros: Cheapest paid plan, decent quality for short copy.
  • Cons: Struggles with technical topics, limited customization.

5. GrammarlyGO

Features: AI rewrite, tone suggestions, real‑time grammar, plagiarism detection, browser extensions.

Pricing: Free (basic), Premium $12/mo, Business $12/user/mo.

  • Pros: Best-in-class grammar, seamless integration with Google Docs.
  • Cons: Not a dedicated content generator, AI output less creative.

6. Wordtune

Features: Real‑time rewriting, expand/shorten, tone shift, Chrome & Microsoft Office add‑ins.

Pricing: Free limited, Premium $9.99/mo.

  • Pros: Improves existing drafts, great for non‑native speakers.
  • Cons: No template library, relies on user‑provided text.

7. INK

Features: SEO score, AI writer, content planner, plagiarism checker, native WordPress plugin.

Pricing: Starter $24/mo, Pro $49/mo, Enterprise custom.

  • Pros: Strong SEO focus, on‑page optimization built‑in.
  • Cons: UI feels dated, slower generation on long articles.

8. Frase AI

Features: Content brief generator, AI writer, question answering, SERP analysis.

Pricing: Solo $14.99/mo, Team $49.99/mo, Enterprise custom.

  • Pros: Excellent for research‑heavy pieces, integrates with Surfer.
  • Cons: Higher cost for teams, learning curve for brief creation.

9. HyperWrite

Features: Story arc assistance, character development, creative prompts, Chrome extension.

Pricing: Free tier, Pro $14/mo.

  • Pros: Ideal for novelists & scriptwriters, imaginative suggestions.
  • Cons: Not suited for SEO‑driven marketing copy.

10. ChatGPT Plus

Features: General purpose chat, code interpreter, plugins, multimodal (text & images).

Pricing: $20/mo for Plus (faster response, GPT‑4 access).

  • Pros: Versatile, constantly updated, massive knowledge base.
  • Cons: No dedicated content templates, output quality varies by prompt.

FAQ

  • What is the best AI writing tool for SEO in 2026? Jasper AI and INK both offer built‑in SEO scoring, but Jasper’s integration with Surfer gives it a slight edge for keyword‑rich content.
  • Can I use AI tools for academic writing? Most platforms have plagiarism checkers, but institutions may consider AI‑generated text a violation. Use them for brainstorming, not final submission.
  • Do these tools support multiple languages? Yes. Jasper, Copy.ai, and Writesonic support 20+ languages, while Rytr covers 12.
  • Is there a free version that’s good enough for occasional use? Rytr’s free tier and Writesonic’s 10‑credit starter let you test basic copy without paying.
  • How do I choose between a credit‑based plan and a word‑limit plan? Credit plans suit irregular usage (e.g., occasional ads). Word‑limit plans are better for steady, high‑volume content creation.
