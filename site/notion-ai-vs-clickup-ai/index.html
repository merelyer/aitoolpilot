Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion AI is an add-on to Notion's popular all-in-one workspace. It brings AI-powered writing assistance, summarization, and brainstorming directly into your notes and documents.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, rewrite sentences, fix grammar, and adjust tone—all within your Notion pages.
  • Summarization: Quickly condense long documents or meeting notes into key points.
  • Brainstorming: Get ideas for projects, blog posts, or strategies with a simple prompt.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers based on your existing notes (available in Notion AI).
  • Translation: Translate text into multiple languages on the fly.

Pricing

Notion AI is priced at $8 per member per month when billed annually, or $10 month-to-month. It's an add-on to any Notion plan (including the free plan). Notion's own plans start at $8/user/month for Plus, but you can use Notion AI with the free plan as well.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's flexible workspace—no context switching.
  • Excellent for writing, editing, and summarizing content.
  • Q&A feature leverages your existing knowledge base.
  • Simple, intuitive interface.

Cons

  • Limited project management features compared to ClickUp.
  • AI capabilities are more focused on text than task automation.
  • Add-on cost can add up for large teams.

Detailed Review: ClickUp AI

ClickUp AI is an add-on to ClickUp, the popular project management platform. It's designed to automate tasks, generate content, and provide insights across your projects.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft emails, project updates, and documents within ClickUp.
  • Task Automation: Generate subtasks, summarize task comments, and create action items from meetings.
  • Project Summaries: Get AI-generated summaries of project status, risks, and progress.
  • Custom AI Templates: Create reusable AI prompts for repetitive tasks.
  • AI Q&A: Ask questions about your projects and get answers based on your data.

Pricing

ClickUp AI costs $5 per user per month as an add-on to any ClickUp plan (including the free plan). ClickUp's own plans start at $7/user/month for Unlimited, but you can use ClickUp AI with the free plan as well.

Pros

  • Deep integration with project management workflows.
  • Automates repetitive tasks and provides project insights.
  • Affordable add-on price.
  • Custom AI templates save time on recurring processes.

Cons

  • Writing assistance is not as refined as Notion AI's.
  • Best value only if you're already using ClickUp for project management.
  • AI features can feel overwhelming for new users.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You already use Notion as your primary workspace for notes, docs, and wikis.
  • Your work involves a lot of writing, editing, and summarizing.
  • You want AI that helps you think and create, not just manage tasks.
  • You need a flexible tool that adapts to your workflow, not the other way around.

Choose ClickUp AI if:

  • You manage projects and teams in ClickUp.
  • You want to automate task creation, status updates, and project summaries.
  • You need AI that integrates with time tracking, goals, and other PM features.
  • You're looking for a more affordable AI add-on with robust project management capabilities.

Bottom Line

Both Notion AI and ClickUp AI are excellent tools, but they serve different purposes. If your work is centered around writing and knowledge management, Notion AI is the clear winner—its writing assistance is more polished and deeply integrated into a flexible workspace. On the other hand, if you're managing projects and want AI to automate tasks and provide insights, ClickUp AI offers better value for money and a more comprehensive project management feature set.

Ultimately, the right choice depends on your primary use case. Many teams actually use both: Notion for documentation and ClickUp for project execution. But if you have to pick one, consider where you spend most of your time.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use Notion AI or ClickUp AI on the free plan?

Yes, both offer limited AI trials on their free plans. Notion AI gives you 20 free AI responses, and ClickUp AI gives you 10. After that, you'll need to purchase the add-on.

2. Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI for writing?

Yes, Notion AI is generally better for writing tasks. It offers more nuanced tone adjustments, better grammar correction, and a more intuitive writing experience. ClickUp AI's writing features are decent but more basic.

3. Which tool is more affordable?

ClickUp AI is more affordable at $5/user/month compared to Notion AI at $8/user/month. However, both require a base plan, so total costs depend on your team size and needs.

4. Can I use ClickUp AI for project management?

Absolutely. ClickUp AI is designed specifically for project management. It can generate tasks, summarize project status, and even predict potential bottlenecks.

5. Do I need to be a Notion or ClickUp user to use their AI?

Yes, both AI tools are add-ons to their respective platforms. You'll need to use Notion or ClickUp as your workspace to access their AI features.

6. Which AI tool is better for team collaboration?

ClickUp AI has an edge for team collaboration because it integrates with task assignments, comments, and project updates. Notion AI is great for collaborative writing and knowledge sharing, but it's less focused on task management.

7. Is there a free trial for the AI add-ons?

Both offer free trials: Notion AI gives you 20 free responses, and ClickUp AI gives you 10. You can also try the full add-on with a paid plan and cancel within the trial period if you're not satisfied.
