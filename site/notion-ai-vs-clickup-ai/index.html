Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion started as a note-taking app and evolved into an all-in-one workspace. With Notion AI, it adds a layer of intelligence that helps you write, summarize, and brainstorm — all without leaving your documents.

Key Features of Notion AI

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generates text, improves grammar, and changes tone.
  • Summarization: Condenses long documents into key points.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers (available in beta).
  • Action Item Extraction: Pulls out tasks from meeting notes.
  • Translation: Translates text into multiple languages.
  • Custom AI Blocks: Create reusable AI prompts for repetitive tasks.

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan. It costs $8 per member per month (billed annually) or $10 month-to-month. This is in addition to your Notion subscription (Free, Plus at $8/user/month, Business at $15/user/month, or Enterprise).

Pros of Notion AI

  • Seamless integration with Notion's powerful document editor.
  • Excellent for writing, editing, and summarizing.
  • Q&A feature leverages your entire workspace knowledge.
  • User-friendly interface; no steep learning curve.
  • Works well for personal use and small teams.

Cons of Notion AI

  • Limited project management capabilities compared to ClickUp.
  • AI features are more focused on text than task automation.
  • Can become expensive when adding AI for large teams.
  • No native time tracking or advanced reporting.

Detailed Review: ClickUp AI

ClickUp is a project management powerhouse, and ClickUp AI brings intelligence to tasks, docs, and workflows. It's designed to help teams automate busywork and focus on what matters.

Key Features of ClickUp AI

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generates and edits text in tasks and docs.
  • Task Summarization: Summarizes task comments and descriptions.
  • Action Item Creation: Turns meeting notes into tasks.
  • AI Standups: Automatically generates standup reports.
  • Project Q&A: Ask questions about your projects and get answers.
  • Custom AI Automations: Trigger AI actions based on project events.

Pricing

ClickUp AI is an add-on available on all paid plans. It costs $7 per member per month (billed annually) or $9 month-to-month. ClickUp plans start at Free, Unlimited at $7/user/month, Business at $12/user/month, and Enterprise.

Pros of ClickUp AI

  • Deep integration with project management features.
  • AI automations save time on repetitive tasks.
  • Comprehensive feature set for teams of all sizes.
  • Competitive pricing, especially for larger teams.
  • Excellent for agile workflows and task management.

Cons of ClickUp AI

  • Steeper learning curve due to the vast feature set.
  • AI writing is good but not as polished as Notion's.
  • Can feel overwhelming for simple note-taking.
  • Some AI features are still in beta.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You primarily need help with writing, editing, and summarizing documents.
  • You run a small team or work solo and want an all-in-one workspace.
  • You value a clean, intuitive interface over complex project management.
  • You already use Notion for note-taking and knowledge management.

Choose ClickUp AI if:

  • You manage projects and need AI to automate task creation and updates.
  • You want AI-powered standups, reports, and project Q&A.
  • You need robust project management features alongside AI.
  • You're willing to invest time in setup for long-term efficiency gains.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Notion AI and ClickUp AI are excellent tools, but they serve different needs. Notion AI excels as a writing and knowledge assistant, while ClickUp AI shines as a project management co-pilot. In 2025, ClickUp AI edges ahead for teams due to its broader automation capabilities and better value for money. However, if your work revolves around documents and notes, Notion AI is the clear winner.

Ultimately, the best choice depends on your workflow. If you're already embedded in one ecosystem, the AI add-on is a no-brainer. If you're starting fresh, consider a free trial of both to see which feels more natural.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for writing, note-taking, and knowledge management. ClickUp AI is better for project management, task automation, and team collaboration. For most teams, ClickUp AI offers more value.

2. Can I use Notion AI and ClickUp AI together?

Yes, you can use both, but it might be redundant. If you need both document editing and project management, you could use Notion for docs and ClickUp for projects, but that means paying for two tools.

3. How much does Notion AI cost?

Notion AI costs $8 per member per month (billed annually) as an add-on to any Notion plan. This is in addition to your Notion subscription.

4. How much does ClickUp AI cost?

ClickUp AI costs $7 per member per month (billed annually) as an add-on to any paid ClickUp plan. It's not available on the Free Forever plan.

5. Which tool has better AI writing capabilities?

Notion AI has slightly better writing capabilities, with more nuanced tone adjustments and summarization. However, ClickUp AI is catching up and is sufficient for most business writing.

6. Do both tools offer a free trial for AI features?

Yes, both Notion and ClickUp offer free trials that include AI features. Notion offers a free plan (AI not included) and a 14-day trial of the AI add-on. ClickUp offers a 14-day free trial of its paid plans, including AI.

7. Can I use AI in Notion or ClickUp for free?

No, AI features are paid add-ons in both tools. However, you can try them free for a limited time.
