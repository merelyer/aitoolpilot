Notion AI Review

What is Notion AI?

Notion AI is an add-on to the popular all-in-one workspace that combines notes, docs, wikis, and databases. It uses AI to help you write, summarize, brainstorm, and edit content without leaving your page. Think of it as a writing assistant that lives inside your second brain.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, continue writing, change tone, fix grammar, and translate.
  • Summarization: Condense long meeting notes or articles into key points.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers sourced from your own docs.
  • Action Item Extraction: Automatically pull to-dos from meeting notes.
  • Database Autofill: Use AI to populate properties like tags or summaries.

Pricing

Notion AI costs $8 per member per month when billed annually, or $10 month-to-month. It's an add-on to any Notion plan, including the free Personal plan. That means you can try Notion AI without upgrading your entire workspace.

Pros

  • Exceptional writing and editing capabilities.
  • Deep integration with Notion pages and databases.
  • Works on the free Notion plan.
  • Fast and responsive.
  • Great for individual knowledge workers.

Cons

  • Limited project management features compared to ClickUp.
  • AI can't automate complex workflows or task dependencies.
  • Add-on cost adds up for large teams.
  • No native time tracking or sprint planning.

ClickUp AI Review

What is ClickUp AI?

ClickUp AI is an add-on to ClickUp, a comprehensive project management platform. It brings AI into tasks, docs, and comments to help you write, summarize, and automate work. Unlike Notion, ClickUp is built for managing projects, so its AI leans more toward productivity and task management.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft task descriptions, comments, and docs.
  • Summarization: Summarize task comments, docs, and threads.
  • Action Items: Extract action items from conversations.
  • AI Templates: Generate project plans, briefs, and more.
  • Standup Reports: Automatically generate team standup summaries.

Pricing

ClickUp AI costs $5 per member per month when billed annually, or $7 month-to-month. It's an add-on to any ClickUp plan, including the Free Forever plan. However, some AI features are limited on lower-tier plans.

Pros

  • Cheaper than Notion AI.
  • Integrated into a full project management suite.
  • Great for teams that live in ClickUp.
  • AI can be used in tasks, docs, and comments.
  • Free plan available to test AI.

Cons

  • AI feels less polished than Notion's, especially for long-form writing.
  • Steeper learning curve due to ClickUp's complexity.
  • Some AI features are only available on paid plans.
  • Not ideal for personal note-taking or knowledge management.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You're a writer, student, or knowledge worker who spends most of your time in docs and notes.
  • You want an AI that helps you think and write better.
  • You already use Notion for personal or team wikis.
  • You need AI to summarize meetings, articles, or research.

Choose ClickUp AI if:

  • You manage complex projects with multiple teams.
  • You need AI to help with task management, standups, and sprint planning.
  • You want a cheaper AI add-on.
  • You're already using ClickUp for project management.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Notion AI and ClickUp AI are excellent additions to their respective platforms, but they serve different masters. Notion AI wins for individual productivity and writing, thanks to its superior language capabilities and seamless integration with a flexible workspace. ClickUp AI is better for team project management, especially if you need AI to handle task-related workflows.

If you're a solo creator or small team focused on content and knowledge, go with Notion AI. If you're a project manager or part of a larger team that needs AI in every task, ClickUp AI is the more practical choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI excels at writing, summarizing, and knowledge management. ClickUp AI is better for project management and task automation. For pure writing assistance, Notion AI is superior; for team project workflows, ClickUp AI has the edge.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion AI is not free, but you can try it free for a limited number of uses. After that, it costs $8 per member per month (billed annually). You can use it on Notion's free plan, so you don't need a paid Notion subscription to add AI.

3. Does ClickUp AI cost extra?

Yes, ClickUp AI is an add-on that costs $5 per member per month (billed annually) on top of your ClickUp plan. It's available on all plans, including the Free Forever plan.

4. Which AI tool is better for small teams?

For small teams focused on content creation and collaboration, Notion AI is often the better fit. For small teams managing multiple projects with tasks and deadlines, ClickUp AI may be more suitable.

5. Can Notion AI and ClickUp AI automate tasks?

Notion AI can automate some content-related tasks like summarizing and generating text, but it doesn't automate project workflows. ClickUp AI can help with task automation, standup reports, and action item extraction, but it's not a full automation engine.

6. Is there a free trial for Notion AI or ClickUp AI?

Yes, both offer free trials. Notion AI gives you a limited number of free AI responses to test. ClickUp AI also offers a free trial period. Check their websites for current offers.
