But they take very different approaches. Notion AI focuses on writing assistance and knowledge management, while ClickUp AI is all about automating tasks and supercharging project management.

So, which one deserves a spot in your toolkit? We put them head-to-head to find out.

Notion AI: The Writing and Knowledge Powerhouse

Notion AI is an add-on to the popular Notion workspace. It brings generative AI directly into your notes, docs, and databases. Whether you're drafting a blog post, summarizing meeting notes, or brainstorming ideas, Notion AI is like having a writing assistant on standby.

Key Features

  • Writing assistance: Generate drafts, improve grammar, change tone, and translate text.
  • Summarization: Instantly summarize long documents or meeting notes.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers based on your content.
  • Action item extraction: Automatically pull out tasks from meeting notes.
  • AI-powered databases: Auto-fill properties, generate summaries, and more.

Pricing

Notion AI is available as an add-on to any Notion plan (including the free plan) for $8 per member per month (billed annually) or $10 month-to-month. You can try it free for a limited number of responses.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's existing workspace
  • Excellent for writing, editing, and summarizing
  • Works across all Notion plans
  • Intuitive and easy to use

Cons

  • Limited project management automation compared to ClickUp
  • Requires a Notion subscription for full functionality
  • No native task dependencies or Gantt charts

ClickUp AI: The Project Management Automation Engine

ClickUp AI is designed to supercharge your project management. It's an add-on to ClickUp's already feature-rich platform, bringing AI to tasks, docs, and everything in between. Think of it as a virtual assistant that helps you plan, execute, and report on projects.

Key Features

  • Task creation and editing: Generate task descriptions, subtasks, and acceptance criteria.
  • Summarization: Summarize task comments, docs, and project updates.
  • Writing assistance: Draft emails, updates, and documentation.
  • Automations: Use AI to trigger actions based on project changes.
  • Standups and reports: Generate standup summaries and project status reports.

Pricing

ClickUp AI is an add-on available on any ClickUp plan (including Free Forever) for $7 per member per month (billed annually) or $9 month-to-month. You get a limited number of free AI credits to try it out.

Pros

  • Deep integration with ClickUp's project management features
  • Powerful automation capabilities
  • Great for teams with complex workflows
  • Affordable add-on pricing

Cons

  • Overwhelming interface for new users
  • AI features are more task-focused than writing-focused
  • Requires ClickUp subscription for full benefits

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You're a writer, student, or knowledge worker
  • You need help with drafting, editing, and summarizing
  • You already use Notion for notes and documentation
  • You prefer a clean, minimalist interface

Choose ClickUp AI if:

  • You manage projects with multiple team members
  • You need task automation and reporting
  • You want AI to help with project planning and execution
  • You're already invested in ClickUp's ecosystem

Bottom Line

Both Notion AI and ClickUp AI are excellent additions to their respective platforms. Your choice ultimately depends on your primary use case. If your work revolves around writing and knowledge management, Notion AI is the clear winner. It's more versatile for text-based tasks and feels like a natural extension of your workspace.

On the other hand, if you're a project manager or part of a team that needs to streamline tasks and automate workflows, ClickUp AI is the better fit. It's tailored for project execution and can save you hours on administrative work.

For most individuals and small teams, we recommend starting with Notion AI due to its broader applicability. But if you're already a ClickUp power user, the AI add-on is a no-brainer.
