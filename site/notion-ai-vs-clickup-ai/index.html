Notion AI: The Writing and Knowledge Assistant

Notion AI is an add-on to Notion's popular all-in-one workspace. It brings AI-powered writing, editing, and summarization directly into your documents, wikis, and databases.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, rewrite sentences, fix grammar, and change tone.
  • Summarization: Condense long documents or meeting notes into key points.
  • AI-Powered Search: Ask questions and get answers from across your entire Notion workspace.
  • Action Item Extraction: Pull out tasks and next steps from meeting notes.
  • Translation: Translate text into multiple languages.
  • Code Generation: Generate code snippets in various languages.

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan. It costs $10 per member per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. You can try it free for a limited number of AI responses.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's powerful workspace.
  • Excellent for writers, researchers, and knowledge workers.
  • AI search is incredibly useful for finding information across pages.
  • Simple, intuitive interface—no learning curve.

Cons

  • Limited project management features compared to dedicated PM tools.
  • AI add-on can get expensive for large teams.
  • Not ideal for complex task dependencies or agile workflows.

ClickUp AI: The Project Management Powerhouse

ClickUp AI is built into ClickUp, a comprehensive project management platform. It's designed to automate tasks, generate content, and provide insights within your projects.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft task descriptions, comments, and documents.
  • Task Automation: Create subtasks, assign work, and set priorities using AI.
  • Summarization: Summarize task threads, comments, and project updates.
  • AI-Powered Search: Find tasks, docs, and information across your workspace.
  • Project Plan Generation: Generate project plans, timelines, and task lists from a prompt.
  • Custom AI Agents: Build AI assistants for specific workflows (available in higher tiers).

Pricing

ClickUp AI is an add-on to ClickUp plans. It costs $5 per user per month (billed annually) or $7 month-to-month. It's available on all paid plans (Unlimited and above).

Pros

  • Deep integration with project management workflows.
  • Powerful automation features that save time on repetitive tasks.
  • More affordable AI add-on compared to Notion.
  • Custom AI agents can be tailored to specific processes.

Cons

  • ClickUp itself has a steeper learning curve.
  • AI features feel more focused on PM use cases—less versatile for general writing.
  • Some AI features are only available on higher-priced plans.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if you:

  • Are a writer, researcher, student, or knowledge worker.
  • Need help drafting, editing, and summarizing long-form content.
  • Want AI-powered search across your notes and documents.
  • Prefer a simple, intuitive tool with minimal setup.
  • Work in a small team or solo.

Choose ClickUp AI if you:

  • Are a project manager, operations lead, or team lead.
  • Manage complex projects with tasks, dependencies, and timelines.
  • Want to automate repetitive project management tasks.
  • Need AI to generate project plans and summaries of task threads.
  • Work in a medium to large team with structured workflows.

Bottom Line: Which Wins in 2025?

There's no one-size-fits-all answer. Notion AI is the better choice for individuals and teams focused on writing, knowledge management, and research. Its AI is more versatile and feels like a natural extension of your thinking process.

ClickUp AI shines for project managers and teams that need AI to handle the grunt work of task management, planning, and reporting. It's more affordable as an add-on, but you'll need to invest time in learning ClickUp's ecosystem.

If I had to pick one for general productivity? Notion AI takes the edge thanks to its broader applicability and smoother user experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for writing, research, and knowledge management. ClickUp AI is better for project management and task automation. For most individuals, Notion AI offers more versatile value.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion AI offers a limited free trial with about 20 AI responses. After that, you need to pay $10/user/month (billed annually) as an add-on to any Notion plan.

3. Does ClickUp AI have a free plan?

ClickUp AI is not available on the free plan. You need a paid ClickUp plan (Unlimited or above) and then add ClickUp AI for $5/user/month (billed annually).

4. Which tool is more affordable?

ClickUp AI is cheaper as an add-on ($5 vs $10 per user/month), but you also need to pay for a ClickUp plan. Notion AI requires a Notion plan, but Notion's free plan is quite generous. Overall, costs can be similar depending on team size and needs.

5. Can I use both Notion AI and ClickUp AI together?

Absolutely. Many teams use Notion for documentation and knowledge base, and ClickUp for project management. You can use both AI add-ons to maximize productivity, though it adds to your software stack.

6. Which AI is better for writing?

Notion AI is generally better for writing tasks. It offers more nuanced control over tone, style, and summarization, and it's integrated into a writing-friendly environment.

7. Does ClickUp AI integrate with other tools?

Yes, ClickUp integrates with over 1,000 apps, and ClickUp AI can work within those integrations (e.g., summarizing Slack messages). Notion also has integrations, but ClickUp's are more focused on project management.
