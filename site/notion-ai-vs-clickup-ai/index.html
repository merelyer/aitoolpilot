Notion AI is the thoughtful writing assistant baked into a flexible workspace. ClickUp AI is the productivity powerhouse embedded in a full project management suite. So which one actually delivers? Notion AI: The Writing Assistant That Lives in Your Workspace

Notion started as a note-taking app and evolved into an all-in-one workspace. Notion AI, launched in 2023, adds a layer of generative AI that can write, summarize, translate, and brainstorm—all without leaving your page.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate blog posts, emails, meeting agendas, and more. You can adjust tone (professional, casual, confident) and length.
  • Summarization: Turn long meeting notes into concise summaries with action items.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace, and Notion AI will pull answers from your notes and documents.
  • Translation: Instantly translate text into multiple languages.
  • Brainstorming: Generate ideas for projects, content, or strategies.
  • Autofill Databases: Automatically fill properties like tags or summaries based on page content.

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan. It costs $10 per member per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. You need a Notion plan (Free, Plus at $10/month, Business at $15/month, or Enterprise). The AI add-on is available on all plans, including Free.

Pros

  • Exceptional writing quality—often indistinguishable from human writing.
  • Seamless integration with your existing Notion workspace.
  • Affordable add-on pricing.
  • Q&A feature is surprisingly accurate for finding information.
  • Works across all devices (web, desktop, mobile).

Cons

  • Requires a separate Notion subscription.
  • AI features are limited to text—no image generation or complex data analysis.
  • Can be slow during peak times.
  • Not ideal for heavy project management (no Gantt charts, limited task dependencies).

ClickUp AI: The Productivity Powerhouse for Teams

ClickUp is a project management platform that aims to replace all your other tools. ClickUp AI, launched in 2023, brings AI into every corner of the app—from task creation to goal tracking.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft task descriptions, comments, and docs with AI.
  • Task Automation: Automatically create subtasks, assignees, and due dates based on task titles.
  • Summarization: Summarize task comments, docs, and meeting notes.
  • Project Q&A: Ask questions about your projects and get answers from your ClickUp data.
  • Standup Reports: Generate daily standup summaries for your team.
  • Email Drafting: Compose emails based on task context.
  • AI Templates: Generate project templates, checklists, and more.

Pricing

ClickUp AI is an add-on to any ClickUp plan. It costs $7 per user per month (billed annually) or $9 month-to-month. You need a ClickUp plan (Free Forever, Unlimited at $7/month, Business at $12/month, or Enterprise). The AI add-on is available on all paid plans.

Pros

  • Deep integration with project management features.
  • Powerful automation—AI can create tasks, subtasks, and assignees.
  • Competitive pricing.
  • Great for teams that need AI in their task management.
  • Includes AI in comments and notifications.

Cons

  • Writing quality is good but not as polished as Notion AI.
  • Requires a ClickUp subscription.
  • Can be overwhelming for new users—ClickUp has a steep learning curve.
  • AI features are more focused on tasks than creative writing.

Bottom Line: Which Wins in 2025?

There's no one-size-fits-all answer. Notion AI wins for individuals and small teams focused on writing, note-taking, and knowledge management. Its AI is more natural, and the pricing is attractive. ClickUp AI wins for teams that need AI baked into their project management workflows. It's more powerful for task automation and team collaboration.

If you're just starting out and want the best AI writing experience, go with Notion AI. If you're running a team and need AI to keep projects on track, ClickUp AI is the better fit. Either way, you can't go wrong—both are excellent tools that will save you hours every week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for writing, note-taking, and knowledge management. ClickUp AI is better for project management, task automation, and team collaboration. If you're a writer, Notion AI wins. If you're a project manager, ClickUp AI wins.

Can I use Notion AI without a Notion subscription?

No, Notion AI is an add-on that requires a Notion plan. However, you can use Notion AI on the free Notion plan by paying the $10/month add-on fee.

Is ClickUp AI available on the free plan?

No, ClickUp AI is only available on paid plans (Unlimited, Business, and Enterprise). The free plan does not include AI features.

Which tool has better AI writing quality?

Notion AI generally produces more natural, human-like writing. ClickUp AI is good but more focused on task-related content. For creative writing, Notion AI is superior.

Do I need both Notion AI and ClickUp AI?

Probably not. Most users will be better off choosing one based on their primary use case. However, some teams use Notion for documentation and ClickUp for project management—in that case, having AI in both could be beneficial.

How much does Notion AI cost compared to ClickUp AI?

Notion AI costs $10 per member per month (add-on). ClickUp AI costs $7 per user per month (add-on). Both require a base subscription, so total costs vary. ClickUp AI is slightly cheaper on the AI add-on itself.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes! Notion offers a free plan, and you can try Notion AI for free for a limited time. ClickUp offers a free forever plan, and you can try ClickUp AI on a free trial of a paid plan.
