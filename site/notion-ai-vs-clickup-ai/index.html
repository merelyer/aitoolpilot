Notion AI Review: The Writing Assistant That Lives in Your Notes

Notion AI isn't just a chatbot—it's woven into every corner of your Notion workspace. Highlight any text, and you can ask AI to summarize, translate, fix grammar, or even generate new content. It's like having a personal editor on standby 24/7.

Key Features of Notion AI

  • Writing assistance: Improve, shorten, or change tone of any text.
  • Summarization: Turn long meeting notes into concise bullet points.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your entire workspace and get instant answers.
  • Autofill: Automatically populate database properties (like priority or tags).
  • Translation: Translate content into 10+ languages.

Notion AI Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan, costing $10 per member per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. It's available on Free, Plus, Business, and Enterprise plans. The base Notion plans start at $0 for personal use, $10/month for Plus (per user), and $18/month for Business.

  • Works across all devices.

Cons of Notion AI

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI excels at writing, summarizing, and knowledge management, while ClickUp AI is superior for task automation and project management. For individual writers, Notion AI is often better; for project managers, ClickUp AI wins.

Can I use Notion AI and ClickUp AI together?

Absolutely. Many teams use Notion for documentation and wikis, and ClickUp for project management. Both AIs can complement each other, though you'll pay for both subscriptions.

How much does Notion AI cost in 2025?

Notion AI is an add-on priced at $10 per member per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. It requires a Notion plan, which starts free for personal use.

How much does ClickUp AI cost in 2025?

ClickUp AI is an add-on available on all paid plans for $7 per member per month (billed annually). ClickUp's base plans start free and go up to $12/month for Business.

Which AI tool is better for small teams?

For small teams focused on content creation and collaboration, Notion AI is often the better choice due to its intuitive interface and strong writing features. For small teams managing multiple projects, ClickUp AI's automation can save more time.

Does Notion AI work offline?

Notion AI requires an internet connection to function, as it processes requests in the cloud. However, you can access your notes offline in Notion, and AI features will resume when you're back online.

Is ClickUp AI worth the money?

If you manage complex projects and repetitive tasks, ClickUp AI can be a worthwhile investment. The $7/month add-on can save hours of manual work each week, especially for team leads and project managers.
