Notion AI Review

Notion AI is an add-on to the popular all-in-one workspace. It brings AI-powered writing assistance, summarization, and Q&A directly into your notes and docs.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate, edit, and summarize text with prompts.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers with citations.
  • Auto-fill Database Properties: Automatically categorize and tag database items.
  • Meeting Notes: Transcribe and summarize meetings (beta).
  • Translation: Translate content into multiple languages.

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on that costs $10 per user per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. It requires a paid Notion plan (Plus at $10/user/month or Business at $15/user/month). The free plan does not include AI.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's writing and database features.
  • Excellent for summarizing long documents and extracting action items.
  • Q&A feature is powerful for finding information across your workspace.
  • User-friendly interface with minimal learning curve.

Cons

  • Limited to Notion's ecosystem—no support for external tasks or projects.
  • AI features are primarily text-focused; less useful for project management.
  • Add-on cost can add up for larger teams.
  • No AI in the free plan.

ClickUp AI Review

ClickUp AI is a comprehensive AI layer on top of ClickUp's project management platform. It assists with task creation, project summaries, and even generating subtasks.

Key Features

  • AI Project Manager: Generate task lists, assignees, and due dates from a prompt.
  • AI Notetaker: Transcribe and summarize meetings, then create action items.
  • AI StandUp: Automatically generate standup reports from task updates.
  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft and edit documents, comments, and descriptions.
  • AI Custom Fields: Auto-populate custom fields based on task content.

Pricing

ClickUp AI is an add-on available on all paid plans. It costs $7 per user per month (billed annually) or $9 month-to-month. ClickUp's paid plans start at $7/user/month for Unlimited, $12 for Business, and $19 for Business Plus. AI is not available on the Free Forever plan.

Pros

  • Deep integration with project management workflows.
  • AI can generate entire project plans, including tasks and dependencies.
  • Notetaker and StandUp features save hours of manual work.
  • More affordable than Notion AI at $7/user/month.

Cons

  • ClickUp's interface can be overwhelming for new users.
  • Some AI features are still in beta and can be inconsistent.
  • Requires a paid ClickUp plan, adding to the cost.
  • Less focused on knowledge management and documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

No, Notion AI is a paid add-on. You need a paid Notion plan (Plus or Business) and pay an additional $10/user/month for AI features.

3. Does ClickUp AI work on the free plan?

No, ClickUp AI is only available on paid plans (Unlimited and above) as an add-on for $7/user/month.

4. Which AI tool is more affordable?

ClickUp AI is more affordable at $7/user/month compared to Notion AI at $10/user/month. However, consider the base plan costs as well.

5. Can I use both Notion AI and ClickUp AI together?

Yes, you can use both if you use both platforms. However, that might be overkill for most users. Choose the one that aligns with your primary workflow.

6. What are the main limitations of Notion AI?

Notion AI is limited to the Notion ecosystem and is primarily text-focused. It lacks advanced project management features like task dependencies and Gantt charts.

7. What are the main limitations of ClickUp AI?

ClickUp AI can be overwhelming due to ClickUp's complex interface, and some AI features are still in beta. It's also less suited for long-form writing and documentation.
