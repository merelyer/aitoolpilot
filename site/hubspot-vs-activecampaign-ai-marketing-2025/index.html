  • Full CRM Suite: Marketing, sales, service, CMS, and operations hubs — all connected. No more syncing tools.

  • Native Integrations: Over 1,500 apps connect seamlessly. If you use Salesforce, Slack, or Shopify, it just works.

Who Should Use What

Choose HubSpot if:

  • You're a mid-market or enterprise company (50+ employees)
  • You need a unified CRM, marketing, sales, and service platform
  • Your sales team relies on deep pipeline management
  • You have budget for a dedicated marketing ops person

Choose ActiveCampaign if:

  • You're a small business or startup (<50 employees)
  • Email marketing and automation are your primary focus
  • You want AI features without enterprise pricing
  • You need to launch campaigns quickly with minimal training

Hybrid approach:

Many teams use ActiveCampaign for email automation and HubSpot's free CRM for contact management. This gives you the best of both worlds without paying premium prices.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both platforms are excellent in 2025, but they serve different audiences.

ActiveCampaign is the smart choice for ROI-focused marketers. You get AI-powered automation, predictive sending, and a capable CRM — all for under $200/month. The AI Copilot alone can save your team 10+ hours per week.

HubSpot is the right pick for organizations that need an all-in-one growth platform. If you're willing to invest $1,000+/month, you get a flawless experience where every tool talks to each other. It's the choice for companies that value scale over savings.

Our final recommendation: Start with ActiveCampaign. It's cheaper, faster to implement, and its AI features are just as strong (if not stronger) than HubSpot's. Upgrade to HubSpot only when you outgrow ActiveCampaign's CRM limitations.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is ActiveCampaign better than HubSpot for email automation?

Yes, for most users. ActiveCampaign's automation builder is more flexible and intuitive, allowing complex multi-step journeys without code. HubSpot's automation is powerful but requires more setup and typically costs more for the same functionality.

2. Does HubSpot have better AI features than ActiveCampaign?

It depends on the feature. HubSpot's AI content assistant is excellent for generating blogs and emails, but many advanced AI features (like predictive lead scoring) are locked behind the $3,600/month Enterprise plan. ActiveCampaign's AI Copilot and predictive sending are available on the Pro plan for $145/month — a fraction of the cost.

3. Can I migrate from ActiveCampaign to HubSpot easily?

Yes, both platforms offer migration tools and support. However, expect a learning curve — HubSpot's interface and terminology differ significantly. Plan for 2–4 weeks of transition time if you're moving complex automations.

4. Which is cheaper: HubSpot or ActiveCampaign?

ActiveCampaign is significantly cheaper. For a typical SMB with 2,500 contacts, ActiveCampaign Pro costs $145/month. HubSpot's equivalent (Professional) starts at $890/month. Over a year, that's a $8,940 difference.

5. Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?

No, ActiveCampaign doesn't offer a permanent free tier. They provide a 14-day free trial with all features. HubSpot does offer a free CRM that includes basic email marketing, but it's limited to 2,000 contacts and lacks automation.

6. Which platform is better for e-commerce?

ActiveCampaign has stronger e-commerce integrations with Shopify and WooCommerce, including abandoned cart automation and purchase tracking. HubSpot offers these too, but its e-commerce features require the higher-tier plans to be effective.

7. Can I use both HubSpot and ActiveCampaign together?

Yes, many companies do. Use HubSpot's free CRM for contact management and ActiveCampaign for email/automation. There are native integrations available, though you'll need to be careful about syncing to avoid duplicates.
