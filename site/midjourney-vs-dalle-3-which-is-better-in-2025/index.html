Detailed Review: Midjourney

Midjourney has evolved significantly. In 2025, it offers a standalone web app (no longer Discord-only) and advanced features like Style References, Character References, and Personalization. Its core strength remains artistic quality: images have a rich, painterly feel that's hard to replicate.

Key Features

  • Style References: Upload an image to match its aesthetic.
  • Character References: Keep consistent characters across scenes.
  • Personalization: Train the model on your preferences.
  • Inpainting/Outpainting: Edit or extend images (limited in DALL-E 3).
  • High Resolution: Up to 1024x1024 or higher with upscaling.

Pricing

  • Basic Plan: $10/month (~200 images/month)
  • Standard Plan: $30/month (~900 images/month, faster generation)
  • Pro Plan: $60/month (~1800 images/month, priority queue)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for teams

Pros

  • Superior artistic style and creativity
  • Excellent for concept art and illustrations
  • Active community and continuous updates

Cons

  • Steep learning curve for prompts
  • Poor text rendering in images
  • No free tier (trial only)

Detailed Review: DALL-E 3

DALL-E 3, integrated into ChatGPT Plus, is all about ease of use and photorealism. It excels at generating images with accurate text (e.g., signs, labels) and handles complex prompts well. The interface is clean and beginner-friendly.

Key Features

  • ChatGPT Integration: Generate images directly from chat prompts.
  • Text-to-Image: Best-in-class for rendering words.
  • Photorealism: Realistic lighting, shadows, and textures.
  • Automatic Prompt Refinement: ChatGPT rewrites your prompts for better results.
  • Safety Filters: Strict content moderation (can be limiting).

Pricing

  • Free Tier: Limited images via ChatGPT (slow, low priority)
  • ChatGPT Plus: $20/month (unlimited images, faster)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for organizations

Pros

  • Easiest to use (no prompt engineering needed)
  • Best text rendering in AI images
  • Photorealistic outputs

Cons

  • Less creative freedom (overly safe)
  • Limited editing tools (no inpainting in standard version)
  • Dependent on ChatGPT subscription

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is better for realistic images, Midjourney or DALL-E 3?

DALL-E 3 generally produces more photorealistic images with accurate lighting and textures. Midjourney can also do realism but excels at artistic, stylized outputs.

2. Can I use Midjourney for free?

No, Midjourney has no free tier. You can use a limited trial, but after that you need a subscription starting at $10/month.

3. Does DALL-E 3 support image editing?

DALL-E 3 has limited editing capabilities. You can regenerate parts of an image via ChatGPT, but it lacks the inpainting/outpainting tools found in Midjourney.

4. Which tool handles text in images better?

DALL-E 3 is significantly better at rendering text accurately (e.g., signs, labels). Midjourney often produces garbled or stylized text.

5. Is Midjourney or DALL-E 3 better for commercial use?

Both allow commercial use, but Midjourney's terms are more flexible for selling generated art. DALL-E 3's terms are also commercial-friendly via OpenAI's usage policies.

6. Can I use DALL-E 3 without ChatGPT?

No, DALL-E 3 is integrated into ChatGPT Plus. You cannot access it as a standalone tool (unlike DALL-E 2).

7. Which one has better community and support?

Midjourney has a large, active community on Discord and dedicated forums. DALL-E 3 benefits from OpenAI's broader ecosystem and documentation.

Still unsure? Try both with their trial options. For most users, we recommend starting with Midjourney if you value artistic control, or DALL-E 3 for speed and simplicity.
