Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the difference between token‑based and per‑user pricing? Token‑based plans charge for the amount of AI output you consume, making them ideal for occasional users. Per‑user pricing bundles a set amount of tokens and adds features like team management.
  • Can I use these assistants offline? Only TurboCoder and PromptCraft (when paired with a self‑hosted LLM) offer true offline capability. The others rely on cloud APIs.
  • Do AI code assistants write secure code? Most tools, including CodeWhisper Pro and GitMate AI, include security linting that flags common vulnerabilities, but they are not a replacement for manual security reviews.
  • Are there free alternatives? Yes—most vendors provide a limited free tier. For truly unlimited free usage, open‑source models like StarCoder can be self‑hosted, though they lack the polished IDE plugins of commercial products.
  • How do I choose the right tool for my team? Match the tool’s strengths to your primary workflow: Git‑centric teams → GitMate AI; UI‑heavy front‑ends → AI‑Scribe; data‑science → Codexly; enterprise compliance → TurboCoder.
