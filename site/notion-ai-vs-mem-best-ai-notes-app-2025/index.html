Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion needs no introduction. It has evolved from a simple note-taking app into the backbone of thousands of companies. In 2025, Notion AI is not just an add-on; it is deeply woven into the fabric of the workspace. It feels less like a chatbot and more like a collaborative assistant that understands your entire wiki.

Key Features of Notion AI

  • Q&A Across Your Workspace: Ask questions like "What were the action items from last week's meeting?" and Notion AI will search across all your pages, databases, and documents to provide a synthesized answer.
  • AI Autofill for Databases: This is a game-changer. You can generate properties for database entries automatically. For example, if you have a database of blog posts, Notion AI can automatically generate SEO meta descriptions or summaries for each row.
  • Writing Assistance: Drafting, summarizing, improving writing, and translating text are all standard. The writing quality is high and can match your brand voice with custom instructions.
  • AI Meeting Notes: Notion AI can join your Google Meet or Zoom calls, take notes, and generate action items automatically, which are then saved to your workspace.
  • Contextual Understanding: The AI understands the context of the page you are on. If you are in a project timeline, it can help you break down tasks or predict potential bottlenecks.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem is the anti-Notion. While Notion asks you to build a system, Mem builds the system for you. It is an AI-native note-taking app that focuses on capturing information with zero friction and letting the AI organize it in the background. The tagline is "Show Notes, Not Folders."

Key Features of Mem

  • Self-Organizing Workspace: You never have to create a folder. Mem uses AI to automatically connect related notes based on context and content. It creates a dynamic "knowledge graph" of your ideas.
  • Smart Capture: The Mem app is built for speed. You can capture a note in seconds from any device. The keyboard shortcuts are intuitive, and the mobile widget is excellent.
  • Related Notes Pane: When viewing any note, Mem shows a sidebar of related notes pulled from your entire collection. This is incredible for discovering past ideas you forgot you had.
  • Chat with Your Notes: Mem's AI chat is powerful. You can ask it to "Summarize my notes on marketing" or "Find the email draft I wrote about pricing." It understands your entire library.
  • Mem It: A browser extension that lets you save web pages, articles, and tweets directly to Mem, where they are automatically tagged and linked.

Who Should Use What?

Choosing between Notion AI and Mem is not about which is "better"—it is about which fits your workflow.

Choose Notion AI If:

  • You manage projects and need to track tasks, deadlines, and dependencies.
  • You work in a team and need a shared workspace with permissions and comments.
  • You love building custom databases and views (Kanban, Calendar, Timeline).
  • You need a single tool to replace your wiki, docs, and project management software.

Choose Mem If:

  • You are a student, researcher, or writer who takes lots of notes.
  • You hate organizing and want an app that does it for you.
  • You need a fast, distraction-free writing environment.
  • You want to build a "second brain" without the setup time.

Bottom Line: Which One Wins in 2025?

If you are a solo user who wants a smart notebook that helps you think, Mem is the winner. It is cheaper, more intuitive, and its AI is more focused on the note-taking experience. It excels at helping you retrieve information you forgot you had.

If you are a team or power user who needs a command center for your work, Notion AI is the winner. The ability to ask questions across your entire workspace and have AI populate database properties is unparalleled. It is a heavier investment, but it pays dividends in productivity.

Our final recommendation is Notion AI for its sheer versatility and long-term potential. However, we strongly encourage you to test both. Start with their free tiers, create a sample project, and see which one feels more natural.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI better than Mem?

Notion AI is better for team collaboration, project management, and complex databases. Mem is better for individual note-taking, automatic organization, and quick capture. The "best" choice depends on whether you need a full workspace or a smart notebook.

Can Mem replace Notion?

No, Mem cannot fully replace Notion for most users. Mem lacks the database, project management, and collaboration features that Notion offers. However, Mem can replace Notion for simple note-taking and personal knowledge management.

Is Notion AI worth the extra cost?

For teams, yes. The AI features included in the Business plan can save hours of manual data entry and searching. For individuals on the Plus plan, the $8/user/month add-on is only worth it if you frequently use AI writing or Q&A features.

Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem has a free plan with unlimited notes and basic search. However, the AI features that make Mem unique (like related notes and AI chat) are only available on the paid Pro plan.

Which app is better for students?

Mem is generally better for students because it is simpler, cheaper, and excels at connecting lecture notes. Notion AI is better for students who need to manage complex projects or collaborate on group assignments.

Can I use Notion AI offline?

No, Notion AI requires an internet connection to function. The basic Notion app also requires online access for syncing, though you can view cached pages offline.
