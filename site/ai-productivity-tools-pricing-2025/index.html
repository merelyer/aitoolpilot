Detailed Reviews of Each Tool

1. Notion AI

Notion AI isn’t a standalone product—it’s an add-on to your existing Notion workspace. But for anyone already using Notion (and millions do), it’s a game-changer. You can generate summaries, rewrite paragraphs, brainstorm ideas, and even draft emails directly in your docs. The AI feels native, not bolted on.

Pricing: $10/month per member (annual) or $12/month per member (monthly). There’s no free tier for AI, but you can use Notion’s free plan without AI features. A 7-day free trial is available.

Pros:

  • Deep integration with Notion’s databases and pages
  • Excellent writing and summarization quality
  • Transparent per-member pricing

Cons:

  • Requires a Notion workspace (not standalone)
  • No advanced automation or scheduling
  • AI can sometimes be too verbose

2. Motion

Motion is an AI-powered calendar and task manager that automatically schedules your day. It prioritizes tasks based on deadlines and effort, then blocks time on your calendar. It’s a lifesaver for people drowning in meetings.

Pricing: $19/user/month (annual) or $34/user/month (monthly). There’s no free tier, but a 7-day trial is available. The price jumps significantly for teams.

Pros:

  • Auto-scheduling saves hours each week
  • Smart prioritization that actually works
  • Integrates with Google Calendar and Outlook

Cons:

  • Expensive for teams
  • Steep learning curve
  • Limited to task management—no document AI

3. Mem

Mem calls itself a “thinking app.” It’s a knowledge base that uses AI to surface relevant notes automatically. You write naturally, and Mem organizes everything in the background. Great for researchers and writers.

Pricing: Free tier (1GB storage, limited AI). Pro is $14.99/month (annual) with unlimited AI and 10GB storage. Teams plan at $25/user/month.

Pros:

  • Generous free tier
  • AI suggestions are contextual and helpful
  • Clean, distraction-free interface

Cons:

  • Limited integrations
  • No project management features
  • AI can be slow at times

4. Taskade

Taskade is a collaborative workspace with AI that helps you generate tasks, outlines, and mind maps. It’s especially strong for remote teams that need real-time collaboration.

Pricing: Free tier (unlimited tasks, limited AI). Pro is $8/month per member (annual) with unlimited AI. Business at $16/month per member.

Pros:

  • Real-time collaboration with video chat
  • Affordable Pro tier
  • Mind map and list views

Cons:

  • AI features are less polished than Notion’s
  • Mobile app can be buggy
  • Limited third-party integrations

5. ClickUp AI

ClickUp AI is an add-on for ClickUp’s already massive project management platform. It helps with writing, summarizing, and generating tasks. If you’re already a ClickUp user, it’s a no-brainer.

Pricing: $5/user/month (add-on to any paid ClickUp plan). Unlimited plan starts at $7/user/month (so total $12/user/month for AI). Free tier available without AI.

Pros:

  • Very affordable add-on
  • Works inside ClickUp’s powerful workflow
  • Good for generating task descriptions and comments

Cons:

  • Requires ClickUp (can be overwhelming)
  • AI can be inconsistent
  • No standalone use

Who Should Use What

Notion AI is your best bet if you live in Notion and want AI to help you write, summarize, and brainstorm. Perfect for content teams, product managers, and students.

Motion is ideal for busy executives or freelancers who struggle with time management. If you’re constantly missing deadlines, Motion’s auto-scheduling will change your life.

Mem shines for researchers, writers, and anyone who collects a lot of information. Its free tier is a great way to dip your toes into AI note-taking.

Taskade works well for small remote teams that need a simple, collaborative workspace with AI assistance. It’s affordable and fun to use.

ClickUp AI is for power users of ClickUp who want to save time on repetitive writing tasks. If you’re already using ClickUp, it’s a cheap upgrade.

Bottom Line Verdict

After comparing all five tools, Notion AI comes out on top for its seamless integration, high-quality AI, and fair pricing. It’s not the cheapest, but it delivers the most value for most people. Motion is a strong contender if scheduling is your pain point. For budget-conscious users, Mem’s free tier is surprisingly capable. Ultimately, the best tool is the one you’ll actually use—so take advantage of free trials before committing.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the cheapest AI productivity tool in 2025?

Taskade offers the lowest starting price at $8/month per member (annual) for its Pro tier with unlimited AI. Mem’s free tier is also a great option if you can live with limited storage.

2. Is Notion AI worth the extra cost?

If you already use Notion, yes. The AI add-on at $10/month per member is reasonably priced for the quality of writing and summarization. If you don’t use Notion, you may find better value elsewhere.

3. Can I use AI productivity tools for free?

Yes. Mem and Taskade both offer free tiers with limited AI features. Notion and ClickUp have free plans but without AI. Motion has no free tier.

4. Which AI productivity tool is best for project management?

ClickUp AI combined with ClickUp’s project management platform is the most comprehensive. However, Motion is better for scheduling and task prioritization.

5. Do these tools offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes, all of them offer discounts for annual billing—typically 15-30% off the monthly price. Notion AI, for example, drops from $12 to $10 per member.

6. How do I choose between Notion AI and Motion?

If you need help writing and organizing notes, go with Notion AI. If you need help managing your calendar and tasks, choose Motion. They solve different problems.

7. Are there any hidden fees in these pricing plans?

Generally no, but always check the fine print. Some tools charge extra for advanced integrations or API access. Enterprise plans are almost always custom-priced.
