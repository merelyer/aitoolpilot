Detailed Review of Each Tool

1. Descript

Descript revolutionizes video editing by treating your footage like a text document. You can edit video by editing the transcript, remove filler words, and even generate AI avatars. Its pricing is straightforward:

  • Free Tier: 1 hour of transcription, limited exports.
  • Starter: $24/month – 10 hours of transcription, 4K video, all core features.
  • Pro: $40/month – 30 hours of transcription, multi-track editing, advanced AI tools.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing – unlimited hours, team management, priority support.

Pros: Intuitive interface, powerful AI transcription, screen recording built-in, collaboration features.

Cons: Limited video effects compared to Adobe, export quality capped at 4K on Starter plan.

2. RunwayML

RunwayML is the go-to for cutting-edge AI effects like inpainting, super-slow motion, and text-to-video generation. It’s a favorite among experimental filmmakers and agencies.

  • Free Tier: 125 credits (about 5 minutes of video processing).
  • Starter: $15/month – 625 credits, 1080p export, watermark removed.
  • Pro: $35/month – 2,500 credits, 4K export, priority processing.
  • Enterprise: Custom – unlimited credits, dedicated API, team accounts.

Pros: Unmatched AI effects, fast processing, good for short-form content.

Cons: Credit system can be confusing, no traditional timeline editing, steep learning curve.

3. CapCut

ByteDance’s CapCut is the most accessible AI video editor, especially for TikTok and Instagram Reels. Its free tier is surprisingly robust.

  • Free Tier: Full editing suite, AI captions, many effects, 1080p export.
  • Starter (CapCut Pro): $7.99/month – 4K export, premium effects, cloud storage.
  • Pro: $12.99/month – Advanced AI features like auto-cut and background removal.
  • Enterprise: Custom – team collaboration, brand kits.

Pros: Excellent free version, easy to use, great for mobile and desktop.

Cons: Limited advanced editing, data privacy concerns, watermark on free exports.

4. Adobe Premiere Pro (AI Features)

Adobe’s flagship editor now integrates AI tools like Auto Reframe, Scene Edit Detection, and Text-Based Editing. It’s the industry standard but comes with a premium.

  • Free Tier: 7-day trial.
  • Starter (Individual): $22.99/month – Full Premiere Pro, 100GB cloud storage.
  • Pro (Creative Cloud All Apps): $59.99/month – All Adobe apps, more storage.
  • Enterprise: Custom – advanced admin, security, and collaboration.

Pros: Unmatched professional features, huge plugin ecosystem, integration with After Effects.

Cons: Expensive, steep learning curve, AI features often require additional plugins or updates.

5. Pictory

Pictory specializes in repurposing long-form videos (e.g., webinars, podcasts) into short clips. It’s ideal for marketers who need quick social media content.

  • Free Tier: 3 projects, limited exports.
  • Starter: $19/month – 10 videos/month, 15 minutes per video.
  • Pro: $39/month – 30 videos/month, 30 minutes per video, branded templates.
  • Enterprise: Custom – unlimited videos, team seats, API access.

Pros: Fast repurposing, AI auto-captioning, easy to use.

Cons: Limited editing control, no timeline, not for original content creation.

Who Should Use What

For Social Media Creators & Beginners

CapCut is your best bet. Its free tier is generous, and the Pro plan is affordable. Perfect for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

For Podcasters & Screen Recorders

Descript is unmatched. Edit your video by editing text, remove filler words, and add captions automatically. The Starter plan covers most needs.

For Professional Filmmakers & Editors

Adobe Premiere Pro remains the industry standard, but be prepared for the cost. If you need advanced AI effects, RunwayML is a powerful companion tool.

For Content Repurposing

Pictory is purpose-built for turning long videos into short clips. If you have a library of webinars or podcasts, it’s a time-saver.

For Experimental & High-End AI Effects

RunwayML leads the pack. Use it for generative video, inpainting, and super-slow motion. The credit system works well for short projects.

If you’re on a tight budget, start with CapCut’s free plan. For professionals who need the full Adobe ecosystem, Premiere Pro is still the king, but budget accordingly. RunwayML is a fantastic specialist tool for AI effects, but not a primary editor.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest AI video editing software in 2025?

CapCut offers the most robust free tier, with no watermark on exports if you use the basic features. For paid plans, CapCut Pro starts at $7.99/month, making it the most affordable paid AI video editor.

Is Descript worth the money for video editing?

Yes, if you frequently edit talking-head videos, podcasts, or screen recordings. Descript’s text-based editing saves hours, and the AI transcription is highly accurate. The Starter plan at $24/month is excellent value for most creators.

Does Adobe Premiere Pro have AI features in 2025?

Yes, Adobe Premiere Pro includes AI features like Auto Reframe (for different aspect ratios), Scene Edit Detection, and Text-Based Editing. However, some advanced AI tools require separate plugins or a Creative Cloud subscription that includes After Effects.

Can I use RunwayML for free?

RunwayML offers a free tier with 125 credits, which is enough for about 5 minutes of video processing. For regular use, you'll need a paid plan starting at $15/month. The free version is good for testing the features.

What is the best AI video editing software for beginners?

CapCut is the most beginner-friendly with its intuitive interface and generous free tier. Descript is also easy to learn if you're comfortable with text editing. Both have excellent tutorials and community support.

Do AI video editors work on mobile?

CapCut has a full-featured mobile app for iOS and Android. Descript and RunwayML are primarily desktop/web-based, though RunwayML offers a mobile app for viewing. Adobe Premiere Rush (mobile version) is available but limited.
