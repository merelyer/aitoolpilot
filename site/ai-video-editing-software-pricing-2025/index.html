Yes, Descript, Runway, InVideo, Pictory, and VEED.io all offer free plans. However, they come with restrictions like watermarks, limited exports, or reduced features.

Can AI video editors replace human editors?

Not entirely. AI tools automate repetitive tasks and assist with creative decisions, but human oversight is still needed for complex storytelling and nuanced editing.

Yes, most offer team or enterprise plans with collaboration features. Descript, InVideo, and Pictory have specific team pricing.
