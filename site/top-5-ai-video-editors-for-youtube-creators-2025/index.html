Who Should Use What

Descript is perfect for vloggers, podcasters, and tutorial creators who want to edit quickly without dealing with traditional timelines. If you dread manual cutting and love editing text, start here.

Runway ML is for creative storytellers and gamers who want to add visual effects, remove backgrounds, or generate AI video clips. It's less about speed and more about creativity.

Pictory is ideal for creators who already have long-form content and want to repurpose it into shorts for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts. It's a time-saver for social media managers.

Adobe Premiere Pro is for serious YouTubers who need full control and are willing to invest time in learning. It's best for filmmaking, high-production value content, and complex edits.

InVideo is great for beginners or those who need quick, template-based videos for promotions, intros, or simple talking-head videos. It's the easiest to start with.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the best AI video editor for YouTube in 2025?

Our top pick is Descript due to its unique text-based editing that saves hours. For creative effects, Runway ML is excellent. For repurposing, Pictory is best.

2. Can AI video editors replace human editors?

Not entirely. AI handles repetitive tasks like transcription, captioning, and simple cuts, but creative decisions, storytelling, and fine-tuning still require human input. They are best used as assistants to speed up your workflow.

3. Are there any free AI video editors for YouTube?

Yes, Descript and InVideo offer free tiers with limitations (watermarks or export caps). Runway ML has a free plan but with a watermark. Adobe Premiere Pro offers a 7-day trial.

4. Which AI video editor is best for beginners?

InVideo is the most beginner-friendly due to its templates and simple interface. Descript is also easy because you edit text, not video. Avoid Premiere Pro until you're comfortable with editing basics.

5. How much does a good AI video editor cost?

Most professional AI video editors range from $15 to $25 per month. Descript Pro is $24/month, Runway ML Starter is $15/month, and InVideo Business is $20/month. Adobe Premiere Pro is $22.99/month.

6. Do AI video editors work on Mac and Windows?

Most are web-based (Runway ML, Descript, Pictory, InVideo) and work on any OS. Adobe Premiere Pro is available for both Mac and Windows.

7. Can I remove background from video without green screen using AI?

Yes, Runway ML and Descript both offer AI background removal without the need for a green screen. Runway's is more advanced for complex scenes.

8. Which AI video editor is best for adding captions automatically?

Descript and Pictory both excel at auto-captioning with high accuracy. Descript allows you to edit captions by editing text, which is very efficient.
