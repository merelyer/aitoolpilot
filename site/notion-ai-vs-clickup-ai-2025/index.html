Notion AI Review

Notion started as a note-taking app and evolved into a full-fledged workspace. Notion AI, its AI add-on, integrates directly into your pages, databases, and wikis. It's like having a writing assistant and research buddy inside your second brain.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate, rewrite, summarize, and change tone of any text.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers sourced from your own notes.
  • Autofill Databases: Automatically populate properties like summaries or action items.
  • Translation: Translate content into multiple languages.
  • Meeting Notes: Transcribe and summarize meetings (via integration).

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any paid Notion plan. It costs $10 per member per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. You can try it free on the Free plan with limited AI responses.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's flexible workspace.
  • Excellent for writing, editing, and summarizing long documents.
  • Q&A feature is a game-changer for knowledge bases.
  • Simple, clean interface—no steep learning curve.

Cons

  • AI is an add-on cost on top of your Notion subscription.
  • Less powerful for task automation and project reporting.
  • Limited AI actions compared to dedicated AI writing tools.

ClickUp AI Review

ClickUp is a project management powerhouse, and ClickUp AI is its answer to the AI productivity wave. It's woven into tasks, docs, comments, and even dashboards, making it a Swiss Army knife for teams that live in ClickUp.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate and edit text in tasks, docs, and comments.
  • Task Automation: Create subtasks, summarize threads, and generate action items.
  • AI Standups: Automatically generate standup reports from recent activity.
  • Project Summaries: Get AI-generated summaries of project status and risks.
  • Custom AI Templates: Build reusable AI prompts for repetitive tasks.

Pricing

ClickUp AI is an add-on available on any paid ClickUp plan. It costs $7 per member per month (billed annually) or $9 month-to-month. There's no free tier for AI, but you can try it with a 7-day free trial.

Pros

  • Deep integration with task management and reporting.
  • More affordable add-on than Notion AI.
  • Great for automating project updates and standups.
  • Custom AI templates save time on repetitive workflows.

Cons

  • ClickUp itself can be overwhelming for new users.
  • AI features are less focused on long-form writing than Notion.
  • No free AI tier (only trial).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI better than ClickUp AI?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for writing, summarization, and knowledge management. ClickUp AI is better for task automation, project reporting, and team collaboration. Neither is universally "better."

Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion offers a limited number of free AI responses on its Free plan. For unlimited use, you need to add Notion AI to a paid plan for $10/user/month.

Does ClickUp AI have a free plan?

No, ClickUp AI is a paid add-on. However, you can try it free for 7 days on any paid ClickUp plan.

Which AI tool is cheaper?

ClickUp AI is cheaper at $7/user/month compared to Notion AI at $10/user/month. But remember, you also need a base subscription for each platform.

Can I use Notion AI and ClickUp AI together?

Absolutely. Many teams use Notion for documentation and ClickUp for project management. You can integrate them via Zapier or Make to sync data.

Which tool has better AI writing features?

Notion AI generally has more robust writing and editing capabilities, including tone adjustment and long-form summarization. ClickUp AI is more focused on short-form task-related content.

Is AI in productivity tools worth it?

If you regularly write, summarize, or manage tasks, AI can save you hours each week. Both Notion AI and ClickUp AI offer solid ROI for the right users.
