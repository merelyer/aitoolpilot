Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion needs no introduction. It's the Swiss Army knife of productivity—part notes, part database, part wiki. Adding AI to that mix sounded like a dream. And in some ways, it is.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft blog posts, emails, or meeting notes directly inside any page. Just hit space and describe what you need.
  • Summarization: Highlight long documents or meeting notes and ask AI to summarize them into bullet points.
  • AI Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace content (e.g., "What's the status of Project X?") and get answers from linked databases.
  • Autofill: Generate table entries or property values using AI.
  • Translations & Tone Changes: Rewrite text in different tones or languages.

Pricing

Notion's base plan is free for individuals. To add AI, you pay $10 per member per month (billed annually) on top of your existing plan. For a team of 5, that's $50/month extra—just for AI.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is cheaper: Notion AI or Taskade?

Taskade is cheaper. Its Pro plan costs $8/month per member (with unlimited AI), while Notion AI is a $10/month add-on on top of any paid plan. For a team of 5, Taskade costs $40/month vs. Notion's $50/month (plus base plan).

2. Can Notion AI create tasks from a prompt?

Not directly. Notion AI can generate text, summaries, and table entries, but it cannot automatically create a project with tasks, due dates, and assignees from a single prompt. You'd need to manually set up the database and then use AI to fill in content.

3. Does Taskade have a free plan with AI?

Yes. Taskade's free plan includes limited AI usage (a few actions per day). Upgrading to Pro ($8/month per member) removes limits.

4. Which tool is better for remote teams?

Both are excellent, but Taskade has built-in video chat and screen sharing, making it more all-in-one for remote collaboration. Notion relies on third-party tools like Zoom or Slack for real-time communication.

5. Can I use Notion AI offline?

No. Notion AI requires an internet connection to process requests. Taskade also requires online access for AI features.

6. Is either tool good for personal task management?

Yes, but Taskade is more intuitive for personal tasks because of its AI-driven task creation. Notion AI is better if you want to keep detailed notes alongside your tasks.

7. Which tool has better AI for writing?

Notion AI has stronger writing capabilities, including tone changes, translations, and long-form drafting. Taskade's AI is more focused on generating tasks and project structures.
