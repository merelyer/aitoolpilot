Detailed Reviews

1. Zendesk AI

Best for: Businesses that want a complete helpdesk with AI built-in.

Zendesk AI isn’t a standalone product; it’s an add-on to Zendesk’s Suite plans. The AI features include intelligent triage (auto-tagging and routing), AI-powered replies, and a conversational bot that can resolve simple queries. It also integrates with your knowledge base to suggest articles.

Pricing: Zendesk Suite Team plan starts at $55/agent/month (annual billing). The AI add-on is included in the Professional plan ($115/agent/month) and above. The Enterprise plan (custom pricing) offers advanced AI capabilities like sentiment analysis and custom models.

Pros: Seamless integration with Zendesk, powerful analytics, and a huge app ecosystem. The AI is easy to train with no code.

Cons: Costs can escalate quickly as you add agents and features. The AI add-on isn’t available on the cheapest plan.

2. Intercom

Best for: SaaS companies and support teams that want proactive messaging.

Intercom’s Fin AI Agent is a popular choice. It can automatically resolve up to 50% of conversations, and it’s trained on your help center articles. The platform also offers a unified inbox, chatbots, and customer engagement tools.

Pricing: Intercom’s Essential plan starts at $29/seat/month (annual billing). The Advanced plan (with Fin AI) starts at $85/seat/month. For full AI features, you’ll need the Expert plan (custom pricing).

Pros: Excellent user interface, strong automation, and great for proactive customer engagement. Fin AI is accurate and easy to set up.

Cons: The Essential plan lacks AI features, and pricing jumps significantly for AI access. Some users report that the AI can be overly cautious with sensitive topics.

3. Forethought

Best for: Enterprises that need deep AI integration and custom workflows.

Forethought’s platform includes a suite of AI agents: Solve (for ticket deflection), Assist (for human agents), and Triage (for routing). It’s built on large language models and can integrate with major helpdesks like Zendesk, Salesforce, and Freshdesk.

Pricing: Forethought doesn’t publish pricing. Based on industry reports, you can expect to pay around $500/month for a basic package, with enterprise contracts reaching $5,000+/month. They offer a free trial.

Pros: Powerful AI that improves over time, excellent for complex support environments, and strong analytics.

Cons: High cost and implementation complexity. It’s overkill for small businesses.

4. Ada

Best for: Brands that want a no-code, customizable AI chatbot.

Ada focuses on AI-powered chatbot automation. You can build conversation flows visually, without coding. It supports multiple languages and integrates with your CRM and helpdesk.

Pricing: Ada’s pricing is custom, but based on user reports, it starts around $300/month for small businesses and scales up. They offer a free trial.

Pros: User-friendly builder, good for non-technical teams, and robust analytics.

Cons: Not a full helpdesk; you’ll need a separate ticketing system. Pricing transparency is poor.

5. LivePerson

Best for: Large enterprises with high-volume omnichannel support.

LivePerson is a veteran in the space, offering AI-powered chatbots, messaging, and voice automation. It’s built for scale and handles millions of conversations daily.

Pricing: LivePerson’s pricing is entirely custom. Expect to pay at least $1,000/month, and often much more for enterprise deployments.

Pros: Excellent for complex, omnichannel environments, and has strong security and compliance features.

Cons: Expensive and complex to implement. Not suitable for SMBs.

6. Zendesk Answer Bot

Best for: Small businesses already using Zendesk Suite.

Answer Bot is a simple AI that suggests help center articles to customers. It’s included in all Zendesk Suite plans, so you don’t pay extra.

Pricing: Included in Zendesk Suite Team plan ($55/agent/mo).

Pros: No extra cost, easy to set up, and reduces ticket volume.

Cons: Limited to article suggestions; it can’t resolve complex issues autonomously.

Who Should Use What

  • Startups & small businesses: Intercom Essential (for proactive support) or Zendesk Suite Team (if you need a full helpdesk).
  • Mid-sized companies: Zendesk Professional (with AI) or Intercom Advanced (with Fin AI). Both offer good automation without breaking the bank.
  • Enterprises: Forethought or LivePerson for deep AI integration and high-volume omnichannel support.
  • E-commerce brands: Ada is great for building a custom chatbot that handles product questions and order tracking.
  • Companies already on Zendesk: Just enable Answer Bot—it’s free and effective for basic deflection.

Bottom Line

AI customer service doesn’t have to be a black box. In 2025, you can get started for as little as $29/month with Intercom, or $55/month with Zendesk. The key is to match the tool to your needs: if you want a simple bot to deflect tickets, Zendesk Answer Bot is a no-brainer. If you need a full AI agent that resolves complex queries, invest in Intercom’s Fin or Forethought.

Remember, the cheapest option isn’t always the most cost-effective. A $300/month tool that resolves 50% of your tickets can save you thousands in agent hours. So, calculate your ROI before you commit.

FAQ

What is the average cost of AI customer service software?

On average, AI customer service software costs between $50 and $200 per agent per month. Some platforms offer entry-level plans under $30, but they often lack advanced AI features. Enterprise solutions can exceed $1,000/month.

Is there a free AI customer service tool?

Yes, some tools offer free tiers or trials. For example, Zendesk has a 30-day free trial, and Intercom offers a 14-day trial. There are also open-source options like Botpress, but they require technical expertise.

How much does Zendesk AI cost?

Zendesk AI is included in the Professional plan ($115/agent/month) and above. The Suite Team plan ($55/agent/month) includes basic Answer Bot but not full AI features.

Can AI customer service replace human agents?

AI can handle up to 70% of routine queries, but it can’t replace human agents entirely. Complex issues, emotional conversations, and high-value customers still need human touch. The best approach is a hybrid model.

What is the ROI of AI customer service?

Most businesses see a positive ROI within 3-6 months. By automating repetitive tasks, you reduce response times, increase customer satisfaction, and lower labor costs. For example, if you have 10 agents and AI resolves 30% of tickets, you could save 30% of your support costs.

Do AI customer service tools integrate with my existing helpdesk?

Most do. Zendesk AI integrates natively with Zendesk, while tools like Forethought and Ada connect to popular helpdesks like Salesforce, Freshdesk, and Zendesk. Always check the integration list before purchasing.
