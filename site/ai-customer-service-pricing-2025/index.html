Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does AI customer service cost per month?

Costs range from $15/agent/month for basic plans to over $2,500/month for premium AI marketing platforms. Most SMBs spend $50–$200/month.

2. Is there a free AI customer service tool?

Freshdesk and Tidio offer free tiers with limited AI features. They're great for testing the waters.

3. What's the difference between per-seat and per-resolution pricing?

Per-seat charges per agent, while per-resolution charges each time AI resolves a query. Per-resolution can be cost-effective if AI handles most queries.

4. Can I switch plans later?

Yes, most platforms allow upgrades or downgrades. Check for annual vs. monthly billing differences.

5. Do these tools integrate with my existing helpdesk?

Most integrate with popular CRMs and helpdesks, but verify compatibility before committing.

6. How accurate is AI customer service in 2025?

Top-tier AI like Intercom's Fin can resolve up to 50% of queries accurately. Accuracy improves with training data.
