Detailed Reviews of Each Tool

1. Jasper AI: The All-Rounder

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is a household name in AI writing, and for good reason. It's built for marketers who need to produce blog posts, social media content, and ad copy quickly. In 2025, Jasper has integrated advanced SEO features like keyword suggestions, readability scoring, and automatic internal linking recommendations.

Key Features:

  • Over 50 templates for different content types
  • Boss Mode: write long-form content with simple commands
  • SEO mode: integrates with Surfer SEO (paid add-on)
  • Plagiarism checker (limited)
  • Multi-language support

Pricing: Plans start at $49/month for the Creator plan (up to 35,000 words). The Pro plan costs $69/month (unlimited words). Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros:

  • High-quality output that requires minimal editing
  • Great for long-form SEO content like guides and listicles
  • User-friendly interface

Cons:

  • Pricier than some alternatives
  • SEO features require additional payment for Surfer integration
  • Occasional repetition in longer content

Verdict: Jasper is ideal for teams and serious content creators who value quality over cost. It's a solid investment if you publish frequently.

2. Writesonic: Best Value for Money

Writesonic has matured into a powerful tool, offering a free plan that lets you test the waters. It's particularly good at generating long-form articles with a simple workflow: input a topic, choose a tone, and get a draft.

Key Features:

  • Article Writer 4.0: generates 2,000+ word posts
  • Built-in SEO checker (keyword density, headings)
  • Integration with WordPress, Shopify, and more
  • AI image generation (limited)

Pricing: Free tier (10,000 words/month). Starter plan at $19/month (64,000 words). Professional plan at $59/month (unlimited words).

Pros:

  • Affordable entry point
  • Good for beginners and small businesses
  • Fast generation

Cons:

  • Quality can be inconsistent; sometimes needs heavy editing
  • SEO features are basic compared to Surfer
  • Customer support can be slow

Verdict: Best for budget-conscious users who need volume. Upgrade to the Professional plan for better quality.

3. Surfer SEO: The Data-Driven Optimizer

Surfer SEO is not a standalone writing tool—it's a content optimization platform that pairs with your preferred writer (like Jasper or a human). It analyzes top-ranking pages for your target keywords and gives you a detailed brief: suggested word count, headings, images, and keyword usage.

Key Features:

  • Content editor with real-time scoring
  • SERP analyzer for any keyword
  • Keyword clustering and research
  • Integration with Google Docs, WordPress, and Jasper

Pricing: Plans start at $69/month (limited to 5 articles). The Pro plan is $149/month (20 articles). Enterprise custom.

Pros:

  • Unmatched SEO insights
  • Improves ranking potential significantly
  • Works with any writer (AI or human)

Cons:

  • No built-in AI writer; requires separate tool
  • Expensive for high-volume users
  • Steep learning curve

Verdict: A must-have for serious SEO professionals. Pair it with Jasper for the ultimate combo.

4. Copy.ai: Speed for Short-Form

Copy.ai is designed for speed, especially for short-form content like emails, social media posts, and product descriptions. It's less suited for long-form SEO articles but excels at generating catchy headlines and ad copy.

Key Features:

  • 90+ copywriting tools
  • Infobase: store brand details for consistent tone
  • Chat interface for brainstorming
  • Basic SEO suggestions

Pricing: Free plan (2,000 words/month). Pro plan at $36/month (unlimited words).

Pros:

  • Very fast generation
  • Great for marketing teams
  • Easy to use

Cons:

  • Not ideal for long-form SEO content
  • Output can be generic without editing
  • Limited SEO depth

Verdict: Perfect for quick copywriting tasks. Not the best for in-depth SEO articles.

5. Frase.io: The Research Assistant

Frase.io is a content research and writing tool that helps you create comprehensive, SEO-optimized articles. It excels at generating content briefs by analyzing the top 10 search results for your target query.

Key Features:

  • Content briefs with recommended headings and questions
  • AI writer integrated into the editor
  • Keyword research and clustering
  • Optimization score for your content

Pricing: Starter plan at $14.99/month (1 user, 5 articles). Basic plan at $49.99/month (unlimited articles). Team plan at $114.99/month.

Pros:

  • Excellent for research and outlining
  • Affordable starter plan
  • Good for content strategy

Cons:

  • AI writer quality is average; needs editing
  • Interface can be clunky
  • Limited integrations

Verdict: Best for content strategists who need data-driven briefs. Use it to guide your writing, not as a sole writer.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are AI writing tools good for SEO?

Yes, when used correctly. They can help generate content quickly, but you need to edit for quality, add original insights, and ensure proper keyword usage. Tools like Surfer SEO can guide optimization.

2. Which AI writing tool is best for long-form SEO content?

Jasper and Writesonic are top choices. Jasper excels in quality, while Writesonic offers better value. For SEO optimization, pair with Surfer SEO.

3. Can AI writing tools replace human writers?

Not entirely. AI can draft content, but human touch is needed for tone, accuracy, and unique perspectives. Think of AI as a productivity booster, not a replacement.

4. What is the cheapest AI writing tool for SEO?

Writesonic's free plan offers 10,000 words, and its Starter plan at $19/month is very affordable. Frase.io also has a low entry point at $14.99/month but with article limits.

5. Do AI writing tools pass plagiarism checks?

Most reputable tools generate original content, but it's wise to run a plagiarism check (e.g., Copyscape) on critical pieces. AI can sometimes produce similar phrasing to existing content.

6. How do I choose the best AI writing tool for my needs?

Consider your budget, content volume, and required features. Test free trials of 2-3 tools. Focus on output quality, SEO integration, and ease of use.

7. Is Surfer SEO worth the extra cost?

For data-driven SEO professionals, yes. It provides actionable insights that can significantly improve rankings. For casual users, built-in SEO features in Jasper or Writesonic may suffice.
