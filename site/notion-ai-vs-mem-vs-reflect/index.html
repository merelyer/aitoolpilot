Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion AI is an add-on to the popular Notion workspace. It brings AI-powered writing assistance, summarization, and Q&A directly into your notes and databases. If you already use Notion, it's a no-brainer. Even if you don't, it's a compelling reason to switch.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, rewrite sentences, fix grammar, and change tone—all without leaving your page.
  • Summarization: Turn long meeting notes or articles into concise summaries with one click.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your notes and get answers pulled from your workspace.
  • Autofill: Automatically populate database properties with AI-generated content.
  • Translation: Translate text into multiple languages instantly.

Pricing

Notion AI is priced at $8 per member per month (billed annually) or $10 month-to-month. It's an add-on to any Notion plan, including the free tier. So you can start with a free Notion account and add AI for $8/month.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's powerful workspace
  • Versatile AI features for writing, summarizing, and Q&A
  • Affordable add-on pricing
  • Works with existing Notion pages and databases

Cons

  • Requires a Notion subscription (free tier available)
  • AI features are not available in the free Notion plan—you must pay for the add-on
  • Can be slow during peak times
  • Limited customization of AI responses

Verdict: Notion AI is the most versatile option, especially if you're already in the Notion ecosystem. It's a powerful productivity booster.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem positions itself as "the self-organizing workspace." It uses AI to automatically organize your notes, surface relevant information, and help you recall what matters. It's less about manual organization and more about letting AI do the work.

Key Features

  • Auto-organization: Mem automatically tags and links related notes, so you don't have to.
  • Smart Recall: When you start typing, Mem suggests relevant notes from your past.
  • AI Chat: Ask questions about your notes and get answers with citations.
  • Voice Notes: Record voice memos and get transcriptions with AI summaries.
  • Email Integration: Forward emails to Mem to turn them into notes.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. The Pro plan is $8.33 per month (billed annually) or $12.99 month-to-month. There's also a Team plan at $10/user/month (annual).

Pros

  • Excellent for capturing ideas quickly without worrying about organization
  • Smart recall surfaces relevant notes proactively
  • Clean, minimalist interface
  • Good mobile app for on-the-go capture

Cons

  • Less structured than Notion—not ideal for project management
  • AI features are still evolving and can be hit-or-miss
  • No free tier for the full AI experience
  • Limited customization and templates

Verdict: Mem is perfect for individuals who want a frictionless way to capture and recall information. It's like a second brain that organizes itself.

Detailed Review: Reflect

Reflect is a note-taking app built for networked thought. It uses AI to help you connect ideas, generate backlinks, and write better. It's popular among writers, researchers, and anyone who thinks in a non-linear way.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate outlines, expand on ideas, and improve your writing.
  • Backlinks and Graph: Automatically link related notes and visualize connections.
  • Whisper AI: AI-powered transcription for voice notes.
  • End-to-end Encryption: Your notes are private and secure.
  • Daily Notes: A streamlined way to journal and capture daily thoughts.

Pricing

Reflect costs $10 per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. There's a 14-day free trial but no free tier.

Pros

  • Strong focus on privacy with end-to-end encryption
  • Excellent for networked thinking and idea development
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • AI features are well-integrated and useful for writers

Cons

  • No free plan—only a trial
  • Lacks project management features
  • Smaller community and fewer integrations compared to Notion
  • AI features are more limited than Notion AI's

Verdict: Reflect is ideal for writers, researchers, and anyone who values privacy and networked thought. It's a niche tool but excels at what it does.

FAQ

1. What is the best AI notes app overall?

For most users, Notion AI is the best overall due to its versatility, integration with a full workspace, and affordable pricing. However, the best app depends on your specific needs—Mem for automated capture, Reflect for networked thought.

2. Is Notion AI free?

Notion AI is not free. It's an add-on that costs $8 per member per month (billed annually) on top of any Notion plan. However, Notion itself has a free tier, so you can start using Notion for free and add AI later.

3. How does Mem compare to Notion AI?

Mem focuses on automated organization and recall, while Notion AI is part of a broader workspace with databases and project management. Mem is better for personal note-taking, while Notion AI is better for teams and structured work.

4. Does Reflect have a free plan?

No, Reflect does not have a free plan. It offers a 14-day free trial, after which you must pay $10/month (billed annually) or $12/month.

5. Which AI notes app is most secure?

Reflect offers end-to-end encryption, making it the most secure option for privacy-conscious users. Notion and Mem offer standard security but not end-to-end encryption.

6. Can I use these apps on mobile?

Yes, all three have mobile apps for iOS and Android, allowing you to capture notes on the go.
