Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion AI is an add-on to the popular Notion workspace. It brings AI writing assistance, summarization, and Q&A directly into your notes, docs, and databases.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, rewrite text, fix grammar, and change tone.
  • Summarization: Condense long documents into key points.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers with sources.
  • Autofill Databases: Use AI to populate properties like tags or summaries.
  • Integration: Works seamlessly with Notion's pages, databases, and collaboration features.

Pricing

Notion AI costs $10 per member per month (billed annually) on top of your Notion plan. Notion's free plan includes limited AI trials, but for full access, you'll need a paid Notion plan (Plus at $10/user/month or Business at $18/user/month) plus the AI add-on.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem is an AI-first notes app that aims to eliminate manual organization. It uses AI to automatically tag, link, and surface related notes as you type.

Key Features

  • Auto-organization: AI tags and links notes without manual effort.
  • Smart Search: Find notes using natural language queries.
  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate text, summarize, and rewrite within notes.
  • Daily Notes: A chronological feed for quick capture.
  • Focus Mode: Distraction-free writing with AI suggestions.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. The Mem Pro plan is $14.99 per month (or $12.99/month billed annually) and includes unlimited AI, advanced search, and priority support. There's also a Mem Teams plan at $10/user/month for collaborative use.

Detailed Review: Reflect

Reflect is a networked note-taking app that combines backlinks, daily notes, and AI assistance. It's designed for thinkers who want to connect ideas.

Key Features

  • Daily Notes: A central hub for daily logging.
  • End-to-end Encryption: Your notes are private and secure.

Pricing

Reflect offers a 14-day free trial but no free tier. The Pro plan is $10 per month (billed annually) or $12 month-to-month. This includes unlimited notes, AI features, and sync across devices.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You need an all-in-one workspace for notes, tasks, and databases.
  • You collaborate with a team and need shared wikis.
  • You want powerful AI writing assistance across documents.
  • You're already using Notion and want to add AI capabilities.

Choose Mem if:

  • You want AI to organize your notes automatically.
  • You prefer a fast, minimalist note-taking experience.
  • You're a solopreneur or knowledge worker managing personal knowledge.
  • You value smart search and recall over complex databases.

Choose Reflect if:

  • You love networked notes and backlinks (like Roam or Obsidian).
  • You prioritize privacy with end-to-end encryption.
  • You want AI assistance without sacrificing a clean interface.
  • You're building a personal knowledge graph.

Bottom Line

There's no one-size-fits-all winner, but for most users, Notion AI offers the best balance of features, flexibility, and value—especially if you need a workspace that does it all. Mem is fantastic for effortless personal note-taking, while Reflect shines for networked thinkers who value privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI free?

Notion AI is not entirely free. While Notion offers a free plan with limited AI trials, full access requires a paid Notion plan plus the AI add-on at $10/user/month.

2. Can I use Mem for team collaboration?

Mem offers a Teams plan at $10/user/month, but its collaboration features are less robust than Notion's. It's better suited for personal use.

3. Does Reflect have a free plan?

Reflect offers a 14-day free trial but no permanent free tier. After the trial, it's $10/month billed annually.

4. Which app has the best AI search?

Mem excels at AI-powered search and recall, automatically linking related notes. Notion AI's Q&A is also strong, especially for large workspaces.

5. Is my data secure with these apps?

Reflect offers end-to-end encryption, making it the most secure for privacy-focused users. Notion and Mem use standard encryption but are generally secure.

6. Can I import my notes from Evernote or other apps?

All three apps support importing from popular formats like Markdown and HTML. Notion has the most robust importers.
