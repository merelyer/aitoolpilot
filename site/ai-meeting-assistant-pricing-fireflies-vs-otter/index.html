Detailed Review of Each Option

Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is a powerful AI meeting assistant that integrates with popular platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex. It records conversations, generates transcripts, and uses AI to create summaries, action items, and even sentiment analysis.

One of Fireflies' standout features is its unlimited transcription on paid plans, which is a game-changer for teams that have back-to-back meetings. Its search functionality is also top-notch—you can search across all recordings by keyword, speaker, or even question type (e.g., "what did the client say about pricing?").

Key Features

  • Unlimited transcription on Pro and Business plans
  • AI-powered summaries with action items, decisions, and risks
  • Advanced search across all meetings (with filters like date, speaker, and sentiment)
  • Integrations with CRM tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier
  • Ability to add up to 10,000 users in the Business plan
  • Multi-language support (over 39 languages)

Pricing

  • Free: Unlimited transcription (up to 3 meetings/day, 800 mins storage), AI summaries, limited search.
  • Pro: $10/user/month (annual) or $18 monthly—unlimited transcription, all integrations, advanced search, and unlimited seats.
  • Business: $19/user/month (annual) or $29 monthly—adds CRM sync, custom privacy modes, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing—includes SSO, dedicated support, and advanced security.

Pros

  • Unlimited transcription on paid plans—no hidden caps.
  • Excellent search functionality across all recordings.
  • Generous free tier (800 minutes of storage).
  • Strong CRM integrations for sales teams.

Cons

  • Live captioning is not as seamless as Otter's (captions appear with a slight delay).
  • Mobile app is less polished than Otter's.
  • Some users report occasional accuracy issues with heavy accents.

Otter.ai

Otter.ai is another leading AI meeting assistant, known for its real-time live captions and user-friendly mobile app. It automatically joins your meetings (on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams) and provides a live transcript as people speak. This makes it perfect for accessibility and for those who want to follow along with captions.

Otter also offers AI summaries, action items, and automated slide capture for presentations. Its free tier is quite generous, with 300 minutes of transcription per month, but paid plans are required for unlimited usage.

Key Features

  • Real-time live captions during meetings (with minimal delay)
  • AI-generated summaries and action items
  • Automated slide capture—pulls out slides from presentations
  • Mobile app for iOS and Android with offline transcription
  • Integrations with Slack, Dropbox, and Microsoft Teams
  • Speaker identification and keyword highlights

Pricing

  • Free: 300 minutes/month, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 maximum imports.
  • Pro: $8.33/user/month (annual) or $16.99 monthly—1,200 minutes/month, 90 minutes per conversation, advanced search, and removal of Otter branding.
  • Business: $20/user/month (annual) or $30 monthly—6,000 minutes/user/month, centralized admin console, and usage analytics.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing—unlimited minutes, SSO, and dedicated support.

Pros

  • Real-time live captions are superb—great for accessibility.
  • Intuitive mobile app with offline transcription.
  • Automated slide capture is a unique feature for presentations.
  • Free tier is generous for occasional users.

Cons

  • Paid plans have monthly transcription caps (not unlimited).
  • Search functionality is less advanced than Fireflies.
  • Limited integrations compared to Fireflies (no native CRM integration).

Who Should Use What

Fireflies.ai is best for:

  • Sales teams that need CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot) to log calls automatically.
  • Project managers who need to search across dozens of meetings to find specific decisions or action items.
  • Teams with high meeting volumes—unlimited transcription means no worries about hitting caps.
  • Remote-first companies that rely on async communication and need thorough meeting notes.

Otter.ai is best for:

  • Journalists and researchers who need accurate real-time transcription for interviews.
  • Students who want to record lectures and capture slides automatically.
  • Accessibility users who rely on live captions during meetings.
  • Individuals on a budget—the free tier is perfect for light use.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Fireflies and Otter are excellent AI meeting assistants, but they cater to slightly different needs. If you want a tool that can handle unlimited meetings, offers robust search, and integrates deeply with your CRM, Fireflies.ai is the clear winner. Its Pro plan at $10/user/month is a steal for the value it provides.

On the other hand, if your priority is real-time captions, a beautiful mobile app, and you don't need unlimited transcription, Otter.ai is a solid choice—especially if you're a student or solo professional.

We recommend starting with Fireflies' free tier to test its features, then upgrading to Pro if it meets your needs. If you're still undecided, consider trying both free plans and see which fits your workflow better.

FAQ

1. Which is cheaper: Fireflies or Otter?

Otter's Pro plan starts at $8.33/user/month (annual), while Fireflies' Pro starts at $10/user/month (annual). So Otter is slightly cheaper on the surface. However, Fireflies includes unlimited transcription, whereas Otter caps at 1,200 minutes/month. If you have many meetings, Fireflies may offer better value per dollar.

2. Can I use Fireflies or Otter for free?

Yes, both offer free tiers. Fireflies gives you 3 meetings per day and 800 minutes of storage, while Otter provides 300 minutes per month (with a 30-minute limit per conversation). Both free plans include AI summaries, making them great for testing.

3. Does Fireflies or Otter integrate with Zoom and Google Meet?

Both integrate with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Fireflies also integrates with Webex, while Otter supports Webex and has a dedicated mobile app for iOS and Android.

4. Which AI meeting assistant is better for sales teams?

Fireflies is generally better for sales teams because it offers native CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot) and allows you to automatically log calls, track sentiment, and search for keywords like "budget" or "timeline." Otter lacks native CRM integrations, though you can use Zapier to connect.

5. Is Otter or Fireflies more accurate in transcription?

Both are highly accurate (around 90-95% for clear English audio). In our tests, Fireflies had a slight edge with strong accents and technical jargon, while Otter excels in real-time captioning. Accuracy can vary based on audio quality and speaker clarity.

6. Can I record meetings without joining the call?

Yes, both tools allow you to record meetings by adding a bot to the calendar invite. You don't need to be present for the recording to happen. Fireflies even lets you upload existing audio files for transcription (up to 3 hours per file).

7. Which tool is better for students?

Otter is often preferred by students because of its mobile app and live captioning during lectures. The free tier is generous enough for a few classes per week. Fireflies is also useful if you need to record and search through many lectures.
