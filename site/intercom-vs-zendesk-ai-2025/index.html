Intercom Review: The AI-First Helpdesk

Intercom has pivoted hard from a live chat tool to a full-blown AI customer service platform. At the center of that shift is Fin, Intercom's GPT-4-powered AI agent that resolves customer questions automatically across chat, email, and WhatsApp.

Key Features

  • Fin AI Agent: Resolves up to 50% of support conversations without human handoff. Trained on your help center, past conversations, and custom content.
  • Copilot: AI assistant for human agents — suggests replies, summarizes conversations, and pulls relevant articles.
  • Unified Inbox: All channels (chat, email, social, WhatsApp) in one place.
  • Workflows & Automation: Drag-and-drop builder for routing, tagging, and follow-ups.
  • Reporting: Real-time dashboards for CSAT, resolution time, and AI performance.

Intercom Pricing (2025)

  • Essential: $39/seat/month — basic helpdesk, 1 Fin AI resolution per seat.
  • Advanced: $99/seat/month — adds Fin AI, workflows, SLA management.
  • Expert: $139/seat/month — advanced automation, A/B testing, HIPAA.
  • Fin AI: $0.99 per resolution (after included quota).

Pros

  • Best-in-class AI agent out of the box — no coding required.
  • Modern, intuitive UI that agents actually enjoy using.
  • Transparent per-resolution pricing for AI.
  • Strong onboarding and support.

Cons

  • Can get expensive as you scale seats.
  • Less customizable than Zendesk for complex workflows.
  • Fin AI works best with a well-maintained help center.

Zendesk Review: The Enterprise Powerhouse

Zendesk has been the go-to helpdesk for enterprises for over a decade. Its AI layer, Zendesk AI, is built on years of support data and offers advanced features like intent detection, sentiment analysis, and intelligent triage. It's not as flashy as Intercom's Fin, but it's deeply integrated into Zendesk's ecosystem.

Key Features

  • Zendesk AI Agents: Automate responses and actions across email, chat, and messaging.
  • Intelligent Triage: Auto-detects intent, language, and sentiment to route tickets.
  • Agent Copilot: Provides real-time suggestions and summaries.
  • Customizable Workflows: Triggers, automations, and macros for granular control.
  • Marketplace: 1,500+ apps and integrations.

Zendesk Pricing (2025)

  • Suite Team: $55/agent/month — ticketing, messaging, basic AI.
  • Suite Growth: $89/agent/month — adds multiple help centers, SLAs.
  • Suite Professional: $115/agent/month — advanced AI, custom analytics.
  • Suite Enterprise: Custom pricing — advanced security, sandbox, HIPAA.
  • Zendesk AI: Add-on starting at $50/agent/month.

Pros

  • Unmatched customization and scalability.
  • Robust integration ecosystem.
  • Advanced AI for triage and routing.
  • Proven track record with large enterprises.

Cons

  • Steeper learning curve and longer setup.
  • AI features cost extra and can be pricey.
  • UI feels dated compared to Intercom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Intercom or Zendesk better for AI customer service?

Intercom is generally better for SMBs and startups due to its advanced Fin AI agent and ease of use. Zendesk is better for enterprises needing deep customization and scalability.

How much does Intercom's AI cost?

Intercom's Fin AI costs $0.99 per resolution after your plan's included quota. Plans start at $39/seat/month.

Does Zendesk include AI in its base plans?

Basic AI features are included in Suite Team and above, but advanced AI like intelligent triage and AI agents require an add-on starting at $50/agent/month.

Can I switch from Zendesk to Intercom easily?

Yes, Intercom offers migration tools and support to help you move from Zendesk, though complex setups may require manual work.

Which platform has better integrations?

Zendesk has a larger marketplace with 1,500+ apps, but Intercom covers all the essentials and integrates well with popular tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack.

Is there a free plan for either tool?

Neither offers a permanent free plan, but both provide 14-day free trials. Intercom's trial includes Fin AI, while Zendesk's trial may require you to request AI add-ons.
