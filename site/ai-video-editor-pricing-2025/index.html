Detailed Reviews

1. Descript

Descript is a game-changer for anyone who edits videos by editing text. It transcribes your video and lets you cut, paste, or delete words to edit the footage. It also includes AI voice cloning (Overdub), screen recording, and collaborative tools.

Pricing: Free plan with 1 hour of transcription per month. Paid plans start at $12/month (annual) for the Creator plan, which includes unlimited transcription and 10 hours of video export. The Pro plan costs $24/month (annual) and adds more features like watermark removal and 4K export.

  • Pros: Intuitive text-based editing, excellent transcription accuracy, built-in collaboration.
  • Cons: Export limits on lower tiers, advanced features can be pricey.

2. Runway

Runway is at the forefront of AI video generation, offering tools like text-to-video, video-to-video, and advanced effects like inpainting and motion tracking. It's used by both independent creators and Hollywood studios.

Pricing: Free plan with limited credits. Standard plan starts at $12/month (annual) and includes 625 credits (roughly 125 video generations). Pro plan is $28/month (annual) for more credits and higher resolution. Enterprise custom pricing.

  • Pros: Cutting-edge AI features, high-quality output, flexible credit system.
  • Cons: Steep learning curve, credits can run out quickly with complex tasks.

3. CapCut

CapCut is a free, mobile-first video editor with a surprising number of AI features, including auto-captions, background removal, and AI effects. It's perfect for quick social media edits.

Pricing: Free forever with basic features. CapCut Pro costs $7.99/month (annual) and unlocks premium AI tools, more storage, and no watermark.

  • Pros: Generous free tier, easy to use, great for TikTok and Reels.
  • Cons: Watermark on free exports, some features require Pro.

4. Pictory

Pictory specializes in turning long-form content (like webinars or podcasts) into short, shareable clips. It uses AI to find key moments and add captions automatically.

Pricing: Free trial with watermarked exports. Starter plan is $19/month (annual) for 30 videos per month. Professional plan is $39/month (annual) for 60 videos and more customization. Custom for teams.

  • Pros: Saves hours of manual clipping, good for repurposing content.
  • Cons: Limited editing control, no advanced effects.

5. InVideo

InVideo offers a massive library of templates and an AI script-to-video feature. It's designed for marketers and business owners who need to produce videos quickly without editing skills.

Pricing: Free plan with watermarked exports. Paid plans start at $20/month (annual) for the Plus plan, which removes watermark and provides more stock media. The Max plan is $48/month (annual) for premium features.

  • Pros: Huge template library, easy for beginners, AI script generation.
  • Cons: Free plan is limited, export quality can be capped.

6. Adobe Premiere Pro (with AI)

Adobe Premiere Pro is the industry standard for professional video editing. Its AI features, powered by Adobe Sensei, include auto-reframe, speech-to-text, and scene edit detection.

Pricing: $22.99/month (annual) for the individual plan. Part of Creative Cloud All Apps for $59.99/month. No free plan, but 7-day free trial.

  • Pros: Professional-grade features, extensive integrations, robust AI tools.
  • Cons: Expensive, steep learning curve, subscription only.

Who Should Use What

  • Podcasters and educators: Descript is your best friend. Text-based editing and transcription are unmatched.
  • Social media creators: CapCut is free and perfect for quick, trendy edits. If you need more AI features, upgrade to Pro.
  • Filmmakers and advanced users: Runway offers cutting-edge AI effects, but be prepared for a learning curve and higher costs.
  • Marketers and business owners: InVideo's templates and AI script-to-video feature make it easy to produce professional-looking ads and promos.
  • Professionals who need full control: Adobe Premiere Pro is worth the investment if you're serious about video production.

Bottom Line Verdict

Choosing the right AI video editor depends on your specific needs and budget. If you're a beginner or casual creator, CapCut's free tier is unbeatable. For most professional creators, Descript offers the best value for its AI capabilities. Advanced users should consider Runway for its innovative features, while those needing a full-fledged professional suite should stick with Adobe Premiere Pro.

Remember, many tools offer free trials or free tiers—take advantage of them to test before you commit. And always check for annual billing discounts, which can save you up to 40%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest AI video editor?

CapCut is completely free for basic use, and its Pro version starts at just $7.99/month (annual). Descript also offers a free plan, but with limited transcription hours.

Are there any free AI video editors with no watermark?

Yes, CapCut's free version allows you to export without a watermark if you use certain features, but some premium exports may include a watermark. Descript's free plan also exports without a watermark, but you're limited to 1 hour of transcription per month.

How much does Descript cost per month?

Descript's Creator plan costs $12/month when billed annually, or $24/month if billed monthly. The Pro plan is $24/month (annual) or $40/month (monthly).

Is Runway worth the price?

If you need advanced AI video generation and have a budget for it, yes. Runway is used by professionals for its high-quality output and unique features. However, the credit system can be limiting for heavy users.

Can I use AI video editors for commercial purposes?

Most paid plans allow commercial use, but always check the terms. Free plans often have restrictions or require attribution. For example, InVideo's free plan includes a watermark, which you must remove for professional use.

What is the best AI video editor for beginners?

CapCut and InVideo are both beginner-friendly due to their simple interfaces and template-based workflows. Descript is also easy to learn, especially if you're comfortable with text editing.

Do AI video editors offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes, most editors offer significant discounts for annual billing—typically 20-40% off the monthly price. Always compare monthly vs. annual rates before subscribing.
