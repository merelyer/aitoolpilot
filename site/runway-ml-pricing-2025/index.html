Runway ML Pricing Plans Explained

Runway ML offers four main plans: Free, Standard, Pro, and Unlimited. Here's the breakdown:

1. Free Plan

Price: $0

Credits: 125 one-time credits

Features: Access to basic AI tools (e.g., Gen-2, Gen-1), 720p exports, watermark on videos.

Pros: Great for testing the waters.

Cons: Limited credits, watermark, no advanced features.

2. Standard Plan

Price: $15/month (billed annually) or $18/month (billed monthly)

Credits: 625 credits/month

Features: Unlimited projects, 1080p exports, no watermark, access to all core tools, 100GB storage.

Pros: Affordable for individuals, enough credits for regular use.

Cons: Credits can run out quickly with heavy usage.

3. Pro Plan

Price: $35/month (billed annually) or $42/month (billed monthly)

Credits: 2,250 credits/month

Features: Everything in Standard, plus 4K exports, advanced tools (e.g., custom AI models), 500GB storage, priority support.

Pros: High credit allowance, 4K exports, priority support.

Cons: Steeper price for casual users.

4. Unlimited Plan

Price: $95/month (billed annually) or $115/month (billed monthly)

Credits: Unlimited (fair use policy applies)

Features: Everything in Pro, plus unlimited credits, 1TB storage, dedicated account manager.

Pros: No credit worries, ideal for teams with high-volume needs.

Cons: Expensive for individuals; fair use policy means heavy usage may be throttled.

Runway ML vs. Alternatives: Which Should You Choose?

Synthesia: Best for AI Avatar Videos

Synthesia excels at creating professional videos with AI avatars. If you need training videos, presentations, or multilingual content, Synthesia is a strong choice. However, it lacks Runway's advanced video generation and editing capabilities.

Pictory: Best for Text-to-Video Editing

Pictory is great for turning scripts or blog posts into videos quickly. It's more affordable for high-volume text-to-video, but it doesn't offer the same level of AI creativity as Runway.

Descript: Best for Podcast & Video Editing

Descript is a powerhouse for editing video and audio by editing text. It's perfect for podcasters and YouTubers who need transcript-based editing. While it has AI features, it's not a dedicated AI video generator like Runway.

InVideo AI: Best for Prompt-Based Video Creation

InVideo AI lets you create videos from simple prompts, making it super accessible. It's ideal for social media content, but Runway offers more advanced controls and higher-quality outputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does Runway ML cost per month?

Runway ML offers plans starting at $15/month (billed annually) for the Standard plan. The Pro plan is $35/month, and the Unlimited plan is $95/month. Monthly billing is slightly higher.

2. What are Runway ML credits and how do they work?

Credits are used to run AI models. For example, generating 1 second of Gen-2 video costs 1 credit. Different tools consume credits at different rates. You get a monthly allotment based on your plan.

3. Does Runway ML have a free trial?

Yes, the Free plan gives you 125 one-time credits to test the platform. No credit card required.

4. Can I upgrade or downgrade my Runway ML plan?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades at the end of your billing cycle.

5. Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes, annual billing saves you about 17% compared to monthly billing.

6. What happens if I run out of credits?

You can purchase additional credits or upgrade your plan. Credits roll over for one month on paid plans.
