Detailed Review of Each Option

Descript

Descript is a game-changer for anyone who works with spoken content. Its AI-powered transcription lets you edit video by editing text, making it incredibly intuitive. Features like Studio Sound (which removes background noise) and filler word removal save hours of manual editing. The free plan includes 1 hour of transcription per month and basic editing. The Pro plan ($24/month billed annually) adds 10 hours of transcription, 4K export, and advanced AI features. The Enterprise plan offers custom pricing with unlimited transcription and team collaboration.

Pros: Intuitive text-based editing, excellent AI audio tools, generous free plan.
Cons: Limited advanced video effects, transcription hours can run out quickly.

Runway ML

Runway ML is a powerhouse for creative professionals who want to push the boundaries of video editing. It offers over 30 AI-powered tools, including green screen removal, motion tracking, and text-to-video generation. Pricing is credit-based: the free plan gives you 125 credits (about 1 minute of video), while the Standard plan ($15/month) provides 625 credits. The Pro plan ($35/month) offers 2,250 credits and 4K export. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros: Cutting-edge AI features, great for experimental projects.
Cons: Credit system can be confusing, steep learning curve.

Explore Runway ML

Adobe Premiere Pro

Adobe Premiere Pro remains the industry standard for professional video editing. Its AI features, powered by Adobe Sensei, include auto-reframe, scene edit detection, and speech-to-text. Pricing is straightforward: $22.99/month for the single app plan, or $54.99/month for all Creative Cloud apps. There's no free tier, but a 7-day free trial is available.

Pros: Comprehensive toolset, seamless integration with other Adobe apps.
Cons: Expensive, requires powerful hardware, subscription only.

Get Adobe Premiere Pro

Kapwing

Kapwing is a browser-based editor perfect for social media marketers and beginners. Its AI tools include smart cut, auto-subtitle, and background removal. The free plan allows exports up to 7 minutes with a watermark. The Pro plan ($16/month billed annually) removes watermarks, increases export length to 40 minutes, and adds 1080p export. The Business plan ($50/month) offers unlimited exports and team collaboration.

Pros: Easy to use, affordable, great for quick edits.
Cons: Limited advanced features, watermark on free plan.

Try Kapwing free

VEED.IO

VEED.IO is another browser-based option that excels at quick edits and subtitles. Its AI features include auto-subtitles, translation, and background noise removal. The free plan includes basic editing with a watermark. The Pro plan ($18/month billed annually) removes watermarks and adds 4K export, 10-minute video length, and unlimited projects. The Business plan ($30/month) adds team features and 20-minute videos.

Pros: Excellent subtitle tools, user-friendly interface.
Cons: Limited advanced editing, watermark on free plan.

FAQ

What is the cheapest AI video editing software?

Kapwing and VEED.IO offer the most affordable paid plans, starting at $16/month and $18/month respectively (billed annually). Both also have free tiers with limitations.

Is there a free AI video editor?

Yes, Descript, Runway ML, Kapwing, and VEED.IO all offer free plans. They typically include watermarks or limited features, but they're great for testing.

Can I use AI video editing tools for commercial projects?

Most paid plans allow commercial use. However, check the terms of service—some free plans restrict commercial use or require attribution.

How does Descript's pricing compare to Adobe Premiere Pro?

Descript's Pro plan is $24/month (annual), while Adobe Premiere Pro is $22.99/month. Descript focuses on AI-powered text-based editing, while Premiere Pro offers a full professional editing suite.

What are the hidden costs of AI video editing software?

Watch out for credit systems (Runway ML), transcription limits (Descript), and export restrictions (Kapwing, VEED.IO). Always read the fine print.

Which AI video editor is best for YouTube?

Descript is excellent for YouTube thanks to its text-based editing and audio enhancement. Kapwing and VEED.IO are also popular for quick edits and subtitles.
