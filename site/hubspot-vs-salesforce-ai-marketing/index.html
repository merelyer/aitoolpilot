HubSpot AI Marketing Features (2025)

HubSpot has always been known for its user-friendly interface, and its AI features are no exception. In 2025, HubSpot's AI is centered around Breeze, a suite of AI agents and tools that work across marketing, sales, and service.

Key AI Features

  • Breeze Copilot: A conversational AI assistant that can answer questions about your data, generate content, and automate tasks. It's like having a marketing analyst on call 24/7.
  • Breeze Agents: These specialized agents handle specific tasks: content creation, social media posting, ad management, and even website personalization. For example, the Content Agent can draft blog posts, emails, and landing pages in your brand voice.
  • Predictive Lead Scoring: This AI analyzes historical data to score leads based on their likelihood to convert. It learns from your sales team's feedback, improving accuracy over time.
  • AI Content Assistant: Integrated into the content editor, it helps with brainstorming, outlining, and rewriting. You can generate entire emails or social posts with a few clicks.
  • ChatSpot: A free AI tool that connects to your CRM, allowing you to pull reports, create contact lists, and even draft emails using natural language commands.

Pricing

HubSpot's Marketing Hub offers a free tier with basic features. Paid plans start at $20/month (Starter), then $890/month (Professional), and $3,600/month (Enterprise) when billed annually. Breeze agents are available as add-ons, with usage-based pricing starting at $99 per agent per month.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Intuitive interface, excellent onboarding, AI is deeply integrated, affordable for SMBs, all-in-one platform (CRM + marketing + sales).
  • Cons: Costs can escalate with add-ons, some advanced features require Enterprise, less customizable than Salesforce for complex workflows.

Salesforce AI Marketing Features (2025)

Salesforce is the giant of CRM, and its AI capabilities are built on Einstein, which has been around for years. In 2025, Einstein has evolved into a full AI platform with generative AI, predictive analytics, and automation.

Key AI Features

  • Einstein GPT (Generative AI): This creates personalized content for emails, ads, and web pages. It can generate copy that matches each customer's preferences and behavior.
  • Einstein Predictive Analytics: This provides advanced forecasting and segmentation. It can predict customer churn, lifetime value, and next best action.
  • Einstein Campaign Management: This uses AI to optimize campaign timing and channel mix, ensuring your messages reach the right people at the right time.
  • Einstein Engagement Scoring: This scores leads based on their engagement across all channels, helping you prioritize follow-ups.
  • Salesforce Data Cloud: This unifies customer data from all sources, and Einstein uses it to power AI insights. It's a game-changer for personalization.

Pricing

Salesforce Marketing Cloud pricing is not public and varies widely based on your needs. However, the base Marketing Cloud Engagement starts around $1,250/month per 10,000 contacts. There's also a Starter plan for small businesses at $25/user/month (billed annually) for basic marketing automation.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Extremely powerful and customizable, scales to enterprise level, advanced AI for complex segmentation, huge ecosystem of apps and integrations.
  • Cons: Steep learning curve, expensive, often requires dedicated admins, pricing is opaque, can be overkill for small teams.

Who Should Use What

Choose HubSpot if:

  • You're a small to mid-sized business (SMB) looking for an all-in-one inbound marketing platform.
  • You value ease of use and quick onboarding over deep customization.
  • You want AI features that are immediately accessible without a data science team.
  • You need a free tier to test the waters.

Choose Salesforce if:

  • You're a large enterprise with complex marketing and sales processes.
  • You require advanced customization and a highly scalable platform.
  • You have the budget for a dedicated admin team and consultants.
  • You need industry-specific solutions (e.g., financial services, healthcare).

We've seen companies make the switch both ways. For example, a 50-person B2B tech company might find HubSpot's AI content agent saves them hours each week. Meanwhile, a global retailer with millions of customers would likely need Salesforce's data cloud to manage real-time personalization at scale.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both HubSpot and Salesforce offer robust AI marketing features in 2025, but they cater to different audiences. HubSpot is our top pick for most businesses because it delivers powerful AI in a user-friendly package at a reasonable price. Its Breeze agents can automate content creation, lead scoring, and even ad management, making it a true marketing copilot.

Salesforce, on the other hand, is the heavy artillery. If you have a complex sales cycle, a massive contact database, and a team of experts, its Einstein AI can drive incredible ROI. But for the average marketer, the learning curve and cost are prohibitive.

Our advice: If you're just starting your AI marketing journey, start with HubSpot's free tools and see how they work for you. You can always upgrade to Salesforce later if your needs outgrow HubSpot.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is better for AI marketing: HubSpot or Salesforce?

For most SMBs, HubSpot is better due to its ease of use, integrated AI, and lower cost. Salesforce is better for large enterprises that need advanced customization and scalability.

2. Can HubSpot and Salesforce integrate with each other?

Yes, there are third-party connectors like Zapier and native integrations that sync data between the two. However, it's uncommon to use both simultaneously for marketing; most companies choose one.

3. Does HubSpot have a free AI tool?

Yes, ChatSpot is a free AI assistant that works with HubSpot's CRM. It can help with content generation, data analysis, and task automation.

4. What is the starting price for Salesforce Marketing Cloud?

Salesforce Marketing Cloud Engagement starts around $1,250/month for 10,000 contacts, but pricing is custom and can be higher depending on your needs.

5. Which platform is easier to learn: HubSpot or Salesforce?

HubSpot is significantly easier to learn due to its intuitive interface and excellent onboarding resources. Salesforce has a steep learning curve and often requires formal training.

6. Can Salesforce's Einstein AI write content?

Yes, Einstein GPT can generate email copy, ad text, and other content. However, it's often used by larger teams with more complex personalization needs.

7. Is HubSpot's AI worth the extra cost?

HubSpot's Breeze agents start at $99/month per agent, which many users find cost-effective compared to hiring additional staff. The time savings can quickly justify the expense.
