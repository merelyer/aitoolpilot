2. DALL-E 3 (via ChatGPT)

DALL-E 3 is integrated into ChatGPT, making it incredibly user-friendly. You can generate images directly in your chat, and it excels at following complex prompts.

  • Features: Text-to-image, editing, variations, integration with ChatGPT for prompt refinement.
  • Pricing: Included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) or via API (pay-per-image).
  • Pros: Easy to use, great prompt adherence, no separate subscription.

3. Stable Diffusion

Stable Diffusion is an open-source model that you can run locally or via cloud services. It offers unparalleled flexibility and control.

  • Features: Custom models, inpainting, outpainting, upscaling, and a vast ecosystem of extensions.
  • Pricing: Free if self-hosted (requires powerful GPU). Cloud options like DreamStudio start at $10 for 1000 credits.
  • Pros: Completely free (if you have hardware), endless customization, no censorship.
  • Cons: Steep learning curve, requires technical skills, hardware costs.

4. Leonardo.ai

Leonardo.ai is tailored for game developers and creatives needing consistent assets. It offers a generous free tier and powerful editing tools.

  • Features: Real-time canvas, 3D texture generation, custom model training, and a variety of presets.
  • Pricing: Free tier with 150 daily credits; Apprentice at $12/month (25,000 credits), Artisan at $30/month (50,000 credits), Maestro at $60/month (100,000 credits).
  • Pros: Free tier, commercial rights on paid plans, excellent for game assets.
  • Cons: Credit system can be confusing, image quality slightly below Midjourney.

5. Adobe Firefly

Adobe Firefly is integrated into Creative Cloud apps and focuses on commercially safe content, trained on Adobe Stock and licensed content.

  • Features: Text-to-image, generative fill, text effects, and integration with Photoshop and Illustrator.
  • Pricing: Free tier with 25 monthly credits; Premium at $9.99/month for 100 credits; also included in Creative Cloud subscriptions.
  • Pros: Commercially safe, seamless Adobe integration, user-friendly.
  • Cons: Limited credits, less artistic flexibility, requires Adobe ecosystem for full benefits.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the cheapest AI image generator?

Stable Diffusion is free if you self-host, but requires a powerful GPU. Leonardo.ai and Adobe Firefly offer free tiers with limited credits.

2. Which AI image generator has the best free plan?

Leonardo.ai provides 150 daily credits, which is generous for casual use. Adobe Firefly offers 25 monthly credits, and Bing Image Creator (DALL-E 3) is free with limits.

3. Do I need a subscription for commercial use?

Most tools require a paid plan for commercial rights. Midjourney's Basic plan doesn't include commercial use; you need Standard or higher. Leonardo.ai and Adobe Firefly include commercial rights on paid plans.

4. Can I use AI-generated images for my business?

Yes, but check the license. Some tools like Adobe Firefly are designed for commercial safety. Always review terms to ensure you're covered.

5. How do credits work in AI image generators?

Credits are typically used per image generation. For example, Leonardo.ai gives 150 daily credits; each image costs a certain number of credits based on resolution and settings.

6. Is Midjourney worth the price?

If you need the highest quality artistic images and don't mind the Discord interface, yes. The $10 Basic plan is a great entry point.
