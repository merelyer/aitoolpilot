FAQ

1. Is there a completely free AI image generator?

Yes, Leonardo AI and Adobe Firefly offer free tiers with limited generations. Stable Diffusion is free if you self-host, but you'll need a powerful GPU.

2. Which AI image generator has the best image quality?

Midjourney consistently produces the highest quality, most artistic images. DALL-E 3 is close and excels at following complex prompts.

3. Can I use AI-generated images for commercial purposes?

It depends on the tool. Adobe Firefly is designed for commercial safety. Midjourney allows commercial use on paid plans. Always check the terms of service.

4. What is the cheapest paid plan?

Adobe Firefly Premium at $9.99/month and Midjourney Basic at $10/month are the most affordable paid options.

5. Do I need a powerful computer to use AI image generators?

Not for cloud-based tools like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Leonardo AI, and DreamStudio. Only Stable Diffusion self-hosted requires a strong GPU.

6. How do credits work in these tools?

Credits are typically used per image generation. For example, Adobe Firefly gives 25 free credits monthly; each image costs one credit. Leonardo AI uses tokens, where more complex generations cost more tokens.
