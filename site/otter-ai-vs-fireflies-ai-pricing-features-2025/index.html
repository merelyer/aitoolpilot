Deep Dive: Otter.ai

What is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is a widely popular AI meeting assistant that provides real-time transcription, automated notes, and collaboration features. It's known for its user-friendly interface and robust mobile app. Otter integrates seamlessly with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, making it a go-to for many professionals.

Key Features of Otter.ai

  • Real-time transcription: Otter transcribes meetings as they happen, with speaker identification and live captions.
  • Automated summaries: After a meeting, Otter generates a concise summary with key points, action items, and decisions.
  • OtterPilot: This feature automatically joins your Zoom or Google Meet meetings, records audio, and sends the transcript to participants.
  • Collaboration: You can share transcripts, comment on specific sections, and highlight important parts.
  • Search: Otter's search function is solid, allowing you to find specific keywords or phrases within your transcripts.
  • Integrations: Works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, and more.

Otter.ai Pricing (2025)

Otter.ai offers three main plans:

  • Basic (Free): Includes 300 minutes of transcription per month, 30 minutes per conversation, and 3 integrations.
  • Pro: $8.33/month (billed annually) or $16.99/month (monthly). Includes 1,200 minutes per month, 90 minutes per conversation, and advanced search.
  • Business: $20/month per user (billed annually) or $30/month (monthly). Adds admin controls, usage analytics, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. Offers SSO, dedicated support, and advanced security.

Note: Prices are in USD and were accurate as of early 2025.

Deep Dive: Fireflies.ai

What is Fireflies.ai?

Fireflies.ai is a powerful AI meeting assistant designed for teams, especially sales and customer-facing roles. It excels in capturing, transcribing, and analyzing conversations with a focus on actionable insights. Fireflies integrates with a wide range of CRMs and project management tools, making it a favorite for revenue teams.

Key Features of Fireflies.ai

  • Automatic recording and transcription: Fireflies records and transcribes meetings from Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and even phone calls.
  • AI-powered search: This is Fireflies' standout feature. You can ask natural language questions like "What did we decide about the pricing?" and get relevant clips from your meetings.
  • Conversation intelligence: Fireflies analyzes your meetings for sentiment, talk-to-listen ratio, and key topics, providing valuable coaching insights for sales teams.
  • Topic tracking: Automatically detects and tracks predefined topics like "budget" or "competitor" across all your meetings.
  • Integrations: Over 50 native integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Zapier.
  • Collaboration: Share clips, add comments, and create action items directly from the transcript.

Fireflies.ai Pricing (2025)

Fireflies.ai offers a tiered pricing structure:

  • Free: Unlimited transcription (with limited storage), 800 minutes of storage, and 1 seat.
  • Pro: $10/month per seat (billed annually) or $18/month (monthly). Includes unlimited transcription, 8,000 minutes of storage, and advanced features like AI summaries and topic tracking.
  • Business: $19/month per seat (billed annually) or $29/month (monthly). Adds automated video recording, custom vocabulary, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. Includes SSO, dedicated success manager, and advanced security.

Note: Prices are in USD and were accurate as of early 2025.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is cheaper: Otter.ai or Fireflies.ai?

Otter.ai's Pro plan starts at $8.33/month (annual billing), while Fireflies.ai's Pro plan starts at $10/month (annual billing). However, Fireflies offers unlimited transcription even on its free plan, which can be more cost-effective for heavy users. For light users, Otter's free tier might be sufficient.

2. Can both tools integrate with Zoom and Google Meet?

Yes, both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai integrate seamlessly with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. They can automatically join and record meetings, transcribe them, and deliver summaries.

3. Which tool has better AI search?

Fireflies.ai has a more advanced AI search that understands natural language queries and returns relevant clips from your meeting history. Otter's search is keyword-based and less powerful.

4. Are there any free plans available?

Yes, both offer free plans. Otter's free plan includes 300 minutes per month with a 30-minute limit per conversation. Fireflies' free plan includes unlimited transcription but limits storage to 800 minutes and only 1 seat.

5. Do these tools support languages other than English?

Yes, both support multiple languages. Otter.ai supports English, Spanish, French, German, and more. Fireflies.ai also supports several languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, and Hindi. Check their official documentation for the full list.

6. Can I use these tools for phone calls?

Fireflies.ai offers a dedicated phone number that you can dial into for recording and transcribing calls. Otter.ai primarily focuses on video conferencing platforms, though it can transcribe audio files you upload.
