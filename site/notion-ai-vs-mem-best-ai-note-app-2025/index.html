Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion has long been a favorite for its all-in-one workspace—notes, tasks, databases, and wikis. With AI, it becomes even more powerful. Notion AI is integrated throughout, not just a separate chatbot.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft, rewrite, summarize, and change tone directly in any note or document.
  • AI Search: Find answers across your entire workspace, even in scanned PDFs and images (with OCR).
  • AI Database Features: Auto-fill properties, generate formulas, and summarize database entries.
  • Q&A: Ask questions in plain English, and get answers with sources cited from your content.
  • Integrations: Works with Slack, Google Drive, and many others.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan for personal use. AI is an add-on: $10/month per member on the free plan, or included in the Business plan at $20/month per member (annual billing). Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros and Cons

Pros:
  • Versatile: notes, projects, databases, and documentation in one place.
  • AI is contextual—it understands your workspace and data.
  • Powerful collaboration features for teams.
  • Extensive templates and customization options.
Cons:
  • Steep learning curve for beginners.
  • AI add-on can get pricey for small teams.
  • Offline mode is limited.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem is designed as a “self-organizing” note app. It uses AI to automatically tag, link, and resurface notes based on context, creating a personal knowledge graph that gets smarter over time.

Key Features

  • Automatic Organization: No need to manually file notes—Mem’s AI does it for you.
  • Contextual Resurfacing: Related notes appear automatically as you type, helping you connect ideas.
  • AI Chat: Ask questions across your notes and get answers with citations.
  • Daily Digest: A daily summary of your notes and tasks.
  • Quick Capture: Add notes via web clipper, email, or mobile app.

Pricing

Mem has a generous free tier. Pro plan costs $8/month (annual) or $10/month monthly. There’s also a Team plan at $15/month per user (annual).

Pros and Cons

Pros:
  • Zero-friction note-taking—just write, Mem organizes.
  • Excellent AI search and discovery.
  • Clean, minimal interface.
  • Affordable compared to Notion AI.
Cons:
  • Limited database and project management features.
  • Not as customizable as Notion.
  • Some users report AI suggestions can be hit-or-miss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI better than Mem?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for all-in-one workspace and team collaboration, while Mem is better for automatic note organization and personal knowledge management.

Can I use Notion AI for free?

Yes, Notion has a free plan, but AI features are a paid add-on at $10/month per member. You can try AI with a limited free trial.

Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem offers a free tier with basic AI features. The Pro plan costs $8/month (annual) and includes advanced AI and unlimited notes.

Which app is better for students?

Mem might be better for students who want to capture and organize lecture notes quickly. Notion AI is better if you need to manage assignments and projects alongside notes.

Can I migrate my notes from Evernote or OneNote?

Both Notion and Mem offer import tools from Evernote, OneNote, and other apps. Notion’s import is more comprehensive, while Mem’s is simpler but still functional.

Which app has better AI search?

Mem’s AI search is specifically designed for note discovery and is very effective. Notion AI search is powerful but may require more setup to get the best results.
