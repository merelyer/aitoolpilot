In-Depth Review: Notion AI

Notion AI is not just a note-taking app; it's a complete workspace that combines notes, docs, wikis, and project management. Launched in 2023, the AI features have evolved rapidly, making it one of the most powerful AI note apps in 2025.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft, summarize, and edit text directly in your notes. You can ask it to write a blog post, create a pros/cons list, or translate content.
  • Q&A Across Workspace: Ask questions and get answers from your entire Notion workspace, including pages and databases.
  • Autofill and Summarize: Automatically fill databases with AI-generated content and summarize long documents.
  • Integration with Notion's Ecosystem: Works seamlessly with Notion's databases, templates, and collaborative features.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan with limited AI features (a few AI responses per month). The paid plans start at $10/month for the AI add-on (if you have a paid Notion plan), or you can get the Business plan with AI included for $20/user/month. For teams, the Enterprise plan offers advanced controls.

In-Depth Review: Mem

Mem is a self-organizing note-taking app that uses AI to automatically organize and surface your notes. It's designed for people who type fast and want to retrieve information without manual tagging or folder creation. In 2025, Mem has become a favorite among productivity enthusiasts.

Key Features

  • Self-Organizing Notes: Mem automatically groups related notes, creates connections, and suggests related content.
  • AI Chat: Chat with your notes to get answers, summarize, and even generate new ideas based on your existing content.
  • Contextual Retrieval: The more you write, the better Mem understands your context and surfaces relevant notes.
  • Slack Integration: Save Slack conversations directly to Mem, which is handy for teams.

Pricing

Mem has a free plan with limited AI interactions (a few per day). The Pro plan costs $14.99/month and includes unlimited AI, more storage, and advanced features. For teams, the Team plan is $24.99/user/month with collaboration tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI free?

Notion offers a free plan that includes limited AI features (a few AI responses per month). To get unlimited AI, you need a paid plan like the Business plan at $20/user/month or the AI add-on for $10/month on a paid workspace plan.

Is Mem AI free?

Mem has a free plan with limited AI interactions (around 5 per day). For unlimited AI and advanced features, you need the Pro plan at $14.99/month.

Which app is better for students?

Both are good, but Mem's self-organizing nature might be more appealing for students who want to capture lecture notes quickly. However, Notion AI offers more flexibility for creating study guides and project plans. It depends on your study style.

Can Notion AI and Mem be used offline?

Notion offers offline access on desktop apps, but AI features require an internet connection. Mem is primarily cloud-based and requires internet for AI features. Both offer mobile apps with limited offline capabilities.

Which app has better AI accuracy?

In our tests, Notion AI tends to produce more polished and context-aware responses, especially for writing tasks. Mem's AI is excellent at retrieval and summarization, but sometimes struggles with complex queries. For note organization, Mem's AI is more proactive.

Can I migrate my notes from Evernote or OneNote?

Notion has built-in importers for Evernote, OneNote, and others. Mem also supports importing from Evernote and Markdown files, but the process may require more manual steps. Check each app's help center for detailed guides.
