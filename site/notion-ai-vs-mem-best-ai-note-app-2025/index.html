Notion AI vs Mem: Best AI Note App 2025

In 2025, note-taking has evolved beyond simple text capture. With the rise of AI-powered tools, your notes can now summarize themselves, answer questions, and even generate content. Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion has long been a favorite for its flexible blocks, databases, and collaborative features. With the AI add-on, it becomes a powerful assistant that can write, summarize, brainstorm, and even translate content directly in your workspace.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, improve writing, and change tone.
  • Summarization: Summarize long documents or meeting notes in seconds.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your notes and get instant answers.
  • Translation: Translate text into multiple languages.
  • Integration: Works seamlessly with Notion's databases, wikis, and project tools.
  • Team Collaboration: Share AI-generated content with teammates.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan for personal use, but the AI add-on costs $10 per member/month when billed annually, or $12 month-to-month. For teams, Notion Business plan starts at $15 per member/month (annual billing), and AI is included in the Business plan. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros

  • All-in-one workspace (notes, docs, databases, projects)
  • Powerful AI features integrated natively
  • Excellent collaboration tools
  • Huge template library
  • Available on web, desktop, and mobile

Cons

  • AI features require an additional cost on the free/Personal plan
  • Steeper learning curve for new users
  • Offline mode is limited
  • AI can occasionally produce generic responses

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem is an AI-first note-taking app that focuses on capturing and connecting your ideas automatically. It uses AI to organize your notes, surface related content, and even answer questions based on your personal knowledge base.

Key Features

  • AI Auto-Organization: Notes are automatically tagged and linked based on context.
  • Natural Language Search: Find notes by typing questions or phrases.
  • Smart Writing: AI helps you draft and refine notes.
  • Related Notes: Automatically suggests related notes to help you connect ideas.
  • Capture from Anywhere: Save web pages, emails, and tweets directly to Mem.
  • Daily Review: Get a daily digest of relevant notes and tasks.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited features. The Pro plan costs $8/month when billed annually, or $10 month-to-month. There's also a Team plan at $15 per user/month (annual billing), and Enterprise pricing is available on request.

Pros

  • True AI-first design—notes organize themselves
  • Excellent for personal knowledge management (PKM)
  • Fast and intuitive search
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Great for capturing ideas on the go

Cons

  • Limited project management features
  • No built-in database or spreadsheet functionality
  • AI suggestions can sometimes be off-target
  • Fewer integrations compared to Notion

Who Should Use What?

Use Notion AI if:

  • You need an all-in-one workspace for notes, tasks, and projects.
  • You work in a team and need collaborative features.
  • You rely on databases and structured data.
  • You want a tool that can replace multiple apps.

Use Mem if:

Bottom Line

Both Notion AI and Mem are excellent AI note apps, but they serve different needs. Notion AI is the best choice for those who need a comprehensive productivity suite with robust AI capabilities. It's ideal for teams and individuals who want to manage projects, databases, and notes in one place. Mem, on the other hand, is perfect for personal knowledge management, offering a more streamlined and automatic approach to note organization. If you're a student, researcher, or writer, Mem's AI can help you connect ideas effortlessly.

We recommend trying both apps with their free plans to see which one fits your workflow. For most users, Notion AI's versatility and collaboration features make it the winner.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than Mem?

Notion AI is better for team collaboration and project management, while Mem excels in personal knowledge management. The best choice depends on your needs. If you need an all-in-one workspace, Notion AI is superior; if you want a dedicated AI note app, Mem might be better.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion offers a free plan, but the AI features are not included. To use Notion AI, you need to purchase the AI add-on for $10/month (annual billing) or subscribe to a paid plan like Personal Plus or Business.

3. Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem has a free plan that includes basic note-taking and limited AI features. The Pro plan costs $8/month (annual) and unlocks full AI capabilities, unlimited notes, and more.

4. Which app is better for students?

For students, Mem is often more intuitive for capturing lecture notes and connecting ideas. However, Notion AI is better for organizing class schedules, assignments, and group projects. It depends on your study habits.

5. Can I export my notes from Notion or Mem?

Yes, both apps allow you to export your notes. Notion supports export to Markdown, HTML, and PDF. Mem allows export in Markdown and other formats, but some features may be limited on the free plan.

6. Are there any hidden costs with Notion AI or Mem?

Neither app has hidden costs, but be aware that Notion's AI add-on is an extra charge on top of the base subscription. Mem's pricing is straightforward, with no additional fees for AI features.

7. Which app has better AI writing assistance?

Notion AI has more advanced writing features, including tone adjustment, summarization, and translation. Mem's AI is more focused on organizing and connecting notes, though it also offers writing help.
