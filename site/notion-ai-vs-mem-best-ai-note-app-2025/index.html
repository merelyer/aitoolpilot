Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion has been a household name in productivity for years, and its AI features have evolved significantly. In 2025, Notion AI is deeply integrated into the platform, offering a seamless experience for individuals and teams.

Key Features of Notion AI

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, improve writing, summarize, translate, and change tone—all within your notes.
  • Q&A: Ask questions across your entire workspace and get instant answers with citations.
  • Autofill: Automatically fill databases with AI-generated content, perfect for CRM or project tracking.
  • Meeting Notes: Record and transcribe meetings, then generate summaries, action items, and next steps.
  • Integrations: Connect with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, and 100+ other tools via Zapier.
  • Customizable Workspace: Build wikis, databases, kanban boards, calendars, and more—all in one place.

Pricing

Notion offers a generous free plan for personal use, with unlimited pages and blocks. For teams, the Plus plan starts at $10/member/month (annual billing). To access AI features, you need to add the AI add-on, which costs $10/member/month. Alternatively, the Business plan at $20/member/month includes AI features. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros and Cons of Notion AI

  • Pros: All-in-one workspace, powerful AI, extensive integrations, collaborative features, highly customizable.
  • Cons: Steep learning curve for new users, AI add-on costs extra on lower tiers, can feel cluttered with too many features.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem takes a different approach. It's designed as a self-organizing knowledge base that uses AI to automatically connect related notes, making your information retrieval effortless. Mem 2.0, released in 2024, introduced a sleek interface and enhanced AI capabilities.

Key Features of Mem

  • Mem 0: An AI assistant that learns from your notes and provides contextual answers, like "What did I decide about the marketing campaign?"
  • Automatic Organization: Notes are automatically linked based on semantic similarity, creating a web of knowledge.
  • Natural Language Search: Search using conversational phrases, and Mem will find relevant notes.
  • Quick Capture: Use the browser extension or mobile app to save content instantly.
  • Smart Writing: AI helps you draft notes, expand ideas, and rewrite text.
  • Integrations: Connect with Slack, Gmail, Twitter, and more to automatically import information.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with 5,000 notes and 5GB storage. The Pro plan costs $8/month (annual billing) and includes unlimited notes, more AI queries, and advanced features. There's also a Team plan at $15/user/month (annual) for collaboration.

Pros and Cons of Mem

  • Pros: Automatic organization saves time, natural language search is powerful, clean and simple interface, great for personal knowledge management.
  • Cons: Limited collaboration features, fewer customization options, AI queries are limited on free tier, not ideal for complex project management.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is Notion AI better than Mem?

Notion AI is generally better for team collaboration and all-in-one workspace needs, while Mem is better for personal knowledge management with automatic organization. The choice depends on your specific requirements.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Yes, Notion's free plan is quite generous, but AI features require an add-on. You can try AI with a limited number of responses for free, but for full access, you'll need to subscribe to the AI add-on or a paid plan.

3. Does Mem have a mobile app?

Yes, Mem offers mobile apps for iOS and Android, allowing you to capture and search notes on the go.

4. Which app is better for meeting notes?

Notion AI has a dedicated meeting notes feature that can record, transcribe, and summarize meetings with action items. Mem can also handle meeting notes but lacks the same level of integration with calendars and video conferencing tools.

5. Can I migrate my existing notes to Notion AI or Mem?

Both apps offer import features. Notion supports import from Evernote, OneNote, and other tools. Mem also provides import options, including from Evernote and plain text files.

6. What is the learning curve for each app?

Notion has a steeper learning curve due to its extensive features and customization options. Mem is simpler and more intuitive, making it easier for beginners to get started quickly.

7. Are there any privacy concerns with AI note apps?

Both Notion and Mem use AI to process your data, which may be stored on their servers. It's important to review their privacy policies and consider using them for non-sensitive information if you have concerns.
