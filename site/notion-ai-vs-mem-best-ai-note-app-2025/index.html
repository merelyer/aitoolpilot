Deep Dive: Notion AI

Notion started as a productivity tool that replaced Trello, Confluence, and Google Docs. In 2023, they added AI features, and by 2025, the AI is deeply integrated into the entire platform. This isn't just a chatbot bolted onto notes; it's a writing assistant, a database helper, and a Q&A engine.

Key Features of Notion AI

  • AI Autofill: Automatically fills database properties. For example, you can ask it to categorize a new sales lead based on the company description.
  • Workspace Q&A: Ask questions across all your pages and get answers with citations. This is a game-changer for team wikis.
  • Writing & Editing: Draft, summarize, translate, and change tone directly in the editor. It handles long-form content well.
  • Meeting Notes: Transcript summaries and action item extraction (requires integration with tools like Zoom or Meet).
  • Custom AI Blocks: You can build workflows where AI processes data on a schedule or in response to triggers.

Pros of Notion AI

  • Unified Workspace: You don't need a separate note app, a wiki, and a project tracker. It's all in one place.
  • Granular Permissions: Perfect for teams—you control who can use AI and what data the AI can access.
  • Database Power: The AI is most impressive when combined with Notion's relational databases.
  • Reliable Infrastructure: Notion is a mature company; you won't worry about the startup shutting down.

Cons of Notion AI

  • Steep Learning Curve: The flexibility is a double-edged sword. New users often feel overwhelmed by the blank page.
  • Cost: AI features require the Business plan; the free plan doesn't include AI.
  • Performance: Sometimes the app can feel sluggish when you have large databases open.

Notion AI Pricing

Notion's pricing is per user, which can add up for teams. The AI is included in the Business plan and above.

  • Free: $0 — limited blocks, no AI.
  • Plus: $10/user/month — unlimited blocks, no AI.
  • Business: $20/user/month — includes AI features.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — includes advanced AI controls.

Deep Dive: Mem

Mem takes a completely different approach. It bills itself as a "self-organizing workspace." You just write notes, and Mem's AI automatically tags, links, and surfaces related content. There are no folders to create, no databases to configure—just write and let the AI do the rest.

Key Features of Mem

  • Self-Organizing Notes: The AI automatically connects related notes and creates a "Related Notes" section on every page.
  • Smart Search: Search is semantic, not just keyword-based. Ask "What did I learn about React last month?" and it knows.
  • AI Chat: Chat with your own knowledge base. You can ask for summaries, action items, or even a draft email based on your notes.
  • Quick Capture: The Chrome extension and mobile app let you capture content fast; Mem handles the organization.
  • Daily Documents: A daily note is automatically generated, and you can dump anything into it.

Pros of Mem

  • Zero Setup: You don't need to learn a system. It works out of the box.
  • Excellent for Personal Knowledge: Great for researchers, writers, and lifelong learners.
  • Beautiful Interface: Clean and minimal; it feels like a modern app.
  • Strong AI Context: Because it learns your notes, the AI answers feel more personalized than generic chatbots.

Cons of Mem

  • Not for Project Management: There are no databases, timelines, or complex task views. It's notes only.
  • AI Hallucinations: Sometimes the AI makes connections that don't exist, leading to confusing "Related Notes."
  • Export Limitations: Moving your data out of Mem is harder than it should be.
  • Team Features: Collaboration is basic; it's not a replacement for a team wiki.

Mem Pricing

  • Free: $0 — limited AI queries (around 25 per month).
  • Pro: $8.33/month (billed annually) — unlimited AI, full search.
  • Teams: $12/user/month — adds shared spaces.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You manage complex projects with multiple stakeholders.
  • You need a structured knowledge base (wikis, SOPs).
  • You rely on databases and properties to organize information.
  • You work in a team and need granular permissions.
  • You want one tool to replace Notion + ChatGPT for drafting.

Choose Mem if:

  • You are a solo user overwhelmed by organizing information.
  • You want to capture ideas quickly without thinking about structure.
  • You are a researcher or writer who needs to retrieve information fast.
  • You dislike Notion's complexity and want a simpler interface.
  • You need a personal assistant that remembers your notes.

Bottom Line Verdict

In 2025, the AI note app market is mature, but these two tools serve different masters. Notion AI is the Swiss Army knife: powerful, structured, and essential for teams. Mem is the smart assistant: intuitive, automatic, and perfect for individuals.

Our pick for most people is Notion AI because it offers more value per dollar when you consider the entire workspace package. However, if you've tried Notion and bounced off the learning curve, don't force it—Mem will make you feel organized without the effort.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI worth the extra cost over the regular Notion plan?

If you use Notion daily for work or study, yes. The AI Q&A feature alone saves hours of searching through pages. For casual users on the free plan, it's not necessary.

2. Can Mem replace Notion for project management?

No. Mem is a note-taking app first. It lacks databases, timeline views, and advanced task dependencies. For project management, stick with Notion or use Mem alongside a dedicated PM tool.

3. Which app has better AI accuracy for summarizing documents?

In our tests, Notion AI was slightly more accurate for long documents and meeting transcripts. Mem excels at connecting ideas across multiple notes, but its summaries can sometimes be too generic.

4. Are my notes used to train the AI models?

Notion states they do not train their AI on your data. Mem also states they do not use your data for training. Both use third-party LLMs (like GPT-4) but with data isolation agreements.

5. Can I import my Evernote or Google Docs into these apps?

Yes. Notion has a built-in importer for Evernote, Google Docs, and Word. Mem offers a Chrome extension that can capture web content, and you can import Markdown files.

6. Which app works better on mobile?

Notion's mobile app is more feature-rich but can be slow. Mem's mobile app is faster for quick capture and offers a clean reading experience. For heavy mobile editing, Notion wins.

7. Can I use these AIs offline?

No. Both require an internet connection for AI features. You can view cached notes offline, but the AI functions are cloud-based.
