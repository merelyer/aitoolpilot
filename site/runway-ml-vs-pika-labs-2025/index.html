Runway ML Review

Runway ML has been a pioneer in AI video editing since 2018. It's used by major studios and independent creators alike. In 2025, it offers a comprehensive suite of AI-powered tools that go far beyond simple text-to-video.

Key Features

  • Text to Video: Generate videos from text prompts with impressive coherence.
  • Video to Video: Transform existing footage with AI styles.
  • Green Screen: Remove backgrounds without a green screen.
  • Motion Tracking: Track objects and apply effects.
  • Inpainting: Remove unwanted objects from videos.
  • AI Training: Custom models for specific styles.
