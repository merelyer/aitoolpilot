Detailed Review of Midjourney

Midjourney has been a favorite among digital artists and designers since its beta in 2022. Its latest version, V6 (and V7 in beta), pushes the boundaries of AI art generation with incredible detail, lighting, and composition.

Key Features

  • High Artistic Quality: Midjourney excels at producing images with a painterly, cinematic, and often surreal aesthetic. It's particularly good at rendering complex scenes, textures, and lighting.
  • Style Control: With parameters like --style, --chaos, and --stylize, you can fine-tune the output to match your vision. You can also use reference images (image prompts) to guide the style.
  • Upscaling and Variations: Generate multiple variations of an image and upscale to high resolution (up to 1024x1024, but with upscale options you can go higher).
  • Community and Collaboration: Midjourney is accessed via Discord, where you can see other users' creations, get inspiration, and participate in challenges.
  • New Web Editor: In late 2024, Midjourney launched a web editor that allows you to edit images directly in the browser, making it more accessible for non-Discord users.

Pricing

Midjourney offers four subscription tiers:

  • Basic: $10/month (or $8/month billed yearly) – 200 images per month, limited commercial use (no selling images).
  • Standard: $30/month (or $24/month yearly) – 15 hours of GPU time, unlimited images (subject to fair use), commercial use allowed.
  • Pro: $60/month (or $48/month yearly) – 30 hours of GPU time, unlimited images, commercial use, and privacy mode.
  • Mega: $120/month (or $96/month yearly) – 60 hours of GPU time, all Pro features, and priority support.

For most serious creators, the Standard plan is the sweet spot.

Detailed Review of DALL-E 3

DALL-E 3, developed by OpenAI, is the third iteration of the DALL-E series. It's integrated into ChatGPT Plus and the Bing Image Creator (powered by DALL-E). DALL-E 3 is known for its ability to understand complex prompts and generate accurate text within images—a major leap from its predecessors.

Key Features

  • Accurate Text Rendering: DALL-E 3 can generate images with clear, readable text, making it ideal for posters, logos, and infographics.
  • Prompt Understanding: It handles nuanced prompts better than most, interpreting intent and context accurately.
  • Integration with ChatGPT: You can generate images directly in ChatGPT, and even use conversational prompts to refine results. This is a game-changer for workflow.
  • Built-in Safety: OpenAI has implemented robust content filters to avoid harmful or inappropriate content.
  • Accessible: Available via ChatGPT Plus (web, mobile), Bing Image Creator (free), and API for developers.

Pricing

DALL-E 3 is available through several channels:

  • Bing Image Creator: Free, but with limited credits and slower generation times.
  • ChatGPT Plus: $20/month – includes DALL-E 3 access (up to 50 images per day), plus GPT-4 and other features.
  • API: Pay-as-you-go, with pricing around $0.040 per image (1024x1024) for standard resolution, and $0.080 for HD.

For heavy users, ChatGPT Plus is the best value.

Who Should Use What

Choosing between Midjourney and DALL-E 3 depends on your specific needs:

  • Digital Artists & Illustrators: Midjourney is the clear winner. Its artistic quality and style control are unmatched.
  • Marketing & Social Media Managers: If you need quick, on-brand images with text overlays (like ads or social posts), DALL-E 3's text rendering is a lifesaver.
  • Graphic Designers: For brainstorming and mood boards, Midjourney offers more creative variety. But for final assets with text, DALL-E 3 is better.
  • Content Creators (Bloggers, YouTubers): Both work, but Midjourney will give your content a unique, premium look.
  • Developers & Startups: If you need API access to generate images programmatically, DALL-E 3 is easier to integrate.
  • Hobbyists: DALL-E 3's free tier makes it accessible, but Midjourney's trial (limited) is worth trying if you want high-quality results.

Bottom Line Verdict

In 2025, both Midjourney and DALL-E 3 are top-tier AI art generators, but they serve different purposes.

Midjourney remains the champion of artistic quality. If you're willing to invest time in learning its nuances, you'll be rewarded with stunning, unique images that stand out. It's perfect for projects where aesthetics are paramount.

DALL-E 3 is the most practical all-rounder. Its integration with ChatGPT, accurate text rendering, and ease of use make it ideal for everyday tasks, especially if you already use OpenAI's ecosystem.

Our recommendation: If you can afford only one, choose Midjourney for its superior visual output. But if you need text accuracy or have a tight budget, start with DALL-E 3.

Both tools offer free trials or low-cost entry points, so there's no harm in testing them yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is better: Midjourney or DALL-E 3?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney is better for artistic, high-quality images with style control; DALL-E 3 is better for accurate text rendering and ease of use. For most creative projects, Midjourney wins.

2. Can I use Midjourney and DALL-E 3 for commercial purposes?

Midjourney allows commercial use on paid plans (Standard and above). DALL-E 3 images generated via ChatGPT Plus are also usable commercially, but check OpenAI's content policy for details.

3. How much does Midjourney cost per month?

Midjourney starts at $10/month for the Basic plan, $30/month for Standard, $60/month for Pro, and $120/month for Mega. Annual billing discounts are available.

4. Is DALL-E 3 free?

You can use DALL-E 3 for free via Bing Image Creator, but with limited credits and slower generation. For unlimited access, you need ChatGPT Plus at $20/month.

5. Can I edit images with Midjourney or DALL-E 3?

Midjourney has a web editor that allows basic editing (inpainting, outpainting). DALL-E 3 doesn't have a built-in editor, but you can use ChatGPT to iterate on prompts.

6. Which tool is better for text in images?

DALL-E 3 is significantly better at rendering text accurately. Midjourney has improved, but still struggles with longer text.
