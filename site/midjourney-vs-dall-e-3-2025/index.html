Detailed Review: Midjourney in 2025

Midjourney has evolved significantly. The V7 model, which rolled out to all users by April 2025, represents a paradigm shift. It is no longer just a 'pretty picture' generator; it now understands complex prompts with multiple objects and spatial relationships.

Key Features

  • V7 Model: Improved coherence, better anatomy, and stunning lighting. Text rendering is now accurate up to 80% of the time for short phrases.
  • Web Editor: The standalone web app (beta) has replaced the Discord-only experience for most users. You can now upload images, use inpainting, and organize your gallery.
  • Style Reference (--sref): This is a game-changer. You can now feed Midjourney a reference image and it will extract the visual style, applying it to new prompts. Perfect for brand consistency.
  • Personalization: The algorithm learns your preferences over time, adjusting color palettes and composition to match your taste.
  • Upscaling: The 2x and 4x upscalers are now sharper than ever, making prints viable.

Detailed Review: DALL-E 3 in 2025

DALL-E 3, accessible exclusively through ChatGPT Plus/Pro, has solidified its position as the most 'intelligent' image generator. It doesn't just follow prompts; it understands context, humor, and nuance. In 2025, it received a significant upgrade: the ability to edit images with natural language conversationally.

Key Features

  • ChatGPT Integration: You can iterate on images conversationally. Ask for a 'red sky instead of blue' and it will modify the existing image, not generate a new one.
  • Superior Text Rendering: DALL-E 3 remains the undisputed champion for rendering legible text. It handles multi-line paragraphs, logos, and signage with high accuracy.
  • Image-to-Image Editing: Upload a photo and ask for changes. It respects the original composition better than ever.
  • GIF Generation: A new feature in 2025 allows for simple animated GIFs (2-3 second loops) directly in ChatGPT.
  • Safety Filters: It is the safest option for commercial use, with robust content moderation.

Pricing (2025 Update)

  • Free Tier: Limited access via ChatGPT free — you get about 2 images per day.
  • ChatGPT Plus: $20/month — Unlimited images (with rate limits) + GPT-4 access.
  • ChatGPT Pro: $200/month — Faster generation, priority access, and higher resolution outputs.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — Includes API access and SSO.

Pros

  • Best-in-class text rendering in images.
  • Intuitive conversational editing.
  • No prompt engineering needed — just describe what you want in plain English.
  • Includes a free tier (limited).

Cons

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3?

For artistic quality and visual impact, yes. Midjourney produces more detailed, cinematic, and aesthetically pleasing images. However, DALL-E 3 is better at following complex instructions and rendering text accurately.

Can I use DALL-E 3 for free?

Yes, you can use DALL-E 3 for free through ChatGPT with a limit of roughly 2 images per day. To get unlimited access, you need a ChatGPT Plus subscription at $20/month.

Which AI image generator is best for logos?

Neither is ideal for final logo design, but DALL-E 3 is better for generating logo concepts with text. Midjourney can create beautiful iconography but often struggles with the precise typography required for a logo.

Does Midjourney have a free trial?

No, Midjourney removed its free trial due to abuse. The only way to test it is to pay the $10/month Basic subscription. You can cancel anytime.

Can I sell images created with Midjourney and DALL-E 3?

Yes, both allow commercial use of generated images for paying users. Midjourney's terms require a paid subscription for commercial rights. DALL-E 3 images generated via ChatGPT can be used commercially, but you must adhere to OpenAI's content policy.

Which is faster: Midjourney or DALL-E 3?

Midjourney is generally faster for generating batches of images (4 at a time in ~30 seconds). DALL-E 3 takes about 10-20 seconds per image but allows for conversational edits that save time in the long run.
