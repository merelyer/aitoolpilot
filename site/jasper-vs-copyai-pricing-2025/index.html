But with tight deadlines and ever-growing content demands, many marketers are turning to AI copywriting tools to stay ahead. Two of the biggest names in this space are Jasper and Copy.ai. In this comprehensive comparison, we'll break down everything you need to know about Jasper vs Copy.ai pricing in 2025, so you can make an informed decision that fits your budget and workflow.

Detailed Review of Each Option

Jasper (formerly Jarvis)

Jasper is one of the most established AI copywriting platforms, known for its high-quality output and marketing-focused features. It uses a combination of GPT-4 and proprietary models, and it's trained extensively on marketing copy, making it particularly good at generating persuasive, brand-aligned content.

Key Features:

  • Brand Voice: Set your brand's tone and style, and Jasper will consistently apply it across all content.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search engines.
  • Workflow (formerly Recipes): Create multi-step processes for complex content generation, like blog posts or email sequences.
  • 50+ Templates: From ad copy to product descriptions, Jasper has a template for almost any need.
  • Browser Extension: Use Jasper anywhere on the web, including in Google Docs, Gmail, and Notion.
  • Multiple Languages: Supports over 30 languages.

Pricing (2025):

  • Creator Plan: $39/month (billed annually) or $49/month (monthly). Includes 1 user, 1 brand voice, and 50,000 words per month.
  • Pro Plan: $59/month (billed annually) or $79/month (monthly). Includes 5 users, 3 brand voices, 150,000 words per month, and access to SEO mode and workflows.
  • Business Plan: Custom pricing. For larger teams with advanced needs.

Pros:

  • High-quality, human-like output
  • Robust features for marketing teams
  • Excellent brand voice consistency
  • SEO integration

Cons:

  • More expensive than some alternatives at higher tiers
  • Steeper learning curve for beginners
  • Word limits can be restrictive on lower plans

Copy.ai

Copy.ai is another major player, focusing on simplicity and speed. It's designed to be user-friendly, making it a great choice for those new to AI writing. Copy.ai offers a free plan, which is a big draw for many.

Key Features:

  • 90+ Copywriting Tools: A wide array of templates for different content types.
  • Free Plan: 2,000 words per month at no cost.
  • Infobase: Store brand information (like product details) and have the AI reference it.
  • Chat Interface: Use the AI in a conversational way for brainstorming and refining.
  • Collaboration: Invite team members and work together on projects.
  • Languages: Supports over 25 languages.

Pricing (2025):

  • Free Plan: $0/month – 2,000 words, 1 seat, access to all tools.
  • Pro Plan: $49/month (billed annually) or $59/month (monthly). Includes unlimited words (fair use), 5 seats, and priority support.
  • Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing. For large organizations with advanced security and support needs.

Pros:

  • Free plan available
  • Very user-friendly interface
  • Unlimited words on Pro plan (fair use)
  • Quick and easy to get started

Cons:

  • Output quality can be less consistent than Jasper
  • Fewer advanced features (no SEO mode, no advanced workflows)
  • Brand voice capabilities are not as robust

Who Should Use What

Now that we've broken down the pricing and features, let's help you decide which tool is right for you.

Use Jasper if:

  • You're a marketing professional or part of a growing team that needs advanced features like SEO optimization and multi-step workflows.
  • Brand voice consistency is critical for your content.
  • You're willing to invest in a premium tool for higher-quality output.
  • You need to generate long-form content like blog posts and reports regularly.

Use Copy.ai if:

  • You're a solo entrepreneur, freelancer, or small business owner on a tight budget.
  • You want to try an AI writer for free before committing.
  • You need quick, short-form copy like social media captions, ad headlines, or product descriptions.
  • You prefer a simple, no-frills interface that's easy to navigate.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Jasper and Copy.ai are excellent AI copywriting tools, but they cater to different needs. Jasper is the more powerful, feature-rich option, ideal for teams that need advanced capabilities and are willing to pay a premium. Copy.ai, on the other hand, offers an unbeatable free plan and a more affordable entry point, making it perfect for individuals and small businesses.

In our opinion, Jasper edges out Copy.ai for most serious marketing use cases due to its superior output quality and brand voice features. However, if budget is your primary concern, Copy.ai is a solid choice that won't disappoint.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Jasper or Copy.ai better for SEO?

Jasper is better for SEO because it offers an integrated SEO mode powered by Surfer SEO, which provides real-time optimization suggestions. Copy.ai does not have a built-in SEO feature, though it can still generate keyword-rich content if you provide the keywords.

2. Can I try Jasper or Copy.ai for free?

Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month. Jasper does not have a free plan, but it offers a 7-day free trial (often with a small fee for the trial, like $5, to verify payment). Both allow you to test the tools before committing.

3. Which tool is more affordable for a small business?

Copy.ai is more affordable for small businesses, especially with its free plan and the Pro plan at $49/month with unlimited words. Jasper's Creator plan starts at $39/month but only includes 50,000 words, which may not be enough for heavy usage.

4. Do Jasper and Copy.ai support multiple languages?

Yes, both support multiple languages. Jasper supports over 30 languages, while Copy.ai supports over 25. The quality of non-English output may vary, but both are adequate for basic translation and content creation.

5. Can I use Jasper or Copy.ai for long-form content?

Yes, both can generate long-form content, but Jasper is better suited for it due to its advanced workflow feature that guides the AI through creating an entire blog post. Copy.ai can also generate long-form, but it may require more manual effort to maintain coherence.

6. What is the difference between Jasper's Creator and Pro plans?

The Creator plan is for individual users and includes 1 user, 1 brand voice, and 50,000 words per month. The Pro plan is for small teams and includes 5 users, 3 brand voices, 150,000 words, and access to SEO mode and workflows.

7. Does Copy.ai have a word limit on its Pro plan?

Copy.ai's Pro plan offers unlimited words under a 'fair use' policy, which means they expect normal usage and may throttle if you abuse it. For most users, this is effectively unlimited.
