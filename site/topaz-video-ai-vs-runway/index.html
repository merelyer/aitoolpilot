Detailed Review: Topaz Video AI

Topaz Video AI has been a staple in the video enhancement space for years. It's a standalone desktop application (Windows and Mac) that uses machine learning models to upscale, deinterlace, denoise, and restore video footage. Unlike many competitors, Topaz focuses solely on video enhancement—no generation, no editing—just making your existing videos look better.

Key Features

  • AI Upscaling: Up to 8K resolution with models trained on thousands of video samples.
  • Denoising & Deblocking: Removes compression artifacts and noise without losing detail.
  • Frame Interpolation: Smooth slow-motion by generating intermediate frames.
  • Stabilization: Reduces shaky footage.
  • Batch Processing: Apply enhancements to multiple clips at once.

Pricing

Topaz Video AI is a one-time purchase of $299. This includes one year of updates. After that, you can continue using the software without updates, or renew for $99/year to keep getting the latest models and features. There's also a free trial available.

Pros

  • Exceptional upscaling quality, especially for older or low-resolution footage.
  • One-time purchase option (no mandatory subscription).
  • Runs locally on your machine—no internet required, no privacy concerns.
  • Regular model updates improve results over time.

Cons

  • Requires a powerful computer (GPU with at least 6GB VRAM recommended).
  • Slow processing times for long videos.
  • Limited to enhancement only—no video generation or editing tools.
  • Interface can be intimidating for beginners.

Detailed Review: Runway

Runway is a cloud-based AI video platform that offers a suite of tools for video generation, editing, and enhancement. It's browser-based, so you don't need a powerful computer. Runway's upscaling feature is just one part of its broader offering, which includes text-to-video, video-to-video, green screen, and more.

Key Features

  • AI Upscaling: Upscale video up to 4K resolution.
  • Text-to-Video: Generate videos from text prompts.
  • Video-to-Video: Transform existing videos with AI styles.
  • Green Screen: Remove backgrounds without a green screen.
  • Inpainting: Remove objects or people from videos.
  • Collaboration: Share projects and work with teams.

Pricing

Runway offers a free tier with limited credits (125 credits one-time). Paid plans start at $12/month (billed annually) for the Starter plan, which includes 625 credits per month. The Pro plan is $28/month (billed annually) with 2,250 credits per month. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros

  • All-in-one platform for video creation and editing.
  • Cloud-based—works on any computer with a browser.
  • Fast processing times (server-side).
  • Regular updates with new AI models.
  • Free tier available to test.

Cons

  • Upscaling quality is not as good as Topaz for restoration.
  • Credit-based system can be confusing and expensive for heavy use.
  • Requires internet connection.
  • Privacy concerns with uploading sensitive footage to the cloud.
  • Subscription only—no perpetual license.

Who Should Use What?

Choosing between Topaz Video AI and Runway depends on your specific needs. Here's a quick guide:

Choose Topaz Video AI if:

  • You need the absolute best upscaling quality for old or low-resolution footage.
  • You prefer a one-time purchase over a subscription.
  • You have a powerful computer and don't mind longer processing times.
  • You work with sensitive footage that you don't want to upload to the cloud.
  • Your primary goal is video restoration and enhancement, not generation.

Choose Runway if:

  • You want an all-in-one AI video toolkit with generation, editing, and upscaling.
  • You don't have a powerful computer and prefer cloud-based processing.
  • You need fast turnaround times and can work within a credit system.
  • You collaborate with a team and need sharing features.
  • You want to experiment with cutting-edge AI video generation.
