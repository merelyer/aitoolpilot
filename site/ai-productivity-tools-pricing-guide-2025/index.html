Detailed Review of Each Option

1. Notion AI

Notion AI is an add-on to the popular Notion workspace. It brings AI-powered writing assistance, summarization, and Q&A directly into your notes and databases. The pricing is straightforward: $8 per member per month when billed annually, or $10 month-to-month. For teams already using Notion, it's a seamless upgrade. The free tier of Notion includes AI trial credits, but full access requires the add-on.

Pros: Deep integration with Notion, excellent for writing and brainstorming, generous free trial.
Cons: Requires Notion subscription for full value, limited automation compared to dedicated PM tools.

2. ClickUp Brain

ClickUp Brain is ClickUp's AI layer that automates task creation, summarizes projects, and answers questions about your work. It costs $7 per member per month (annual) on top of any ClickUp plan. ClickUp itself has a robust free tier, so small teams can start free and add Brain later. The AI is particularly strong for project management—it can generate subtasks, assign owners, and predict timelines.

Pros: Affordable add-on, powerful automation, integrates with all ClickUp features.
Cons: Only useful if you're already in the ClickUp ecosystem, can feel overwhelming for simple tasks.

3. Monday.com AI

Monday.com's AI features are baked into its paid plans, starting at $10 per seat per month (annual) for the Basic plan. AI capabilities include automated workflows, sentiment analysis, and predictive scheduling. The platform is known for its visual interface and ease of use. However, AI is more limited on lower tiers; advanced AI like forecasting requires the Pro plan at $19 per seat per month.

Pros: User-friendly, strong visual project management, AI included in base price.
Cons: AI features are less advanced than dedicated AI tools, higher entry price.

4. Motion

Motion is an AI-powered calendar and task manager that automatically schedules your day based on priorities and deadlines. It's priced at $19 per user per month (annual) or $34 month-to-month. There's no free tier, but a 7-day trial is available. Motion is ideal for busy professionals who struggle with time blocking. The AI is genuinely impressive—it rearranges your schedule in real-time as tasks change.

Pros: Saves hours of manual scheduling, integrates with Google Calendar and Outlook, great for individuals.
Cons: Expensive for teams, no free plan, limited collaboration features.

5. Reclaim.ai

Reclaim.ai focuses on smart calendar scheduling, habit tracking, and task integration. It offers a free plan for individuals with basic features, and paid plans start at $8 per user per month (annual) for the Starter plan. The Pro plan is $12 per user per month. Reclaim is particularly good at defending your focus time and automatically rescheduling conflicts. It integrates with Google Calendar, Slack, and Asana.

Pros: Free tier available, affordable, excellent for calendar optimization.
Cons: Limited to calendar-centric tasks, no native project management.

Who Should Use What

  • Freelancers & solopreneurs: Start with Reclaim.ai's free plan or Notion AI's trial. If you need automated scheduling, Motion is worth the investment.
  • Small teams (2-10): ClickUp Brain offers the best balance of price and features. Notion AI is great if you already use Notion for docs.
  • Growing teams (10-50): Monday.com's Pro plan provides advanced AI and collaboration. Consider ClickUp Brain for automation-heavy workflows.
  • Enterprises: All tools offer custom enterprise plans with SSO, advanced security, and dedicated support. Negotiate based on seat count.

Bottom Line

When it comes to AI productivity tools pricing, there's no one-size-fits-all. Notion AI and ClickUp Brain deliver the most value for teams already in their ecosystems. Motion is a premium choice for scheduling automation, while Reclaim.ai offers a solid free tier for individuals. Evaluate your biggest pain point—writing, task management, or scheduling—and pick the tool that solves it without bloating your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest AI productivity tool in 2025?

Reclaim.ai offers a free plan, and its paid plan starts at $8/month. ClickUp Brain is also affordable at $7/month as an add-on. For free options, Notion's AI trial and ClickUp's free plan are good starting points.

Do I need to pay for AI features separately?

It depends. Notion AI and ClickUp Brain are add-ons to existing paid plans. Monday.com includes AI in its base price. Motion and Reclaim.ai bundle AI into their subscriptions.

Is Motion worth the high price?

If you struggle with time management and scheduling, Motion can save you 5+ hours per week. For professionals billing over $50/hour, it pays for itself quickly. For casual users, it may be overkill.

Can I use these tools for free?

Yes, Reclaim.ai has a free tier, and Notion offers a trial of AI features. ClickUp and Monday.com have free plans without AI. Motion only offers a 7-day trial.

Which AI tool is best for team collaboration?

ClickUp Brain and Monday.com AI are built for teams, with features like task automation, shared calendars, and project insights. Notion AI is also collaborative but more document-focused.

Are there discounts for annual billing?

Yes, most tools offer 20-30% off when you pay annually. For example, Notion AI drops from $10 to $8 per month, and Motion from $34 to $19.
