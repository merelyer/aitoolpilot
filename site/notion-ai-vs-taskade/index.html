Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion needs no introduction. It’s the darling of startups, remote teams, and productivity nerds. The AI add-on (launched in early 2023) supercharges the platform with writing, summarization, and brainstorming capabilities.

Key Features

  • AI Writing & Editing: Generate drafts, rewrite paragraphs, fix grammar, change tone, and translate content directly inside your notes and docs.
  • AI Summarization: Summarize long pages, meeting notes, or even entire databases into bullet points or concise paragraphs.
  • AI Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get instant answers from your own content (e.g., “What were the action items from last sprint?”).
  • Project Management: Kanban boards, timelines, calendars, databases, and custom views. It’s a full-featured project manager.
  • Integrations: Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Figma, Jira, and 500+ via Zapier.

Detailed Review: Taskade

Taskade started as a simple to-do list app but has evolved into a collaborative workspace with an AI twist. It’s particularly strong for visual thinkers and teams that love mind maps and real-time editing.

Key Features

  • AI Assistant: Generate tasks, write project briefs, brainstorm ideas, and create outlines. Also offers AI-powered chat for team discussions.
  • Mind Maps & Outlines: Switch between list, board, calendar, org chart, and mind map views with one click. Great for brainstorming sessions.
  • Real-Time Collaboration: Multiple users can edit simultaneously with live cursors and built-in video chat.
  • Task Management: Assign tasks, set due dates, add dependencies, and track progress with filters.
  • Integrations: Zapier, Google Calendar, Slack, and a few native connectors (fewer than Notion).

Who Should Use What

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You need a central knowledge base for your team (docs, wikis, SOPs).
  • Your workflows involve complex databases with custom properties and formulas.
  • AI summarization and Q&A across your entire workspace is critical.
  • You already use Notion and want to extend its capabilities.

Choose Taskade if:

  • Your team loves visual brainstorming and mind maps.
  • You want a simple, collaborative to-do list with AI assistance for task generation.
  • Real-time editing with built-in video chat is a must-have.
  • You’re on a tight budget and need a generous free plan.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both tools are excellent, but they serve different primary needs. Notion AI is the Swiss Army knife for teams that need deep documentation, powerful databases, and AI that can search across everything. Taskade is the nimble, visual collaborator that gets out of your way and helps you brainstorm and execute quickly.

If you’re building a company wiki, managing complex projects, or need AI to summarize long meeting notes, go with Notion AI. If you want a lightweight, real-time workspace with mind maps and a more affordable AI plan, try Taskade.

My recommendation: Start with a free trial of both. Use Notion AI for documentation and Taskade for brainstorming sessions. But if I had to pick one for a team of 5–50, I’d choose Notion AI for its depth and flexibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than Taskade for project management?

Notion AI offers more advanced project management features like databases, custom views, and formulas, making it better for complex projects. Taskade is simpler and better for lightweight task tracking and visual planning.

2. Does Taskade have an AI writing feature like Notion?

Yes, Taskade has an AI assistant that can generate tasks, write outlines, and brainstorm ideas. However, it lacks the deep AI Q&A and summarization across the entire workspace that Notion AI provides.

3. Which tool is more affordable for a team of 10?

Taskade is generally cheaper. For 10 users on the Pro plan with AI, it’s $50/month (annual). Notion AI with Plus + AI add-on would be $180/month for the same number of users. However, Notion offers more value if you need heavy documentation.

4. Can I use Notion AI and Taskade together?

Absolutely. Many teams use Notion as their knowledge base and Taskade for daily task management and brainstorming. They integrate via Zapier or manually.

5. Which tool has better real-time collaboration?

Taskade wins here. It offers live cursors, built-in video chat, and simultaneous editing that feels smoother than Notion’s real-time features. Notion’s collaboration is good but not as seamless.

6. Does Taskade have a mobile app?

Yes, Taskade has mobile apps for iOS and Android. They are functional for task management and basic editing but less feature-rich than the desktop version. Notion’s mobile app is more mature but can be slow.

7. Can I import data from other tools into Notion AI or Taskade?

Notion supports imports from Evernote, Trello, Asana, Google Docs, and more. Taskade imports from Todoist, Trello, and plain text. Notion has a wider range of import options.
