Detailed Review of Each Option

Fireflies.ai

Overview: Fireflies is a comprehensive AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and analyzes conversations across multiple platforms, including Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and even web conferencing tools like Webex and RingCentral. Its standout feature is the ability to search across all your conversations using natural language, making it a powerful knowledge base.

Key Features:

  • Advanced NLP Search: Ask questions like "What did we decide about the marketing budget?" and get relevant clips.
  • Automatic Summaries: Fireflies generates concise summaries, action items, and key topics after each meeting.
  • CRM Integrations: Syncs with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs to update records automatically.
  • Conversation Intelligence: Tracks talk-to-listen ratio, sentiment, and keywords to provide coaching insights.
  • Custom Vocabulary: Train the AI to recognize industry-specific jargon.

Pricing: Fireflies offers a free plan (up to 800 minutes of transcription), a Pro plan at $10/user/month (billed annually), and a Business plan at $19/user/month with advanced features like custom vocabulary and API access.

Pros:

  • Excellent search functionality—find any conversation in seconds.
  • Wide range of integrations (50+ apps).
  • Generous free tier compared to Otter.
  • Works with multiple conferencing platforms.

Cons:

  • Transcription accuracy can drop with heavy accents or poor audio.
  • The interface can be overwhelming for new users.
  • Some users report occasional sync issues with third-party apps.

Otter.ai

Overview: Otter is one of the pioneers in AI meeting transcription. It's known for its simplicity and reliability, making it a favorite among journalists, students, and small business owners. Otter works primarily with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, and it offers live transcription in real-time.

Key Features:

  • Live Transcription: See captions as the meeting happens—great for accessibility.
  • Automatic Summaries: Otter generates a brief summary and action items after each meeting.
  • Speaker Identification: Distinguishes between different speakers and labels them.
  • Collaboration: Share transcripts and highlight key moments with your team.
  • Otter Assistant: Automatically joins Zoom meetings and takes notes.

Pricing: Otter has a free plan (300 minutes/month), Pro at $8.33/user/month (billed annually) with 1,200 minutes, and Business at $20/user/month with advanced admin controls.

Pros:

  • User-friendly interface—easy for anyone to pick up.
  • Reliable transcription with good accuracy.
  • Live captions are a huge plus for accessibility.
  • Affordable entry point.

Cons:

  • Limited integrations compared to Fireflies.
  • Free tier is restrictive (300 minutes/month).
  • No advanced search like Fireflies' NLP.
  • Can struggle with multiple speakers talking over each other.

Fathom

Overview: Fathom is a free AI meeting assistant that focuses on Zoom and Microsoft Teams. It's designed to be lightweight and unobtrusive, recording meetings, generating summaries, and identifying action items without cluttering your workflow. Fathom is particularly popular among sales teams and consultants.

Key Features:

  • Free Forever: No paywall for core features—unlimited recording and transcription.
  • Automatic Summaries: Get a clean summary with key takeaways and next steps.
  • CRM Integration: Sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, and others to log calls automatically.
  • Highlight Reels: Create short clips of important moments to share.
  • Mobile App: Record in-person meetings or calls on the go.

Pricing: Fathom is free for individuals and small teams. For enterprise features like admin controls and advanced security, there's a paid plan—but pricing is custom (contact sales).

Pros:

  • Completely free for most users—no hidden costs.
  • Clean, intuitive interface with no ads.
  • Fast setup—works seamlessly with Zoom.
  • Great for sales teams with CRM integrations.

Cons:

  • Limited to Zoom and Microsoft Teams (no Google Meet support yet).
  • No advanced search or analytics.
  • Enterprise features require contacting sales—not transparent pricing.
  • Smaller user community compared to Otter and Fireflies.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the best AI meeting assistant?

The best AI meeting assistant depends on your needs. Fireflies.ai excels in search and integrations, Otter.ai is reliable and user-friendly, and Fathom is a great free option for Zoom users. Assess your priorities—budget, features, or platform compatibility—to make the right choice.

2. Is Fireflies.ai better than Otter?

Fireflies.ai offers more advanced features like NLP search and broader integrations, making it better for teams that need to manage large volumes of meeting data. Otter is simpler and more affordable for individual use. If you need robust search and CRM sync, Fireflies is the winner.

3. Can Fathom record Google Meet?

As of now, Fathom does not support Google Meet. It works exclusively with Zoom and Microsoft Teams. If you primarily use Google Meet, consider Fireflies.ai or Otter.ai, which both support it.

4. Are these AI meeting assistants secure?

All three tools take security seriously, offering encryption in transit and at rest, and compliance with GDPR and SOC 2. However, always review their security policies to ensure they meet your organization's requirements, especially for enterprise use.

5. Do these tools work with Microsoft Teams?

Yes, all three support Microsoft Teams to some extent. Fireflies and Otter have native integrations, while Fathom also works with Teams. However, functionality may vary, so check the latest documentation.

6. Which AI meeting assistant is the most affordable?

Fathom is the most affordable for basic use—it's free. Otter's Pro plan starts at $8.33/user/month, and Fireflies' Pro is $10/user/month. For enterprise needs, pricing varies, so contact sales.

7. Can I use these tools for free?

Yes, all three offer free tiers. Fireflies provides 800 minutes/month, Otter gives 300 minutes/month, and Fathom is free with unlimited recording (though some advanced features may be limited). Start with the free tier to test the waters.
