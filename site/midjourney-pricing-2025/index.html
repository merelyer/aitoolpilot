Midjourney Plans and Pricing in 2025

Midjourney offers four subscription tiers, each designed for different usage levels. All plans are billed monthly, and you can cancel anytime. Here's the breakdown:

1. Basic Plan – $10/month

Best for: Hobbyists and casual users who want to experiment with AI art.

  • Fast GPU time: ~3.3 hours/month (200 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited generations (slower queue)
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Member gallery: Access to browse and like images
  • Stealth mode: Not included

Pros: Affordable entry point; unlimited relaxed generations let you create without worrying about credits.

Cons: Limited fast hours; images are public by default (no stealth mode).

2. Standard Plan – $30/month

Best for: Regular users and freelancers who need more fast generations.

  • Fast GPU time: 15 hours/month (900 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Member gallery: Yes
  • Stealth mode: Not included

Pros: Generous fast hours; ideal for those who need quick turnarounds.

Cons: Still no stealth mode; higher price point.

3. Pro Plan – $60/month

Best for: Professionals and small teams who require privacy and more fast hours.

  • Fast GPU time: 30 hours/month (1,800 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Member gallery: Yes
  • Stealth mode: Yes (images are private)

Pros: Stealth mode for confidential work; double the fast hours of Standard.

Cons: Expensive for individuals; overkill for casual users.

4. Mega Plan – $120/month

Best for: Agencies and power users with high-volume needs.

  • Fast GPU time: 60 hours/month (3,600 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Member gallery: Yes
  • Stealth mode: Yes

Pros: Massive fast hours; priority support.

Cons: High cost; only worth it for heavy users.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does Midjourney have a free trial?

No, Midjourney does not offer a free trial. However, the Basic Plan at $10/month is an affordable way to try the service. You can cancel anytime.

2. Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

Yes, all paid plans include commercial usage rights. However, if you're on the Basic or Standard plan, your images are public, so others can see and use them (though they can't claim copyright). For private commercial work, choose the Pro or Mega plan.

3. How many images can I generate with the Basic Plan?

The Basic Plan includes about 3.3 hours of fast GPU time, which translates to roughly 200 image generations. After that, you can continue generating in relaxed mode (unlimited, but slower).

4. What is stealth mode, and do I need it?

Stealth mode makes your images private—they won't appear in the public gallery. It's essential for professionals working on confidential projects. It's only available on the Pro and Mega plans.

5. Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and you'll be prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle.

6. Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney excels at artistic, high-quality images with a distinctive style, while DALL-E 3 (via ChatGPT) is better at following complex prompts and integrating with text generation. For pure aesthetics, Midjourney often wins.
