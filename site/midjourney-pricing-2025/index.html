Midjourney Pricing Plans in 2025: Detailed Breakdown

Midjourney offers four subscription tiers: Basic, Standard, Pro, and Mega. All plans are billed monthly, and you can cancel anytime. Prices are in USD. Note: Midjourney no longer offers a free trial, but you can test the waters with the Basic plan.

1. Basic Plan – $10/month

Best for: Hobbyists and casual users who want to experiment.

  • Fast GPU time: ~3.3 hours per month (200 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Not included
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes, if you meet revenue criteria (see below)
  • Stealth mode: No
  • Gallery access: Yes

Pros: Affordable entry point; great for testing.

Cons: Very limited fast hours; no relaxed mode means you pay per generation after your hours run out.

2. Standard Plan – $30/month

Best for: Regular users and freelancers who need consistent output.

  • Fast GPU time: 15 hours per month (~900 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Stealth mode: No
  • Gallery access: Yes

Pros: Unlimited relaxed mode is a game-changer for experimentation; good balance of cost and output.

Cons: Stealth mode missing, so your creations are public.

3. Pro Plan – $60/month

Best for: Professionals and businesses that need privacy and high volume.

  • Fast GPU time: 30 hours per month (~1,800 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Stealth mode: Yes (images are private)
  • Gallery access: Yes
  • Extra: 15 hours of fast time can be purchased for $15 if needed.

Pros: Stealth mode is crucial for client work; high fast-hour allowance.

Cons: Pricey for casual users.

4. Mega Plan – $120/month

Best for: Agencies, studios, and power users with heavy workloads.

  • Fast GPU time: 60 hours per month (~3,600 generations)
  • Relaxed mode: Unlimited
  • Commercial usage rights: Yes
  • Stealth mode: Yes
  • Gallery access: Yes
  • Extra: Additional fast hours at $15 per 15 hours.

Pros: Massive generation capacity; ideal for teams.

Cons: Overkill for most individuals.

Important note on commercial usage: Midjourney grants commercial usage rights to all paid plans, but if your company earns more than $1 million USD per year, you must subscribe to the Pro or Mega plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does Midjourney have a free trial?

No, Midjourney discontinued its free trial in 2023. The cheapest way to try it is the Basic Plan at $10/month, which you can cancel anytime.

2. Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

Yes, all paid plans include commercial usage rights. However, if your company's annual revenue exceeds $1 million, you must be on the Pro or Mega plan.

3. What happens if I run out of fast GPU hours?

You can continue generating in relaxed mode (if your plan includes it) or purchase additional fast hours at $15 per 15 hours. Basic plan users do not have relaxed mode, so they must buy more fast hours or wait until the next billing cycle.

4. Can I switch plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and you'll be prorated for the difference.

5. Is there a discount for annual billing?

As of 2025, Midjourney only offers monthly billing. No annual discounts are available.

6. How does Midjourney compare to DALL-E 3 in terms of cost?

DALL-E 3 is accessible via ChatGPT Plus for $20/month, which also includes other GPT-4 features. Midjourney starts at $10/month but focuses solely on image generation. For pure image quality, Midjourney often wins; for versatility, DALL-E 3 is a strong contender.
