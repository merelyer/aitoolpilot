Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use AI‑generated images for commercial projects?
A: Yes, but licensing varies. Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, and Leonardo AI provide explicit commercial rights; DALL·E 3’s policy allows commercial use with attribution; open‑source models like Stable Diffusion depend on the model’s license.

Q: Which tool produces the highest resolution images?
A: Midjourney V7 now supports up to 8 K output, while Stable Diffusion XL can generate up to 4 K. Adobe Firefly also offers vector‑based export for infinite scalability.

Q: Do any of these services offer a free trial?
A: All seven provide a free tier or trial credits. Midjourney, DALL·E 3, Stable Diffusion (DreamStudio), and NightCafe let you test before paying.

Q: How fast are the generations?
A: Typical latency is 5‑15 seconds for a 512×512 image on Midjourney and DALL·E 3. Stable Diffusion on DreamStudio averages 8‑12 seconds. Adobe Firefly is slightly slower due to cloud rendering, around 12‑18 seconds.

Q: Can I integrate these generators into my own app?
A: DALL·E 3, Stable Diffusion (via API), and Runway Gen‑2 all provide robust REST APIs. Midjourney currently only offers Discord‑based interaction, which limits direct integration.
