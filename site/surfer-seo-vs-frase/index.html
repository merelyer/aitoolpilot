Detailed Reviews

Surfer SEO: The Data-Driven Powerhouse

Surfer SEO has been a favorite among SEO professionals since its launch in 2017. It focuses on content optimization by analyzing top-ranking pages and providing real-time recommendations to help you outrank them. Unlike Frase, Surfer is not primarily an AI writer—it’s an optimization tool that integrates with various writing platforms.

Key Features

  • Content Score: Surfer’s signature feature gives your content a score from 0 to 100 based on how well it aligns with top-ranking pages. You can see exactly what to improve in real time.
  • SERP Analyzer: Enter a keyword, and Surfer pulls data from the top 20 results, including word count, headings, images, and readability. This helps you understand what Google wants.
  • Content Editor: The editor integrates with Google Docs and WordPress, allowing you to optimize as you write. It provides suggestions for keywords, headings, and structure.
  • AI Writing (Surfer AI): Surfer introduced its own AI writer that generates full articles based on your chosen keyword and settings. It’s not as advanced as Frase’s, but it’s improving.
  • Audit: Analyze existing content and get actionable recommendations to improve rankings.
  • Integrations: Works with WordPress, Google Docs, Jasper, Copy.ai, and more.

Pricing

  • Essential: $79/month (billed annually) – 30 articles/month, 5,000 SERP queries, 1 user.
  • Advanced: $159/month – 100 articles/month, 20,000 SERP queries, 5 users.
  • Max: $299/month – 200 articles/month, 50,000 SERP queries, 10 users.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing – unlimited everything.

Note: Prices are based on annual billing; monthly billing is higher.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Accurate content scoring, extensive SERP data, real-time optimization, great for scaling content, strong integrations.
  • Cons: No free tier (only 7-day trial), AI writer is basic compared to Frase, can be expensive for small businesses.

Frase: The All-in-One AI Writing & Optimization Tool

Frase started as a content brief generator and has evolved into a full-fledged AI writing tool. It’s designed to help you research, write, and optimize content in one place. Frase is often praised for its affordability and user-friendly interface.

Key Features

  • AI Writer: Frase’s AI can generate entire articles, paragraphs, or outlines based on your input. It’s more advanced than Surfer’s built-in AI.
  • Content Briefs: Generate comprehensive briefs that include recommended headings, questions to answer, and relevant statistics.
  • Optimization: Similar to Surfer, Frase provides a content score and suggestions for improving your article.
  • Research: Frase aggregates data from top-ranking pages to help you understand what to include.
  • Integrations: Works with Google Docs, WordPress, and several other platforms.

Pricing

  • Solo: $15/month – 4 articles/month, 10,000 words, 1 user.
  • Basic: $30/month – 30 articles/month, 50,000 words, 1 user.
  • Team: $115/month – 100 articles/month, 200,000 words, 3 users.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing – unlimited articles, 10+ users.

Note: Prices are based on annual billing; monthly billing is higher.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Affordable entry-level plan, excellent AI writer, user-friendly, includes content brief generation.
  • Cons: Content scoring is less accurate than Surfer’s, limited SERP data, no free tier (only 7-day trial).
