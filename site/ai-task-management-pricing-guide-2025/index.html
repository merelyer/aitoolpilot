Detailed Reviews of Each Tool

1. Motion – Best for Auto-Scheduling

Motion is a game-changer if you struggle with time blocking. Its AI automatically schedules tasks based on deadlines, priority, and your calendar availability. It even reschedules when things slip.

Pricing:

  • Individual: $19/month (billed monthly) or $12/month (annual)
  • Team: $12/user/month (annual)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing

Pros:

  • Truly intelligent auto-scheduling
  • Integrates with Google Calendar, Outlook, Zoom
  • Gantt chart view for projects

Cons:

  • No free tier (only 7-day trial)
  • Steep learning curve for new users
  • Limited integrations compared to ClickUp

2. ClickUp Brain – Best All-in-One

ClickUp Brain is an AI assistant that lives inside ClickUp. It can write task descriptions, summarize comments, generate status updates, and even answer questions about your projects.

Pricing:

  • Free Forever: 100MB storage, limited AI
  • Unlimited: $7/month per user (includes ClickUp Brain add-on for $5/user/month)
  • Business: $12/user/month
  • Enterprise: Custom

Pros:

  • Extremely feature-rich (docs, whiteboards, goals)
  • AI is context-aware within your workspace
  • Generous free tier

Cons:

  • Interface can feel bloated
  • AI add-on costs extra
  • Occasional performance lag

3. Notion AI – Best for Creators

Notion AI is an add-on to Notion’s workspace. It helps you draft, summarize, edit, and brainstorm—directly inside your notes and databases. It’s less about task scheduling and more about content creation and knowledge management.

Pricing:

  • Free: Basic features
  • Plus: $10/month per user
  • Business: $15/month per user
  • AI add-on: $10/month per user (on top of plan)

Pros:

  • Excellent for writing and brainstorming
  • Flexible database and project views
  • Strong community templates

Cons:

  • Not a dedicated task manager
  • AI is an extra cost
  • No native time tracking or Gantt charts

4. Asana Intelligence – Best for Enterprise

Asana Intelligence uses AI to predict timelines, suggest task assignments, and automate workflows. It’s built for complex, multi-team projects.

Pricing:

  • Personal: Free (limited AI)
  • Starter: $10.99/month per user
  • Advanced: $15.99/month per user
  • Enterprise: Custom

Pros:

  • Powerful workflow automation
  • AI-driven timeline predictions
  • Excellent reporting dashboards

Cons:

  • Expensive for small teams
  • AI features still maturing
  • Steep learning curve

5. Todoist Premium – Best Budget Option

Todoist’s AI is called “Smart Schedule.” It suggests the best times to complete tasks based on your habits and deadlines. Simple, effective, and cheap.

Pricing:

  • Free: Basic features
  • Premium: $4/month (annual) or $5/month (monthly)
  • Business: $6/month per user

Pros:

  • Extremely affordable
  • Fast and minimalist interface
  • Natural language input

Cons:

  • Limited AI capabilities (no generative AI)
  • No Gantt charts or dependencies
  • Not ideal for complex projects

6. Any.do AI – Best for Simplicity

Any.do offers an AI assistant that helps you organize daily tasks, set reminders, and even suggests when to focus. It’s lightweight and mobile-first.

Pricing:

  • Free: Basic tasks
  • Premium: $3/month (annual) or $5.99/month (monthly)
  • Teams: $5/user/month

Pros:

  • Very easy to use
  • Good mobile experience
  • Affordable

Cons:

  • Limited project management features
  • AI is basic (smart suggestions only)
  • No integrations with complex tools like Jira

Who Should Use What?

Freelancers & Solo Professionals: Go with Todoist Premium or Any.do AI for affordability and simplicity. If you need heavy scheduling, Motion is worth the investment.

Small to Mid-Sized Teams (5–50 people): ClickUp Brain gives you the most bang for your buck with its all-in-one platform. Motion is also excellent if auto-scheduling is a priority.

Enterprise & Large Teams: Asana Intelligence is built for scale. ClickUp Brain is also viable, but Asana’s workflow automation is more robust for complex orgs.

Content Creators & Writers: Notion AI is your best friend for drafting, summarization, and brainstorming. Pair it with Todoist for task tracking.

The Bottom Line

There’s no one-size-fits-all answer for AI task management pricing. Your choice depends on your budget, team size, and the depth of AI you need. For most professionals, Motion offers the best balance of intelligent scheduling and reasonable pricing. If you’re on a tight budget, Todoist Premium punches way above its weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest AI task management software?

Any.do AI starts at $3/month (annual) and Todoist Premium at $4/month. Both offer basic AI features like smart scheduling and reminders.

Does ClickUp have a free AI feature?

Yes, ClickUp’s free plan includes limited AI functionality (e.g., AI-powered task descriptions). Full ClickUp Brain access requires the add-on ($5/user/month) on a paid plan.

Is Motion worth the price for individuals?

If you struggle with time management and often miss deadlines, Motion’s auto-scheduling can save you hours weekly. At $19/month, it’s a solid investment for busy professionals.

Can I use Notion AI without a paid plan?

No, Notion AI is an add-on that costs $10/month per user on top of a paid Notion plan (starting at $10/month per user for Plus). The free plan does not include AI features.

Which AI task manager integrates best with Google Calendar?

Motion offers the deepest Google Calendar integration, automatically blocking time for tasks and rescheduling conflicts. Todoist also syncs well but doesn’t auto-schedule.

Are there any free AI task management tools with no limits?

Most free tiers have limitations. ClickUp’s free plan is the most generous (100MB storage, unlimited tasks) but AI features are restricted. Todoist free is good for basic task management but lacks smart scheduling.
