Midjourney Review: The Artist's Choice

Midjourney has long been the gold standard for AI-generated art. Version 6 (and the newer 6.1) pushed the boundaries even further, delivering images that often look like they were crafted by a professional illustrator. But it's not without quirks.

Key Features

  • Exceptional aesthetic quality: Midjourney excels at lighting, composition, and texture. Its default style is more "artistic" and less literal than DALL-E's.
  • Style referencing: You can upload reference images to guide the style, mood, or color palette of your generations.
  • Character consistency: The --cref parameter lets you maintain a consistent character across multiple images—a game-changer for comic artists and storytellers.
  • Inpainting & outpainting: Midjourney's "Vary (Region)" and "Pan" features let you edit specific parts of an image or extend the canvas.
  • Discord-based (and web alpha): Originally Discord-only, Midjourney now offers a web interface in alpha, making it more accessible.

Pricing

Midjourney starts at $10/month for the Basic plan (about 200 generations). The Standard plan ($30/month) gives you unlimited relaxed generations and 15 hours of fast GPU time. Pro ($60/month) and Mega ($120/month) plans add stealth mode and more fast hours. There's no free tier, but you can test the waters with a $10 subscription.

Pros

  • Unmatched artistic quality and detail
  • Powerful style and character controls
  • Active community and frequent updates
  • Web interface now available (alpha)

Cons

  • No free tier
  • Steeper learning curve (prompt engineering required)
  • Discord dependency (though web is improving)
  • Less accurate at following complex, literal prompts than DALL-E 4

DALL-E 4 Review: The Accessible Powerhouse

DALL-E 4 is OpenAI's latest image generator, integrated directly into ChatGPT (Plus and Enterprise) and Microsoft Copilot. It's designed to be intuitive, safe, and remarkably good at following detailed instructions. While it may not always match Midjourney's artistic flair, it's catching up fast.

Key Features

  • Superior prompt adherence: DALL-E 4 understands complex, multi-part prompts better than ever. You can specify exact compositions, text rendering, and spatial relationships.
  • Text rendering: It can generate legible text within images—a huge win for logos, posters, and memes.
  • ChatGPT integration: You can refine images conversationally. Ask ChatGPT to "make the background bluer" or "add a cat," and it will update the image.
  • Safety and moderation: Strong filters prevent harmful or copyrighted content (though sometimes overly restrictive).
  • Free tier via Bing/Copilot: You can use DALL-E 4 for free through Microsoft Copilot (with limits).

Pricing

DALL-E 4 is available with a ChatGPT Plus subscription ($20/month), which also gives you access to GPT-4, browsing, and more. For free users, Microsoft Copilot offers limited DALL-E 4 generations. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros

  • Free tier available (via Copilot)
  • Extremely user-friendly—no prompt engineering needed
  • Excellent at following detailed instructions
  • Seamless integration with ChatGPT for iterative editing

Cons

  • Image quality can be less "artistic" than Midjourney
  • Strict content filters may block legitimate prompts
  • No advanced features like style referencing or character consistency (yet)
  • Requires ChatGPT Plus for full access

Who Should Use What?

Choose Midjourney if you:

  • Are a digital artist, illustrator, or designer who needs portfolio-quality images.
  • Want fine control over style, lighting, and composition.
  • Need character consistency for comics or storyboards.
  • Don't mind spending a few minutes learning prompt syntax.

Choose DALL-E 4 if you:

  • Are new to AI image generation and want a simple, conversational interface.
  • Need images that accurately reflect specific details (e.g., "a red car on a bridge at sunset with text 'Sale' in the corner").
  • Already use ChatGPT and want image generation without switching apps.
  • Are on a tight budget—free access via Copilot is a huge plus.

Bottom Line: Which Is the Best AI Image Generator in 2025?

There's no one-size-fits-all answer, but if we had to pick one, Midjourney wins for pure image quality and artistic control. Its output is consistently more polished and visually striking, making it the go-to for professionals. That said, DALL-E 4 is the better choice for beginners, marketers, and anyone who values ease of use and accurate prompt following. The free tier via Copilot makes it incredibly accessible.

Ultimately, your choice depends on your priorities: if you want to create art, go with Midjourney. If you want to get things done quickly and accurately, DALL-E 4 is your best bet.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 4?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney generally produces more aesthetically pleasing, artistic images with better lighting and composition. DALL-E 4 is better at following precise instructions and rendering text. For professional art, Midjourney is often preferred; for quick, accurate visuals, DALL-E 4 is superior.

2. Can I use DALL-E 4 for free?

Yes, you can access DALL-E 4 for free through Microsoft Copilot (with some limitations on generations per day). For unlimited access and faster generation, you'll need a ChatGPT Plus subscription ($20/month).

3. Does Midjourney have a free trial?

No, Midjourney does not offer a free trial. The cheapest plan is $10/month for the Basic tier, which includes about 200 generations. You can cancel anytime.

4. Which tool is better for generating text in images?

DALL-E 4 is significantly better at rendering legible text within images. Midjourney has improved but still struggles with longer words or complex typography.

5. Can I use these images commercially?

Yes, both Midjourney and DALL-E 4 allow commercial use of generated images, but there are caveats. Midjourney requires a paid subscription, and DALL-E 4's terms depend on your plan (e.g., ChatGPT Plus). Always check the latest terms of service.

6. Which is easier to use: Midjourney or DALL-E 4?

DALL-E 4 is much easier for beginners because it's integrated into ChatGPT and uses natural language. Midjourney requires learning specific parameters and uses Discord (though a web interface is now in alpha).
