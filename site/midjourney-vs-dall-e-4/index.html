DALL-E 4 Review: The Versatile Integrator

DALL-E 4, developed by OpenAI, is the latest iteration of the popular image generator. It builds on DALL-E 3's strengths—like precise prompt adherence and seamless integration with ChatGPT—and adds improved resolution, faster generation, and better handling of complex scenes. If you're already using ChatGPT, DALL-E 4 feels like a natural extension of your workflow.

Key Features

  • ChatGPT Integration: Generate images directly within ChatGPT conversations.
  • Precise Prompt Adherence: DALL-E 4 excels at following detailed instructions.
  • Inpainting and Outpainting: Edit images by describing changes in natural language.
  • Safety and Ethics: Robust content filters and watermarking for responsible AI use.
  • API Access: Developers can integrate DALL-E 4 into their own applications.

Pricing

DALL-E 4 is available through ChatGPT Plus and OpenAI's API:

  • ChatGPT Plus: $20/month – includes DALL-E 4 access with usage limits

  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for high-volume API usage

There's no standalone free tier, but you can try DALL-E 4 with a free ChatGPT account (limited generations).

FAQ

Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 4?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney produces more artistic and stylized images, while DALL-E 4 excels at following precise prompts and integrating with ChatGPT. For pure artistry, Midjourney wins; for versatility, DALL-E 4 is better.

Can I use Midjourney or DALL-E 4 for free?

Midjourney has no free tier, but DALL-E 4 offers limited free generations through a free ChatGPT account. For regular use, you'll need a paid subscription.

Which tool is easier to use?

DALL-E 4 is generally easier for beginners, thanks to its intuitive ChatGPT interface. Midjourney requires learning Discord commands and has a steeper learning curve.

Do both tools allow commercial use?

Yes, both Midjourney and DALL-E 4 grant commercial usage rights to paying subscribers, though DALL-E 4's terms may have restrictions on certain types of content.

Can I edit images with Midjourney or DALL-E 4?

Both offer editing capabilities. Midjourney has upscaling and variations, while DALL-E 4 supports inpainting and outpainting via natural language.

Which tool has better resolution?

Both produce high-resolution images. Midjourney's upscalers can achieve print-quality resolution, while DALL-E 4 offers up to 1024x1024 natively, with upscaling available.
