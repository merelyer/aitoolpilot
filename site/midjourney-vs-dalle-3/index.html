Midjourney – In‑Depth Review

Key Features (2026)

  • Discord‑first workflow with real‑time preview.
  • Up to 8K resolution for Pro tier.
  • Style‑presets (e.g., "cinematic", "illustration", "pixel art").
  • Community prompt library and public galleries.
  • Commercial usage rights on all paid plans.

Pricing

Midjourney operates on a subscription model. Prices are listed in USD and billed monthly.

  • Basic: $10 / month – 200 GPU minutes, up to 1024×1024 px.
  • Standard: $30 / month – 15 hours GPU, up to 4K output, commercial license.
  • Pro: $60 / month – Unlimited GPU, up to 8K, priority queue.

Pros

  • Exceptional stylization – artists love the "artistic flair".
  • Fast community feedback via Discord.
  • High‑resolution outputs suitable for print.

Cons

  • Steeper learning curve for non‑Discord users.
  • Limited free trial (only 25 minutes).
  • Occasional queue delays during peak hours.

DALL·E 3 – In‑Depth Review

Key Features (2026)

  • Integrated directly into OpenAI platform and Microsoft Designer.
  • Text‑to‑image with built‑in safety filters.
  • Inpainting and outpainting for editing existing images.
  • Unlimited aspect ratios up to 3:2.
  • Commercial rights included with paid credits.

Pricing

DALL·E 3 uses a credit‑based system. One credit generates one image (standard resolution) or one edit.

  • Free Tier: 15 credits/month (good for testing).
  • Starter: $15 / month for 115 credits.
  • Pro: $30 / month for 250 credits.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing, bulk credits, API access.

Pros

  • Very intuitive web UI – no Discord needed.
  • Strong safety system reduces policy‑violating outputs.
  • Excellent for clean, realistic illustrations and UI mockups.

Cons

  • Resolution capped at 1024×1024 for standard credits (higher needs extra credits).
  • Less stylized “artistic” output compared to Midjourney.
  • Credit consumption can add up for heavy users.
