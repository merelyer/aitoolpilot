Detailed Review of Each AI Writing Tool

Jasper

Overview: Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is a powerhouse in the AI writing space, known for its high-quality output and extensive template library. It’s designed for marketing teams and businesses that need to produce a high volume of content quickly.

Pricing: Jasper offers a 7-day free trial, but no free plan. The Boss Mode plan starts at $39/month (billed annually) for 50,000 words, and the Business plan is custom-priced.

Features: 50+ templates, Boss Mode for long-form content, SEO mode (Surfer SEO integration), plagiarism checker, and team collaboration.

Pros:

  • Exceptional output quality, especially for long-form content.
  • Wide range of templates for various use cases.
  • Strong integration with SEO tools.

Cons:

  • No free plan; relatively expensive for individuals.
  • Word limits can be restrictive for high-volume users.

Copy.ai

Overview: Copy.ai is a popular choice for freelancers and small businesses looking for an affordable yet capable AI writer. It focuses on short-form copy but has expanded to support long-form content.

Pricing: Free plan available (limited to 2,000 words/month). Pro plan at $49/month (or $36/month annually) for unlimited words and premium features.

Features: 90+ templates, brand voice, workflow automation, and team collaboration.

Pros:

  • Generous free plan for testing.
  • Unlimited words on the Pro plan.
  • User-friendly interface.

Cons:

  • Output quality can be inconsistent for long-form content.
  • Fewer advanced features compared to Jasper.

Writesonic

Overview: Writesonic is an AI writer that emphasizes SEO and affordability. It’s a great option for bloggers and content marketers who need to produce SEO-friendly articles quickly.

Pricing: Free trial with 25 credits. Paid plans start at $19/month (billed annually) for 50,000 words, up to $99/month for 200,000 words.

Features: Article writer, SEO checker, bulk content generation, and chatbot integration.

Pros:

  • Affordable entry-level pricing.
  • Built-in SEO tools.
  • Good for generating articles from keywords.

Cons:

  • Credit system can be confusing.
  • Output may require more editing than competitors.

Rytr

Overview: Rytr is a budget-friendly AI writing tool that’s perfect for beginners and those who need basic content generation without breaking the bank.

Pricing: Free plan with 10,000 characters/month. Unlimited plan at $9/month (or $7.50/month annually).

Features: 40+ use cases, 20+ tones, and a Chrome extension.

Pros:

  • Extremely affordable.
  • Simple and easy to use.
  • Good for short-form content.

Cons:

  • Limited features for advanced users.
  • Output quality is decent but not top-tier.

Anyword

Overview: Anyword is a data-driven AI writing assistant that focuses on performance marketing. It uses predictive analytics to help you create copy that converts.

Pricing: No free plan, but a 7-day trial. Starter plan at $39/month (billed annually) for 10,000 words, Pro at $79/month for 30,000 words.

Features: Predictive performance scores, audience targeting, and A/B testing suggestions.

Pros:

  • Unique predictive analytics for copy optimization.
  • Great for ad copy and landing pages.

Cons:

  • Expensive for the word count.
  • Limited long-form capabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the cheapest AI writing tool in 2025?

Rytr is the most affordable, with a free plan and an unlimited plan at just $9/month. However, it lacks advanced features and may not suit professional needs.

2. Do AI writing tools offer free trials?

Yes, most do. Jasper, Anyword, and Writesonic offer free trials (typically 7 days or limited credits). Copy.ai and Rytr have free forever plans with usage limits.

3. Can I use AI writing tools for commercial purposes?

Generally, yes—but check the terms of service. Most paid plans grant you full commercial rights to the content you generate. Free plans may have restrictions.

4. How do word limits work? Are they monthly?

Word limits are usually monthly. For example, Jasper’s Boss Mode gives you 50,000 words per month. Some tools use a credit system where 1 credit equals 1 word or character.

5. Is Jasper worth the price compared to cheaper alternatives?

If you need high-quality long-form content and team collaboration, yes. Jasper’s output is consistently better, and the time saved often justifies the cost. For basic needs, cheaper tools suffice.

6. What happens if I exceed my word limit?

You’ll typically need to upgrade your plan or purchase add-on credits. Some tools throttle your usage until the next billing cycle.
