Detailed Review: Midjourney

Midjourney remains the benchmark for aesthetic quality. Its latest V6 and V7 alpha models produce photorealistic and painterly images that often require little to no post-processing. The big caveat? You need Discord to use it (though a web interface is rolling out to subscribers).

Key Features

  • Unmatched Aesthetic Quality: Midjourney's default output is consistently beautiful — great for mood boards, key art, and cinematic stills.
  • Style Reference (--sref): Lock in a specific visual style across hundreds of generations.
  • Pan & Zoom: Outpaint seamlessly in any direction to extend your image beyond its original canvas.
  • Remix Mode: Alter prompts while preserving composition — perfect for iterative exploration.
  • Upscaling & Variations: Native upscaling to 4K with multiple variation modes.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Leonardo AI If…

  • You need precise control over composition (pose, depth, edges).
  • You create game assets, UI mockups, or product renders that require consistency.
  • You want to train your own model on your unique art style.
  • You're on a budget and need a genuinely useful free tier.
  • You want a web-based platform without Discord.

Choose Midjourney If…

  • You prioritize out-of-the-box beauty and don't want to fiddle with settings.
  • You're a concept artist or filmmaker looking for cinematic keyframes.
  • You thrive in a community-driven Discord environment (and don't mind the noise).
  • You need consistent stylistic output via --sref for mood boards.
  • You're okay paying a premium for zero learning curve.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Leonardo AI better than Midjourney?

For most use cases in 2025, yes — Leonardo AI offers more control, a free tier, and model training capabilities. Midjourney still produces more aesthetically pleasing images by default, but Leonardo's flexibility makes it a better long-term investment for professionals.

Can I use Leonardo AI for free?

Yes, Leonardo AI offers a free plan with 150 tokens per day, which is roughly 150 standard generations. This is more generous than any paid Midjourney tier's trial period.

Does Midjourney have a free trial?

Midjourney occasionally offers a limited free trial (usually 25 images) for new users, but it's not guaranteed. As of 2025, the platform generally requires a paid subscription starting at $10/month.

Which AI image generator is best for commercial use?

Both platforms allow commercial use on paid plans. However, Leonardo AI's free tier also grants commercial rights for personal projects, while Midjourney requires at least a Basic paid plan for commercial usage.

Can I train my own model in Midjourney?

No, Midjourney does not offer custom model training. Leonardo AI allows you to train a custom model on your own dataset, which is a major advantage for artists with a distinctive style.

Which tool is better for game asset creation?

Leonardo AI is the clear winner for game assets due to its ControlNet support, consistent character feature, and transparent licensing. Midjourney's outputs are beautiful but harder to keep consistent across a set of assets.
