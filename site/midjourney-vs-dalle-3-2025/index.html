Detailed Review: Midjourney

What Is Midjourney?

Midjourney is a generative AI tool that creates images from text prompts. It operates through Discord (and now a web app), offering a highly interactive experience where you can iterate, remix, and refine images. It's known for its artistic, painterly style and strong community.

Key Features (2025)

  • Version 6.5 engine — improved coherence, lighting, and detail
  • Style customization — parameters like --ar, --stylize, --chaos, --v
  • Upscaling & inpainting — edit specific areas after generation
  • Image-to-image — upload a base image and remix it
  • Private mode (Pro plan) — keep your generations hidden
  • Community gallery — endless inspiration

Pricing (2025)

  • Basic Plan: $10/month — 3.3 hours of GPU time, commercial use
  • Standard Plan: $30/month — 15 hours, parallel jobs
  • Pro Plan: $60/month — 30 hours, private mode, fast GPU
  • Mega Plan: $120/month — 60 hours, priority support

Pros

  • Stunning artistic quality with rich textures and lighting
  • Highly customizable with parameters
  • Active community for learning and inspiration
  • Strong style diversity (from anime to oil painting)

Cons

  • Steep learning curve (Discord interface, prompt engineering)
  • No free tier — paid plans only

  • Less photorealistic than DALL-E 3 in many cases

Detailed Review: DALL-E 3

What Is DALL-E 3?

DALL-E 3 is OpenAI's latest image generation model, integrated into ChatGPT Plus and available via API. It focuses on understanding complex prompts and producing accurate, high-fidelity images—especially photorealism and text.

Key Features (2025)

  • ChatGPT integration — generate images directly in chat conversations
  • Superior prompt adherence — understands detailed instructions
  • Strong text rendering — generates readable words and logos
  • Inpainting & outpainting — edit or expand images
  • Safety filters — blocks harmful or copyrighted content
  • Multiple image variants per prompt

Pricing (2025)

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3 in 2025?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney produces more artistic and stylized images, while DALL-E 3 excels in photorealism and text accuracy. For creative projects, Midjourney is better; for practical, realistic images, DALL-E 3 wins.

2. Which AI image generator is cheaper?

DALL-E 3 is cheaper to start—there's a free tier via ChatGPT, and the Plus plan is $20/month. Midjourney starts at $10/month but offers more value for heavy users. For occasional use, DALL-E 3 is more affordable.

3. Can I use Midjourney and DALL-E 3 commercially?

Yes, both allow commercial use of generated images. Midjourney's free tier doesn't exist, but all paid plans include commercial rights. DALL-E 3 images generated via ChatGPT Plus or API are also free to use commercially, though OpenAI's content policy may restrict certain uses.

4. Which one generates better text in images?

DALL-E 3 is significantly better at rendering readable text. Midjourney often produces garbled or misspelled words. If you need logos, posters, or infographics with text, choose DALL-E 3.

5. Do I need to know how to write prompts to use these tools?

Both are designed for natural language prompts, but Midjourney benefits from learning its parameter syntax (e.g., --ar 16:9, --v 6). DALL-E 3 is more forgiving—you can just describe what you want in plain English.

6. Which AI image generator has the best community?

Midjourney has a very active and supportive community on Discord, with thousands sharing prompts and techniques. DALL-E 3's community is smaller and spread across ChatGPT forums and social media.

7. Can I edit images after generating them?

Yes, both support inpainting (editing parts of an image). Midjourney's inpainting is more advanced and allows precise region selection. DALL-E 3's editing is simpler but effective for basic adjustments.
