Detailed Reviews

1. Notion AI

Features: AI‑generated drafts, task suggestions, knowledge‑base search, integration with 50+ apps, real‑time collaboration.

Pricing: Free tier (up to 1,000 blocks). Paid plans start at $8 per user per month (Pro) and $20 per user per month (Enterprise).

  • Pros: Unified workspace, powerful AI writer, excellent for docs + databases.
  • Cons: Learning curve for database features; AI suggestions can be generic if context is thin.

2. ClickUp AI

Features: AI task creation from natural language, smart reminders, docs, whiteboards, time‑tracking.

Pricing: Free Forever plan. Paid starts at $5 per user/mo (Unlimited) and $19 per user/mo (Business).

  • Pros: Very customizable, AI reduces manual entry.
  • Cons: UI can feel cluttered; AI module is an add‑on for some plans.

3. Microsoft Copilot for Teams

Features: AI summarises chats, generates meeting agendas, integrates with Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams.

Pricing: Included with Microsoft 365 E5 or $15 per user/mo as an add‑on.

  • Pros: Deep Office integration, enterprise‑grade security.
  • Cons: Expensive for small teams; limited outside Microsoft ecosystem.

4. Monday.com AI

Features: AI‑driven project timelines, workload balancing, automations library.

Pricing: Basic $8/user/mo, Standard $10, Pro $16, Enterprise custom.

  • Pros: Visual project boards, strong automation library.
  • Cons: AI features locked behind Pro tier.

5. Asana AI

Features: AI task‑assignment, predictive due‑dates, natural‑language project creation.

Pricing: Free for up to 15 members. Premium $10.99/user/mo, Business $24.99, Enterprise custom.

  • Pros: Clean UI, solid integration ecosystem.
  • Cons: AI still in beta; limited reporting.

6. Zapier AI

Features: AI‑suggested Zaps, natural‑language trigger creation, multi‑step automation.

Pricing: Free 100 tasks/mo. Starter $19.99/mo, Professional $49, Team $299, Company custom.

  • Pros: Connects 5,000+ apps, AI speeds Zap building.
  • Cons: No native collaboration view; pricing jumps quickly.

7. Figma AI

Features: AI design suggestions, auto‑layout, content generation for mockups.

Pricing: Free starter. Professional $12/mo/editor, Organization $45/mo/editor.

  • Pros: Real‑time design collaboration, AI speeds prototyping.
  • Cons: AI features still experimental; heavy on bandwidth.

8. Grammarly Business

Features: AI grammar, tone, style suggestions, plagiarism detection, team style guide.

Pricing: $12.50 per user/mo (annual).

  • Pros: Improves written communication, easy browser extension.
  • Cons: Not a full‑project tool; focuses only on text.

9. Otter.ai

Features: Live transcription, AI summary, searchable meeting notes, integration with Zoom and Teams.

Pricing: Free 600 mins/mo. Pro $8.33/mo, Business $20/mo.

  • Pros: Accurate transcription, great for remote stand‑ups.
  • Cons: Audio quality heavily impacts accuracy.

10. Superhuman AI

Features: AI‑crafted email replies, inbox triage, read‑later, keyboard‑first workflow.

Pricing: $30 per user/mo (annual).

  • Pros: Lightning‑fast email handling, AI saves time.
  • Cons: High price; only works with Gmail/Google Workspace.

Bottom Line Verdict

For most remote teams looking for a versatile, cost‑effective solution, Notion AI remains the best overall pick. It combines note‑taking, project tracking, and AI‑assisted writing in a single interface, and its free tier lets you test the waters before committing. If your team is heavily task‑oriented, ClickUp AI offers deeper customization, while Microsoft Copilot is unbeatable for enterprises already locked into the Microsoft ecosystem.
