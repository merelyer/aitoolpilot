Detailed Review of Notion AI

What is Notion AI?

Notion has been a household name in productivity for years, and with the addition of AI, it's become even more powerful. Notion AI isn't a separate app—it's an integrated feature that works across your workspace. You can use it to draft content, summarize meeting notes, generate action items, and even answer questions about your notes. It's like having a smart assistant that knows everything you've written.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistance: Generate drafts, improve writing, fix grammar, and change tone directly in your documents.
  • Q&A with AI: Ask questions about your workspace, and the AI pulls answers from your notes, databases, and pages.
  • Summarization: Quickly summarize long documents, meeting notes, or articles into bullet points.
  • Auto-Fill and Autocomplete: Speed up data entry with AI suggestions for databases and repetitive tasks.
  • Integrations: Works with Slack, Google Drive, and 100+ other apps to bring context into your notes.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan for personal use, but AI is only available on paid plans. Currently, AI is included in the Plus plan ($10/user/month) and Business plan ($20/user/month) as an add-on, but note that pricing may vary. For 2025, the AI add-on is typically $8–$10 per user/month when billed annually, but some plans bundle it. Always check the official pricing page for the latest.

Detailed Review of Mem

What is Mem?

Mem is a self-proclaimed 'self-organizing' note-taking app that uses AI to automatically tag, link, and surface your notes when you need them. It's designed for knowledge workers who capture a lot of information and want to find it effortlessly. Instead of forcing you to create folders and tags, Mem's AI does the heavy lifting in the background.

Key Features

  • Automatic Organization: Notes are automatically linked and tagged based on content and context.
  • AI Chat: Chat with your notes—ask questions, get summaries, and even generate new content.
  • Related Notes: See a sidebar of related notes as you type, helping you connect ideas.
  • Quick Capture: Use the Mem app or browser extension to capture ideas instantly.
  • Integrations: Connect with Slack, GitHub, and other tools to bring in context.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. The Pro plan starts at $14.99/month (billed annually) and includes unlimited AI, more integrations, and advanced features. There's also a Team plan at $24.99/user/month for collaboration.

FAQ

1. Is Notion AI worth it in 2025?

Yes, if you're already using Notion, the AI add-on is a valuable upgrade. It saves time on drafting, summarizing, and finding information. For new users, the Plus plan includes AI, making it a good deal for the feature set.

2. Can Mem replace Notion entirely?

It depends on your needs. Mem is not a project management tool—it's focused on note-taking and knowledge management. If you rely on databases and task tracking, Notion is better. But for pure note-taking, Mem can be a great replacement.

3. Which app has better AI features?

Notion AI is more versatile because it works across documents, databases, and can perform actions like creating tables. Mem's AI is more focused on connecting and retrieving information. If you want a general-purpose AI assistant, choose Notion; if you want a smart search and organization, Mem is superior.

4. Are there any free alternatives?

Yes, both offer free tiers. Notion's free plan is generous but limits AI usage. Mem's free plan includes basic AI but restricts the number of notes. For heavy AI use, you'll need a paid plan.

5. Which is easier to learn: Notion AI or Mem?

Mem is easier to learn because it's simpler. Notion has a steep learning curve due to its many features. If you're a beginner, start with Mem; if you're willing to invest time, Notion offers more power.

6. Can I use these apps on mobile?

Both have mobile apps for iOS and Android. Notion's mobile app is robust but can be slow with large databases. Mem's mobile app is fast and great for quick capture.

7. Which is better for students?

Notion AI is better for students because it can organize class notes, create study schedules, and collaborate on group projects. Mem is better for individual research and idea linking.
