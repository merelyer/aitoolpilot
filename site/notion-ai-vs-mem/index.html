Notion AI: The AI-Powered All-in-One Workspace

Notion started as a flexible note-taking and project management tool and has evolved into a full-fledged workspace. In 2023, Notion introduced Notion AI, a suite of AI features that can write, summarize, translate, and answer questions based on your notes.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, rewrite text, fix grammar, and change tone directly in your Notion pages.
  • Summarization: Instantly summarize long documents or meeting notes.
  • Q&A: Ask questions and get answers based on your workspace content (available in Notion AI).
  • Autofill: Automatically populate database properties using AI.
  • Translation: Translate content into multiple languages.
  • Integration: Works seamlessly with Notion's databases, pages, and blocks.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan for personal use with limited blocks. Notion AI is an add-on that costs $8 per member per month (billed annually) on top of your Notion plan. The Plus plan starts at $10/user/month (billed annually). Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros

  • Extremely versatile—can be used for notes, wikis, projects, databases, and more.
  • AI features are well-integrated and context-aware.
  • Generous free tier for individuals.
  • Large community and extensive templates.
  • Collaboration features are robust.

Cons

  • AI is an add-on, increasing the cost.
  • Can be overwhelming for new users due to its flexibility.
  • AI Q&A only works within your workspace, not external sources.
  • Not as "automatic" as Mem—requires manual organization.

Mem: The AI-First Notes App That Organizes Itself

Mem (formerly Mem.ai) positions itself as "the self-organizing workspace." It uses AI to automatically organize your notes, surface relevant information, and help you write. Unlike Notion, Mem is built from the ground up with AI at its core.

Key Features

  • Auto-Organization: Mem automatically tags and links related notes, so you don't have to manually create folders or databases.
  • AI Search: Ask natural language questions and get answers from your notes.
  • Smart Writing: AI assists with drafting, summarizing, and rewriting.
  • Meeting Notes: Integrates with calendars to automatically capture meeting notes.
  • Focus Mode: A distraction-free writing environment.
  • Mem Chat: Chat with your notes to extract insights.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited features. The Mem Pro plan is $8.33 per month (billed annually) or $10 month-to-month. There's also a Mem Teams plan at $12/user/month (billed annually) and an Enterprise plan with custom pricing.

Pros

  • Truly AI-first—organization is automatic, saving time.
  • Clean, minimalist interface that's easy to use.
  • Excellent for personal knowledge management and capturing ideas.
  • AI search is powerful and intuitive.
  • Meeting notes integration is a nice touch.

Cons

  • Less versatile than Notion—not ideal for project management or databases.
  • Limited collaboration features compared to Notion.
  • No free tier for teams.
  • AI can sometimes be overzealous with auto-tagging.
  • Smaller community and fewer integrations.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You need an all-in-one workspace for notes, projects, and databases.
  • You collaborate with a team and need robust sharing and permissions.
  • You want a generous free tier to start with.
  • You value a large ecosystem of templates and integrations.
  • You're already using Notion and want to add AI capabilities.

Choose Mem if:

  • You want an AI-first notes app that organizes itself automatically.
  • You're a solopreneur, writer, or creative who primarily takes personal notes.
  • You prefer a minimalist, distraction-free interface.
  • You want powerful AI search and chat with your notes.
  • You don't need complex project management features.

Bottom Line: Which AI Notes App Wins?

For most users, Notion AI is the winner. Its versatility, generous free tier, and seamless integration with a powerful workspace make it the better investment. The AI add-on is reasonably priced and adds genuine value to an already excellent tool.

However, if your primary need is a smart, self-organizing notes app for personal use, Mem is a worthy contender. Its AI-first approach saves time and reduces the friction of manual organization. It's especially appealing for creatives and solopreneurs who want their notes to organize themselves.

Ultimately, the best choice depends on your workflow. If you need an all-in-one workspace, go with Notion AI. If you want a dedicated AI notes app that thinks for you, give Mem a try.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than Mem?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for teams and all-in-one workspace needs, while Mem excels at AI-first personal note-taking with automatic organization. For most users, Notion AI's versatility makes it the better overall choice.

2. Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem offers a free plan with limited features, including a cap on the number of notes and AI queries. The Pro plan starts at $8.33/month (billed annually) for full access.

3. Can Notion AI search the web?

No, Notion AI's Q&A feature only searches within your Notion workspace. It does not have web browsing capabilities. Mem's AI also focuses on your notes, not the web.

4. Which is better for team collaboration?

Notion AI is significantly better for team collaboration. It offers robust sharing, permissions, comments, and real-time editing. Mem's collaboration features are more limited and geared towards small teams.

5. Is Mem worth the price?

If you value automatic organization and AI-powered search, Mem is worth it. However, if you're on a budget, Notion's free plan (without AI) might suffice, or you can add AI for $8/month.

6. Can I use Notion AI and Mem together?

Absolutely. Some users use Notion for project management and Mem for personal notes. They serve different purposes and can complement each other.
