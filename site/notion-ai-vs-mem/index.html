Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion started as a flexible workspace for notes, databases, and wikis. In 2023, it added Notion AI, a suite of AI features that help you write, summarize, and search your content. In 2025, Notion AI has evolved into a robust assistant that's deeply integrated into the Notion ecosystem.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate blog posts, emails, meeting agendas, and more directly in your pages. You can also improve existing text with options like "fix spelling & grammar," "make longer," "summarize," and "change tone."
  • Q&A: Ask natural language questions about your workspace (e.g., "What did we decide in the Q3 planning meeting?") and get answers with citations to source pages.
  • Autofill: Use AI to automatically populate database properties—like extracting action items from meeting notes or summarizing project updates.
  • AI Connectors: Connect Slack, Google Drive, and GitHub to bring external context into your Notion Q&A.
  • Custom AI Blocks: Create reusable AI-powered blocks that perform specific tasks (e.g., "translate to Spanish" or "generate tweet thread").

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan. It costs $10 per member per month when billed monthly, or $8 per member per month when billed annually. You can try it free for a limited number of AI responses.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem (formerly Mem.ai) launched in 2020 with a bold vision: an AI-powered workspace that organizes itself. Instead of folders and tags, Mem uses AI to automatically link related notes, surface relevant information, and even generate drafts. It's designed for individuals and small teams who want to offload organization to AI.

Key Features

  • Self-Organizing Notes: Mem automatically connects related notes and surfaces them as you type, eliminating manual filing.
  • Mem Chat: An AI assistant that answers questions about your notes and can perform actions like creating reminders or drafting emails.
  • Smart Write: AI-powered writing that helps you draft documents, summarize meetings, and generate ideas based on your notes.
  • Voice Notes & Transcription: Record voice memos and get AI transcriptions with summaries.
  • Integrations: Connect Google Calendar, Gmail, and Slack to bring in context automatically.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. The Mem Pro plan is $14.99 per month (or $10.99 per month billed annually). There's also a Team plan at $12 per user per month (annual only).

Pros

  • Truly AI-first—minimal manual organization required.
  • Excellent for capturing quick thoughts and retrieving them later.
  • Mem Chat is powerful for querying your knowledge base.
  • Clean, distraction-free interface.

Cons

  • Less customizable than Notion—no databases or advanced layouts.
  • More expensive than Notion AI for individuals.
  • Limited team collaboration features (no granular permissions).
  • AI can sometimes make incorrect connections or suggestions.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than Mem?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is better for teams, customization, and value. Mem is better for individuals who want automatic organization and an AI-first experience. For most users, Notion AI is the more versatile choice.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion AI offers a limited free trial with about 20 AI responses. After that, you need to add the AI add-on to a paid Notion plan ($10/user/month monthly or $8/user/month annual).

3. Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem offers a free plan with basic AI features and limited usage. For full AI capabilities, you'll need Mem Pro at $14.99/month (or $10.99/month annual).

4. Which tool is better for meeting notes?

Both are good, but Mem excels at capturing and transcribing voice notes, while Notion AI is better for summarizing and organizing meeting notes within a structured workspace. If you want automatic linking and recall, Mem has an edge.

5. Can I use Notion AI and Mem together?

Technically yes, but it's redundant. Most users pick one. If you need both, you might use Mem for quick capture and Notion for project management, but that adds complexity.

6. Is Mem secure for sensitive data?

Mem uses encryption and complies with GDPR. However, Notion has more enterprise-grade security features (SOC 2, SSO, audit logs) and is often preferred by larger organizations.
