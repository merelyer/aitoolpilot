Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion has been a productivity powerhouse for years, and the integration of AI has made it even more versatile. Notion AI isn’t just a chatbot—it’s woven into your workspace. You can ask it to summarize pages, generate meeting agendas, create action items from notes, and even translate content. It works across all your databases, making it a true productivity companion.

Key Features of Notion AI

  • AI-powered writing assistant: Draft, edit, summarize, and translate text directly in your pages.
  • Database integration: Ask AI to generate formulas, filter views, or pull insights from your data.
  • Slash commands: Use “/AI” to trigger AI actions quickly.
  • Context-aware answers: AI can reference your workspace content to answer questions.
  • Collaborative AI: Teams can share AI-generated content and use it in projects.

Pricing

Notion offers a generous free plan for personal use. For AI, you need to add the AI add-on to any paid plan. As of 2025, Notion AI costs $10 per member per month when billed annually, or $12 month-to-month. If you’re on the free plan, you can still try AI with a limited number of responses before upgrading.

Pros of Notion AI

  • Deep integration with Notion’s powerful database system.
  • Excellent for team collaboration and project management.
  • AI is contextually aware of your workspace, making answers more relevant.
  • Highly customizable—you can build anything from a simple note to a complex CRM.

Cons of Notion AI

  • Steep learning curve for beginners.
  • AI can be slow on large workspaces.
  • No automatic organization—you have to set up your own structure.
  • Pricing adds up for teams (AI add-on per member).

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem is a relatively new player that’s making waves with its “self-organizing” note app. It uses AI to automatically tag, link, and surface related notes. The core idea is that you capture everything, and Mem’s AI does the heavy lifting to keep things organized. Mem also has a powerful AI chat that can answer questions based on your notes, making it a true second brain.

Key Features of Mem

  • Automatic organization: Mem uses AI to group related notes and create connections automatically.
  • AI chat: Ask Mem anything, and it will pull answers from your notes.
  • Quick capture: Use the browser extension, mobile app, or email to add notes instantly.
  • Smart search: Find anything with natural language queries.
  • Zapier integration: Connect Mem to thousands of apps for automated workflows.

Pricing

Mem has a free plan with limited AI features. The Pro plan costs $8.33 per month when billed annually (or $10 monthly) and includes unlimited AI and more storage. There’s also a Team plan at $12.50 per user per month (billed annually) that adds collaborative features.

Pros of Mem

  • Zero-friction note capture—just write, and Mem organizes.
  • AI chat is genuinely impressive for retrieving information.
  • Clean, minimalist interface that’s easy to use.
  • Great for building a personal knowledge base.

Cons of Mem

  • Less customizable than Notion—you can’t create databases or complex views.
  • Limited formatting options for notes.
  • AI organization isn’t perfect; sometimes you’ll have to manually fix tags.
  • Not ideal for project management or team collaboration.

What is the pricing difference between Notion AI and Mem?
