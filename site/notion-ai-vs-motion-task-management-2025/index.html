Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion started as a note-taking app and evolved into a full-blown workspace with databases, wikis, and project management. The AI add-on (launched in 2023 and refined through 2025) adds writing assistance, summarization, and smart search. But is it a true task management tool?

Key Features

  • Flexible databases: Create custom tables, kanban boards, calendars, and lists. You can build a task manager exactly how you want it.
  • AI assistant: Generate text, summarize meeting notes, rewrite paragraphs, and ask questions about your content.
  • Collaboration: Real-time editing, comments, and shared workspaces.
  • Templates: Hundreds of pre-built templates for project management, OKRs, and more.
  • Integrations: Slack, Google Drive, Figma, and 50+ other apps via Zapier or native connectors.

Pricing (as of 2025)

Notion has a generous free plan for individuals (unlimited pages, 7-day page history). Paid plans start at $10/month (Plus) per user, billed annually. The AI add-on costs an additional $10/month per user. So for a single user with AI, you're looking at $20/month. Business and Enterprise plans are $18–$25/month per user (with AI).

Pros

  • Extremely customizable—build any workflow you can imagine.
  • AI features are useful for writing and summarizing, not just scheduling.
  • Great for knowledge management and documentation alongside tasks.
  • Free plan is very functional for solo users.

Cons

  • Steep learning curve; setting up a task system takes time.
  • No automatic scheduling—you have to manually set due dates and priorities.
  • AI is an add-on, not core to task management.
  • Can become slow with large databases.

Detailed Review: Motion

Motion is a different beast. It's an AI-powered calendar that automatically schedules your tasks, meetings, and habits into your day. You tell it what to do and when it's due, and Motion finds the best time slots, adjusting dynamically as new tasks come in. It's less about building a custom system and more about letting AI handle the logistics.

Key Features

  • Auto-scheduling: AI places tasks on your calendar based on priority, deadlines, and available time. It reschedules automatically when things change.
  • Smart calendar: Syncs with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar. Blocks focus time and prevents overbooking.
  • Task management: Create tasks with estimated durations, priorities, and deadlines. Motion handles the rest.
  • Meeting scheduler: Share a link so others can book time with you, and Motion protects your deep work blocks.
  • Project management: Kanban boards, subtasks, and dependencies—but everything is tied to the calendar.

Pricing (as of 2025)

Motion offers a 7-day free trial (no credit card required). Paid plans start at $19/month (Individual) billed annually. The Team plan is $12/month per user (billed annually, minimum 5 users). There's also a Business plan with advanced features at $20/month per user. No free tier beyond the trial.

Pros

  • AI truly automates scheduling—saves hours each week.
  • Great for people who struggle with time management or procrastination.
  • Dynamic rescheduling is a lifesaver when priorities shift.
  • Beautiful, intuitive interface.

Cons

  • Expensive for individuals ($19/month).
  • Less flexible—you work within Motion's system, not your own.
  • Not ideal for complex project management or knowledge bases.
  • Limited integrations compared to Notion.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can Notion AI automatically schedule tasks like Motion?

No. Notion AI can help you write and summarize, but it does not automatically schedule tasks on a calendar. You must manually set due dates and reminders. Motion is specifically designed for auto-scheduling.

2. Which tool is cheaper: Notion AI or Motion?

Notion AI is cheaper for individuals if you use the free plan (without AI) or the Plus plan ($10/month). However, adding AI brings the cost to $20/month. Motion's Individual plan is $19/month. For teams, Motion's per-user pricing ($12/user) can be cheaper than Notion AI with AI add-on ($20/user).

3. Does Motion work with Google Calendar?

Yes, Motion syncs seamlessly with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar. It automatically blocks time for tasks and protects your calendar from overbooking.

4. Can I use Notion AI for project management?

Absolutely. Notion's databases, kanban boards, and timelines make it a powerful project management tool. However, it lacks automatic scheduling—you'll need to manually update progress and deadlines.

5. Is Motion good for teams?

Yes, Motion's Team and Business plans include shared calendars, task assignments, and project views. However, it's best for teams that focus on time-blocking and daily task execution rather than complex project tracking.

6. What are the main limitations of Motion?

Motion is expensive for individuals, has limited integrations, and is less customizable than Notion. It also doesn't offer a free tier beyond the trial. For power users who want to build a custom system, Motion can feel restrictive.

7. Which tool is better for students?

Notion AI is better for students because of its free plan, note-taking capabilities, and flexibility for organizing classes and projects. Motion's $19/month fee may be too high for most students.
