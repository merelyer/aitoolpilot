Detailed Reviews of Each Tool

1. Descript

Best for: Anyone who edits by typing. Descript transcribes your video, then lets you edit the text like a document. It’s revolutionary for podcasters and tutorial creators.

Pricing (2025):

  • Free: 1 hour of transcription, basic export (720p)
  • Pro: $24/month (billed yearly) – unlimited transcription, 4K export, AI voice cloning (Studio Sound)
  • Business: $40/month – team collaboration, priority support
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing

Pros: Intuitive, powerful AI features (Filler Word Removal, Eye Contact Correction), excellent collaboration. Cons: Can be buggy with long videos; AI voice cloning can feel uncanny if not tuned.

2. RunwayML

Best for: Filmmakers and creatives who want to experiment with generative AI – text-to-video, inpainting, motion tracking.

Pricing (2025):

  • Free: 125 credits, limited exports
  • Standard: $12/month (billed yearly) – 625 credits, 4K export, watermark removal
  • Pro: $28/month – 2,250 credits, priority processing, team seats
  • Enterprise: Custom (API access, dedicated support)

Pros: State-of-the-art AI models, huge creative potential. Cons: Steep learning curve, credit system can be confusing, not ideal for simple edits.

3. Pictory

Best for: Repurposing long-form content (webinars, podcasts) into short social clips.

Pricing (2025):

  • Free: 3 videos (10 min each) with watermark
  • Starter: $19/month – 10 videos, 30 min each, no watermark
  • Professional: $39/month – 30 videos, 60 min each, custom branding
  • Teams: $99/month – 60 videos, 90 min each, team collaboration

Pros: Fast, automated, great for SEO (auto-captions, transcripts). Cons: Limited creative control, AI can miss context in scene selection.

4. InVideo

Best for: Marketers who need quick, template-driven videos for ads and social media.

Pricing (2025):

  • Free: 5 minutes/week, watermark, 60 templates
  • Plus: $20/month (billed yearly) – unlimited videos, 5,000+ templates, no watermark
  • Max: $60/month – priority support, brand kit, team collaboration

Pros: Huge template library, easy to use, good for non-editors. Cons: AI editing is basic, can feel template-y, export times can be slow.

5. Adobe Premiere Pro (AI Features)

Best for: Professional editors who need full control plus AI assist (Auto Reframe, Scene Edit Detection, Text-Based Editing).

Pricing (2025):

  • Individual: $22.99/month (annual plan)
  • Team: $35.99/month per user
  • Enterprise: Custom (includes Creative Cloud All Apps)

Pros: Industry standard, unmatched power, deep integration with After Effects. Cons: Steep learning curve, resource-heavy, AI features are add-ons (not core to the experience).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the cheapest AI video editor in 2025?

A: The cheapest paid AI video editor is RunwayML at $12/month (Standard plan). However, many tools offer free tiers—Descript and InVideo are great free options.

Q: Can I edit videos with AI for free?

A: Yes. Descript offers 1 hour of free transcription per month, InVideo gives 5 minutes of video per week, and RunwayML provides 125 free credits. All are limited but functional.

Q: Which AI video editor is best for beginners?

A: InVideo is the most beginner-friendly due to its massive template library and intuitive drag-and-drop interface. Descript is also easy if you're comfortable typing.

Q: Do AI video editors remove watermarks for free?

A: No. Most free tiers include watermarks. You need to upgrade to a paid plan (e.g., Descript Pro or InVideo Plus) to remove them.

Q: Is Adobe Premiere Pro worth it for AI features?

A: Only if you already need a professional editor. The AI features (Auto Reframe, Text-Based Editing) are useful but not as advanced as dedicated AI tools like RunwayML.

Q: Are there any hidden costs in AI video editing subscriptions?

A: Some tools like RunwayML use a credit system—once you run out, you may need to buy more credits or upgrade. Always check if the plan includes enough credits for your typical video length.
