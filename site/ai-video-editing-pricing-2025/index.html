Detailed Reviews of Top AI Video Editing Tools

1. Descript

Descript is a game-changer for anyone who edits videos that involve talking heads, podcasts, or tutorials. Its core feature is text-based editing: you edit the transcript and the video edits itself. It also includes AI voice cloning, filler word removal, and studio sound enhancement.

Pricing: Free tier includes 1 hour of transcription per month. Paid plans: Hobbyist at $12/month (annual) or $19 month-to-month; Creator at $24/month (annual) for more hours and advanced features; Business at $40/user/month for teams. All paid plans include watermark-free export and 4K video.

  • Pros: Intuitive for beginners, powerful AI features, great for podcasts and screen recordings.
  • Cons: Limited multi-track editing, can be slow with long videos.

2. Runway

Runway is at the forefront of generative AI for video. It offers tools like text-to-video, video-to-video, and advanced motion tracking. It's not your typical NLE; it's more of a creative sandbox for AI-driven effects and generation.

Pricing: Free tier with limited credits. Standard plan at $12/user/month (annual) includes 625 credits/month and 4K export. Pro plan at $28/user/month (annual) offers more credits and features. Enterprise is custom. Credits refresh monthly and unused ones don't roll over.

  • Pros: Cutting-edge AI tools, high-quality output, great for experimental projects.
  • Cons: Steep learning curve, credits system can be confusing, not a full editing suite.

3. CapCut

CapCut (the video editor from TikTok's parent company) has become a favorite for quick social media edits. It offers auto-captions, background removal, and a huge library of effects—all in a simple drag-and-drop interface.

Pricing: Free version is surprisingly robust. Pro version costs $7.99/month or $74.99/year, unlocking premium effects, more cloud storage, and advanced AI features.

  • Pros: Free tier is excellent, mobile and desktop apps, very easy to use.
  • Cons: Some AI features are limited to Pro, privacy concerns (data usage), not for professional-grade projects.

4. Adobe Premiere Pro (with AI features)

Adobe Premiere Pro isn't an AI-first tool, but it has integrated AI via Adobe Sensei and third-party plugins. Features like Auto Reframe, Scene Edit Detection, and AI-powered audio cleanup make it a strong contender for editors who need full control.

Pricing: Single app $22.99/month (annual) or $34.49 month-to-month. All plans include access to Adobe Firefly AI tools. There's a 7-day free trial, but no permanent free tier.

  • Pros: Industry standard, vast plugin ecosystem, AI features integrated into pro workflow.
  • Cons: Expensive, steep learning curve, subscription only.

5. Pictory

Pictory focuses on turning long-form content (like blogs or webinars) into short, shareable videos. It uses AI to pick key moments and add captions automatically. It's less about editing and more about repurposing content.

Pricing: Free trial (no card required) with 3 video projects. Starter plan at $19/month (annual) or $23 month-to-month; Professional at $39/month (annual) for more videos and features; Teams at $99/month (annual) for up to 3 users.

  • Pros: Fast way to create videos from text, good for marketers, easy to use.
  • Cons: Not for cinematic edits, limited customization, output can feel template-y.

Who Should Use What?

Choosing the right tool depends on your specific needs. Here's a quick guide:

  • Podcasters and YouTubers: Descript is perfect because of its text-based editing and studio sound features.
  • Social media marketers: CapCut offers the best free features and is designed for short-form content.
  • AI enthusiasts and filmmakers: Runway is for you if you want to experiment with generative AI and don't mind a learning curve.
  • Professional editors: Stick with Adobe Premiere Pro for its full control and AI-assisted tools.
  • Content repurposers: Pictory is your best bet to turn blogs into videos quickly.

Bottom Line: What Should You Choose?

After comparing all the options, our recommendation is clear: Descript offers the most value for the price, especially if you're creating videos that involve people talking to the camera. Its AI features are practical and save real time. If you're a social media creator on a tight budget, CapCut is a no-brainer. For professional work, Adobe Premiere Pro remains the industry standard, but you'll pay for it. Runway is a niche tool for those who want to push creative boundaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the cheapest AI video editing software?

The cheapest is CapCut, which offers a robust free version. For paid plans, CapCut Pro at $7.99/month is the most affordable, followed by Descript and Runway at $12/month.

2. Are there any free AI video editors with no watermark?

Yes, CapCut's free version allows you to export without a watermark. Descript's free tier also exports without a watermark but includes a Descript logo in the video.

3. Which AI video editor is best for beginners?

CapCut is the most beginner-friendly due to its intuitive interface and simple timeline. Descript is also easy if you prefer text-based editing.

4. Can AI video editors replace traditional editors like Adobe Premiere Pro?

For most casual and intermediate projects, yes. However, for complex, multi-track projects with fine-tuned control, traditional editors still have the upper hand. AI tools are improving rapidly, though.

5. Do AI video editing tools offer student discounts?

Adobe offers a student discount of around 65% off for the first year. Descript and Runway do not have specific student plans, but they have free tiers that may suffice.

6. What is the best AI video editor for long videos?

Descript is excellent for long videos, especially if they are interview or podcast-style, because you can edit by text. However, free tiers have limits on transcription hours.
