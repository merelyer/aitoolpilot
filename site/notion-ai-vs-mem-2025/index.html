Notion AI Review: The AI-Powered All-in-One Workspace

Notion has been the darling of productivity nerds for years, and its AI add-on takes things to the next level. But is it worth the extra cost? Let's dig in.

What Makes Notion AI Special?

Notion AI isn't a standalone app—it's an AI layer that sits on top of your existing Notion pages, databases, and wikis. That means you can use AI to draft content, summarize meeting notes, extract action items, and even translate text without leaving your workspace.

Here are the standout features:

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate blog posts, emails, and reports directly in Notion. You can also ask it to improve your writing, fix grammar, or change the tone.
  • Summarization: Turn long meeting transcripts or research docs into concise summaries with one click.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your notes and get answers based on your content. It's like having a search engine for your brain.
  • Autofill Databases: Use AI to automatically fill properties in your databases, like categorizing tasks or extracting key info from documents.
  • Translation: Translate content into multiple languages without leaving Notion.

Notion AI Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan (including the free plan). It costs $10 per member per month when billed annually, or $12 month-to-month. You can also get it bundled with Notion's Business plan for $20 per member per month (annual).

Notion's base plans:

  • Free: $0 for individuals, unlimited pages for personal use.
  • Plus: $8/member/month (annual) for small teams.
  • Business: $15/member/month (annual) for larger teams with advanced permissions.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing.

So if you want Notion + AI on the Plus plan, you're looking at $18/member/month (annual). That's still competitive with other AI tools.

Pros of Notion AI

  • Seamless integration with your existing Notion workspace—no context switching.
  • Powerful AI writing and summarization tools that actually save time.
  • Flexible: works for individuals, teams, and enterprises.
  • Regular updates and new AI features (they're constantly improving).
  • Generous free tier for personal use (AI costs extra).

Cons of Notion AI

  • The AI add-on is pricey, especially for teams.
  • AI features are limited to Notion's ecosystem—no integration with external apps (yet).
  • Can be overwhelming for new users due to Notion's flexibility.
  • AI responses can sometimes be generic or miss context.

Mem Review: The AI-Native Note-Taking App

Mem takes a different approach. Instead of folders and pages, Mem uses AI to automatically organize your notes and surface relevant information when you need it. It's like having a personal assistant who files everything for you.

What Makes Mem Special?

Mem's core philosophy is "no organization required." You just write, and Mem's AI connects the dots. Here's what it offers:

  • AI-Powered Organization: Mem automatically tags and links related notes, so you don't have to create folders or hierarchies.
  • Smart Search: Ask natural language questions like "What did I learn about marketing last month?" and Mem will find the answer.
  • AI Writing Assistant: Similar to Notion AI, Mem can help you draft, summarize, and rewrite content.
  • Meeting Notes: Record meetings and get AI-generated summaries and action items.
  • Email Integration: Forward emails to Mem and they become searchable notes.

Mem Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features and a Pro plan at $8.33/month (billed annually) or $9.99 month-to-month. The Pro plan includes unlimited AI, advanced search, and more.

Mem also has a Teams plan at $12/user/month (annual) with collaborative features.

Pros of Mem

  • Truly AI-native: organization is automatic, which is great for people who hate filing.
  • Fast and distraction-free writing experience.
  • Excellent for personal knowledge management and research.
  • More affordable for individuals than Notion AI (if you don't need Notion's other features).

Cons of Mem

  • Lack of structure can be disorienting if you prefer folders and databases.
  • Not as feature-rich as Notion for project management or team collaboration.
  • AI organization isn't perfect—sometimes it misses connections.
  • No free tier for AI features (only a 14-day trial).

Who Should Use What?

Choosing between Notion AI and Mem depends on your workflow and priorities. Here's a quick guide:

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You're already a Notion user. The AI add-on integrates seamlessly with your existing setup.
  • You need a flexible workspace. Notion can be a notes app, project manager, wiki, and database—all in one.
  • You collaborate with a team. Notion's sharing and permissions are more robust than Mem's.
  • You want AI writing assistance for content creation. Notion AI excels at drafting and editing.

Choose Mem if:

  • You hate organizing notes. Mem's AI does the filing for you.
  • You're a solo knowledge worker. Mem is designed for personal use, not team collaboration.
  • You want a fast, distraction-free writing experience. Mem's editor is cleaner and simpler than Notion's.
  • You're on a tighter budget. Mem's Pro plan is cheaper than Notion + AI for individuals.

Bottom Line: Which AI Notes App Wins?

After extensive testing, Notion AI is our overall winner for 2025. It's more versatile, better for teams, and the AI features are genuinely useful for a wide range of tasks. The pricing is reasonable for what you get, especially if you're already using Notion.

That said, Mem is a fantastic choice for individuals who want a streamlined, AI-first note-taking experience without the overhead of managing a complex workspace. If you're a researcher, writer, or student who values speed and simplicity, Mem might be your new best friend.

Ultimately, the best tool is the one that fits your brain. Try both and see which one clicks.

FAQ

Is Notion AI worth the extra cost?

If you use Notion daily and frequently write or summarize content, yes. The AI features can save hours each week. For casual users, the free Notion plan might suffice.

Can Mem replace Notion?

For personal note-taking, yes. But Notion offers more features for project management, databases, and team collaboration. Mem is more focused on AI-powered notes.

Does Mem have a free plan?

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features, but the Pro plan is required for unlimited AI and advanced search. There's also a 14-day free trial of Pro.

Which is better for students?

Mem is often better for students due to its automatic organization and lower price. However, Notion AI is better if you need to collaborate on group projects or manage research databases.

Can I use Notion AI offline?

Notion AI requires an internet connection to function. Notion itself has limited offline capabilities, but AI features are not available offline.

Is Mem's AI organization accurate?

Mem's AI is good but not perfect. It sometimes misses connections or mis-tags notes. However, it improves over time as you add more content.
