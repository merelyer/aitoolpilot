Key Features

  • Auto-organization: Mem automatically tags notes and suggests connections between ideas.
  • AI Search: Ask natural language questions and get answers from your notes.
  • Smart Summaries: Get quick summaries of long notes or threads.
  • Meeting Notes: Integrates with calendars to automatically capture meeting notes and action items.

FAQ

Is Notion AI free?

Notion AI is not entirely free. You can try it with a limited number of free AI responses on the Notion Free plan. For unlimited use, you need the AI add-on at $10/member/month (billed annually).

Can Mem replace Notion?

Mem is a dedicated notes app, while Notion is an all-in-one workspace. If you only need note-taking with AI organization, Mem can replace Notion. But if you rely on Notion's databases, project management, or wikis, Mem won't be a full substitute.

Which has better AI: Notion AI or Mem?

It depends on your use case. Notion AI excels at writing assistance and summarization within documents. Mem's AI is better at automatic organization and recall. For writing help, Notion AI wins; for effortless note organization, Mem is superior.

Does Mem work offline?

Mem has limited offline capabilities. You can view and edit notes offline, but AI features require an internet connection. Notion also has offline mode with similar limitations.

Is Notion AI secure for business use?

Yes, Notion AI is designed with enterprise-grade security. Notion offers SOC 2 compliance, encryption, and admin controls for business and enterprise plans. Your data is not used to train AI models.

Can I use Mem for team collaboration?

Mem offers a Teams plan with shared workspaces and collaborative features, but it's less robust for team collaboration compared to Notion. Notion is better suited for teams that need shared databases, permissions, and project management.
