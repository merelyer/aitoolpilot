Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion AI is an add-on to Notion, the popular all-in-one workspace that combines notes, databases, wikis, and project management. Launched in 2023, Notion AI has evolved significantly, and in 2025 it's more powerful than ever.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate text, summarize, fix grammar, change tone, and translate directly within your pages.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers with citations from your notes.
  • Autofill: Automatically populate database properties using AI.
  • AI Connectors: Integrate with Slack, Google Drive, and GitHub to pull in information.
  • Custom AI Blocks: Create reusable AI-powered blocks for repetitive tasks.
  • Enterprise Search: Available on enterprise plans, it searches across all connected apps.

Pricing

Notion AI is an add-on to any Notion plan. It costs $10 per member per month when billed annually, or $12 month-to-month. You can also get it as part of the Notion Business plan ($20/member/month annually) or Enterprise (custom pricing). Notion's free plan includes limited AI trials.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's powerful workspace
  • Affordable add-on pricing
  • Versatile AI features for writing, organizing, and Q&A
  • Regular updates and new features

Cons

  • AI is an add-on, so you need a Notion plan first
  • Can be overwhelming for new users
  • AI Q&A sometimes misses context in large workspaces

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem (formerly Mem.ai) is an AI-native note-taking app that aims to eliminate the need for manual organization. It uses AI to automatically tag, link, and surface related notes, so you can focus on capturing ideas.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Organization: Automatically tags and links notes based on content.
  • Smart Search: Natural language search that understands context.
  • Mem Chat: Chat with your notes to get answers and summaries.
  • Auto-write: Generate drafts and expand on ideas using AI.
  • Collections: Group notes into collections for projects or topics.
  • Integrations: Connect with Google Calendar, Gmail, and Slack.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. The Mem Pro plan costs $14.99 per month (or $10.83/month billed annually). There's also a Mem Enterprise plan with custom pricing for teams.

Pros

  • Zero manual organization—AI does the heavy lifting
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Excellent for capturing quick thoughts and ideas
  • Strong AI search and chat capabilities

Cons

  • More expensive than Notion AI
  • Less flexible for project management and databases
  • Limited customization compared to Notion
  • No free tier for unlimited notes (free plan has limits)

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Notion AI better than Mem?

For most users, yes. Notion AI offers more features, better value, and integrates with a full workspace. Mem excels at automatic organization but is more expensive and less versatile.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion offers a free plan with limited AI trials. To unlock full AI features, you need to add the AI add-on for $10/month (billed annually) on top of any paid Notion plan.

3. Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem has a free plan with basic features and limited AI usage. For full AI capabilities, you'll need the Pro plan at $14.99/month.

4. Which app is better for team collaboration?

Notion AI is far better for teams. It supports shared workspaces, permissions, and collaborative editing. Mem is more personal and lacks robust team features.

5. Does Mem work offline?

Mem has limited offline support. Notion also has offline mode, but both apps are primarily cloud-based and require internet for full functionality.

6. Can I import my notes from Evernote or other apps?

Both Notion and Mem offer import options from popular apps like Evernote, Google Keep, and plain text files. Notion has more advanced import tools for databases.
