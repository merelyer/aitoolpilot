Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion AI isn't a standalone app; it's an add-on to Notion, the beloved workspace tool that combines notes, databases, wikis, and project management. In 2025, Notion AI has evolved into a robust assistant that can write, summarize, translate, and even answer questions about your workspace content.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate blog posts, emails, meeting agendas, and more. You can also improve existing text with tone adjustments, grammar fixes, and summarization.
  • Q&A: Ask questions like "What were the action items from last week's meeting?" and Notion AI will search your workspace to find answers.
  • Auto-fill Databases: Automatically populate properties like tags, summaries, and even translations.
  • AI-Powered Search: Find information faster with natural language queries.
  • Custom AI Blocks: Create reusable AI prompts that can be embedded anywhere in your workspace.

Pricing

Notion AI is available as an add-on to any Notion plan (Free, Plus, Business, or Enterprise). The AI add-on costs $10 per member per month when billed annually, or $12 month-to-month. You can try it free for a limited number of AI responses.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with Notion's powerful workspace features.
  • Versatile AI that handles writing, summarizing, and Q&A.
  • Familiar interface for existing Notion users.
  • Custom AI blocks allow for tailored workflows.

Cons

  • Requires a Notion subscription (or at least the free plan) to use.
  • AI add-on is an extra cost on top of your Notion plan.
  • Can be overwhelming for users who just want simple note-taking.
  • AI responses can sometimes be generic or miss context.

Detailed Review: Mem

Mem (formerly Mem.ai) is an AI-first note-taking app designed to think for you. It automatically organizes your notes, connects related ideas, and surfaces information when you need it—without you having to manually tag or file anything. It's like having a personal assistant for your brain.

Key Features

  • Auto-Organization: Mem uses AI to automatically tag and link related notes, so you don't have to.
  • Smart Search: Ask natural language questions and get answers pulled from your notes.
  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate drafts, summarize, and rewrite text directly in your notes.
  • Mem Chat: A conversational AI that can answer questions and help you brainstorm based on your notes.
  • Meeting Notes: Integrates with calendars to automatically capture and summarize meetings.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. The Mem Pro plan costs $14.99 per month (billed annually) or $19.99 month-to-month. There's also a team plan for $12/user/month (billed annually).

Pros

  • Truly AI-first: requires minimal manual organization.
  • Clean, distraction-free interface.
  • Excellent for capturing and connecting ideas.
  • Strong AI search and chat capabilities.

Cons

  • Less feature-rich than Notion for project management and databases.
  • No free tier for unlimited notes (free plan has limits).
  • Steeper learning curve for the AI features.
  • Limited integrations compared to Notion.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Notion AI if:

  • You already use Notion for project management, wikis, or databases.
  • You need a versatile AI assistant for writing, summarizing, and Q&A.
  • You want a single tool for notes, tasks, and collaboration.
  • You're comfortable with a slightly steeper learning curve.

Choose Mem if:

  • You want an AI-first note-taking app that organizes itself.
  • You primarily capture ideas, meeting notes, and research.
  • You prefer a clean, minimalist interface.
  • You don't need extensive project management features.

Bottom Line

Both Notion AI and Mem are excellent tools, but they cater to different audiences. Mem, on the other hand, is perfect for those who want a dedicated AI note-taking app that feels like a second brain—especially if you're drowning in unstructured notes and ideas.

FAQ

1. Is Notion AI better than Mem?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI is more versatile and integrates with a full workspace, while Mem is more focused on AI-first note-taking. For most users, Notion AI offers more value.

2. Can I use Notion AI for free?

Notion offers a free plan with limited AI responses. To unlock full AI features, you'll need to add the AI add-on to a paid Notion plan.

3. Does Mem have a free plan?

Yes, Mem offers a free plan with basic features and limited AI usage. For full AI capabilities, you'll need the Pro plan.

4. Which app is better for meeting notes?

Mem has a dedicated meeting notes feature that automatically captures and summarizes meetings. Notion AI can also summarize meeting notes, but it requires manual input.

5. Can I import my notes from Evernote or other apps?

Both Notion and Mem support importing from various formats, including Evernote, Markdown, and CSV. Notion has more robust import options.

6. Is Mem worth the price?

If you value automatic organization and AI-powered search, Mem is worth it. However, if you need a more comprehensive workspace, Notion AI is more cost-effective.
