Detailed Reviews

1. Mailmetheus

Best for: Personalized cold emails and Gmail users who want quick, effective drafts.

Mailmetheus integrates seamlessly with Gmail and Outlook, using AI to analyze the recipient's LinkedIn profile and previous interactions to generate highly personalized emails. It offers a browser extension that lets you draft emails directly in your inbox, saving you from switching tabs. The free plan includes 10 emails per month, which is perfect for casual users. Paid plans start at $9/month for the Starter tier, which bumps you to 50 emails per month and adds advanced personalization fields. The Pro plan at $19/month offers unlimited emails and priority support.

Pros:

  • Excellent personalization using real data
  • Seamless Gmail integration
  • Generous free tier

Cons:

  • Limited to email (no other content types)
  • Requires a LinkedIn account for best results

2. Outreach

Best for: Sales teams running large-scale outreach campaigns.

Outreach is a full-fledged sales engagement platform that includes AI-powered email writing. It offers advanced sequencing, A/B testing, and detailed analytics. The AI writer can generate emails based on your CRM data, ensuring consistency across your team. Outreach is not cheap—pricing starts at around $99 per user per month for the basic plan, but it includes powerful features like email tracking and meeting scheduling. The Enterprise plan, tailored for large organizations, offers custom pricing and advanced security.

Pros:

  • Comprehensive sales features
  • Scalable for large teams
  • Deep integration with CRMs

Cons:

  • Expensive for small businesses
  • Steep learning curve

3. Rytr

Best for: Budget-conscious individuals and small teams needing a versatile AI writer.

Rytr is an affordable AI writing assistant that covers emails, blog posts, and social media. Its email templates are solid, and you can generate multiple variations in seconds. The free plan gives you 5,000 characters per month, which is roughly 10-15 emails. The Unlimited plan at $9/month (billed annually) removes all limits and adds a plagiarism checker. Rytr is a great entry point if you want to test AI writing without a big commitment.

Pros:

  • Very affordable
  • Multiple use cases beyond email
  • User-friendly interface

Cons:

  • Less advanced personalization than Mailmetheus
  • Free plan has watermark

4. Copy.ai

Best for: Marketers who need email copy plus broader content creation.

Copy.ai is known for its marketing copy generation. It has specific workflows for cold emails, follow-ups, and newsletters. The free plan includes 2,000 words per month, which is fine for occasional use. The Pro plan at $49/month (billed annually) offers 25,000 words and access to the latest AI models. Copy.ai shines when you need to generate a full email sequence from a simple prompt, but it can be overkill if you only need basic email drafting.

Pros:

  • Great for email sequences
  • Good value for marketers
  • Supports multiple languages

Cons:

  • Higher price point for full features
  • No direct email integration (copy-paste required)

5. Jasper

Best for: Enterprises and content teams that need high-volume, brand-consistent writing.

Jasper is a powerful AI content platform with a dedicated email writing mode. It allows you to train the AI on your brand voice and use templates for different email types. Jasper’s pricing starts at $49/month for the Creator plan, which includes 50,000 words. The Pro plan at $99/month adds collaboration features and more advanced controls. For large teams, Jasper offers custom enterprise solutions with dedicated support.

Pros:

  • Excellent brand voice customization
  • High-quality output
  • Enterprise-grade security

Cons:

  • Expensive for casual users
  • Overwhelming for simple email tasks

FAQ

What is the best AI email writer for Gmail?

Mailmetheus is the best for Gmail due to its native extension and deep personalization. It integrates directly with Gmail, allowing you to draft emails without leaving your inbox.

Are there free AI email writing tools?

Yes, Rytr offers a free plan with 5,000 characters per month, and Mailmetheus gives 10 free emails monthly. Copy.ai also has a free tier with 2,000 words.

How much does an AI email writer cost per month?

Costs vary widely. Budget tools like Rytr start at $9/month, while professional tools like Mailmetheus are $19/month for unlimited use. Enterprise solutions like Outreach can cost over $100/user/month.

Can AI email writers improve my response rates?

Yes, AI email writers can improve response rates by personalizing content, optimizing subject lines, and suggesting follow-up times. Tools like Mailmetheus use recipient data to craft tailored messages, which typically outperform generic emails.

Is it safe to use AI for business emails?

Generally, yes. Reputable tools like Jasper and Outreach have strong security measures. However, always review AI-generated content for accuracy and compliance with your company’s data policies.
