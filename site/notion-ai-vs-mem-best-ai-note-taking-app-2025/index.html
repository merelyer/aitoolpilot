Detailed Review: Notion AI

Overview

Notion is a household name in productivity, and with the integration of AI, it has evolved into a powerful assistant that can generate content, summarize notes, and help with project management. Notion AI is deeply embedded into the workspace, allowing you to use it across pages, databases, and documents.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft, brainstorm, and rewrite content directly in your documents.
  • AI Summarization: Summarize long notes or meeting minutes instantly.
  • Ask AI: Ask questions about your workspace and get answers based on your content.
  • Database Automation: Automate actions like filling properties or generating summaries based on database entries.
  • Integration: Works seamlessly with Notion's existing features—wikis, tasks, calendars, and more.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan for personal use, but to access AI features, you need to add the AI add-on. As of 2025, the AI add-on costs $10 per member per month when billed annually, or $12 for monthly billing. For teams, the Business plan costs $15 per member/month (annual) and includes AI features. There's also an Enterprise plan with custom pricing.

Pros

  • Powerful all-in-one workspace (notes, tasks, databases, wikis).
  • AI is integrated across all features, not just notes.
  • Excellent collaboration features for teams.
  • Flexible and customizable.

Cons

  • Steep learning curve for new users.
  • AI add-on is an extra cost on top of the base plan.
  • Can become slow with large databases.
  • AI features are not as context-aware as dedicated AI note-takers.

Detailed Review: Mem

Overview

Mem is an AI-first note-taking app that promises to organize your notes automatically. It uses AI to connect related ideas, surface relevant information when you need it, and even generate summaries. Mem is designed for individuals who want a smart second brain without the manual effort of tagging and linking.

Key Features

  • Automatic Organization: Mem uses AI to categorize and connect notes automatically.
  • Contextual Recall: The AI surfaces relevant notes based on your current context, like a conversation or a project.
  • AI Chat: Chat with your notes—ask questions and get answers with citations.
  • Smart Collections: Group notes by topic or project, and Mem will suggest related notes.
  • Email Integration: Save emails directly to Mem and have them automatically organized.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI queries and storage. The Pro plan costs $10 per month (or $8/month billed annually) and includes unlimited AI, more storage, and advanced features. There's also a Team plan at $15 per user/month (annual) with collaboration features.

Pros

  • True AI-native experience—organization is automatic.
  • Excellent contextual recall and search.
  • Clean, minimal interface that's easy to learn.
  • Fast and responsive.

Cons

  • Limited formatting and customization compared to Notion.
  • No built-in database or project management features.
  • AI can occasionally make mistakes in organization.
  • Less suitable for team collaboration.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is better for personal note-taking: Notion AI or Mem?

For personal note-taking, Mem is generally better because it automatically organizes your notes and provides contextual recall without requiring manual setup. Notion AI is more powerful but requires more effort to organize.

2. Can Notion AI replace a dedicated AI note-taker like Mem?

Notion AI can handle many tasks, but it's not as context-aware as Mem. If your primary need is intelligent note-taking, Mem's AI is more advanced in that area. Notion AI is better as a productivity suite.

3. What are the pricing differences between Notion AI and Mem in 2025?

Notion AI requires a base plan (free or paid) plus an AI add-on of $10/member/month. Mem's Pro plan starts at $8/month (annual) and includes all AI features. For individuals, Mem is more affordable.

4. Does Mem have a mobile app?

Yes, Mem offers mobile apps for iOS and Android, allowing you to capture notes on the go. The AI features are available on mobile, though the experience is slightly more limited than the desktop web app.

5. Can I import my existing notes from other apps into Notion AI or Mem?

Both apps support importing from common formats like Markdown, text, and other note-taking apps. Notion has a wider range of import options (e.g., Evernote, OneNote), while Mem focuses on Markdown and text imports.

6. Which app is better for team collaboration?

Notion AI is clearly better for team collaboration with its shared workspaces, permissions, and project management features. Mem's Team plan is newer and less mature, making it less suitable for large teams.

7. Is there a free trial for the AI features?

Notion offers a free plan without AI, but you can get a 30-day trial of the AI add-on. Mem offers a free plan with limited AI queries, so you can test the AI capabilities before upgrading.
