Detailed Review of Each Tool

Jasper: The Marketing-Focused Powerhouse

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has positioned itself as the premier AI writing assistant for marketers. It's not just a text generator; it's a comprehensive content platform that integrates with your brand voice, SEO tools, and team workflows.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper allows you to train its AI on your unique brand voice by uploading style guides, past content, or even your website. This ensures every piece of content sounds like you.
  • Jasper Chat: A conversational AI interface that can answer questions, brainstorm ideas, and refine content in real-time.
  • SEO Mode: Integrated with Surfer SEO, Jasper helps you optimize content for search engines by providing keyword suggestions, content scores, and SERP analysis.
  • Campaign Manager: This feature lets you manage multi-channel marketing campaigns (e.g., email, social, ads) from a single dashboard, generating consistent messaging across all platforms.
  • Collaboration Tools: With team features, you can assign roles, review content, and maintain a consistent workflow.
  • 50+ Templates: From blog posts to Google Ads, Jasper offers a wide array of templates for various content types.

Pricing

Jasper offers three main tiers:

  • Creator: $49/month (annual) or $59/month (monthly) – includes 1 Brand Voice, 1 Seat, and up to 50,000 words.
  • Pro: $99/month (annual) or $119/month (monthly) – includes 3 Brand Voices, 5 Seats, and unlimited words.
  • Business: Custom pricing – includes unlimited Brand Voices, 15+ Seats, and advanced admin controls.

Pros

  • Exceptional content quality with a natural, human-like tone.
  • Advanced brand voice customization.
  • Excellent SEO integration.
  • Strong collaboration features for teams.
  • Regularly updated with new AI models (currently using GPT-4).

Cons

  • Higher price point compared to some competitors.
  • Steeper learning curve due to the abundance of features.
  • No completely free tier (only a 7-day trial).

Copy.ai: The Accessible All-Rounder

Copy.ai has made a name for itself as an accessible, user-friendly AI writing tool. It's particularly popular among startups and small businesses for its simplicity and generous free plan.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Copy.ai offers a vast library of templates for social media posts, product descriptions, email subject lines, and more.
  • Copy.ai Chat: Similar to Jasper Chat, this feature allows you to generate content through a conversational interface.
  • Brand Voice: Copy.ai also allows you to set up a brand voice, but it's less sophisticated than Jasper's.
  • Workflow Automation: A unique feature that lets you create automated content generation workflows, connecting with tools like Zapier.
  • SEO Tools: Copy.ai has added basic SEO features, including keyword integration and content scoring.
  • Multilingual Support: Supports over 25 languages, making it a good choice for global teams.

Pricing

  • Free Plan: 2,000 words per month, 1 seat, and access to limited templates.
  • Starter: $49/month (annual) or $59/month (monthly) – includes unlimited words, 5 seats, and all features.
  • Advanced: $249/month (annual) or $299/month (monthly) – includes unlimited words, 20 seats, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing – for large teams with advanced security and support.

Pros

  • Generous free tier (2,000 words per month).
  • Simple, intuitive interface.
  • Affordable paid plans.
  • Large template library.
  • Workflow automation capabilities.

Cons

  • Content quality can be inconsistent, sometimes requiring more editing.
  • Brand voice features are less advanced.
  • SEO tools are not as robust as Jasper's.
  • No direct integration with Google Docs or other popular writing tools.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is Jasper or Copy.ai better for SEO?

Jasper is better for SEO due to its integration with Surfer SEO, which provides detailed optimization scores, keyword suggestions, and SERP analysis. Copy.ai has basic SEO features but lacks the depth and accuracy of Jasper's integration.

2. Can I use Jasper and Copy.ai for free?

Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month. Jasper does not have a free tier but provides a 7-day free trial that allows you to test all features.

3. Which tool is more affordable?

Copy.ai is more affordable at the entry level, with its paid plan starting at $49/month (annual) for unlimited words. Jasper's Creator plan also starts at $49/month (annual) but includes only 50,000 words, making Copy.ai better for high-volume, low-budget users.

4. Can Jasper and Copy.ai write in multiple languages?

Yes, both tools support multiple languages. Copy.ai supports over 25 languages, while Jasper also offers multilingual support, though the exact number may vary.

5. Which tool is better for social media content?

Both tools offer templates for social media, but Jasper's brand voice feature ensures your social content is consistently on-brand. Copy.ai is also good for quick social posts, especially with its free plan.

6. Do Jasper and Copy.ai offer team collaboration features?

Yes, both offer team features. Jasper's Pro plan includes 5 seats, while Copy.ai's Starter plan includes 5 seats. Jasper provides more advanced role management and review workflows.

7. How do Jasper and Copy.ai compare to other AI writers like ChatGPT?

Jasper and Copy.ai are specifically designed for marketing content, offering templates, brand voices, and SEO tools. ChatGPT is more general-purpose and requires more manual prompting. For marketers, Jasper and Copy.ai are more efficient.
