Jasper: The Enterprise-Grade AI Writer

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a heavyweight in the AI writing space since 2021. It’s built for marketing teams that need to produce a high volume of on-brand content across multiple channels.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Train Jasper on your brand’s tone, style, and vocabulary. It’s like having a junior copywriter who’s read your entire style guide.
  • 50+ Templates: From blog posts to ad copy, social media captions, and email sequences—Jasper has a template for almost any content type.
  • Team Collaboration: Shared workspaces, document editing, and comments make it easy for teams to collaborate.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content as you write.
  • Browser Extension: Use Jasper anywhere on the web—Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, etc.
  • API Access: For enterprises that want to integrate Jasper into their own apps.

Pricing

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial (no credit card required), but no free tier. Plans:

  • Creator: $39/mo (annual) or $49/mo (monthly) — 1 seat, 1 brand voice, 50+ templates.
  • Pro: $59/mo (annual) or $69/mo (monthly) — 1 seat, 3 brand voices, 3 SEO modes, collaboration features.
  • Business: Custom pricing — unlimited brand voices, 5+ seats, API access, dedicated account manager.

Pros

  • Excellent brand voice customization
  • Wide range of templates and use cases
  • Strong team collaboration features
  • Seamless Surfer SEO integration
  • Reliable output quality

Cons

  • No free tier (only a trial)
  • Expensive for small teams
  • Learning curve for advanced features

Copy.ai: The Budget-Friendly AI Writer

Copy.ai started as a copywriting assistant but has evolved into a more general AI writing tool. It’s known for its generous free plan and user-friendly interface, making it a favorite among startups and solo entrepreneurs.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Covers blog posts, social media, emails, product descriptions, and more.
  • Brand Voice (Pro plan): Define your brand’s tone and apply it to generated content.
  • Workflows: Automate repetitive tasks by chaining templates together (e.g., generate a blog outline, then expand each section).
  • Chat Interface: Conversational AI for brainstorming and quick content generation.
  • Integrations: Zapier, HubSpot, Salesforce, and more.
  • Multi-Language Support: Generate content in 25+ languages.

Pricing

Copy.ai has a free forever plan (limited to 2,000 words/month). Paid plans:

  • Pro: $49/mo (annual) or $49/mo (monthly) — unlimited words, 5 seats, brand voice, workflows.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — advanced security, dedicated support, custom workflows.

Pros

  • Generous free tier (2,000 words/month)
  • Affordable Pro plan with unlimited words
  • User-friendly interface
  • Workflows automate complex content tasks
  • Good for small teams and solopreneurs

Cons

  • Brand voice only on Pro plan
  • Less robust collaboration features than Jasper
  • Output quality can be inconsistent
  • No dedicated SEO integration (unlike Jasper’s Surfer SEO)

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper If…

  • You’re a marketing team of 3+ people who need consistent brand voice across all content.
  • You produce high volumes of content and need advanced collaboration and workflow features.
  • You want seamless SEO integration (Surfer SEO) to rank higher.
  • Budget isn’t the primary concern—you value quality and scalability.

Choose Copy.ai If…

  • You’re a solopreneur or small team (1-2 people) just starting with AI writing.
  • You want to test the waters with a free plan before committing.
  • You need a budget-friendly tool with unlimited words on the Pro plan.
  • You rely on workflows to automate repetitive content tasks.

Bottom Line: Which AI Writer Wins for Teams?

For teams, Jasper is the winner. Its brand voice customization, team collaboration features, and SEO integration make it a more robust solution for scaling content production without sacrificing quality. Yes, it’s more expensive, but the ROI from consistent, on-brand content is worth it.

That said, Copy.ai is a fantastic alternative for small teams or solopreneurs who need a cost-effective AI writer with a free tier. It’s not as feature-rich for teams, but it gets the job done for basic content needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper is better for teams that need brand voice consistency, collaboration, and SEO integration. Copy.ai is better for individuals or small teams on a budget who want a free tier.

2. Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free forever plan with 2,000 words per month. Jasper does not have a free plan, but offers a 7-day free trial.

3. Can I use Jasper for SEO?

Yes, Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO to help you optimize content for search engines. Copy.ai does not have a dedicated SEO integration.

4. Which tool is more affordable for teams?

Copy.ai’s Pro plan is $49/mo for 5 seats, while Jasper’s Pro plan is $59/mo for 1 seat. However, Jasper’s Business plan offers more features for larger teams at custom pricing.

5. Do both tools support multiple languages?

Yes, both Jasper and Copy.ai support multiple languages. Jasper supports 25+ languages, and Copy.ai also supports 25+ languages.

6. Which tool is easier to use?

Copy.ai has a simpler, more intuitive interface, making it easier for beginners. Jasper has a steeper learning curve but offers more advanced features.

7. Can I integrate these tools with other apps?

Yes, both offer integrations. Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO, Grammarly, and Zapier. Copy.ai integrates with Zapier, HubSpot, Salesforce, and more.
