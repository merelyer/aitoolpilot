Detailed Review: Jasper

What is Jasper?

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is an AI writing assistant built specifically for marketers. It uses advanced language models (including GPT-4) to generate blog posts, social media content, ad copy, emails, and more. Jasper's standout feature is its ability to learn your brand voice and produce content that sounds like you—not a robot.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper lets you define your brand's tone, style, and vocabulary. You can create multiple brand voices for different products or audiences. This is a game-changer for maintaining consistency across all content.
  • 50+ Templates: From AIDA frameworks to blog post outlines, Jasper offers templates for almost every marketing use case. It also has a "Boss Mode" for long-form content.
  • Jasper Chat: An interactive AI assistant that helps you brainstorm, rewrite, and expand content in a conversational interface.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search engines as you write.
  • Team Collaboration: Share projects, leave comments, and manage permissions—ideal for teams.
  • Browser Extension: Use Jasper anywhere on the web (e.g., Google Docs, email clients).

Pricing

Jasper offers three plans:

  • Creator: $39/mo (annual) or $49/mo (monthly). Includes 1 brand voice, 1 seat, and unlimited words.
  • Pro: $59/mo (annual) or $69/mo (monthly). Includes 3 brand voices, 5 seats, and advanced collaboration.
  • Business: Custom pricing. Includes unlimited brand voices, unlimited seats, SSO, and dedicated support.

All plans come with a 7-day free trial (no credit card required).

Pros

  • Exceptional brand voice customization
  • Huge template library for marketers
  • High-quality output that requires minimal editing
  • Robust team features
  • Regular updates with new AI models

Cons

  • Expensive compared to competitors
  • No free plan (only trial)
  • Learning curve for advanced features

Detailed Review: Copy.ai

What is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai is an AI writing tool that focuses on simplicity and speed. It's designed to help you generate copy for ads, emails, social media, and websites in seconds. With a user-friendly interface and a generous free tier, it's popular among freelancers and small businesses.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Copy.ai offers a wide range of templates for various content types, including blog posts, product descriptions, and email subject lines.
  • Workflows: Automate repetitive tasks by creating workflows that combine multiple AI actions (e.g., generate a blog post outline, then expand each section).
  • Brand Voice: Available on the Pro plan and above, allowing you to define your tone and style.
  • Chat Interface: Similar to Jasper Chat, you can converse with the AI to refine content.
  • Multi-Language Support: Generate content in 25+ languages.
  • Free Plan: 2,000 words per month, access to 90+ templates, and 1 seat.

Pricing

Copy.ai has four plans:

  • Free: 2,000 words/month, limited features.
  • Starter: $36/mo (annual) or $49/mo (monthly). Unlimited words, 5 seats, and brand voice.
  • Advanced: $186/mo (annual) or $249/mo (monthly). 20 seats, workflows, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. Includes unlimited seats, SSO, and dedicated account manager.

Pros

  • Generous free plan
  • Affordable paid plans
  • Easy to use—no steep learning curve
  • Workflows automate complex tasks
  • Good for short-form content

Cons

  • Brand voice only on paid plans

  • Fewer advanced features for teams compared to Jasper

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper if:

  • You're part of a marketing team that needs to maintain a consistent brand voice across multiple channels.
  • You produce a high volume of long-form content (blog posts, whitepapers) and need advanced features like SEO integration.
  • Budget isn't a primary concern—you're willing to pay for premium quality and collaboration tools.
  • You want a tool that scales with your team (multiple brand voices, seats, and permissions).

Choose Copy.ai if:

  • You're a freelancer or small business owner looking for an affordable AI writer.
  • You need a free plan to test the waters before committing.
  • You primarily create short-form content (social media posts, ads, product descriptions).
  • You want to automate repetitive tasks with workflows without paying enterprise prices.

Bottom Line

Both Jasper and Copy.ai are excellent AI writing tools, but they cater to different needs. Jasper is the clear winner for marketing teams that prioritize brand consistency, collaboration, and high-quality long-form content. Its brand voice feature is unmatched, and the extensive template library covers every marketing scenario. Yes, it's more expensive, but for many businesses, the ROI justifies the cost.

Copy.ai, on the other hand, is a fantastic choice for budget-conscious users who need a reliable AI writer for short-form content. The free plan is generous, and the paid plans are affordable. However, if you need advanced brand voice control and team features, you might find it limiting.

Ultimately, your decision should hinge on your specific use case and budget. If you're serious about content marketing and want a tool that grows with you, go with Jasper. If you're just starting out or have limited funds, Copy.ai is a solid starting point.

FAQ

1. Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper is better for teams needing brand voice customization and advanced features, while Copy.ai is more affordable and easier for beginners.

2. Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month and access to 90+ templates. Jasper does not have a free plan, only a 7-day trial.

3. Can Jasper and Copy.ai write long-form blog posts?

Yes, both can generate long-form content. Jasper's Boss Mode and SEO integration make it more suitable for in-depth articles, while Copy.ai's long-form output may require more editing.

4. Which tool is more affordable?

Copy.ai is generally more affordable, especially with its free plan. Jasper's paid plans start at $39/mo (annual), while Copy.ai's start at $36/mo (annual).

5. Do these tools integrate with other marketing software?

Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO, Google Docs, and has a browser extension. Copy.ai offers integrations with Zapier, making it easy to connect with other apps.

6. Is there a steep learning curve for either tool?

Copy.ai is more beginner-friendly with a straightforward interface. Jasper has more features, so it may take some time to explore everything, but it's still user-friendly.

7. Can I use these tools for non-English content?

Yes, both support multiple languages. Copy.ai supports 25+ languages, and Jasper supports over 25 languages as well.
