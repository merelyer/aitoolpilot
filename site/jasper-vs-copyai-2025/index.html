Jasper Review

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a leader in AI writing since 2017. It's designed for marketing teams that need to scale content while maintaining a consistent brand voice.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper learns your brand's tone from existing content and applies it to new pieces. You can create multiple voices for different audiences.
  • Templates: Over 50 templates for blog posts, ads, emails, social media, and more.
  • Jasper Chat: An AI chatbot that helps you brainstorm, outline, and write content conversationally.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search engines.
  • Team Collaboration: Shared workspaces, user roles, and approval workflows.
  • Browser Extension: Use Jasper anywhere you write online.

Pros

  • Exceptional output quality with minimal editing.
  • Robust brand voice customization.
  • Extensive template library and integrations.
  • Strong collaboration features for teams.

Cons

  • Higher price point than competitors.
  • No free plan (only a 7-day trial).
  • Learning curve for advanced features.

Pricing

Jasper offers three plans: Creator ($39/mo), Pro ($59/mo), and Business (custom). The Creator plan is for individuals, Pro adds collaboration and SEO mode, and Business includes custom workflows and API access. All plans come with a 7-day free trial.

Who It's For

Jasper is ideal for marketing teams, agencies, and businesses that produce high volumes of content and need to maintain a consistent brand voice across all channels.

Copy.ai Review

Copy.ai launched in 2020 and quickly gained popularity for its user-friendly interface and generous free plan. It's a favorite among solopreneurs and small teams.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Covering social media, blog, email, sales, and more.
  • Free Plan: 2,000 words per month, no credit card required.
  • Chat Interface: Conversational AI for brainstorming and writing.
  • Workflows: Automate repetitive tasks with multi-step workflows.
  • Brand Voice: Available on Pro plan and above.
  • Integrations: Zapier, HubSpot, Salesforce, and more.

Pros

  • Generous free plan for testing.
  • Lower starting price for paid plans.
  • Intuitive interface, easy for beginners.
  • Workflows automate complex content tasks.

Cons

  • Output quality can be inconsistent.
  • Less robust brand voice customization.
  • Limited collaboration features.

Pricing

Copy.ai has a free plan (2,000 words/mo), Pro ($49/mo), and Team ($249/mo). The Pro plan includes 5 user seats, unlimited words, and brand voice. The Team plan adds more seats and priority support.

Who It's For

Copy.ai is perfect for solopreneurs, small businesses, and anyone who wants to try AI writing without commitment. It's also great for quick copywriting tasks like social media posts and product descriptions.

FAQ

Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper offers better brand voice customization and collaboration, making it ideal for teams. Copy.ai is more affordable and easier for beginners.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month. Jasper does not have a free plan, but offers a 7-day free trial.

Can I use Jasper for SEO?

Yes, Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO to help optimize content for search engines. Copy.ai also has SEO features but they are less robust.

Which AI writer is cheaper?

Copy.ai is cheaper overall, with a free plan and paid plans starting at $49/mo. Jasper starts at $39/mo but lacks a free tier.

Is Jasper worth the money?

For marketing teams that need high-quality, on-brand content at scale, yes. The time saved and quality output often justify the cost.

Can I switch between Jasper and Copy.ai?

Absolutely. Both offer monthly plans, so you can try one and switch if it doesn't meet your needs.
