Jasper: The AI Writer for Marketing Teams

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has established itself as the go-to AI writing assistant for marketing teams. It's designed to help businesses maintain brand voice, scale content production, and collaborate seamlessly.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper allows you to define your brand's tone, style, and personality, ensuring all generated content aligns with your guidelines.
  • Templates: Over 50 templates for various content types, including blog posts, social media ads, emails, and product descriptions.
  • Long-Form Assistant: A dedicated editor for creating long-form content like articles and guides, with features like document outlining and paragraph generation.
  • SEO Mode: Integration with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search engines.
  • Team Collaboration: Shared workspaces, brand voice assets, and document editing for teams.
  • Integrations: Connects with tools like Google Docs, WordPress, and Zapier.

Pricing

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial, after which you can choose from:

  • Creator: $39/month (annual) or $49/month (monthly) – 1 seat, 1 brand voice, 50+ templates.
  • Pro: $59/month (annual) or $69/month (monthly) – 1 seat, 3 brand voices, 50+ templates, SEO mode, and collaboration features.
  • Business: Custom pricing – for larger teams with advanced needs.

Pros

  • Excellent for maintaining brand voice across all content.
  • High-quality long-form content generation.
  • User-friendly interface with a wide range of templates.
  • Strong SEO integration with Surfer.
  • Robust team collaboration features.

Cons

  • More expensive than some competitors.

Copy.ai: The Affordable AI Copywriter

Copy.ai is a popular AI writing tool known for its generous free plan and focus on short-form copy. It's a great choice for startups, solopreneurs, and anyone who needs quick, effective copy without breaking the bank.

Key Features

  • Templates: Over 90 templates for various copywriting tasks, including social media posts, product descriptions, and email subject lines.
  • Workflow Automation: Copy.ai's Workflows allow you to automate repetitive tasks by chaining together multiple AI actions.
  • Brand Voice: Available on the Pro plan, allowing you to define your brand's tone.
  • Chat Interface: A conversational AI interface for generating content and brainstorming ideas.
  • Integrations: Connects with Zapier, HubSpot, and other tools.
  • Multilingual Support: Generate content in multiple languages.

Pricing

Copy.ai offers a free plan with limited features, plus:

  • Starter: $36/month (annual) or $49/month (monthly) – unlimited words, 5 seats, 1 brand voice.
  • Advanced: $186/month (annual) or $249/month (monthly) – unlimited words, 20 seats, 5 brand voices, workflow automation.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing – for large teams with advanced security and support needs.

Pros

  • Generous free plan (up to 2,000 words per month).

  • Wide range of templates for short-form copy.
  • Easy to use with a clean interface.

Cons

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper if:

  • You're a marketing team that needs to maintain a consistent brand voice across all content.
  • You produce a lot of long-form content like blog posts, articles, and guides.
  • You need robust collaboration features for team workflows.
  • You want seamless SEO integration to optimize content for search.
  • Budget is less of a concern, and quality is paramount.

Choose Copy.ai if:

  • You're a solopreneur, freelancer, or small business on a tight budget.
  • You primarily need short-form copy like social media posts, ads, and product descriptions.
  • You want to automate repetitive copywriting tasks with workflows.
  • You need a free plan to get started without commitment.
  • You work with multiple languages and need multilingual support.

Bottom Line

Both Jasper and Copy.ai are powerful AI writing tools, but they cater to different needs. Jasper is the better choice for marketing teams and businesses that prioritize brand voice, long-form content, and collaboration. It's a premium tool with a premium price tag, but the quality and features justify the investment.

Copy.ai is an excellent option for individuals and small teams who need affordable, high-volume short-form copy and automation. Its free plan and unlimited words on paid plans make it accessible to a wide audience.

Ultimately, the winner depends on your specific use case. For most businesses, Jasper edges out as the more comprehensive solution. But if budget is a primary concern, Copy.ai is a worthy contender.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper excels at brand voice consistency, long-form content, and team collaboration, making it ideal for marketing teams. Copy.ai is more affordable and better for short-form copy and workflow automation. For overall quality and features, Jasper is often considered superior, but Copy.ai offers great value for the price.

2. Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with limited features, including up to 2,000 words per month and access to some templates. Jasper does not have a free plan but offers a 7-day free trial.

3. Can Jasper write long-form blog posts?

Yes, Jasper has a dedicated long-form assistant that helps you create outlines, generate paragraphs, and maintain coherence throughout a blog post. It also integrates with Surfer SEO for optimization.

4. Which tool is more affordable?

Copy.ai is generally more affordable, with plans starting at $36/month (annual) and a free tier. Jasper starts at $39/month (annual) but offers more advanced features for the price.

5. Do both tools offer brand voice customization?

Yes, both offer brand voice customization, but Jasper's is more robust and available on all paid plans. Copy.ai's brand voice is only available on the Advanced plan and above.

6. Which tool is better for SEO content?

Jasper is better for SEO content due to its integration with Surfer SEO, which helps optimize content for search engines. Copy.ai lacks native SEO integration, though you can manually optimize content.
