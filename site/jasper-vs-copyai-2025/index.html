Detailed Review: Jasper

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a leader in AI copywriting since 2021. It's built for marketing teams that need to produce consistent, on-brand content at scale. With the acquisition of AI image generator Clipdrop in 2023, Jasper expanded beyond text into visuals, making it a more complete content platform.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Train Jasper on your brand's tone and style by uploading documents or pasting text. It then generates copy that sounds like you.
  • 50+ Templates: From blog posts and social media captions to product descriptions and email subject lines.
  • Long-Form Assistant: Create outlines, then expand into full articles with a document editor that supports SEO mode.
  • Jasper Chat: A conversational AI interface for brainstorming and refining content.
  • SEO Mode: Integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search rankings.
  • Team Collaboration: Shared workspaces, user roles, and approval workflows.
  • Browser Extension: Use Jasper anywhere you write online.
  • AI Images: Generate images with Jasper's built-in tools (powered by Clipdrop).

Pricing

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial (no credit card required). Paid plans start at $39/month billed annually, or $49/month billed monthly. The Pro plan is $59/month annually ($69 monthly) and includes 1 brand voice, 3 seats, and 10,000 words per month. For larger teams, the Business plan is custom-priced.

Detailed Review: Copy.ai

Copy.ai started as a simple copywriting assistant and has evolved into a full-fledged GTM (go-to-market) AI platform. In 2025, it focuses on helping sales and marketing teams automate repetitive tasks, from prospecting emails to content creation. It's known for its generous free tier and unlimited words on paid plans.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Covers everything from blog intros to LinkedIn posts and sales emails.
  • Workflows: Automate multi-step processes like generating a blog post from a keyword or creating personalized outreach sequences.
  • Brand Voice: Available on the Pro plan and above; define your tone and style.
  • Chat by Copy.ai: Conversational AI for brainstorming and content generation.
  • Infobase: Upload documents to give the AI context about your business.
  • Integrations: Connect with CRM, Slack, Zapier, and more.
  • Multi-Language Support: Generate content in 25+ languages.

Pricing

Copy.ai has a free forever plan with 2,000 words per month and limited features. The Pro plan is $49/month (billed annually) and includes unlimited words, 5 seats, and brand voice. The Sales plan (custom pricing) adds advanced workflows and CRM integrations.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper if:

  • You're a marketing team or agency needing brand consistency across multiple clients.
  • You produce long-form content (blog posts, whitepapers) regularly.
  • You need advanced collaboration and approval workflows.
  • You want SEO integration to rank higher.

Choose Copy.ai if:

  • You're a solopreneur or small business on a budget.
  • You need unlimited words without breaking the bank.
  • You focus on short-form content (social media, ads, emails).
  • You want to automate sales outreach and GTM tasks.

Bottom Line

Both Jasper and Copy.ai are powerful AI writing tools, but they serve different needs. Jasper is the premium choice for teams that demand brand consistency and advanced features—it's worth the investment if content quality and collaboration are top priorities. Copy.ai offers incredible value with a free tier and unlimited words on paid plans, making it ideal for budget-conscious users and solopreneurs.

FAQ

Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper excels at long-form content and brand voice customization, making it better for teams. Copy.ai is more affordable and offers unlimited words, making it better for individuals and small businesses.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free forever plan with 2,000 words per month and access to basic features.

Can I use Jasper for free?

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial with full access. No free plan is available after the trial.

Which tool is better for SEO content?

Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content for search engines, giving it an edge for SEO-focused writing.

Do these tools support multiple languages?

Both Jasper and Copy.ai support multiple languages, including Spanish, French, German, and more.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes, both tools offer discounts for annual billing. Jasper's annual plans save about 20%, and Copy.ai's Pro plan is $49/month billed annually vs. $59/month billed monthly.
