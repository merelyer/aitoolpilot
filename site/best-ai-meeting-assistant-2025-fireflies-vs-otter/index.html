Detailed Review: Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and analyzes conversations across 60+ platforms (including Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Webex). It also supports voice calls and even audio files you upload.

Key Features

  • Automatic Transcription: Real-time transcription with speaker identification. Supports 69+ languages (though accuracy drops for non-English).
  • AI-Powered Summaries: Generates concise meeting summaries, action items, and key talking points using its own LLM (or you can connect GPT-4).
  • Searchable Database: Every meeting is indexed, so you can search by topic, speaker, or keyword. This is a game-changer for research teams.
  • Integrations: Native integrations with Slack, Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, and more. You can push notes directly to your CRM or project management tool.
  • Conversation Intelligence: Tracks talk-to-listen ratios, sentiment, and question prompts—great for sales teams.

Pricing

  • Free: 5 hours of transcription per week (per user), unlimited meetings, 1,800 minutes of storage.
  • Pro: $18/user/month (annual) or $24 monthly. Includes unlimited transcription, 8,000 minutes of storage, and advanced analytics.
  • Business: $39/user/month (annual) or $49 monthly. Adds CRM integrations, custom vocabulary, and security controls.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. SSO, dedicated support, and unlimited storage.

Pros

  • Excellent integration ecosystem—works with almost every meeting platform
  • Robust search functionality across all meetings
  • Cheaper than Otter for the same tier
  • Allows uploading pre-recorded audio (e.g., podcasts, interviews)

Cons

  • Mobile app is clunkier than Otter's
  • Free tier is more limited than Otter's (5 hrs/week vs 300 min/meeting for Otter)
  • Non-English transcription accuracy can be hit-or-miss

Detailed Review: Otter.ai

Otter.ai has been a household name in meeting transcription for years. It specializes in real-time transcription and collaboration, with a polished mobile experience that's hard to beat.

Key Features

  • Real-Time Transcription: Works natively with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Transcribes as you speak, with live captions.
  • OtterPilot: AI that automatically joins meetings, takes notes, and sends summaries to participants.
  • Collaborative Notes: Team members can comment, highlight, and assign action items within the Otter interface.
  • Mobile-First: Excellent iOS and Android apps. You can record in-person meetings and get transcripts on the go.
  • Otter AI Chat: Ask questions about your meetings in natural language (e.g., "What was the budget decision?")

Pricing

  • Free: 300 minutes of transcription per month (30 minutes per meeting).
  • Pro: $16.99/user/month (annual) or $19.99 monthly. Includes 1,200 minutes/month, custom vocabulary, and advanced search.
  • Business: $30/user/month (annual) or $33.33 monthly. Adds admin console, usage analytics, and team features.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing. SSO, dedicated success manager, and security compliance (SOC 2, GDPR).

Pros

  • Superb mobile app—perfect for on-the-go note-taking
  • Live captions during meetings are incredibly accurate
  • Free tier offers more minutes per meeting (30 min vs Fireflies' unlimited but capped weekly)
  • User-friendly interface, minimal learning curve

Cons

  • Limited integrations—no native Salesforce or HubSpot connectors
  • Search across meetings is less powerful than Fireflies
  • Non-English support is weak (only English in most plans)
  • Storage limits are stricter on paid tiers

Who Should Use What

Fireflies.ai is best for:

  • Sales teams: Integration with CRM and conversation intelligence helps track customer sentiment.
  • Research-heavy roles: The searchable database lets you find any mention of a topic across hundreds of meetings.
  • Teams using multiple meeting platforms: Fireflies supports 60+ apps, including Webex and BlueJeans.

Otter.ai is best for:

  • Solo professionals: If you just need simple transcription and summaries, Otter is more affordable at the entry level.
  • Journalists and students: The mobile app is perfect for recording interviews or lectures.
  • Those who value a polished mobile experience: Otter's apps are slicker and more reliable than Fireflies'.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Fireflies and Otter are excellent AI meeting assistants, but they serve different needs. Fireflies.ai is the more powerful tool for teams—it scales better, integrates with your entire tech stack, and offers superior search. Otter.ai is the easier, more approachable option for individuals who want a reliable, mobile-friendly assistant.

If you're still undecided, start with the free tiers. Test both for a week, and see which one feels more natural in your daily workflow. But if you're ready to commit, we recommend Fireflies.ai for its versatility and value.

Both Fireflies and Otter offer free tiers. Fireflies gives you 5 hours of transcription per week, while Otter gives 300 minutes per month (30 minutes per meeting). For unlimited use, you'll need a paid plan.

6. How accurate are the transcriptions?

In our tests, both tools achieve 85-95% accuracy in clear audio conditions. Otter slightly edges out Fireflies in English accuracy, but Fireflies is more consistent across different accents and languages.
