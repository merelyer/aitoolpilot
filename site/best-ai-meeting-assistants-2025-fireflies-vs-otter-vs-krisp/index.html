Detailed Review of Each Option

Fireflies.ai — The All-Rounder

Overview: Fireflies is a purpose-built AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and analyzes meetings across multiple platforms (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and even phone calls). Its standout feature is the AskFred AI assistant, which lets you ask questions about your meetings in natural language.

Key Features:

  • Automatic transcription with 90%+ accuracy in English, plus support for 60+ languages.
  • AI-powered search — find any keyword, topic, or sentiment across all your meetings.
  • Action item detection — automatically extracts tasks and assigns them to specific people.
  • Integrations — native integrations with CRM tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack, plus Zapier for endless possibilities.
  • Conversation analytics — track talk-to-listen ratio, sentiment, and more for coaching purposes.

Pricing:

  • Free: 800 minutes of transcription, unlimited users, 7-day history.
  • Pro: $10/user/month (billed annually) — includes unlimited transcription, AI summaries, and integrations.
  • Business: $19/user/month — adds CRM sync, custom vocabulary, and advanced analytics.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — includes SSO, dedicated support, and more.

Pros:

  • Excellent transcription accuracy in noisy environments.
  • Robust integration ecosystem.
  • AskFred AI is genuinely useful for meeting recap.

Cons:

  • The interface can feel cluttered for some users.
  • Free tier has limited storage history.

Verdict: Fireflies is the most feature-complete assistant, making it ideal for sales teams, project managers, and anyone who lives in multiple meeting tools.

Otter.ai — The Zoom-Friendly Option

Overview: Otter is one of the most popular AI meeting assistants, especially among Zoom users. It offers real-time transcription, automated slide capture, and a collaborative workspace for notes.

Key Features:

  • Live transcription with speaker identification.
  • OtterPilot — automatically joins and records meetings, then sends summaries afterward.
  • Custom vocabulary — teach Otter your industry jargon.
  • Collaborative notes — team members can comment, highlight, and edit transcripts.
  • Integration with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams (but not as deep as Fireflies).

Pricing:

  • Basic (Free): 300 minutes/month, 30-minute per conversation limit.
  • Pro: $16.99/user/month (annual) — 1,200 minutes/month, advanced search, and more.
  • Business: $30/user/month — adds admin controls, usage analytics, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing.

Pros:

  • Clean, intuitive user interface.
  • Great for live collaboration during meetings.
  • Strong Zoom integration (works seamlessly with Zoom's native transcription).

Cons:

  • More expensive than Fireflies for similar features.
  • Limited integrations compared to Fireflies.
  • Transcription accuracy can drop with heavy accents.

Verdict: Otter is perfect for Zoom-heavy teams that prioritize ease of use and collaboration over deep integrations.

Krisp — The Noise-Canceling Sidekick

Overview: Krisp is primarily known for its AI-powered noise cancellation, but it also offers meeting transcription and live summaries. It's a lightweight tool that focuses on improving call quality and providing real-time assistance.

Key Features:

  • Noise cancellation — removes background noise, echo, and even other people's voices.
  • Live meeting summaries — get key points after each call.
  • Meeting transcription — available in the Pro plan.
  • Works with any communication app — Zoom, Teams, Slack, etc., without needing a dedicated integration.

Pricing:

  • Free: 60 minutes of noise cancellation per day, unlimited meeting summaries.
  • Pro: $8/month (annual) — unlimited noise cancellation, transcription, and more.
  • Team: $12/user/month — includes admin controls and priority support.

Pros:

  • Best-in-class noise cancellation.
  • Affordable pricing.
  • Lightweight and easy to set up.

Cons:

  • Not a full-featured meeting assistant — lacks action item detection and CRM integrations.
  • Transcription is less accurate than Fireflies or Otter.

Verdict: Krisp is ideal for remote workers who struggle with noisy environments and want a simple, budget-friendly solution.
