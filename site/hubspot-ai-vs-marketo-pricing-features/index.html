HubSpot AI vs Marketo: Pricing & Features Compared (2025)

Choosing between HubSpot and Marketo is like picking between a Swiss Army knife and a scalpel. Both are powerful, but they're designed for different surgeons. If you're a growing business looking for an all-in-one platform with built-in AI, HubSpot might be your jam. If you're an enterprise needing deep automation and sophisticated lead management, Marketo (now Adobe Marketo Engage) could be your weapon. But here's the catch: pricing varies wildly, and a wrong choice can cost you thousands and months of wasted effort. In this comprehensive comparison, we'll break down the features, pricing, and real-world usability of both platforms, so you can make an informed decision without the headache.

TL;DR: Our #1 Pick

For most small-to-mid-sized businesses and even some enterprises, HubSpot AI is the clear winner due to its user-friendly interface, transparent pricing, and integrated AI tools. It offers a free CRM and a free tier, making it accessible for testing. Marketo is powerful but complex and pricey—better suited for large enterprises with dedicated marketing operations teams and complex lead scoring needs. If you want to get started quickly and scale without a steep learning curve, go with HubSpot.

Detailed Review of Each Option

HubSpot AI (Marketing Hub)

HubSpot has evolved from a simple CRM to a full-fledged AI-powered marketing platform. Its AI features include content generation (blog ideas, email copy), predictive lead scoring, and even a chatbot builder. The platform is known for its intuitive interface, seamless integration with the free CRM, and a marketplace of apps.

Key Features

  • AI Content Assistant: Generates blog posts, social media captions, and email subject lines based on your inputs and brand voice.
  • Predictive Lead Scoring: Uses AI to identify which leads are most likely to convert, so you can prioritize your outreach.
  • Conversational Bots: Build AI-powered chatbots that qualify leads and book meetings automatically.
  • Marketing Automation: Visual workflow builder for email sequences, lead nurturing, and multi-channel campaigns.
  • Analytics & Reporting: Real-time dashboards with AI-driven insights on campaign performance.

Pricing

HubSpot offers a free CRM and a free tier for Marketing Hub with limited features. Paid plans start at $20/month (Starter) and scale to $1,200+/month for Enterprise. The Pro plan (around $890/month) includes all AI features and is popular among mid-sized businesses.

Pros

  • Extremely user-friendly; no coding required.
  • Transparent pricing with a free tier.
  • AI features are integrated natively, no extra cost.
  • Excellent customer support and extensive knowledge base.
  • All-in-one: CRM, marketing, sales, and service tools.

Cons

  • Can become expensive as you add contacts and features.
  • Advanced automation (like custom objects) is only in Enterprise.
  • Some users find the AI content generation generic without heavy editing.

Marketo Engage (Adobe)

Marketo, now part of Adobe Experience Cloud, is a powerhouse for B2B marketing automation. It excels at complex lead management, multi-touch attribution, and account-based marketing (ABM). It’s not for the faint-hearted—the learning curve is steep, and the pricing is opaque (you need to contact sales). But for enterprises with dedicated ops teams, it’s a beast.

Key Features

  • Lead Management: Advanced lead scoring, routing, and lifecycle management.
  • Email Marketing: Dynamic content, A/B testing, and sophisticated personalization.
  • Account-Based Marketing: Target specific accounts with personalized ads and content.
  • Revenue Attribution: Multi-touch attribution models to tie revenue to marketing efforts.
  • AI Capabilities: Adobe Sensei powers predictive audiences, content recommendations, and lead scoring.

Pricing

Marketo does not publish pricing. Based on industry reports, it starts around $1,200/month for a basic package (10,000 contacts) and can go up to $5,000+/month for enterprise tiers. You must request a quote, and there's no free tier.

Pros

  • Powerful automation and lead scoring capabilities.
  • Robust ABM and attribution features.
  • Scalable for large enterprises with complex needs.
  • Deep integration with Adobe Experience Cloud.

Cons

  • Steep learning curve; requires technical expertise.
  • Expensive, with hidden costs for add-ons and overages.
  • Outdated UI compared to modern tools.
  • Customer support can be slow and less helpful.

Who Should Use What?

Choose HubSpot AI if:

  • You're a startup or SMB with limited marketing resources.
  • You want an all-in-one platform that's easy to learn and use.
  • You need AI assistance for content creation and lead scoring without a huge budget.
  • You value transparent pricing and a free tier to test the waters.

Choose Marketo if:

  • You're a large enterprise with a dedicated marketing operations team.
  • You need advanced lead scoring, ABM, and multi-touch attribution.
  • You're already invested in Adobe's ecosystem.
  • You have the budget to pay for premium support and add-ons.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both HubSpot AI and Marketo are excellent tools, but they serve different audiences. For most businesses, HubSpot AI offers a better ROI with its lower entry cost, ease of use, and built-in AI features. Marketo is a high-investment, high-reward platform for enterprises that need advanced capabilities and have the team to manage them. If you're ready to start, we recommend trying HubSpot's free tier first. If you outgrow it, you can always consider Marketo later.

FAQ

1. Is HubSpot AI cheaper than Marketo?

Yes, HubSpot AI is significantly cheaper. HubSpot offers a free tier and paid plans starting at $20/month, while Marketo starts at around $1,200/month. For SMBs, HubSpot is clearly more affordable.

2. Can I integrate HubSpot with Marketo?

No, they are competitors and don't integrate natively. You would need a middleware solution like Zapier or custom APIs to sync data, which is not recommended due to complexity.

3. Which tool has better AI features?

HubSpot's AI is more user-friendly and accessible, with features like content generation and predictive scoring included in the platform. Marketo uses Adobe Sensei, which is powerful but requires more technical expertise to leverage effectively.

4. What is the learning curve for Marketo vs HubSpot?

HubSpot has a gentle learning curve; most users can get started in a day. Marketo has a steep learning curve and often requires formal training or an experienced admin.

5. Can I migrate from Marketo to HubSpot?

Yes, HubSpot offers migration services and tools to help you move your data from Marketo. The process can be time-consuming, but HubSpot's support team assists with the transition.

6. Does HubSpot offer a free trial for paid plans?

Yes, HubSpot offers a 14-day free trial for its paid Marketing Hub plans. You can test the features before committing.

7. Which tool is better for B2B vs B2C?

Marketo is traditionally stronger for B2B due to its lead management and ABM features. HubSpot works well for both B2B and B2C, but it's especially popular among B2B companies for its CRM integration.

Ready to Make the Move?

If you're leaning towards HubSpot AI, start with their free tier to see if it fits your needs. If you need more advanced features, their paid plans offer a 14-day trial. For Marketo, you'll need to contact Adobe for a demo and pricing. Weigh the costs and benefits carefully—your marketing team will thank you.
