Detailed Review: Notion AI

Notion AI is not a standalone product; it's an add-on to Notion's all-in-one workspace. Launched in early 2023, it has evolved rapidly, now offering features like auto-fill, Q&A over your notes, and AI-powered project management.

Key Features

  • Writing assistance: Generate drafts, improve tone, fix grammar, and summarize long documents directly in Notion.
  • Q&A over your workspace: Ask questions like "What's the status of the marketing campaign?" and get answers from your notes and databases.
  • Auto-fill and database automation: Automatically fill properties in databases, generate summaries for rows, and create project plans.
  • Meeting notes and action items: Turn meeting transcripts into structured notes and action items.
  • Integrations: Works with Slack, Google Drive, and more, but AI features are most powerful within Notion's ecosystem.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Seamless integration with your docs, no context switching, great for team collaboration, and AI suggestions are context-aware.
  • Cons: Requires a Notion subscription (not cheap for large teams), AI can be slower than ChatGPT, and it's less flexible for open-ended conversation.

Pricing

Notion's free plan includes basic features. To access Notion AI, you need to add it to any paid plan:

  • Plus Plan: $10/month per user (annual) + $8/month per user for AI (total $18)
  • Business Plan: $15/month per user (annual) + $8/month per user for AI (total $23)

Detailed Review: ChatGPT

ChatGPT, developed by OpenAI, is the world's most popular AI chatbot. In 2025, it's powered by GPT-4, offering advanced reasoning, image generation (via DALL-E), and custom GPTs. It's a versatile tool for individuals and businesses.

Key Features

  • Conversational AI: Engage in deep, multi-turn conversations on any topic—from philosophy to coding.
  • Web browsing and plugins: Search the web, use plugins for data analysis, and integrate with third-party apps (via API).
  • Custom GPTs: Create tailored AI assistants for specific tasks (e.g., a legal assistant, a marketing copywriter).
  • Image generation: Generate images with DALL-E 3 (in Plus and Enterprise).
  • Code interpreter: Run Python code, analyze datasets, and create charts.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Incredible versatility, strong reasoning, constantly updated, and has a generous free tier.
  • Cons: No native workspace for docs or project management, requires copying/pasting into other tools, and can sometimes produce inaccurate info (hallucinations).

Pricing

  • Free: Limited GPT-3.5 access (with occasional GPT-4 messages)
  • Plus: $20/month (GPT-4, faster responses, DALL-E, plugins)
  • Team: $25/month per user (min 2 users)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing (unlimited GPT-4, dedicated support)

Who Should Use What

Choosing between Notion AI and ChatGPT depends on your primary workflow:

  • Project Managers and Teams: Use Notion AI if your team lives in Notion—it turns meetings into action items, answers questions about your projects, and keeps everything in one place.
  • Writers and Content Creators: ChatGPT is superior for generating fresh ideas, drafting long-form content, and refining tone. Notion AI is great for editing and summarizing existing drafts.
  • Developers: ChatGPT excels at code generation and debugging. Notion AI can help document code but isn't designed for heavy programming tasks.
  • Researchers and Analysts: ChatGPT's web browsing and code interpreter make it ideal for data analysis and research. Notion AI can organize your findings.
  • Small Business Owners: If you need a simple, affordable AI for daily tasks, ChatGPT's free tier is a great start. Notion AI is better if you need structured knowledge management.

Bottom Line: Which Should You Choose?

There's no one-size-fits-all answer. Notion AI shines when you're deeply embedded in Notion's ecosystem—it reduces friction and boosts productivity without leaving your workspace. ChatGPT is a powerhouse for general AI tasks, offering more advanced reasoning and flexibility. For most professionals, we recommend starting with Notion AI if you're already using Notion, and adding ChatGPT Plus as a complementary tool for complex tasks. If you're new to both, try ChatGPT's free version first to see its capabilities, then consider Notion AI if you need integrated documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI better than ChatGPT?

Notion AI is better for integrated writing and knowledge management within Notion. ChatGPT is better for general-purpose AI, research, and coding. The best choice depends on your workflow.

Can Notion AI replace ChatGPT?

No, Notion AI is not a replacement for ChatGPT. It's an add-on that enhances Notion with AI capabilities, while ChatGPT is a standalone assistant. They can complement each other.

Is Notion AI free?

Notion AI is not free. It's an add-on that costs $8/month per user (annual billing) on top of a paid Notion plan. There's no free trial for AI, but you can try Notion free without AI.

What is the cost of ChatGPT Plus vs Notion AI?

ChatGPT Plus costs $20/month per user. Notion AI costs $8/month per user (add-on) plus the base Notion plan (starting at $10/month). So Notion AI is cheaper if you need a workspace, but ChatGPT Plus is cheaper if you only need AI.

Which AI tool is best for writing long-form content?

ChatGPT is generally better for generating long-form content from scratch, thanks to its advanced language model. Notion AI is better for summarizing, editing, and improving existing content.
