Detailed Review: Jasper

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a dominant player since 2021. It's known for its high-quality output, enterprise-level features, and strong focus on brand voice. In 2025, Jasper continues to evolve with AI models that understand context and tone remarkably well.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Train Jasper on your brand's style, tone, and vocabulary to ensure every piece of content sounds like you.
  • Campaign Manager: A workflow tool that helps you plan, create, and publish multi-channel campaigns in one place.
  • Jasper Chat: A conversational AI interface for quick brainstorming, rewriting, or answering questions.
  • SEO Integration: Surfer SEO integration for optimizing content (available on higher tiers).
  • Templates & Recipes: Over 50 templates for blog posts, ads, emails, and more.
  • Collaboration: Team workspaces, approval workflows, and document sharing.

Pricing

Jasper offers three main plans (billed annually):

  • Creator: $49/month – 1 user, 1 brand voice, 20,000 words/month.
  • Pro: $99/month – 5 users, 3 brand voices, 50,000 words/month, plus SEO mode.
  • Business: Custom pricing – unlimited users, advanced admin controls, API access.

There's a 7-day free trial (no credit card required).
