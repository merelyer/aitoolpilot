Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

What is Intercom Fin?

Intercom Fin is an AI chatbot built on OpenAI's GPT-4 technology, designed to handle customer inquiries with human-like understanding. Launched in 2023, Fin has quickly become a favorite for its ability to resolve complex questions without human intervention. It's integrated into Intercom's customer service platform, which includes live chat, email, and messaging tools.

Key Features

  • Advanced AI with GPT-4: Fin understands context and nuance, providing accurate answers.
  • High Resolution Rate: Resolves over 50% of queries out of the box, reducing workload.
  • Seamless Integration: Works natively with Intercom's suite and integrates with Slack, Salesforce, etc.
  • Continuous Learning: Improves over time by learning from interactions and feedback.
  • Multi-channel Support: Handles chat, email, and messaging apps.
  • Transparent Pricing: Pay per resolution, making costs predictable.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully answers a customer query without human intervention. There's no additional platform fee if you're already on Intercom. For new users, Intercom's starter plan begins at $74/month (billed annually) for essential features, plus Fin's resolution fees.

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

What is Zendesk AI?

Zendesk AI is a suite of AI-powered tools integrated into the Zendesk customer service platform. It includes chatbots, agent assistance, and analytics. Zendesk has been a leader in customer support for years, and its AI offerings are designed to enhance efficiency and personalization.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Chatbot: Automates responses and routes complex issues to agents.
  • Agent Assist: Provides real-time suggestions and knowledge base articles to agents.
  • Advanced Analytics: Insights into customer behavior and bot performance.
  • Customization: Highly configurable workflows and bot behavior.
  • Integration Ecosystem: Connects with thousands of apps via Zendesk Marketplace.
  • Multi-channel Support: Handles chat, email, social media, and more.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually) and includes AI features. For advanced AI capabilities like intelligent triage and bot building, you may need the Suite Enterprise plan at $115 per agent/month. Zendesk also offers a free trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the main difference between Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI?

Intercom Fin is a standalone AI chatbot focused on high resolution rates with pay-per-resolution pricing. Zendesk AI is a suite of AI tools integrated into Zendesk, offering customization and analytics but requiring a Zendesk subscription.

2. Which chatbot resolves more queries automatically?

Intercom Fin claims a resolution rate of over 50% out of the box. Zendesk AI's resolution rate depends on training and customization, but with proper setup, it can achieve similar results.

3. Is Intercom Fin more expensive than Zendesk AI?

It depends on volume. Intercom Fin charges $0.99 per resolution plus platform fees, while Zendesk AI charges per agent. For high-volume, low-complexity queries, Fin may be cost-effective; for low-volume, complex queries, Zendesk might be cheaper.

4. Can I use Intercom Fin without Intercom's platform?

No, Fin is integrated into Intercom's customer service platform and requires an Intercom subscription.

5. Does Zendesk AI offer a free trial?

Yes, Zendesk offers a free trial of its Suite plans, which include AI features.

6. Which is better for small businesses?

Intercom Fin is often better for small businesses due to its ease of setup and predictable pricing. Zendesk AI may be overkill unless you need extensive customization.
