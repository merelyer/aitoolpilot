Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

Intercom has been a leader in customer messaging for years, and Fin is their latest AI-powered chatbot designed to resolve customer queries instantly. Built on OpenAI's GPT-4, Fin is trained on your help center content and past conversations to deliver accurate, human-like responses.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Resolutions: Fin can handle up to 50% of support conversations without human intervention, according to Intercom.
  • Seamless Integration: Works natively with Intercom's inbox, help center, and messaging channels.
  • Continuous Learning: Fin improves over time by learning from resolved conversations and feedback.
  • Multi-Channel Support: Deploy Fin on your website, in-app, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.
  • Analytics Dashboard: Track resolution rates, customer satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

Pricing

Intercom Fin is priced at $0.99 per resolution (a resolution is counted when Fin successfully resolves a customer's query without human handoff). This pay-per-resolution model is cost-effective for businesses with high volumes of simple queries. However, you'll also need an Intercom plan (starting at $74/month for Essential) to use Fin.

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

Zendesk is a veteran in customer service software, and Zendesk AI (formerly Answer Bot) is their AI-powered solution for automating support. It leverages machine learning to suggest answers, route tickets, and even resolve conversations automatically.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Automation: Zendesk AI can suggest responses, triage tickets, and resolve common queries.
  • Advanced Routing: Uses AI to route tickets to the right agent based on content and sentiment.
  • Agent Assist: Provides real-time suggestions to human agents, improving efficiency.
  • Multi-Channel Support: Works across email, chat, voice, and social media.
  • Analytics & Reporting: In-depth insights into AI performance and customer satisfaction.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually) and includes AI features. For advanced AI capabilities like intelligent triage, you may need the Suite Enterprise plan ($115/agent/month) or the AI add-on (starting at $50/agent/month).

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the main difference between Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI?

Intercom Fin is an AI chatbot built into the Intercom platform, priced per resolution. Zendesk AI is a suite of AI features within Zendesk, priced per agent. Fin is often easier to deploy for pure chatbot use, while Zendesk AI offers broader automation across support workflows.

2. Can I use Intercom Fin without an Intercom subscription?

No, Fin requires an Intercom plan. However, you can start with a 14-day free trial to test both.

3. Is Zendesk AI included in all Zendesk plans?

Basic AI features are included in the Suite Professional plan and above. Advanced features like intelligent triage may require higher tiers or add-ons.

4. Which tool has a better resolution rate?

Intercom claims Fin can resolve up to 50% of conversations, while Zendesk AI's resolution rate varies by use case. In our tests, Fin performed slightly better on simple queries.

5. Which is more cost-effective for small businesses?

Intercom Fin's pay-per-resolution model can be more affordable for small businesses with low to moderate query volumes. Zendesk's per-agent pricing may be cheaper for teams with high volumes but few agents.

6. Do both tools support multiple languages?

Yes, both Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI support multiple languages, though Fin's language detection and response quality are particularly strong.

7. Can I integrate these chatbots with other tools?

Both offer extensive integrations. Intercom Fin integrates seamlessly with Intercom's ecosystem and other apps via APIs. Zendesk AI integrates with the Zendesk Suite and third-party apps.
