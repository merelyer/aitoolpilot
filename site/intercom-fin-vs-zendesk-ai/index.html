Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

Intercom Fin is the AI chatbot component of Intercom's Customer Service Platform. Launched in 2023, Fin leverages large language models (LLMs) to resolve customer queries instantly, drawing from your help center, previous conversations, and internal articles. It's designed to feel like a natural extension of your support team.

Key Features

  • Instant Answers: Fin uses generative AI to provide accurate responses to customer questions, pulling from your knowledge base and past interactions.
  • Seamless Handoff: When Fin can't resolve an issue, it smoothly transfers the conversation to a human agent with full context.
  • Continuous Learning: Fin improves over time by learning from resolved conversations and feedback.
  • Multi-channel Support: Works across chat, email, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Messenger.
  • Analytics Dashboard: Track resolution rates, customer satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully answers a customer query without human intervention. There's no additional platform fee beyond your existing Intercom subscription (starting at $74/month for Essential). This pricing is transparent and scales with your support volume, making it attractive for businesses of all sizes.

Pros

  • High automation rate: Fin resolves up to 50% of queries out of the box, reducing workload significantly.
  • Easy setup: Connects to your existing help center and starts learning immediately.
  • Transparent pricing: Pay only for successful resolutions, aligning costs with value.
  • Unified platform: Integrates seamlessly with Intercom's inbox, ticketing, and automation tools.
  • Fast deployment: Businesses can go live in days, not weeks.

Cons

  • Requires Intercom subscription: You must be an Intercom customer to use Fin.
  • Limited customisation: While Fin learns from your content, deep customisation of its behaviour is limited compared to Zendesk AI.
  • Cost can add up: At scale, $0.99 per resolution may become expensive for high-volume support teams.
  • Newer entrant: Fin is relatively new, so some advanced features are still maturing.

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

Zendesk AI is a suite of AI-powered tools integrated into the Zendesk customer service platform. It includes Answer Bot for automated responses, intelligent triage for routing, and agent assist features. Zendesk has been a stalwart in customer support for over a decade, and its AI capabilities are deeply embedded in its ecosystem.

Key Features

  • Answer Bot: Automatically responds to customer inquiries using your help center content, with the ability to escalate to human agents.
  • Intelligent Triage: Uses AI to automatically route tickets to the right agent or team based on content and intent.
  • Agent Assist: Provides real-time suggestions and relevant articles to human agents during conversations.
  • Advanced Analytics: Robust reporting on bot performance, resolution rates, and customer satisfaction.
  • Customisation: Extensive APIs and developer tools allow deep integration and custom workflows.
  • Multi-channel Support: Works across chat, email, voice, and social media.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as add-ons or bundled within Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually) and includes AI features like Answer Bot and intelligent triage. For advanced AI like agent assist, you may need the Suite Enterprise plan ($115 per agent/month) or AI add-ons. Pricing is per agent, which can be predictable for teams but may be costly for large support organisations.

Pros

  • Deep customisation: Extensive APIs and developer tools allow tailoring to complex workflows.
  • Robust analytics: Comprehensive reporting helps optimise bot performance and agent productivity.
  • Mature ecosystem: Zendesk has a large partner network and integrations with thousands of apps.
  • Scalable: Proven in enterprise environments with high ticket volumes.
  • Comprehensive AI suite: Covers automation, agent assistance, and analytics in one platform.

Cons

  • Complex setup: Requires significant configuration and training to maximise AI effectiveness.
  • Higher entry cost: AI features are only available on higher-tier plans, making it less accessible for small businesses.
  • Per-agent pricing: Can become expensive as your team grows, regardless of bot usage.
  • Steeper learning curve: The platform's depth means it takes time to master.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the main difference between Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI?

Intercom Fin is an AI chatbot built into the Intercom platform, focusing on fast deployment and high automation with per-resolution pricing. Zendesk AI is a suite of AI tools within Zendesk, offering deep customisation and analytics, typically priced per agent.

2. Can I use Intercom Fin without an Intercom subscription?

No, Intercom Fin is an add-on to Intercom's customer service platform. You need an active Intercom subscription to use Fin.

3. Is Zendesk AI included in all Zendesk plans?

No, AI features like Answer Bot and intelligent triage are available on higher-tier plans such as Suite Professional and Enterprise, or as add-ons. Lower-tier plans have limited or no AI capabilities.

4. Which chatbot resolves more queries automatically?

Intercom Fin boasts a higher out-of-the-box resolution rate (up to 50%) due to its advanced LLM and seamless integration with your knowledge base. Zendesk AI's performance depends heavily on configuration but can achieve similar results with proper setup.

5. How does pricing compare for a small team of 5 agents?

For a small team, Intercom Fin might be more cost-effective: Intercom Essential at $74/month plus $0.99 per resolution. Zendesk AI would require Suite Professional at $55/agent/month, totaling $275/month for 5 agents.

6. Can both chatbots hand off to human agents?

Yes, both Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI seamlessly transfer conversations to human agents when needed, providing full context.
