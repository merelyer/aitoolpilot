Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

Intercom Fin isn’t just a chatbot—it’s a fully fledged AI agent that can handle complex conversations, pull from your help center, and even take actions like processing refunds or updating orders. Launched in 2023, Fin has quickly become the gold standard for AI-powered customer service.

Key Features

  • Advanced NLP: Understands natural language, slang, and multiple languages out of the box.
  • Knowledge Base Integration: Connects to Intercom Articles, Zendesk Help Center, and even external URLs to learn your content.
  • Actionable Responses: Can execute workflows (e.g., cancel subscription, check order status) via APIs.
  • Seamless Handoff: Escalates to human agents with full context when needed.
  • Analytics Dashboard: Tracks resolution rate, containment, and customer satisfaction.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully answers a customer’s query without human intervention. There’s no monthly platform fee for Fin itself—you only pay when it works. However, you’ll still need an Intercom plan (starting at $39/month per seat) to use it.

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

Zendesk AI is a suite of AI-powered tools added to the Zendesk platform. It includes a chatbot (Answer Bot), agent assistance (suggested replies), and analytics. While Zendesk has been in the support game for decades, its AI offerings have been slower to evolve compared to Intercom Fin.

Key Features

  • Answer Bot: Automatically responds to common questions using your help center content.
  • Agent Assist: Provides real-time suggestions to human agents.
  • Sentiment Analysis: Detects customer mood and escalates negative interactions.
  • Workflow Builder: Allows some automation of routine tasks.
  • Integration with Zendesk Suite: Native to Zendesk, so no third-party connectors needed.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk plans. The Advanced plan (which includes AI) starts at $50 per agent/month (billed annually). For the AI add-on specifically, you’ll need the Zendesk AI Advanced package, which is custom-priced but typically ranges from $20–$50 per agent/month on top of your base plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the main difference between Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI?

Intercom Fin is a standalone AI agent that resolves customer queries end-to-end, while Zendesk AI is a suite of AI features (including a chatbot) integrated into the Zendesk platform. Fin generally achieves higher resolution rates and offers more advanced conversational abilities.

2. Is Intercom Fin really worth the per-resolution cost?

For many companies, yes. At $0.99 per resolution, you’re paying only when the AI successfully handles a query. If Fin resolves 1,000 tickets per month, that’s $990—often far less than hiring additional agents. However, high-volume businesses should calculate their potential costs carefully.

3. Can I use Zendesk AI without a Zendesk subscription?

No. Zendesk AI is an add-on to Zendesk plans, so you must have an active Zendesk subscription to use it.

4. Which platform has better multilingual support?

Intercom Fin supports over 40 languages out of the box and performs well in non-English conversations. Zendesk AI also supports multiple languages, but its accuracy in non-English languages is generally lower.

5. How long does it take to set up each chatbot?

Intercom Fin can be set up in a few hours—simply connect your knowledge base and customize the bot’s appearance. Zendesk AI requires more configuration and typically takes a few days to train and integrate fully.

6. Do either of these tools offer a free trial?

Yes, both offer free trials. Intercom provides a 14-day free trial of Fin, and Zendesk offers a trial of its suite including AI features.
