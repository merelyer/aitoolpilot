Intercom Fin Review: The AI Chatbot That Actually Resolves Tickets

Intercom launched Fin in 2023 and it quickly became one of the most talked-about AI chatbots. Unlike traditional bots that rely on decision trees, Fin uses large language models (LLMs) to understand and answer customer questions naturally. It's built directly into Intercom's customer service platform, so it works seamlessly with your existing helpdesk.

Key Features

  • AI-powered resolutions: Fin can resolve up to 50% of support tickets without human intervention. It pulls answers from your help center, internal docs, and past conversations.
  • Multilingual support: Fin speaks over 40 languages, making it great for global teams.
  • Easy setup: Connect your knowledge base and Fin starts learning. No complex coding required.
  • Seamless handoff: When Fin can't help, it escalates to a human agent with full context.
  • Analytics dashboard: Track resolution rates, customer satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully answers a customer's question without needing a human. You also need an Intercom plan, which starts at $39 per seat/month for the Essential plan. This pricing is transparent and scales with your usage, making it predictable.

  • Simple, usage-based pricing

  • Excellent multilingual capabilities
  • Fast setup and training

Zendesk AI Review: Powerful but Complex

Zendesk is a veteran in customer support, and its AI offerings have evolved significantly. Zendesk AI isn't a single product; it's a suite of AI features integrated across Zendesk's platform, including bots, agent assistance, and analytics. In 2025, Zendesk AI includes Answer Bot, Agent Workflow, and Advanced AI add-ons.

Key Features

  • Answer Bot: Automatically responds to customer inquiries using your help center content.
  • Agent Assist: Provides real-time suggestions to human agents, improving efficiency.
  • Sentiment analysis: Detects customer mood and prioritizes urgent tickets.
  • Workflow automation: Routes tickets, tags conversations, and triggers actions based on AI insights.
  • Integration with Zendesk Suite: Works natively with Zendesk's ticketing system, Guide, and Chat.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is not sold separately; it's bundled into higher-tier Zendesk plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually) and includes basic AI features. Advanced AI capabilities like Answer Bot require the Suite Enterprise plan, which starts at $89 per agent/month. There are also add-ons like Advanced AI for $50 per agent/month. This pricing model can get expensive quickly.

Pros

  • Comprehensive AI suite covering bots, agent assist, and analytics
  • Deep integration with Zendesk's robust ticketing system
  • Scalable for large enterprises
  • Strong reporting and customization options

Cons

  • Expensive, especially for small businesses
  • AI features are fragmented and require multiple add-ons
  • Setup and training can be complex
  • Lower out-of-the-box resolution rates compared to Fin
