Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

Intercom has long been a leader in customer messaging, and Fin is its AI-powered chatbot designed to resolve customer queries instantly. Launched in 2023, Fin has quickly gained traction for its impressive accuracy and out-of-the-box functionality.

Key Features

  • GPT-4 Powered: Fin leverages OpenAI's GPT-4 to understand and respond to complex queries naturally.
  • High Resolution Rate: Intercom claims Fin can resolve up to 50% of support tickets without human intervention, and some customers report even higher rates.
  • Easy Setup: Fin trains on your existing help center articles and past conversations, requiring minimal configuration.
  • Omnichannel Support: Works across chat, email, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.
  • Continuous Learning: Fin improves over time by learning from every interaction, including those escalated to human agents.
  • Seamless Handoff: When Fin can't resolve a query, it smoothly transfers to a human agent with full context.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully resolves a customer's issue without human intervention. There's no upfront cost for the AI itself; you only pay for results. This makes it accessible for businesses of all sizes, but costs can add up if you have high volumes.

  • Integrated with Intercom's powerful suite (Inbox, Help Center, etc.)

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

Zendesk is a veteran in the customer service space, and its AI capabilities have been steadily evolving. Zendesk AI encompasses several features, including intelligent triage, answer bot, and agent assist, all aimed at improving efficiency and automating support.

Key Features

  • Intelligent Triage: Automatically routes tickets to the right agent based on intent, sentiment, and language.
  • Answer Bot: Provides instant answers to common questions by pulling from your help center content.
  • Agent Assist: Suggests responses and articles to human agents in real-time, reducing handling time.
  • Sentiment Analysis: Detects customer sentiment to prioritize urgent or negative interactions.
  • Workflow Automation: Triggers actions based on AI-detected intents, such as escalating to a specialist.
  • Integration with Zendesk Suite: Natively integrated with Zendesk Support, Chat, Talk, and more.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually) and includes AI features. For advanced AI like Answer Bot, you may need the Suite Enterprise plan ($115/agent/month) or purchase the AI add-on. Pricing is per agent, which can be costly for large teams.

Pros

  • Deep integration with Zendesk's robust ticketing system
  • Comprehensive AI features beyond just chatbots
  • Scalable for large enterprises with complex workflows
  • Strong analytics and reporting

Cons

  • Steeper learning curve and longer setup time
  • AI capabilities are more fragmented; not as unified as Fin
  • Higher total cost for small teams due to per-agent pricing
  • Answer Bot's accuracy may not match Fin's out-of-the-box performance

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the main difference between Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI?

Intercom Fin is a standalone AI chatbot that uses GPT-4 to resolve customer queries end-to-end, while Zendesk AI is a suite of AI features (like Answer Bot and intelligent triage) integrated into the Zendesk platform. Fin focuses on automation, whereas Zendesk AI enhances agent productivity.

2. Can Intercom Fin handle multiple languages?

Yes, Fin supports multiple languages and can automatically detect and respond in the customer's language.

3. How does Zendesk AI pricing work?

Zendesk AI is included in the Suite Professional plan ($55/agent/month) and higher. Some advanced AI features may require the Enterprise plan or an add-on. Pricing is per agent, per month.

4. Is there a free trial for these AI chatbots?

Both Intercom and Zendesk offer free trials. Intercom offers a 14-day free trial of its platform, and Fin can be tested during that period. Zendesk also offers a free trial of its Suite plans.

5. Which one is more accurate?

Intercom Fin generally boasts higher resolution rates out of the box, thanks to its GPT-4 engine and training on your help center content. Zendesk AI's accuracy depends on how well you configure it and the quality of your knowledge base.

6. Can I use Zendesk AI without Zendesk Support?

No, Zendesk AI is designed to work within the Zendesk Suite, so you need a Zendesk subscription to use it.

7. Does Intercom Fin replace human agents?

No, Fin is designed to augment human agents by handling routine inquiries and freeing them for complex issues. It seamlessly hands off conversations to humans when needed.
