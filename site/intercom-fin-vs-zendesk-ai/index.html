Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

What is Intercom Fin?

Fin is Intercom's AI chatbot designed to resolve customer queries instantly. Built on large language models and trained on your help center content, Fin can handle everything from simple FAQs to complex troubleshooting. It's available as an add-on to Intercom's customer service platform.

Key Features

  • Instant Resolution: Fin uses natural language processing to understand and resolve up to 50% of support tickets without human intervention.
  • Easy Setup: Connect your knowledge base (Zendesk, Salesforce, or Intercom articles) and Fin starts learning immediately.
  • Continuous Learning: Fin improves over time by analyzing unresolved conversations and suggesting new content.
  • Seamless Handoff: When Fin can't resolve a query, it smoothly transfers to a human agent with full context.
  • Multichannel Support: Works across chat, email, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.

Pricing

Intercom Fin is priced at $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully resolves a customer's query without human intervention. There's no upfront cost or monthly fee for Fin itself; you only pay for results. However, you'll need an Intercom subscription (starting at $74/month for Essential) to use Fin.

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

What is Zendesk AI?

Zendesk AI is a suite of AI-powered tools integrated into the Zendesk platform. It includes chatbots, agent assistance, and analytics to automate customer service. Zendesk AI leverages the data in your Zendesk account to provide personalized responses and streamline workflows.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Chatbot: Automates responses to common questions and routes complex issues to agents.
  • Agent Assist: Provides real-time suggestions and relevant knowledge articles to human agents.
  • Sentiment Analysis: Detects customer sentiment to prioritize urgent tickets.
  • Workflow Automation: Uses AI to triage tickets, assign priorities, and automate repetitive tasks.
  • Integration with Zendesk Suite: Native integration with all Zendesk products for a unified experience.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually) and includes basic AI features. Advanced AI capabilities like the AI chatbot are available in the Suite Enterprise plan (starting at $115 per agent/month) or as an add-on to lower tiers. Pricing can get complex, so it's best to contact sales for a quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the main difference between Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI?

Intercom Fin is a standalone AI chatbot focused on resolving customer queries automatically, while Zendesk AI is a broader suite of AI tools integrated into the Zendesk platform, including chatbots, agent assist, and analytics.

2. Can I use Intercom Fin without an Intercom subscription?

No, Fin is an add-on to Intercom's customer service platform, so you need an Intercom subscription to use it.

3. How does pricing work for Intercom Fin?

Fin is priced at $0.99 per resolution, where a resolution is counted when Fin successfully resolves a customer's query without human intervention. You only pay for successful outcomes.

4. Is Zendesk AI suitable for small businesses?

Zendesk AI can be cost-prohibitive for small businesses, as advanced features require higher-tier plans. However, basic AI features are available in the Suite Professional plan, which may be sufficient for smaller teams.

5. Which tool has a better resolution rate?

Intercom Fin boasts a resolution rate of up to 50% out of the box, while Zendesk AI's performance varies based on configuration and data quality. Fin is generally considered to have a higher initial resolution rate.

6. Can I integrate Intercom Fin with other help desks?

Fin works best within the Intercom ecosystem. While you can import content from other help desks, full functionality is achieved when using Intercom as your primary support platform.

7. Does Zendesk AI offer a free trial?

Yes, Zendesk offers a free trial of its suite, including AI features, so you can test it before committing.
