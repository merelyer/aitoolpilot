Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

What is Intercom Fin?

Fin is Intercom's AI chatbot designed to resolve customer queries instantly, 24/7. Powered by GPT-4 and trained on your help center content, Fin can handle complex, multi-turn conversations without human intervention. It's not just a chatbot—it's a full-fledged AI agent that learns from your existing support data.

Key Features

  • Advanced AI engine: Uses GPT-4 to understand and respond to nuanced queries.
  • Self-learning: Continuously improves by learning from past conversations and content updates.
  • Omnichannel support: Works across chat, email, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Messenger.
  • Seamless handoff: Transfers complex issues to human agents with full context.
  • Analytics dashboard: Tracks resolution rates, customer satisfaction, and more.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully resolves a customer's query without human intervention. There's no monthly platform fee for Fin itself, but you'll need an Intercom plan (starting at $74/month for Essential) to use it. This pricing is attractive for businesses with fluctuating volumes.

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

What is Zendesk AI?

Zendesk AI is a suite of AI-powered tools integrated into the Zendesk platform. It includes chatbots, agent assistance, and analytics. Unlike Fin, which is a standalone AI agent, Zendesk AI is a collection of features aimed at augmenting human agents and automating routine tasks.

Key Features

  • AI-powered chatbots: Automate responses to common queries using intent detection.
  • Agent assist: Provides real-time suggestions and knowledge base articles to human agents.
  • Workflow automation: Automatically route tickets, tag issues, and trigger actions.
  • Analytics and insights: Uses AI to uncover trends and predict customer behavior.
  • Integration with Zendesk Suite: Native to Zendesk, so it works seamlessly with existing workflows.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually), which includes basic AI features. Advanced AI capabilities like Answer Bot require the Suite Enterprise plan ($115 per agent/month) or an add-on. Pricing is per agent, which can be costly for large teams.
