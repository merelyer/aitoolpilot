Detailed Review: Intercom Fin

What is Intercom Fin?

Intercom Fin is an AI chatbot built on GPT-4 technology, designed to resolve customer queries instantly. Unlike traditional chatbots that rely on rigid decision trees, Fin uses natural language processing to understand and respond to complex questions. It's trained on your help center articles, internal documents, and past conversations, making it highly accurate from day one.

Key Features

  • Instant Answers: Fin can answer up to 50% of support questions immediately, without human intervention.
  • Seamless Handoff: When Fin can't resolve a query, it smoothly transfers to a human agent with full context.
  • Multichannel Support: Works across chat, email, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Messenger.
  • Continuous Learning: Fin improves over time by learning from resolved conversations and agent feedback.
  • Easy Setup: Connect your knowledge base and Fin starts working in minutes—no coding required.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted only when Fin successfully resolves a customer's query without human help. This aligns costs with value—you pay for results, not seats. Intercom also offers a free trial and various plans for the broader Intercom platform (starting at $39/month for Essential).

Detailed Review: Zendesk AI

What is Zendesk AI?

Zendesk AI is a suite of AI-powered tools integrated into the Zendesk customer service platform. It includes intelligent triage, agent assistance, and a chatbot (Answer Bot) that can handle routine inquiries. Zendesk AI leverages machine learning to automate workflows, suggest responses, and route tickets efficiently.

Key Features

  • Intelligent Triage: Automatically categorizes and routes tickets to the right agents based on intent and sentiment.
  • Answer Bot: Provides instant answers to common questions using your help center content.
  • Agent Assist: Suggests responses and relevant articles to agents in real-time, boosting productivity.
  • Workflow Automation: Automates repetitive tasks and approvals to streamline support operations.
  • Analytics & Insights: Offers dashboards to track AI performance and customer satisfaction.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite starts at $55 per agent/month (Suite Growth), and AI features are included in the Suite Professional plan ($115 per agent/month) or as an add-on. There's no pay-per-resolution model; pricing is per agent seat.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI be used together?

No, they are separate platforms. Intercom Fin is exclusive to Intercom, while Zendesk AI is part of Zendesk. You'd need to choose one platform.

2. How accurate is Intercom Fin?

Intercom Fin boasts a resolution rate of up to 50% for common queries, depending on the quality of your help center content. It continuously learns from interactions to improve accuracy.

3. Does Zendesk AI offer a free trial?

Yes, Zendesk offers a free trial of its Suite, which includes AI features in higher tiers. However, there's no free tier for ongoing use.

4. Which is cheaper for small businesses?

Intercom Fin's pay-per-resolution model can be more affordable for small businesses with low ticket volumes, as you only pay for successful resolutions. Zendesk's per-agent pricing may be costlier for small teams.

5. Can I customize the chatbot's responses?

Both platforms allow some level of customization. Intercom Fin uses your help center content and allows you to review and refine responses. Zendesk AI's Answer Bot can be customized with your knowledge base and workflows.

6. Which integrates better with other tools?

Zendesk offers extensive integrations with enterprise software (CRM, ecommerce, etc.). Intercom also integrates with many popular tools but is more focused on modern messaging and support channels.
