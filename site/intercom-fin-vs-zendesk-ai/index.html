Intercom Fin Review

Intercom has been a leader in customer messaging for over a decade. With Fin, they've doubled down on AI, creating a chatbot that actually resolves tickets—not just deflects them.

What is Intercom Fin?

Fin is Intercom's AI chatbot that uses large language models (LLMs) to understand and resolve customer queries. It's built on top of Intercom's existing platform, so it integrates seamlessly with your help center, inbox, and workflows.

Key Features

  • AI-powered resolutions: Fin can handle complex, multi-turn conversations and resolve up to 51% of support tickets without human intervention.
  • Easy training: Fin automatically learns from your existing help center articles, macros, and past conversations—no coding required.
  • Seamless handoff: When Fin can't resolve a query, it escalates to a human agent with full context.
  • Multichannel support: Works across chat, email, and messaging apps like WhatsApp and Messenger.
  • Analytics: Detailed reporting on resolution rates, CSAT, and areas for improvement.

Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a pay-per-resolution model: $0.99 per resolution. A resolution is counted when Fin successfully resolves a customer's issue without human intervention. You also need an Intercom plan (starting at $74/month for Essential).

Zendesk AI Review

Zendesk is a household name in customer support. Its AI suite, Zendesk AI, is an add-on to the Zendesk Suite that brings automation, bots, and agent assistance to the platform.

What is Zendesk AI?

Zendesk AI is a collection of AI-powered tools integrated into Zendesk, including Answer Bot (for automated responses), agent assist (suggested replies), and intelligent triage (routing and prioritization).

Key Features

  • Answer Bot: Provides automated responses based on your help center content. Resolution rates hover around 30%.
  • Agent Assist: Suggests replies and next steps to human agents, improving efficiency.
  • Intelligent triage: Automatically routes tickets and detects intent, sentiment, and language.
  • Customizable workflows: Extensive options to tailor AI behavior to your processes.
  • Integration with Zendesk Suite: Native to Zendesk, so no third-party tools needed.

Pricing

Zendesk AI is available as an add-on to Zendesk Suite plans. The Suite Professional plan starts at $55 per agent/month (billed annually), and AI add-ons start at $50 per agent/month. There's no pay-per-resolution model; you pay per agent.

Pros

  • Deep integration with Zendesk
  • Highly customizable
  • Strong agent-assist features
  • Scalable for large teams

Cons

  • Lower automation rates (around 30%)
  • Complex setup and training
  • Pricing can be steep for small teams
  • AI feels bolted-on rather than native

Who Should Use What?

Choose Zendesk AI if:

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can Intercom Fin handle multiple languages?

Yes, Fin supports multiple languages and can automatically detect and respond in the customer's language.

2. How does Zendesk AI pricing work?

Zendesk AI is priced per agent per month as an add-on to your Zendesk Suite plan. The exact cost depends on your plan and contract terms.

3. Is there a free trial for these chatbots?

Both Intercom and Zendesk offer free trials (typically 14 days). Intercom Fin also offers a pilot program for qualified businesses.

4. Which platform has better AI accuracy?

Intercom Fin reports resolution rates of up to 51%, while Zendesk AI's Answer Bot resolves around 30% of tickets. However, results vary based on training data and use case.

5. Can I use Intercom Fin without Intercom's helpdesk?

No, Fin is built on Intercom and requires an Intercom subscription. However, it can integrate with other tools via APIs.

6. Does Zendesk AI work with third-party help centers?

Zendesk AI is designed to work with Zendesk Guide (its knowledge base). While you can import content from other sources, it's most effective within the Zendesk ecosystem.
