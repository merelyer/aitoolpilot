Detailed Review: Runway

What is Runway?

Runway is a cutting-edge AI video editing platform that has become synonymous with generative AI in filmmaking. Founded in 2018, Runway made waves with its Gen-2 model, which can generate video from text or images. In 2025, Runway offers a suite of AI tools that go far beyond basic editing—think AI green screen, motion tracking, and even AI-powered script-to-video.

Key Features

  • Text-to-Video Generation: Create short clips from text prompts (up to 10 seconds in Gen-2, longer with Gen-3).
  • AI Green Screen: Remove backgrounds without a physical green screen.
  • Motion Brush: Animate specific parts of an image.
  • Inpainting: Remove objects or replace them seamlessly.
  • AI Training: Train custom AI models on your own footage.
  • Collaboration: Real-time editing with team members.

Pricing

Runway offers a free tier with limited credits (125 credits one-time). Paid plans start at $12/month (billed annually) for the Standard plan, which includes 625 credits per month. The Pro plan is $28/month and includes 2,250 credits, while the Unlimited plan is $76/month for unlimited credits (with some fair use restrictions). Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros & Cons

Pros:

  • Pioneering AI features that are genuinely useful for creative projects.
  • High-quality output, especially for generative video.
  • Regular updates with new AI models.
  • Web-based, so no heavy downloads.

Cons:

  • Steep learning curve for beginners.
  • Credits can be consumed quickly with heavy AI use.
  • Limited traditional editing tools compared to dedicated NLEs.
  • Expensive for casual users.

Detailed Review: CapCut

What is CapCut?

CapCut (owned by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok) started as a mobile video editor but has grown into a full-fledged desktop and web platform. It's known for its user-friendly interface and a generous free tier that includes many AI-powered features. In 2025, CapCut is a go-to for social media creators who want to produce polished content quickly.

Key Features

  • Auto-Captions: Generate subtitles automatically with high accuracy.
  • Background Removal: Remove backgrounds without a green screen (AI-powered).
  • Text-to-Speech: Convert text to natural-sounding voiceovers.
  • Templates: Thousands of customizable templates for social media.
  • AI Effects: Fun filters and effects powered by AI.
  • Direct Publishing: Export directly to TikTok, YouTube, etc.

Pricing

CapCut is free for most features, with a Pro subscription at $7.99/month (or $74.99/year) that unlocks advanced features like cloud storage, premium effects, and higher export resolution. There's also a Team plan for $24.99/month per user.

Pros & Cons

Pros:

  • Free tier is incredibly robust—great for beginners.
  • Intuitive interface with drag-and-drop simplicity.
  • Excellent for social media content with templates and direct publishing.
  • Fast rendering and smooth performance.

Cons:

  • Limited advanced AI video generation (no text-to-video).
  • Some features require watermark or Pro subscription.
  • Less suitable for professional filmmaking.
  • Privacy concerns due to ByteDance ownership.

FAQ

1. Is Runway better than CapCut?

It depends on your needs. Runway excels at advanced AI video generation and professional editing, making it better for filmmakers and VFX artists. CapCut is better for social media content and beginners due to its ease of use and free tier.

2. Can CapCut generate video from text?

No, CapCut does not offer text-to-video generation. It focuses on editing existing footage with AI-powered enhancements like auto-captions and background removal. For text-to-video, Runway is the tool to use.

3. Which is cheaper, Runway or CapCut?

CapCut is cheaper. Its free tier is very generous, and Pro costs $7.99/month. Runway's paid plans start at $12/month, and its free tier is limited to 125 one-time credits.

4. Does CapCut have AI features?

Yes, CapCut includes AI-powered features such as auto-captions, background removal, text-to-speech, and AI effects. However, it lacks generative AI like text-to-video.

5. Can I use Runway for free?

Yes, Runway offers a free tier with 125 credits (one-time). This allows you to test the waters, but heavy use will require a paid plan.

6. Which tool is better for YouTube videos?

For YouTube, CapCut is often sufficient and more cost-effective, especially for vlogs and tutorials. For high-end cinematic content or AI-generated visuals, Runway is superior.

7. Is CapCut safe to use?

CapCut is generally safe, but as it's owned by ByteDance, some users have privacy concerns. It's always good practice to review privacy policies. Runway, being a US-based company, may be preferred by those sensitive to data privacy.
