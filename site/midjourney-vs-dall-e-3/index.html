Midjourney Review: The Artist's Choice

What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an independent AI image generator developed by Midjourney, Inc. It first launched in 2022 and has since become the gold standard for AI-generated art. Unlike DALL-E 3, Midjourney operates primarily through a Discord bot (though a web app is now available), allowing users to generate images via text prompts and refine them with parameters.

Key Features

  • Exceptional Image Quality: Midjourney v6 and the newer v7 models produce incredibly detailed, photorealistic, and artistic images with a distinct aesthetic.
  • Advanced Prompt Control: Use parameters like --ar for aspect ratio, --stylize for artistic influence, and --chaos for variation.
  • Image Prompting & Blending: Upload reference images to guide the AI, or blend multiple images together.
  • Inpainting & Outpainting: Edit specific parts of an image or extend the canvas seamlessly.
  • Community & Inspiration: The Discord server is a massive gallery of prompts and results, perfect for learning.
  • Web App: A dedicated web interface (still in beta) offers a more streamlined experience.

Pricing

Midjourney offers four subscription tiers, all with a 20% discount if you pay annually:

  • Basic: $10/month — ~200 generations, 3 concurrent jobs, no fast hours.
  • Standard: $30/month — 15 fast hours, unlimited relaxed generations, 3 concurrent jobs.
  • Pro: $60/month — 30 fast hours, unlimited relaxed, stealth mode, 12 concurrent jobs.
  • Mega: $120/month — 60 fast hours, unlimited relaxed, stealth mode, 12 concurrent jobs.

DALL-E 3 Review: The Prompt Master

What is DALL-E 3?

DALL-E 3 is OpenAI's latest image generation model, integrated directly into ChatGPT (for Plus and Enterprise users) and available for free via Microsoft Copilot (formerly Bing Image Creator). It's designed to understand natural language prompts with remarkable accuracy, making it the most user-friendly AI image generator on the market.

Key Features

  • Superior Prompt Adherence: DALL-E 3 can handle complex, multi-part prompts and generate images that match your description closely.
  • Seamless ChatGPT Integration: Generate images directly in your chat, and refine them conversationally.
  • Text Rendering: Unlike many AI generators, DALL-E 3 can render legible text within images (though not always perfect).
  • Safety & Ethics: Built-in safeguards prevent harmful or copyrighted content.
  • Free Access via Copilot: Microsoft Copilot offers DALL-E 3 for free (with some limitations).
  • API Access: Developers can integrate DALL-E 3 into their apps via OpenAI's API.

Pricing

DALL-E 3 is available through multiple channels:

  • Free: Via Microsoft Copilot (formerly Bing Image Creator) — limited to 15 boosts per week, slower generation.
  • ChatGPT Plus: $20/month — unlimited image generation (with fair use policy), faster speeds, and conversational editing.
  • API: Pay-per-image, starting at $0.040 per image (standard 1024x1024) or $0.080 for HD.

Who Should Use What?

Choose Midjourney if you:

  • Are a professional artist, designer, or content creator who needs top-tier visuals.
  • Want fine-grained control over style, aspect ratio, and artistic parameters.
  • Enjoy being part of a community and drawing inspiration from others.
  • Don't mind a learning curve (especially if using Discord).

Choose DALL-E 3 if you:

  • Are a beginner or just want to experiment with AI image generation for free.
  • Need images that accurately follow complex prompts (e.g., for presentations or mockups).
  • Prefer a simple, conversational interface (ChatGPT or Copilot).
  • Want to generate images with text (like logos or posters).
  • Are a developer looking to integrate image generation via API.

Bottom Line Verdict

In 2025, the choice between Midjourney and DALL-E 3 comes down to your priorities. Midjourney remains the undisputed champion for artistic quality, realism, and creative control. It's the tool of choice for professionals who demand the best-looking images. DALL-E 3, on the other hand, is the most accessible and prompt-accurate option, perfect for beginners and those who need reliable, context-aware results without a steep learning curve.

If you can only pick one, we recommend Midjourney for its superior output and flexibility. But if budget is tight or you value ease of use above all, DALL-E 3 via Copilot is a fantastic free alternative.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney produces higher-quality, more artistic images with advanced customization, but DALL-E 3 excels at understanding complex prompts and is more user-friendly. For pure aesthetics, Midjourney wins; for prompt accuracy, DALL-E 3 wins.

2. Can I use DALL-E 3 for free?

Yes, DALL-E 3 is available for free through Microsoft Copilot (formerly Bing Image Creator). However, free users have limited boosts and slower generation times. For unlimited access, you'll need ChatGPT Plus ($20/month).

3. Does Midjourney have a free trial?

No, Midjourney does not offer a free trial. The cheapest plan is $10/month for the Basic tier, which includes around 200 generations.

4. Which tool is better for generating text in images?

DALL-E 3 is significantly better at rendering legible text within images, thanks to its advanced language understanding. Midjourney has improved but still struggles with longer text.

5. Can I use these images commercially?

Yes, both Midjourney and DALL-E 3 allow commercial use of generated images, but you must comply with their terms. Midjourney requires a paid subscription for commercial use, while DALL-E 3's terms depend on the platform (OpenAI or Microsoft).

6. Which one is easier to use?

DALL-E 3 is much easier to use, especially via ChatGPT or Copilot, as it requires no technical knowledge. Midjourney has a steeper learning curve, particularly if you use the Discord interface.

7. Do they offer API access?

DALL-E 3 offers API access through OpenAI, allowing developers to integrate it into applications. Midjourney does not have a public API, though there are unofficial workarounds.
