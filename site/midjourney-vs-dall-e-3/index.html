Midjourney Review: The Artist's Choice

Midjourney has long been the darling of digital artists and designers. Known for its distinctive, highly aesthetic output, it consistently produces images that look like they belong in a gallery. In 2025, Midjourney continues to push boundaries with its v6 and v7 models, offering even greater realism and detail.

Key Features

  • Exceptional image quality: Midjourney's images are often indistinguishable from professional photography or digital art.
  • Artistic control: Advanced parameters like --stylize, --chaos, and --ar let you fine-tune every aspect of your generation.
  • Community and inspiration: The Discord-based platform fosters a vibrant community where you can browse and remix others' creations.
  • Continuous updates: Regular model improvements keep Midjourney at the cutting edge.

Pricing

Midjourney offers four subscription tiers:

  • Basic: $10/month – 200 generations, 3 concurrent jobs, basic features.
  • Standard: $30/month – 15 hours of fast GPU time, unlimited relax mode, 3 concurrent jobs.
  • Pro: $60/month – 30 hours fast GPU, unlimited relax, stealth mode, 12 concurrent jobs.
  • Mega: $120/month – 60 hours fast GPU, unlimited relax, stealth mode, 12 concurrent jobs.

All plans include commercial usage rights, but only Pro and Mega offer stealth mode (private generations).

DALL-E 3 Review: The Accessible Powerhouse

Developed by OpenAI, DALL-E 3 is integrated directly into ChatGPT and Bing Image Creator. Its standout feature is its ability to understand and follow detailed prompts with remarkable accuracy. If you've ever struggled to get Midjourney to produce exactly what you envisioned, DALL-E 3 might feel like a breath of fresh air.

Key Features

  • Superior prompt adherence: DALL-E 3 excels at generating images that match complex, multi-part descriptions.
  • Seamless ChatGPT integration: You can refine images through conversation, asking for tweaks in natural language.
  • Built-in safety filters: OpenAI's content policy ensures generated images are safe for most audiences.
  • Easy to use: No need to learn parameters or commands; just describe what you want.

Pricing

DALL-E 3 is available through several channels:

  • Free: Limited generations via Bing Image Creator (powered by DALL-E 3).
  • ChatGPT Plus: $20/month – includes access to DALL-E 3 with higher generation limits.
  • ChatGPT Pro: $200/month – for power users, includes even higher limits and priority access.
  • API: Pay-per-image pricing for developers (varies by resolution).

Commercial usage rights are granted for images created with DALL-E 3, but you should review OpenAI's terms for specifics.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3?

It depends on your needs. Midjourney generally produces more aesthetically pleasing and artistic images, while DALL-E 3 is better at following complex prompts accurately. For pure image quality, Midjourney often wins; for prompt adherence and ease of use, DALL-E 3 is superior.

2. Can I use Midjourney or DALL-E 3 for free?

Midjourney does not offer a free tier. DALL-E 3 can be used for free via Bing Image Creator, though with limitations. ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) provides more generations.

3. Which tool is better for commercial use?

Both allow commercial use, but you should review their terms. Midjourney requires a paid subscription for commercial rights, while DALL-E 3 grants commercial usage even for images created via Bing, but check OpenAI's policy for details.

4. Do Midjourney and DALL-E 3 have mobile apps?

Midjourney can be accessed via Discord's mobile app. DALL-E 3 is accessible through the ChatGPT mobile app and Bing app.

5. Which one is easier to learn?

DALL-E 3 is significantly easier to learn because it uses natural language prompts and doesn't require parameters. Midjourney has a steeper learning curve due to its Discord interface and advanced options.

6. Can I edit images in Midjourney or DALL-E 3?

Midjourney offers limited editing through variations, upscaling, and inpainting (via external tools). DALL-E 3 allows conversational editing within ChatGPT, but neither is a full-fledged photo editor.
