Detailed Review: Jasper

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) has been a frontrunner in AI writing since its launch in 2021. It's designed for marketing teams and businesses that need to produce high-quality, on-brand content at scale.

Key Features

  • Brand Voice: Jasper's standout feature lets you define your brand's tone, style, and vocabulary, ensuring all generated content aligns with your identity.
  • 50+ Templates: From blog posts to social media ads, Jasper offers a wide range of templates for various content types.
  • Long-Form Assistant: Helps you outline, write, and edit long-form articles with ease.
  • Team Collaboration: Multiple users can work simultaneously, with shared projects and brand voice settings.
  • Integrations: Works with Google Docs, WordPress, Surfer SEO, and more.
  • Plagiarism Checker: Built-in tool to ensure originality (available on higher plans).

Pricing

Jasper offers a 7-day free trial (no credit card required). Paid plans start at $39/month (billed annually) for the Creator plan, which includes one seat and 20+ templates. The Pro plan ($59/mo annual) adds collaboration and brand voice. The Business plan (custom pricing) offers advanced features like SSO and API access.

Pros

  • Exceptional long-form content quality
  • Powerful brand voice customization
  • Extensive template library
  • Strong team collaboration features
  • Regular updates and improvements

Cons

  • No free forever plan
  • Learning curve for advanced features
  • Word limits on lower tiers

Detailed Review: Copy.ai

Copy.ai launched around the same time as Jasper and quickly gained popularity for its user-friendly interface and focus on short-form copy. It's a favorite among solopreneurs and small businesses.

Key Features

  • 90+ Templates: Copy.ai offers a vast collection of templates for everything from product descriptions to email subject lines.
  • Free Forever Plan: Get started with up to 2,000 words per month at no cost.
  • Workflows: Automate repetitive tasks by chaining templates together.
  • Brand Voice: Available on the Pro plan and above, but less sophisticated than Jasper's.
  • Multi-Language Support: Generate content in over 25 languages.
  • Integrations: Connects with Zapier, HubSpot, and other tools.

Pricing

Copy.ai's free plan includes 2,000 words per month. The Pro plan starts at $36/month (billed annually) for unlimited words and 5 seats. The Enterprise plan offers custom pricing with advanced security and support.

Pros

  • Generous free plan
  • Very user-friendly interface
  • Large template library
  • Affordable for small teams
  • Good for short-form content

Cons

  • Long-form content quality lags behind Jasper
  • Brand voice not as robust
  • Fewer advanced collaboration features
  • Limited integrations compared to Jasper

Who Should Use What?

Choose Jasper if:

  • You're a marketing team that needs consistent brand voice across all content.
  • You produce a lot of long-form content (blog posts, whitepapers, etc.).
  • You need advanced collaboration and project management features.
  • Budget is less of a concern and quality is paramount.

Choose Copy.ai if:

  • You're a solopreneur or small business on a tight budget.
  • You primarily need short-form copy (social media, ads, product descriptions).
  • You want to test the waters with a free plan.
  • You value simplicity and ease of use.

Bottom Line

Both Jasper and Copy.ai are excellent AI writing tools, but they cater to different needs. Jasper is the clear winner for businesses and teams that require high-quality, brand-consistent content at scale. Its long-form capabilities and brand voice feature are unmatched. Copy.ai, on the other hand, is a fantastic choice for individuals and small teams who need affordable, quick copy generation.

Our verdict: If you can afford it, go with Jasper. The quality and time savings are worth the investment. Start your free Jasper trial today.

FAQ

Is Jasper better than Copy.ai?

It depends on your needs. Jasper excels at long-form content and brand voice, making it ideal for marketing teams. Copy.ai is better for short-form copy and budget-conscious users.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free forever plan with up to 2,000 words per month. Jasper only offers a 7-day free trial.

Can I use Jasper for free?

Jasper does not have a free plan, but you can try it free for 7 days without a credit card.

Which tool is better for SEO content?

Jasper is generally better for SEO content due to its long-form assistant and integration with Surfer SEO. However, Copy.ai can also produce SEO-friendly content with the right prompts.

Do both tools offer a brand voice feature?

Yes, but Jasper's brand voice is more advanced and customizable. Copy.ai offers a basic brand voice feature on its Pro plan.

Which is more affordable?

Copy.ai is more affordable, especially with its free plan and lower starting price for paid plans.
