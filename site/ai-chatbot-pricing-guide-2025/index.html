Yes, Tidio and HubSpot offer free tiers with basic chatbot functionality. ChatGPT API also has a free tier with limited usage.

Tidio is excellent for ecommerce due to its Shopify integration and affordable pricing. Intercom also offers strong ecommerce features for larger stores.

6. Do AI chatbots require coding?

Most modern platforms like Tidio and HubSpot offer no-code builders. However, custom solutions using APIs require coding.
