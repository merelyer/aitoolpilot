Intercom: Best for Scaling Support Teams

Intercom is a heavyweight in customer service, offering a unified inbox, ticketing, and a powerful AI agent called Fin. Fin can resolve up to 50% of support tickets without human intervention, but it comes at a cost. Intercom's pricing is based on seats and monthly active people (MAP), which can make costs unpredictable.

Pricing: Essential plan starts at $74/month per seat (billed annually) for up to 500 MAP. Advanced plan is $99/month per seat for up to 1,000 MAP. Fin AI is priced at $0.99 per resolution. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros: Robust feature set, excellent for large teams, Fin AI is highly effective, extensive reporting.

Cons: Expensive, especially as you scale; Fin charges per resolution, which can add up quickly.

Drift: Best for B2B Sales & Marketing

Drift is all about conversational marketing and sales. It's designed to engage website visitors, qualify leads, and book meetings automatically. The AI chatbot is part of a larger revenue acceleration platform. Pricing is steep and primarily aimed at mid-market to enterprise B2B companies.

Pricing: Premium plan starts at $2,500/month (billed annually). Advanced and Enterprise plans require custom quotes. There's no free tier, but a 14-day free trial is available.

Pros: Excellent for lead generation, strong integrations with CRM and marketing automation, advanced routing.

Cons: Very expensive, overkill for small businesses, contract lock-in.

Chatfuel: Best for Social Media Chatbots

Chatfuel specializes in building chatbots for Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp. It's a no-code platform that's popular with small businesses and creators. The AI features are basic compared to dedicated support tools, but it's a cost-effective way to automate social media interactions.

Pricing: Free plan available (limited to 1,000 contacts). Pro plan is $19.99/month for up to 2,500 contacts. Business plan is $99.99/month for up to 10,000 contacts. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Pros: Affordable, easy to use, great for social media automation, supports multiple channels.

Cons: AI capabilities are limited, not suitable for complex customer support, limited integrations.

Pros: Generous free tier, affordable, excellent for Instagram automation, easy to set up.

Cons: Limited to social media channels, AI features are basic, not a full customer support solution.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an AI chatbot cost per month?

Prices range from $0 (free tiers) to over $2,500/month for enterprise solutions. Most small businesses can expect to pay between $20 and $100 per month for a capable AI chatbot.

Is there a free AI chatbot for websites?

Yes, Tidio, Chatfuel, and ManyChat all offer free plans. They come with limitations (e.g., number of conversations or contacts), but they're a great way to start.

What is the difference between per-seat and per-conversation pricing?

Per-seat pricing charges you for each human agent using the platform, while per-conversation pricing charges based on the number of chat sessions. Per-conversation can be cheaper for small teams but unpredictable at scale.

Do AI chatbots really save money?

Yes, they can reduce support costs by automating routine inquiries. Many businesses report resolving 30-70% of tickets with AI, freeing up human agents for complex issues.

Which AI chatbot is best for e-commerce?

Tidio is our top pick for e-commerce due to its deep integrations with Shopify and WooCommerce, plus its affordable AI add-on. Intercom is also strong but pricier.

Can I use an AI chatbot for lead generation?

Absolutely. Drift and Intercom are excellent for lead gen, as they can qualify visitors and book meetings automatically. Tidio also offers lead generation features on higher plans.
