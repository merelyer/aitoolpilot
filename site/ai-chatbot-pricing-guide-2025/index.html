FAQ

How much does an AI chatbot cost?

AI chatbot pricing varies widely. Basic plans start around $15–$29 per month, while advanced platforms like Intercom and Drift can cost hundreds or thousands per month. Factors include features, number of contacts, and AI capabilities.

Is there a free AI chatbot?

Yes, some platforms like Tidio, Chatfuel, and ManyChat offer free tiers with limited features. These are great for testing the waters but may not suffice for growing businesses.

What is the best AI chatbot for small business?

Tidio is often the best for small businesses due to its affordability, ease of use, and free plan. ManyChat is also a strong contender for social media-focused businesses.

Can I build my own AI chatbot?

You can, using platforms like Dialogflow or Rasa, but it requires technical expertise. For most businesses, using a pre-built solution like Intercom or Tidio is more cost-effective and faster to deploy.

How do AI chatbots handle complex queries?

Advanced AI chatbots like Intercom's Fin can handle complex queries by pulling from knowledge bases and learning from past interactions. They can also seamlessly hand off to human agents when needed.

Do AI chatbots integrate with my existing tools?

Most modern AI chatbots integrate with popular CRMs, helpdesks, and e-commerce platforms. Check the specific integrations for each tool to ensure compatibility.
