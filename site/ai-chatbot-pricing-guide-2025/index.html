By the end, you'll know exactly which tool fits your budget and business size.

Detailed Reviews: Features, Pricing, and Pros & Cons

1. Tidio – Best Overall for Small Businesses

Tidio combines live chat and AI chatbot (Lyro) in one platform. It's designed for small to mid-sized businesses that want to automate support without hiring a developer. The free plan includes 50 chatbot conversations per month, which is enough to test the waters.

Pricing: Starter at $29/month (billed annually) for 100 chatbot conversations; Growth at $59/month for 500 conversations; Plus at $749/month for 5,000 conversations. Lyro AI is available as an add-on starting at $39/month for 100 conversations.

Pros: Easy setup; generous free tier; Lyro AI resolves up to 64% of queries automatically; integrates with Shopify, WordPress, and more.

Cons: Conversation limits can be restrictive; Lyro AI costs extra; no phone support on lower tiers.

2. Chatfuel – Best for No-Code Facebook & Instagram Bots

Chatfuel is a veteran in the chatbot space, focusing on Facebook Messenger and Instagram automation. It's ideal for small businesses that want to build a bot without coding. The AI-powered bot can handle FAQs, qualify leads, and even process payments.

Pricing: Free plan includes 50 contacts and basic features; Pro at $19.99/month (annual) for 500 contacts; Premium at $59.99/month for 1,000 contacts; Enterprise custom pricing. Note: Contact limits apply to users who interact with your bot.

Pros: Intuitive drag-and-drop builder; strong Facebook/Instagram integration; built-in e-commerce features.

Cons: Limited to Meta platforms; contact-based pricing can get expensive as you grow; AI features are basic compared to competitors.

3. ManyChat – Best for Conversational Marketing

ManyChat is a powerhouse for social media automation, especially on Instagram and Messenger. It's popular among creators, e-commerce brands, and marketers who want to engage audiences with automated DMs, comment replies, and story mentions.

Pricing: Free plan for up to 1,000 contacts; Pro at $15/month for 1,000 contacts (annual); additional contacts cost $10 per 1,000. ManyChat also offers an AI add-on for $29/month.

Pros: Affordable for small lists; excellent for Instagram growth; robust automation and segmentation.

Cons: Limited to social channels (no website chat); AI features are add-on; can feel complex for beginners.

4. Drift – Best for B2B Sales Teams

Drift pioneered conversational marketing and remains a strong choice for B2B companies focused on lead generation. Its AI chatbot can qualify leads, book meetings, and route conversations to sales reps. However, it's not for the faint of budget.

Pricing: Premium starts at $2,500/month (annual) for 1,000 conversations; Advanced at $3,500/month; Enterprise custom. There's no free tier, and you'll need to talk to sales for a quote.

Pros: Powerful lead qualification; seamless CRM integration; strong analytics.

Cons: Very expensive for small businesses; annual contracts only; steep learning curve.

5. Intercom – Best for Enterprise Customer Support

Intercom is a full-fledged customer service platform with a powerful AI agent (Fin) that can resolve up to 50% of support queries. It's designed for larger teams that need a scalable, multi-channel solution.

Pricing: Essential at $74/month per seat (annual) for 500 AI resolutions; Advanced at $115/month per seat; Expert at $499/month per seat. Fin AI is included in all plans, but resolution limits apply.

Pros: All-in-one platform; Fin AI is highly accurate; extensive integrations.

Cons: Per-seat pricing gets expensive; overage fees for AI resolutions; complex setup.
