Detailed Review: Otter.ai

Otter.ai has been a household name in transcription for years. It’s known for its live captioning and collaborative features that make it feel like a shared Google Doc for your meetings.

Key Features

  • Live transcription & captions: Real-time transcription with speaker identification, perfect for virtual meetings or in-person brainstorming sessions.
  • Collaborative notes: Multiple users can highlight, comment, and add images to notes during the meeting—great for team alignment.
  • Otter Assistant: Automatically joins and records meetings from Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams (on paid plans).
  • Otter Chat: Ask questions about your meeting content and get answers with cited moments (Pro plan).
  • Mobile app: Solid iOS and Android apps with voice recording and playback.

Pricing

  • Free: 300 minutes/month, 30-minute per conversation cap.
  • Pro ($16.99/user/month): 1,200 minutes/month, advanced search, custom vocabulary.
  • Business ($30/user/month): 6,000 minutes/user/month, admin controls, SSO.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing with unlimited minutes.

Pros

  • Excellent live collaboration features
  • Intuitive mobile app
  • Accurate speaker identification
  • Strong free tier for casual users

Cons

  • Paid plans have minute caps (Pro 1,200 min/month)
  • AI summaries are less detailed than Fireflies
  • No native CRM integrations (requires Zapier)
  • Uploaded files count against monthly minutes

Detailed Review: Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is a newer, but rapidly growing player. It’s designed with sales teams in mind, offering deep CRM integrations and a robust AI notetaker that goes beyond simple transcription.

Key Features

  • Unlimited transcription on paid plans: No minute caps—record as many meetings as you want.
  • AI summaries & action items: Automatically generates meeting summaries, action items, and even sentiment analysis.
  • CRM integrations: Native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and others—auto-log calls, update contacts.
  • Conversation intelligence: Tracks talk-to-listen ratio, keyword mentions, and customer sentiment for sales coaching.
  • Fireflies Chat: Ask questions about meeting transcripts and get answers with timestamps.
  • Channel-based organization: Organize meetings by project or team using channels.

Pricing

  • Pro ($10/user/month): Unlimited transcription, AI summaries, integrations, and more.
  • Business ($19/user/month): Advanced analytics, privacy controls, and priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing with SSO and dedicated support.

Pros

  • Unlimited transcription on paid plans
  • More affordable than Otter Pro
  • Powerful CRM integrations
  • Detailed AI summaries and insights
  • Generous free tier (800 min/month)

Cons

  • Live collaboration features are less polished than Otter

Who Should Use What?

Choose Fireflies.ai if you:

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can Otter and Fireflies transcribe meetings from Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams?

Yes, both tools can join and transcribe meetings on these platforms. Otter requires a paid plan for automatic joining; Fireflies offers this on free and paid plans (with limits on free).

2. Which tool has better AI summaries?

Fireflies generally provides more detailed and structured summaries, including action items and sentiment analysis. Otter’s summaries are decent but less comprehensive.

3. Can I use Otter or Fireflies to transcribe podcasts or pre-recorded files?

Both allow you to upload audio/video files for transcription. Otter counts these against your monthly minutes, while Fireflies also counts them (but on paid plans, transcription is unlimited).

4. Are there any free options that are actually useful?

Yes, both have free tiers. Fireflies offers 800 minutes/month (about 13 hours) which is generous for casual use. Otter’s free tier gives 300 minutes/month (5 hours) with a 30-minute per conversation cap, which can be limiting.

5. Which tool is better for sales teams?

Fireflies is clearly better for sales. It has native CRM integrations, conversation intelligence (talk-to-listen ratio, sentiment), and automated call logging. Otter lacks these features out of the box.

6. Can I share meeting notes with others who don’t have an account?

Otter allows sharing via a public link, but viewers need to create an account to view. Fireflies allows sharing via a link, but viewers may need to sign in to access full features.

7. Is my data secure with these tools?

Both offer encryption in transit and at rest, and comply with GDPR. Fireflies offers additional privacy controls on Business plans, including the ability to restrict access to transcripts.
