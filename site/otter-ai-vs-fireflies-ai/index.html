Detailed Review of Otter.ai

Otter.ai has been a pioneer in AI meeting transcription since its inception. It's known for its real-time transcription and live captioning features, making it a favorite for those who need instant access to what's being said.

Key Features

  • Live Transcription: Otter provides real-time transcription during meetings, which is incredibly useful for hearing-impaired users or for those who want to follow along.
  • Automatic Meeting Notes: It automatically generates meeting summaries, action items, and key points, which are then searchable.
  • Zoom Integration: Otter integrates natively with Zoom, allowing it to join meetings automatically and transcribe them.
  • Speaker Identification: It can distinguish between different speakers, which is essential for interviews and panel discussions.
  • Otter Assistant: This feature can join meetings on your behalf, take notes, and share them with your team.

Pricing

Otter.ai offers a free tier that includes 300 minutes of transcription per month, with a limit of 30 minutes per conversation. The Pro plan costs $16.99 per user per month (billed annually) and includes 1,200 minutes of transcription per month, plus advanced features like custom vocabulary and priority support. The Business plan is priced at $30 per user per month (billed annually) and offers admin controls, usage analytics, and more.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Excellent live captioning, native Zoom integration, user-friendly interface, mobile apps for iOS and Android.
  • Cons: Limited transcription minutes on the free tier, no on-premise deployment, occasional inaccuracies with heavy accents or technical jargon.

Detailed Review of Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is a powerful AI meeting assistant that focuses on not just transcription, but also on meeting analytics and team collaboration. It's designed to work across multiple platforms and offers a more extensive set of integrations.

Key Features

  • Multi-Platform Support: Fireflies works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and even dial-in calls.
  • AI-Powered Search: You can search across all your meetings using natural language queries, such as "What did we decide about the marketing budget?"
  • Meeting Analytics: Fireflies provides sentiment analysis, talk-to-listen ratios, and other metrics that are useful for sales teams.
  • Integration with CRM: It integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs, allowing you to automatically log calls and update records.
  • Conversation Intelligence: This feature highlights key moments, such as objections, questions, and action items, making it easier to review important parts of a meeting.

Pricing

Fireflies.ai also has a free tier with 800 minutes of transcription per month (about 10 hours), which is more generous than Otter's. The Pro plan costs $10 per user per month (billed annually) and includes unlimited transcription, advanced integrations, and more. The Business plan is $19 per user per month (billed annually) and adds features like custom bots, API access, and priority support.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: More generous free tier, extensive integrations, powerful analytics, supports multiple meeting platforms, and offers a mobile app.
  • Cons: The interface can be overwhelming for new users, and some advanced features are only available on higher-tier plans.

Who Should Use What

Choosing between Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai largely depends on your specific use case. Here's a breakdown:

  • If you are a Zoom power user: Otter.ai's native integration with Zoom is seamless. It can automatically join meetings, provide live captions, and generate notes without any extra setup. If you live in Zoom, Otter is your best bet.
  • If you need robust integrations with CRM and other tools: Fireflies.ai shines here. With its integration with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack, sales teams can automatically log calls and get insights without manual data entry.
  • If you want a more generous free tier: Fireflies offers 800 minutes per month compared to Otter's 300. If you have a lot of meetings but a tight budget, Fireflies is the clear winner.
  • If you need real-time live captioning: Otter's live captions are more accurate and faster than Fireflies. This is crucial for accessibility or for following along in real-time.
  • If you value meeting analytics: Fireflies provides detailed analytics like talk-to-listen ratio and sentiment analysis, which are invaluable for sales coaching and team performance.

Bottom Line Verdict

Both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai are excellent AI meeting assistants, but they cater to slightly different needs. Otter.ai is perfect for individuals and teams that heavily rely on Zoom and need accurate live captions. Its simplicity and ease of use make it a great choice for personal note-taking.

On the other hand, Fireflies.ai is a more comprehensive solution for teams that require extensive integrations, meeting analytics, and a more affordable price point. Its ability to work across multiple platforms and its superior CRM integrations make it a more versatile tool for modern businesses.

In our book, Fireflies.ai wins overall due to its better value for money, more generous free tier, and robust feature set. However, if you're a Zoom loyalist, Otter.ai won't disappoint.

FAQ: Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai

1. Which is more accurate: Otter.ai or Fireflies.ai?

In our testing, Fireflies.ai generally provides slightly better transcription accuracy, especially with multiple speakers and background noise. However, Otter.ai is not far behind and excels in live captioning scenarios.

2. Can both tools transcribe meetings in languages other than English?

Yes, both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai support multiple languages. Otter supports English, Spanish, French, and more, while Fireflies supports over 60 languages. However, accuracy may vary depending on the language and accent.

3. Do these tools work with Microsoft Teams?

Yes, both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai have integrations with Microsoft Teams. However, Fireflies' integration is more seamless, allowing it to join Teams meetings directly with a single click.

4. Can I record meetings with these tools?

Yes, both tools allow you to record meetings. Otter.ai records audio and video (if the platform supports it), while Fireflies.ai also offers recording capabilities with optional video capture.

5. Are there any security concerns with these AI meeting assistants?

Both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai take security seriously, offering encryption in transit and at rest. However, Fireflies.ai provides more advanced security features like single sign-on (SSO) and compliance with SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR on higher-tier plans.

6. Can I use these tools for free?

Yes, both offer free tiers. Otter.ai provides 300 minutes per month, while Fireflies.ai offers 800 minutes per month. The free tiers are great for trying out the tools, but they have limitations on features and integrations.

7. Which tool is better for sales teams?

Fireflies.ai is generally better for sales teams due to its CRM integrations and conversation intelligence features. It can automatically log calls, track objections, and provide coaching insights, making it a valuable tool for sales managers.
