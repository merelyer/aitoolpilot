Copy.ai Plans: What You Get at Each Price Point

1. Free Plan ($0/month)

Copy.ai’s free tier is one of the most generous in the industry. You get 2,000 words per month, access to over 90 copywriting tools (like email subject lines, product descriptions, and social media captions), and the ability to use the tool in more than 25 languages. You also get 1 seat and access to the Copy.ai community.

Pros:

  • No credit card required
  • Solid word count for testing
  • All core features included

Cons:

  • No access to GPT-4 models (only GPT-3.5)
  • No brand voice customization
  • No collaboration features

2. Starter Plan ($49/month)

The Starter plan (formerly Pro) is designed for individuals and small teams. At $49/month (billed annually), you get unlimited words, 5 seats, and access to GPT-4 and Claude 3 models. You also get brand voice customization, chat support, and the ability to create custom workflows (called “Workflows”).

Pros:

  • Unlimited word generation
  • Multiple seats for team collaboration
  • Access to advanced AI models

Cons:

  • No free trial of this plan (only free plan)
  • Some users report occasional output quality issues

3. Advanced Plan ($249/month)

The Advanced plan is aimed at scaling teams. Priced at $249/month (billed annually), it includes everything in Starter, plus 20 seats, priority support, and advanced workflow automation with API access. You also get custom brand voice and style guides.

Pros:

  • More seats for larger teams
  • API access for integrations
  • Priority support

Cons:

  • Expensive for small businesses
  • May be overkill if you only need basic copy

4. Enterprise Plan (Custom Pricing)

For large organizations, Copy.ai offers a custom enterprise plan. Pricing is not publicly listed, but you can expect to pay $1,000+ per month depending on your needs. This plan includes unlimited seats, SSO/SAML, dedicated account manager, custom data retention, and advanced security compliance (SOC 2, GDPR).

Pros:

  • Tailored to enterprise needs
  • Enhanced security and compliance

Cons:

  • Must contact sales for pricing
  • Not suitable for individuals or small teams

Who Should Use What: Matching Tools to Use Cases

For Solo Entrepreneurs & Freelancers

If you’re just starting out or only need occasional copy help, Copy.ai’s Free Plan is a no-brainer. You can generate product descriptions, social posts, and email subject lines without spending a cent. As you grow, upgrading to the Starter plan for unlimited words is a cost-effective way to scale.

For Marketing Teams

Teams that need consistent brand voice and collaboration should consider Copy.ai’s Advanced Plan or Jasper’s Pro plan. Copy.ai’s workflow automation is a standout feature—you can create templates that pull data from your CRM and generate personalized outreach at scale.

For Agencies

Agencies managing multiple clients will benefit from Copy.ai’s multi-seat plans. The Advanced plan’s 20 seats allow you to onboard clients without extra costs. Alternatively, Writesonic’s lower entry price might be appealing if you’re primarily writing SEO articles.

For Enterprise

If you have strict security and compliance requirements, Copy.ai Enterprise is a strong choice. SSO, audit logs, and custom data retention give you control. However, if you need advanced brand voice training, Jasper’s enterprise offering is worth a look.

Bottom Line: Is Copy.ai Worth It in 2025?

Copy.ai remains a top contender in the AI writing space, especially for marketing copy. Its free plan is generous, and the Starter plan offers excellent value at $49/month for unlimited words. The main downside is that it can be pricey for advanced features, and some users find the output quality inconsistent compared to Jasper.

If you’re a solo user or small team focused on short-form copy, Copy.ai is a solid investment. For long-form content or heavy SEO needs, you might be better served by Writesonic or Jasper.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Copy.ai free?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a free plan with 2,000 words per month and access to all core features. No credit card is required.

How much does Copy.ai cost after the free trial?

Copy.ai doesn’t have a traditional free trial. Instead, the free plan is permanent. Paid plans start at $49/month for the Starter plan (billed annually).

Does Copy.ai offer a student discount?

No, Copy.ai does not currently offer a student discount. However, the free plan is sufficient for most student projects.

Can I cancel my Copy.ai subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you’ll have access to paid features until the end of your billing cycle.

Is Copy.ai better than ChatGPT for copywriting?

Copy.ai is specifically designed for marketing copy and includes templates and workflows that make it easier to generate consistent, on-brand content. ChatGPT is more general-purpose and may require more prompt engineering. For most marketers, Copy.ai is more user-friendly.

What is the difference between Copy.ai Starter and Advanced?

The Advanced plan adds more seats (20 vs. 5), API access, priority support, and advanced workflow automation. It’s designed for larger teams and businesses that need deeper integrations.

Does Copy.ai have an annual discount?

Yes, Copy.ai offers a discount for annual billing. The Starter plan is $49/month billed annually, while monthly billing is $49/month (no discount). Actually, the monthly price is $49 as well, but annual billing gives you 2 months free (i.e., $49/month for 12 months vs. $49/month for 10 months). Check the current pricing page for exact figures.
