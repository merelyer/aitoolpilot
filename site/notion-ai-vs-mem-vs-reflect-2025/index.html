Notion AI Review

Notion started as a flexible workspace for notes, databases, and project management. In 2023, it added Notion AI—a suite of AI features baked right into the editor. By 2025, Notion AI has evolved into a true productivity powerhouse.

Key Features

  • AI Writing Assistant: Generate summaries, fix grammar, change tone, translate, and more—all without leaving your page.
  • Q&A: Ask questions about your entire workspace and get instant answers with links to source pages.
  • AI Autofill: Automatically populate database properties based on page content.
  • Connected Apps: Pull data from Slack, Google Drive, and GitHub into your Notion AI queries.
  • Custom AI Blocks: Build reusable AI prompts to streamline repetitive tasks.

Pricing

Notion offers a free plan for personal use. The AI add-on costs $8 per member per month (billed annually) or $10 monthly. It's available on any paid plan (Plus, Business, Enterprise) and can also be added to the free plan for a single user.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Extremely versatile; excellent collaboration; generous free tier; AI is deeply integrated; huge template library.
  • Cons: AI is an add-on cost; can be overwhelming for simple note-taking; no native offline mode (though it works offline in the desktop app).

Mem Review

Mem positions itself as "the self-organizing workspace." It uses AI to automatically tag, link, and surface related notes, so you spend less time organizing and more time thinking.

Key Features

  • Auto-Organization: AI automatically tags notes and suggests connections.
  • Mem Chat: Ask questions about your notes and get answers with citations.
  • Smart Search: Finds relevant notes even if you don't remember exact keywords.
  • AI Writing: Generate drafts, summaries, and ideas directly in the editor.
  • Daily Notes: A quick capture inbox that AI sorts for you.

Pricing

Mem offers a free plan with limited AI features. Mem X (the paid plan) costs $10 per month (billed annually) or $12 monthly. It includes unlimited AI, advanced search, and priority support.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Truly effortless organization; fast capture; strong AI search; clean interface.
  • Cons: Limited collaboration features; no database views; less flexible for project management; AI can sometimes mis-tag.

Reflect Review

Reflect is a networked note-taking app that combines daily notes, backlinks, and AI to help you think better. It's designed for individuals who want a beautiful, fast, and private space for their thoughts.

Key Features

  • AI Summaries: Get concise summaries of long notes or meeting transcripts.
  • AI Q&A: Ask questions across your entire knowledge graph.
  • Daily Notes: A dedicated space for daily journaling and quick capture.
  • Backlinks & Graph: See how ideas connect visually.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Your notes are private and secure.

Pricing

Reflect costs $10 per month (billed annually) or $12 monthly. There's a 14-day free trial, but no free tier.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Gorgeous, distraction-free interface; excellent AI integration; strong privacy; fast performance.
  • Cons: No free plan; limited collaboration; no databases or project views; mobile app is basic.

Who Should Use What?

  • Choose Notion AI if: You want an all-in-one workspace for notes, projects, and collaboration. It's ideal for teams, freelancers, and anyone who needs databases, kanban boards, and wikis alongside AI.
  • Choose Mem if: You're a solo knowledge worker who wants AI to automatically organize your notes. Perfect for researchers, writers, and students who capture lots of ideas.
  • Choose Reflect if: You value a beautiful, private, and fast note-taking experience with networked thought. Great for journaling, deep thinking, and personal knowledge management.

Bottom Line

There's no one-size-fits-all answer, but for the majority of users, Notion AI offers the best balance of features, flexibility, and value. Its free plan lets you test the waters, and the AI add-on is reasonably priced for what you get. That said, if you're a solo note-taker who hates organizing, Mem is a worthy contender. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion AI free?

Notion AI is not entirely free. While Notion's core app has a free plan, the AI features require an add-on subscription of $8 per member per month (billed annually). However, you can try AI features for free in a limited capacity.

Can I use Mem offline?

Mem is primarily a web-based app and requires an internet connection. There is no dedicated offline mode, though the mobile app may cache recent notes.

Does Reflect have a free plan?

No, Reflect does not offer a free plan. It provides a 14-day free trial, after which you must subscribe for $10 per month (billed annually).

Which app has the best AI for note-taking?

It depends on your needs. Notion AI excels at writing assistance and workspace Q&A. Mem shines at auto-organization and smart search. Reflect offers excellent AI summaries and Q&A within a networked note system. For most, Notion AI is the most versatile.

Can I collaborate with others in Mem or Reflect?

Mem and Reflect are primarily designed for individual use. They offer limited sharing options (e.g., public links) but lack real-time collaborative editing and team workspaces. Notion AI, on the other hand, is built for collaboration.

Which app is best for students?

Students on a budget might prefer Notion AI due to its free plan and educational discounts. However, Mem's auto-organization can be a lifesaver for research-heavy subjects. Reflect is great for focused writing but comes at a cost.
