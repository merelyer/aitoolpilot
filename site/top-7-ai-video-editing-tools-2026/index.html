2. Adobe Firefly Video

Adobe’s AI suite finally includes a dedicated video editor that leverages Firefly’s generative models.

  • AI scene detection and auto‑reframe for multiple aspect ratios.
  • Generative text overlays that adapt style to the video’s mood.
  • Integrated with Creative Cloud libraries.

3. Descript Overdub Video

Best known for audio, Descript now adds AI video editing that syncs directly with its transcript engine.

  • Instant video cuts by editing the transcript.
  • AI voice‑over (Overdub) for dubbing.
  • Screen‑recording + auto‑highlights.

Pricing: $12/mo (Creator), $24/mo (Pro), $48/mo (Enterprise).

Pros: Perfect for content repurposing, simple UI.

Cons: Limited visual effects, relies heavily on good transcription.

4. Pictory AI

Automates turning long‑form content into short social clips.

  • AI script extraction from webinars.
  • Automatic captioning in 30+ languages.
  • Template‑driven branding.

Pricing: $19/mo (Standard), $39/mo (Premium), custom Enterprise.

Pros: Saves hours on repurposing, excellent subtitle accuracy.

Cons: Less control over frame‑by‑frame edits.

5. Magisto Business

Owned by Vimeo, Magisto focuses on quick marketing videos.

  • AI storyboard suggestions based on industry.
  • Music licensing library.
  • Analytics dashboard for video performance.

Pricing: $15/mo (Basic), $35/mo (Professional), $79/mo (Business).

Pros: Very fast turn‑around, built‑in distribution tools.

Cons: Limited customization, watermarks on the free tier.

6. Veed.io AI Suite

A browser‑based editor that added AI modules in 2025.

  • Auto‑subtitle generation with speaker identification.
  • AI background blur and replace.
  • Real‑time collaborative editing.

Pricing: $16/mo (Pro), $30/mo (Team), $99/mo (Enterprise).

Pros: No software install, strong collaboration.

Cons: Rendering can be slower on large 4K files.

7. Clipchamp AI (Microsoft)

Microsoft’s free video editor now includes AI‑driven trimming and auto‑caption.

  • Direct export to OneDrive and Teams.

Pricing: Free tier with watermark, Premium $9/mo, Business $25/mo.

Pros: Very beginner‑friendly, integrates with Microsoft 365.
