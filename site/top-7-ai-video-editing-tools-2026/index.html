2. Descript Overdub Video

  • AI transcription with auto‑captioning.
  • Overdub voice cloning for narration.
  • Simple drag‑and‑drop timeline.
  • Screen‑record + video editing in one app.

Pricing: Free (up to 3 hrs transcription), Creator $15/mo, Pro $30/mo, Enterprise custom.

3. Adobe Firefly Video

  • Style‑transfer filters powered by Firefly.
  • Collaboration tools for teams.

Pricing: $49.99/mo (includes Firefly credits), $99.99/mo for Creative Cloud All Apps.

4. Pictory AI

  • Turn long‑form content into short clips automatically.

Pricing: Free (5 min per month), Starter $19/mo, Premium $39/mo, Business $79/mo.
