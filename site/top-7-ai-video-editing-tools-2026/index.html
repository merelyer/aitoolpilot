FAQ

  • What is the difference between AI video editing and traditional editing? AI video editing uses machine‑learning models to automate tasks like cutting, color correction, subtitle generation, and even generating new footage from text prompts, whereas traditional editing requires manual keyframing and manual adjustments for each step.
  • Can I use AI video editors on a Mac, PC, or mobile? Most of the tools listed are cloud‑based and work in a browser, so they are OS‑agnostic. Runway, Adobe Firefly, and Descript also offer native desktop apps for macOS and Windows. Veed.io and Clipchamp have mobile‑optimized interfaces.
  • Do AI video editors keep my data private? Reputable services (Runway, Adobe, Descript) comply with ISO 27001, GDPR, and CCPA. Always review the privacy policy, especially if you upload client footage.
  • How accurate are AI‑generated subtitles? Accuracy varies by language and audio quality. Platforms like Descript and Veed.io report 95%+ accuracy for clear English speech; other languages may range from 85‑92%.
  • Is there a free version I can test before buying? Yes. Runway, Veed.io, Clipchamp, and Kapwing all offer free tiers with limited export minutes or watermarks. Descript provides a free creator plan with 30 minutes of transcription.
